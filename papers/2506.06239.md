## Technical note

MTMH (Multi-Task Multi-Head) from Meta is an item-to-item (I2I) retrieval model that jointly optimizes recall and semantic relevance, addressing the trade-off where co-engagement-based models achieve high recall but poor relevance while content-encoder models are relevant but have <1% recall. It combines a multi-task loss (co-engagement loss for recall plus a semantic-relevance loss distilling knowledge from a pre-trained content encoder) with a multi-head architecture: an engagement head for co-engaged items and a relevance head trained on the multi-task loss for both relevant and co-engaged items. On billion-user proprietary data MTMH improves recall by up to 14.4% and semantic relevance by up to 56.6%, and live experiments show gains in both consumption and long-term user-experience metrics like novel-interest discovery and diversity.
