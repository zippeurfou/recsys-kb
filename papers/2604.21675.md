## Technical note

CM-DCM (Counterfactual Multi-task Delayed Conversion Model, Alibaba) is, to the authors' knowledge, the first CVR method for the e-commerce sales pre-promotion phase, where users browse and add-to-cart but delay purchases to the promotion day (~45% of promotion-day orders originate from pre-promotion clicks), producing an out-of-distribution shift and a skewed delay distribution that break standard delayed-feedback models assuming continuous delay windows. It combines a multi-task architecture jointly modeling direct and delayed conversions from historical pre-promotion data, a personalized gated transfer module (inspired by PEPNet) to mitigate data sparsity, and a counterfactual Doubly-Robust causal regularizer enforcing the causal effect of add-to-cart on delayed conversion. It outperforms SOTA delayed-CVR models offline and, in online A/B tests during major promotions, improved advertising revenue, delayed-conversion GMV, and overall GMV.
