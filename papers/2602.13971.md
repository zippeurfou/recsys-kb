## Technical note

DAIAN (Deep Adaptive Intent-Aware Network, Taobao/Xianyu) targets intent myopia in Trigger-Induced Recommendation, where CTR models over-focus on items strongly relevant to the clicked trigger. It models user intent as a probability distribution over similarity levels to the trigger (learned by equal-width binning cosine similarity of Qwen-VL multimodal embeddings), extracts diverse intents via Implicit Intent Exploration (multi-head target attention) and Explicit Intent Exploitation, and reinforces sparse collaborative signals with a Hybrid Similarity Enhancer that fuses ID and semantic multimodal similarity plus an intent-aware adaptive shifting network; a three-stage training strategy stabilizes convergence. On the Xianyu and Alimama datasets DAIAN beats DIN/DIEN/DIHN/DIAN/DEI2N (e.g. +5.75% AUC, +8.65% GAUC RelaImpr over DIN on Xianyu), and online it delivered +1.59% CTR, +1.73% recommendation diversity, and +2.37% bills.
