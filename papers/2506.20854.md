## Technical note

This Meta paper extends counterfactual learning to rank (CLTR) to two-stage ranking systems (candidate generator plus re-ranker), whereas prior CLTR assumed a single-stage top-K policy or only trained the candidate generator with the ranker fixed. They propose a two-stage CLTR estimator that accounts for the interaction between the two stages and estimates the joint value of both policies offline via inverse propensity scoring, plus a joint alternating optimization method to train candidate generator and re-ranker together. This mitigates distribution shift when a new candidate generator is deployed; semi-synthetic benchmark experiments show the joint method outperforms independently-trained two-stage baselines.
