## Summary

This paper introduces ChatShopBuddy, an RL-optimized conversational shopping agent designed to handle complex, interdependent objectives like correctness, persuasiveness, and efficiency. To achieve this, the authors developed SmartShopBench, a hierarchical benchmark, and Hierarchical Reward Modeling (HRM) to structure mixed rewards logically. They also proposed Dynamic Contrastive Policy Optimization (DCPO) for efficient training, resulting in an agent that outperformed larger models by achieving superior stability across various metrics.

## Technical note

ChatShopBuddy applies RL post-training to LLM conversational shopping agents that must jointly optimize product correctness, persuasiveness, structural coherence, and tool-call efficiency. Hierarchical Reward Modeling (HRM) uses conditional gating so an L1 correctness grader must pass before L2 quality and process (tool-efficiency) rewards are granted, preventing reward hacking from eloquent-but-wrong responses. Dynamic Contrastive Policy Optimization (DCPO) over-samples K trajectories per query and selects K/2 by joint utility of reward score and reasoning length to promote concise reasoning and lower deployment latency. The SmartShopBench benchmark spans six query types (fuzzy, multi-constraint, bundle, general search plus QA-compare and QA-consultation). Empirically RL mainly improved response stability rather than peak performance, and longer reasoning alone did not help on this domain task.
