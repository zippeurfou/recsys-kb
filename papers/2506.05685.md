## Technical note

NGA (Non-autoregressive Generative Auction with global externalities) from Meituan is an end-to-end ad-auction framework for industrial online advertising that models global externalities by jointly capturing dependencies among ads and effects of adjacent organic content, overcoming GSP's separability assumption and autoregressive generative auctions' latency. It uses a non-autoregressive constraint-based decoding strategy and a parallel multi-tower evaluator for unified list-wise reward and payment computation while preserving incentive compatibility and individual rationality. Extensive offline experiments and large-scale online A/B tests show NGA consistently outperforms existing methods in both effectiveness and efficiency.
