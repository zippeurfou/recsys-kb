In recent years, social media users have spent significant amounts of time on short-form video platforms. As a result, established platforms in other domains, such as e-commerce, have begun introducing short-form video content to engage users and increase their time spent on the platform. ## Technical note

This Zalando paper builds short-form video recommendations for an e-commerce setting where the platform actively recommends single videos (swipe UI) rather than offering a list of choices, tackling cold-start and bias challenges. It uses multimodal content embeddings to represent videos and address cold-start for new content, combined with debiasing methods to counter exposure/selection bias inherent to the actively-recommended single-item interface. The approach targets increasing user time-spent on newly-introduced short-form video experiences (best-effort from abstract; fulltext is abstract-fallback).
