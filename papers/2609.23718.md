## Summary

1.  Industrial feed recommendation systems face challenges with unstable candidate retrieval due to hierarchical quantization and information loss between separate retrieval and ranking stages.
2.  To address these issues, the paper introduces UNIQUE, a unified framework that combines retrieval and ranking into a single early-fusion model using flat quantization and generative code-based techniques, preserving efficiency while allowing end-to-end training.
3.  UNIQUE features a balanced quantization mechanism to improve long-tail item representation, and its deployment shows significant real-world performance gains (around 1% increase in key metrics) and high efficiency with low latency.

## Technical note

Mobile Baidu's feed pipeline suffered from hierarchical quantization instability in code-based retrieval and information loss between separate retrieval and ranking stages, hurting long-tail and cold-start content. UNIQUE unifies generative code-based retrieval and target-aware ranking in one early-fusion architecture trained end to end on a shared representation, using feedback-aware single-layer flat quantization with a balanced-quantization mechanism that mitigates codebook imbalance. Deployed since February 2026 in the homepage feed, discovery page and short-video scenarios, a one-week online A/B test against the previous retrieval pipeline gave +0.96% total watch duration, +1.08% total distribution volume and +0.70% user retention (p<0.05), with larger gains for new and highly active users. UNIQUE supplies 22.5% of the retrieval candidate pool and serves at 89 ms P99 latency with 44.23% online inference MFU.
