## Technical note

IDProxy tackles item cold-start CTR prediction at Xiaohongshu by using multimodal large language models (MLLMs) to generate proxy item embeddings from content signals for new items lacking usage data. Through a lightweight coarse-to-fine mechanism, these proxies are explicitly aligned with the existing item ID embedding space (which shows irregular, non-clustered structure in industrial systems) and optimized end-to-end under CTR objectives jointly with the ranking model, exploiting multi-layer MLLM hidden representations and reusing the mature ranking architecture. Deployed in both Content Feed and Display Ads of Xiaohongshu's Explore Feed serving hundreds of millions of users daily, with offline and online A/B improvements.
