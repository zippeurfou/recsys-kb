## Summary

MOON is a comprehensive system for multimodal representation learning deployed across Taobao's search advertising stages, significantly boosting performance. Its core contribution is a three-stage training paradigm ("Pretraining, Post-training, Application") and the concept of "exchange rate" to measure the translation between intermediate metrics like image recall and downstream task gains like CTR prediction. Over three years and five iterations, MOON evolved substantially through data processing, training, model, and application improvements, achieving a 20% online CTR uplift and sharing valuable insights into multimodal scaling laws.

## Technical note

MOON is a three-year, five-iteration industrial practice report on multimodal representation learning for CTR prediction, deployed across all stages (retrieval, relevance, ranking) of Taobao search advertising for +20% online CTR. It argues against naive end-to-end joint training of multimodal and CTR models and instead pioneers a multi-stage, decoupled 'Pretraining, Post-training, Application' paradigm to bridge the objective misalignment between representation learning and downstream ranking. It introduces an 'exchange rate' metric to quantify how intermediate gains translate downstream, identifying image-based search recall as the key intermediate signal, and studies scaling laws over training tokens, negative samples, and user-behavior sequence length. It also details scalable infrastructure spanning production to consumption of multimodal representations.
