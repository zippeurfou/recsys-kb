## Technical note

MOON (Taobao) is a three-year set of iterative practices for multimodal representation learning deployed across all stages (retrieval, relevance, ranking) of Taobao search advertising, delivering an overall +20.00% online CTR improvement over five full-scale iterations. Rejecting naive end-to-end joint training of multimodal and CTR models, it pioneers a decoupled multi-stage 'Pretraining, Post-training, Application' paradigm and defines an 'exchange rate' to quantify how intermediate-metric gains translate downstream, identifying image-based search recall as the critical intermediate metric. It systematically studies scaling laws over training tokens, negative samples, and user behavior sequence length, and builds efficient infrastructure for producing and consuming multimodal representations.
