## Summary

Enhancing Video Large Language Models for Dense Video Captioning via Temporal Anchors":

1.  Dense video captioning aims to describe all events in a video, but current Large Language Models (LLMs) struggle with accurately identifying the start and end of these events, leading to less grounded captions.
2.  To address this, the paper introduces TA-Prompting, which uses learned temporal anchors to precisely locate events and prompts the LLMs to focus on temporal grounding during generation.
3.  The approach also includes a sampling strategy to select coherent event captions across the video timeline, and experiments show that TA-Prompting significantly outperforms existing methods on dense captioning and related temporal understanding tasks.

## Technical note

Existing VideoLLMs describe events but fail to localize precise event boundaries, producing ungrounded dense captions. TA-Prompting introduces an event localizer that predicts temporal anchors marking event spans, then conditions the VideoLLM on those anchors to generate temporally grounded captions in a two-stage training scheme. The localizer is trained with an anchor loss plus a temporal denoising objective instead of plain cross-entropy, and an event-coherent sampling strategy selects captions with high cross-event coherence and video similarity at inference. It outperforms state-of-the-art VideoLLMs on dense video captioning, moment retrieval, and temporal QA.
