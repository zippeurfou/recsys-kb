## Technical note

This paper decomposes explicit chain-of-thought reasoning pipelines for LLM-based Generative Recommendation over Semantic IDs and reveals three limitations: weakened world-knowledge verbalization, SID/natural-language embedding-space misalignment, and sensitivity to rationale quality. To avoid these, it proposes PauseRec, a lightweight implicit reasoning paradigm that inserts pause/thinking capacity without costly reasoning-trace acquisition or alignment training. PauseRec outperforms standard explicit CoT methods by up to 6.22%, reduces training cost by up to 65% GPU hours, and speeds inference by up to 71.3%, positioning it as a practical alternative to explicit rationale generation for LLM-based GR.
