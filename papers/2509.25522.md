## Technical note

Snap and academic collaborators study scaling behavior of Generative Recommendation (GR). They show SID-based GR -- which quantizes modality-encoder embeddings into discrete semantic IDs for autoregressive interaction-sequence modeling -- saturates quickly when scaling the modality encoder, quantization tokenizer, or the recommender, identifying the limited capacity of semantic IDs to encode item semantics as a fundamental bottleneck. They revisit Text-based GR that directly uses textual item representations, finding superior scaling and up to 20% improvement over the best achievable SID-based GR. Across 44M-14B parameter models, they also challenge the belief that LLMs cannot capture collaborative-filtering signal, showing user-item modeling improves as LLMs scale, positioning Text-based GR toward GR foundation models.
