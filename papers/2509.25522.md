## Technical note

This Snap study analyzes generative recommendation with semantic IDs (SID-based GR) from a model-scaling perspective and finds it hits bottlenecks: performance saturates quickly as the modality encoder, quantization tokenizer, and RS backbone are scaled up. It identifies the limited capacity of SIDs to encode item semantics as a fundamental bottleneck. As an alternative, it revisits Text-based GR (using textual item representations), which exhibits superior scaling behavior, arguing text-based item representations scale better than discrete semantic IDs.
