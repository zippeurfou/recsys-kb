## Technical note

Red-Rec is an AI-initiated exploration interface targeting 'vague intent' - the state between passive browsing and explicit search where users find their feed stale but cannot articulate an alternative. Instead of waiting for a query, the system summarizes the current feed composition, surfaces clickable low-effort options, asks at most one follow-up, and gradually blends new content in. In a mixed-design lab study (n=28) against a passive feed, search, and user-initiated chat, Red-Rec led to broader exploration (7.4 vs 4.1 categories, p<.001), higher serendipity (5.72 vs 4.31, p<.01), and much lower typing effort (median 0 vs 73 characters). This is an HCI/interaction paper rather than an algorithmic recsys paper.
