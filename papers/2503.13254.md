## Technical note

FMoE-CDSR tackles non-overlapped cross-domain sequential recommendation from a federated-learning perspective, where domains share only trained model-parameter checkpoints (not user logs or embeddings) due to non-overlapping users and privacy constraints. Improving on FedAvg parameter averaging (which loses domain-specific knowledge and forces a shared architecture), it treats each domain's model as an expert, synchronizes other-domain experts to the target domain with extra adaptation parameters, and uses a Mixture-of-Experts mechanism to adaptively select and aggregate in-domain and synchronized experts. Experiments on three real-world CDR datasets show significant performance gains, with ablations validating the components.
