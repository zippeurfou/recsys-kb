## Technical note

AgentDR (Amazon) is an LLM-agent recommendation framework that delegates full-catalog ranking to traditional recommenders while using the LLM to (i) integrate multiple recommendation-tool outputs based on personalized tool suitability and (ii) reason over substitute and complement item relationships grounded in user history. This design mitigates item hallucination and token-length limits that plague direct LLM-agent recommenders, and scales to large catalogs. On three public grocery datasets it achieves superior full-ranking performance, averaging a twofold improvement over its underlying tools, and it introduces an LLM-based metric jointly measuring semantic alignment and ranking correctness.
