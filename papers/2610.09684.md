## Summary

This paper addresses the limitation of existing test-time scaling (TTS) methods which only optimize accuracy against one resource (cost or latency) at a time. It introduces **PersonTTS**, an amortized agentic policy discovery framework designed to find executable controllers optimizing the joint satisfaction of user-specific accuracy, latency, and cost requirements. Experiments demonstrate that PersonTTS significantly improves performance and reduces discovery time/cost by reusing prior search experience using requirement-matched initialization and source-distilled procedural guidance.

## Technical note

Existing test-time scaling (TTS) methods for LLM reasoning optimize accuracy against cost or latency one at a time, while users specify accuracy, latency and inference-cost requirements jointly; PersonTTS formulates Personalized TTS as discovering executable controllers (code governing model routing, reasoning width/depth, self-refinement, pruning, stopping) that maximize a joint satisfaction rate (JSR) under a user requirement profile. An LLM discovery agent revises controller code against cached offline replay traces, warm-started from the controller of the most requirement-similar source profile in a Policy Experience Bank and steered by a frozen Guide distilled from past searches. On AIME and HMMT with six Qwen3 models (0.6B-32B), PersonTTS reaches 96.6/83.9 JSR on AIME discovery/held-out problems vs 35.6/35.5 for AutoTTS and 10.2/9.4 for Parallel-Probe; cross-user reuse adds roughly 4-11 JSR points over independent discovery and the Guide cuts discovery-agent time ~46% and cost ~36%. Not a recommender paper: the 'personalization' is per-user resource requirement profiles for math-reasoning inference.
