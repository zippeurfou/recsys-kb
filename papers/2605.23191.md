## Summary

1.  The paper addresses the "embedding collapse" problem in the RankMixer recommendation model, where learned representations have low dimensionality (effective rank), hindering performance and limiting the model's capacity.
2.  The authors identify that rigid token mixing and P-FFN modules within RankMixer cause this issue by inducing a damped oscillatory trajectory in effective-rank dynamics across layers.
3.  To solve this, they introduce RankElastor, a new architecture with parameterized full mixing and GLU-style P-FFNs, providing spectrum-robust representations that demonstrably improve recommendation performance, mitigate collapse, and offer better scaling.

## Technical note

This Tencent paper diagnoses embedding collapse in the RankMixer recommendation model, where learned representations fall to low effective rank and cap model capacity and performance. The authors show that rigid token mixing and parallel-FFN (P-FFN) modules induce a damped oscillatory trajectory in effective-rank dynamics across layers. They propose RankElastor, which replaces these with parameterized full mixing and GLU-style P-FFNs to give spectrum-robust representations. The redesign demonstrably mitigates collapse, improves recommendation performance, and offers better dense-scaling behavior, supported by theory on effective-rank contraction and algebraic lifting.
