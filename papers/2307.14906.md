## Summary

This paper introduces TRON, a new session-based recommendation system using Transformers. It aims to overcome the limitations of existing models like SASRec by combining optimized negative sampling with listwise loss functions to improve accuracy. Evaluations demonstrate that TRON significantly boosts recommendation quality and click-through rates in real-world A/B tests, while maintaining training efficiency similar to SASRec.

## Technical note

TRON is a scalable session-based Transformer recommender built on SASRec that combines optimized negative sampling with a listwise loss to overcome accuracy and scalability limits of SASRec and GRU4Rec+. It mixes uniform batchwise and in-batch sessionwise negative sampling (avoiding CPU-GPU transfer bottlenecks) and adds a top-k negative sampling strategy that only backpropagates through the hardest (top-scoring) negatives, retaining the benefit of large negative sets while cutting computation. It uses sampled softmax (a listwise loss) which alleviates popularity bias and better maximizes ranking metrics compared to BCE or BPR-MAX. Evaluated on Diginetica, Yoochoose, and OTTO with full (non-sampled) Recall@20/MRR@20, TRON beats baselines at SASRec-comparable training speed, and a live A/B test yields an 18.14% click-through-rate increase over SASRec.
