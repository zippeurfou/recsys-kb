Recently, Meta introduced a generative ranking paradigm based on HSTU block that enables end-to-end learning from raw user behavior sequences and demonstrates scaling law on large datasets that can be regarded as the state-of-the-art (SOTA). ## Technical note

DFGR (Dual-Flow Generative Ranking Network, Meituan) tackles the doubled sequence length of Meta's HSTU generative ranking, which interleaves item and action tokens and causes quadratic self-attention cost and unbalanced token information density. DFGR duplicates the user behavior sequence into a real flow and a fake flow based on action authenticity and defines a novel interaction between them within the QKV self-attention module, cutting computation while improving training/inference efficiency. On open-source and real industrial datasets DFGR outperforms the heavily feature-engineered DLRM online baseline, Meta's HSTU, and DIN/DCN/DIEN/DeepFM, and the paper studies optimal parameter allocation under compute constraints.
