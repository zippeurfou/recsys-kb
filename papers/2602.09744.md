## Technical note

DiffuReason (Tencent) is a unified 'Think-then-Diffuse' framework for sequential recommendation that treats latent reasoning as a probabilistic condition rather than a deterministic chain. In the Think stage the model generates autoregressive Thinking Tokens over user history to form an initial intent hypothesis; in the Diffuse stage a diffusion process iteratively denoises this hypothesis to model intent uncertainty; and end-to-end GRPO alignment replaces the usual disjoint two-stage pipeline, letting reasoning and refinement co-evolve and be optimized directly against ranking metrics (fixing the MSE-vs-listwise mismatch of prior diffusion recommenders). It is model-agnostic across ID-based (SASRec) and SID-based (TIGER) backbones, consistently improves over baselines on four benchmarks, and is validated with online A/B tests on a large-scale industrial platform.
