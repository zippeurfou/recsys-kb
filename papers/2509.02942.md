## Summary

1. Cross-domain recommendation systems need to integrate diverse product relationships, and this paper introduces **RankGraph**, a scalable framework designed for recommendation foundation models (FMs) that handles complex user, post, ad, and item interactions across domains.
2. RankGraph uses heterogeneous graphs combined with GPU-accelerated Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and contrastive learning to dynamically extract relevant subgraphs like item-item or user-user relationships, supporting similarity-based retrieval and real-time clustering.
3. By incorporating graph-based pre-trained representations as contextual tokens into FM sequence models, RankGraph enriches the recommendations with structured relational knowledge, proving effective through online A/B tests that showed improved click (+0.92%) and conversion rates (+2.82%).

## Technical note

RankGraph is a scalable graph-learning framework serving as a component of cross-domain recommendation foundation models at Meta. It builds a heterogeneous graph of users, posts, ads and semantic edges, then learns node embeddings with a type-aware feature encoder, RGCN-style relational message passing, and contrastive learning using in-batch, out-of-batch, and semantic negatives with combined triplet + InfoNCE losses. Graph embeddings are served for real-time retrieval/clustering and injected as contextual tokens into sequence-based foundation models. It beats the Filament2 baseline on next-day-edge and engagement recall, and online A/B tests show +0.92% click and +2.82% conversion.
