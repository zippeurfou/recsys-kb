## Technical note

RankGraph is a GPU-accelerated heterogeneous graph learning framework serving as a component of recommendation foundation models at Meta. It builds graphs over users, posts, ads and other entities, aggregates via an RGCN-style message-passing encoder, and trains with contrastive learning combining triplet loss (local) and InfoNCE loss (global) using in-batch, out-of-batch, and semantic negative sampling. Graph embeddings are injected as contextual tokens into sequence foundation models and support ANN retrieval/clustering. Online A/B tests showed +0.92% click and +2.82% conversion rate.
