## Summary

1.  The paper proposes a feed ranking system using a two-term value model, keeping a baseline component (representing inherent engagement due to viewer habits, item popularity, or request context) separate and adding an incremental component (representing the candidate's excess value beyond the baseline).
2.  This approach, parameterized by an operator and chosen via a design procedure, outperformed single-term models (using either baseline or incremental) in 11 out of 13 judged metric pairs during production testing at Instagram, significantly beating them on impression metrics and showing only one negative comparison.
3.  While the two-term model debiases specific signal families, media-type bias is addressed differently by assigning debiased labels. Meta has since adopted this methodology company-wide across different apps and ranking stages, successfully boosting core engagement metrics.

## Technical note

Meta argues that each engagement prediction in Instagram Feed's value model (a Bayesian-optimization-tuned weighted sum of multi-task predictions) conflates a baseline the viewer, item or request would produce anyway (the like prediction has rank correlation 0.69 with the viewer's own like rate) with a candidate-specific increment, and that instead of removing the baseline the ranker should keep the raw term and add the incremental part as a separately weighted term. The three deployed debiasing families (viewer habit, item popularity, request/batch context) are shown to be one operator, Delta = m(p,b)[sigma(k d(p,b)) - shift], covering logit-gap sigmoid gates and linear hinges, with an offline design procedure (quantile correlation and variance-ratio diagnostics, shape-selected batch statistic such as p75, and a rule routing media-type/duration bias to bucketised debiased labels rather than a scoring-time term). In a four-arm live ablation (none/raw/incremental/both) of six production terms, the two-term arm beats both single-term arms on 11 of 13 judged (term, metric) pairs, with 17 of 26 direct contrasts significantly positive and 1 negative (e.g. +21.05% external shares and +0.359% feed time spent over arms already carrying one term). The terms have served Instagram Feed for most of a year and the operator has been adopted across Reels, Threads and Facebook with session and DAU wins of +0.03% to +0.08%; the stated lesson is that the cheap offline diagnostic should screen signals, not rank them.
