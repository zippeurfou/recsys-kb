## Technical note

RTTP (Real-Time Trend Prediction), deployed on Facebook and Meta AI, tackles the cold-start problem of trend detection in low-traffic search by generating search-style queries directly from newly created news posts instead of waiting for query volume to form. A continual-learning LLM (CL-LLM, LLaMA 3.3 70B) converts posts into queries and scores them via engagement strength plus creator authority. To adapt without degrading reasoning, it introduces Mix-Policy DPO, blending on-policy stability with off-policy novelty to mitigate catastrophic forgetting and the DPO 'squeezing effect.' RTTP delivers +91.4% tail-trend detection precision@500 and +19% query-generation accuracy over industry baselines.
