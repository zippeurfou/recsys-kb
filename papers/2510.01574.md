## Technical note

A Walmart data-centric method mitigates presentation bias in real-time neural query autocomplete by generating synthetic prefixes from complete queries collected in sessions where autocomplete was inactive, enriching learning-to-rank training with less biased, more diverse examples. The neural ranker uses features like query popularity, seasonality, fuzzy match, and contextual signals (department affinity, device type), and introduces a task-specific listwise loss simplification exploiting the single-ground-truth-per-prefix structure to cut complexity from O(n^2) to O(n). Deployed at large-scale e-commerce, it yields statistically significant improvements in mean reciprocal rank and engagement.
