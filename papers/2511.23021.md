## Technical note

MaskGR (Snap) replaces the standard autoregressive modeling of Semantic-ID sequences in generative recommendation with masked diffusion, which applies discrete masking noise and models masked tokens as conditionally independent given unmasked tokens, enabling parallel decoding. This addresses AR drawbacks: sequential token-wise decoding latency, inefficient data use, and bias toward short-context relationships. Experiments show masked diffusion consistently outperforms AR modeling, with the gap especially pronounced in data-constrained settings and in coarse-grained recall, while allowing multiple SIDs to be predicted in parallel at inference without losing quality.
