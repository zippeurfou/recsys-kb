## Technical note

GRAM (Generative Retrieval and Alignment Model) is JD's e-commerce retrieval paradigm that uses an LLM to jointly generate shared text identifiers for both queries and products (built from NER attributes in natural language), avoiding the word-distribution mismatch of separately built term-set identifiers and the from-scratch ID systems of static/semantic IDs. It distinguishes identifier quality by product recall efficiency and applies preference alignment to raise the probability of generating high-quality identifiers, improving recall efficiency. Offline experiments and online A/B tests show GRAM significantly outperforms sparse, dense, and prior generative-retrieval baselines; it is deployed serving hundreds of millions of product retrievals daily on JD.
