## Technical note

Kuaishou's COINS enhances cold-start item representations for e-commerce search CTR using fused alignment of semantic IDs, building on OneSearch's RQ-OPQ five-layer encoding (three RQ-Kmeans hierarchical layers plus two OPQ layers). It applies a two-step alignment: an adaptive bidirectional transfer between high-frequency collaborative signals in item IDs and the coarse-grained RQ encodings, and an OPQ-encoding-based contrastive-learning strategy that injects each item's fine-grained discriminative information, addressing the asymmetry between content and collaborative signals. Offline experiments on large industrial datasets and online A/B tests show statistically significant gains: item CTR +1.66%, buyers +1.57%, order volume +2.17%.
