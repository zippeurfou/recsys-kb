## Technical note

This Google paper proposes a hierarchical framework where fine-tuned Multimodal LLMs (MLLMs) provide high-level guidance to conventional recommendation models to increase serendipity on a billion-user short-form video platform, avoiding the complexity of applying MLLMs directly to vast action spaces. It uses a chain-of-thought strategy where the MLLM first understands video content then identifies relevant yet unexplored interest clusters to break self-reinforcing feedback loops, while traditional models retain efficient item-level recommendation. Live experiments on a commercial short-video platform serving billions of users show significant improvements in recommendation serendipity, novelty/diversity, and user satisfaction.
