## Summary

1.  **Problem:** The abstract notes that existing methods for billion-node graph-based recommendation treat separate stages (graph construction, representation learning, serving) independently, which limits effectiveness due to their tightly coupled nature.
2.  **Solution:** RankGraph-2 presents a lifecycle co-design framework deployed at Meta that specifically integrates these three stages, with each stage's requirements influencing the design of the others for similarity-based retrieval.
3.  **Impact & Results:** By optimizing graph construction (subsampling with popularity correction), pre-computing neighborhoods via Personalized PageRank, and jointly learning residual quantization during training to aid serving efficiency, RankGraph-2 significantly improves recall compared to prior models on billion-node bipartite graphs while boosting key business metrics like CTR (+0.96%) and CVR (+2.75%).

## Technical note

RankGraph-2 (Meta) co-designs graph construction, representation learning, and serving for billion-node similarity-based retrieval (U2U2I and U2I2I). It subsamples hundreds-of-trillions of edges to hundreds of billions with popularity-bias correction, pre-computes multi-hop neighborhoods via Personalized PageRank, and co-learns a residual-quantization cluster index that cuts serving cost by 83%. It achieves 3.8x higher recall than GAT+Deep Graph Infomax and 2.1x over PyTorch-BigGraph, delivering up to +0.96% CTR and +2.75% CVR across 20+ retrieval launches.
