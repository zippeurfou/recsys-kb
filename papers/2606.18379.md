## Summary

This paper introduces **RankGraph-2**, a novel framework for large-scale billion-node recommendation systems. It uniquely addresses the tightly coupled challenges of graph construction, representation learning, and real-time serving through lifecycle co-design from the ground up.

Key innovations include reducing edges with popularity bias correction, precomputing multi-hop neighborhoods via personalized PageRank to leverage training efficiency, and co-learning a cluster index during training that significantly aids fast online retrieval by eliminating expensive KNN computations. This integrated approach drastically cuts computational costs while boosting recall compared to previous methods on bipartite graphs.

The system achieves substantial improvements in recommendation quality (CTR/CVR) and serves as an efficient engine for numerous billion-node scale retrieval applications at Meta, demonstrating practical success alongside theoretical advancements.

## Technical note

RankGraph-2, deployed at Meta, argues that billion-node graph retrieval must co-design its three lifecycle stages (construction, training, serving) rather than optimize each in isolation, focusing on similarity-based retrieval (U2U2I, U2I2I). Construction builds a heterogeneous co-engagement graph (U-U, I-I, U-I edges from engagement alone), applies edge subsampling with popularity-bias correction to cut hundreds of trillions of edges to hundreds of billions, and pre-computes multi-hop neighbors via Personalized PageRank into self-contained edge-centric data, eliminating online graph infrastructure. Training is inductive with type-aware encoders and a heterogeneous aggregator under a combined margin-ranking + InfoNCE contrastive link-prediction loss (in-batch, out-of-batch, and negative-augmentation negatives), and co-learns a residual-quantization cluster index (with a regularization loss and biased code selection to prevent codebook collapse) that replaces online KNN with a U2Cluster2I lookup, cutting serving cost by 83%. The simple architecture reaches 3.8x higher Recall@5 than GAT + Deep Graph Infomax and 2.1x higher than PyTorch-BigGraph, and 14-day A/B tests deliver up to +0.96% CTR and +2.75% CVR across 20+ launches.
