## Summary

RankGraph-2 is a Meta framework designed to co-design the three tightly coupled stages of similarity-based retrieval (U2U2I/U2I2I) for billion-node recommendation graphs: graph construction, representation learning, and real-time serving. It optimizes performance through subsampling edges with popularity bias correction, precomputing multi-hop neighborhoods using personalized PageRank, and co-training a cluster index during training that reduces the computational cost of online similarity search by 83%. This lifecycle approach allows RankGraph-2 to achieve significantly higher recall (over 2x compared to existing methods) and improve user engagement metrics like CTR (+0.96%) and CVR (+2.75%), powering over twenty recommendation launches on major Meta platforms.

## Technical note

RankGraph-2 co-designs graph construction, representation learning, and serving for billion-node similarity-based retrieval (U2U2I, U2I2I) at Meta, where each stage's requirements cascade to the others. Construction builds a heterogeneous co-engagement graph (U-U, I-I, U-I edges) from engagement data alone, applies log-normalized edge weighting and I-I popularity bias correction (alpha=0.3), subsamples hundreds of trillions of edges down to hundreds of billions, and pre-computes multi-hop neighborhoods via Monte-Carlo personalized PageRank so training needs no online graph infrastructure. Training is an inductive link-prediction loop combining margin ranking and InfoNCE losses with in-batch/out-of-batch/multi-head-augmented negatives, and co-learns a two-layer residual-quantization cluster index (5000x50 codes) with a regularization loss and biased code selection to prevent codebook collapse under continuous training. Serving replaces online KNN with cluster-queue lookups (U2Cluster2I), cutting serving cost by 83%; offline recall is 3.8x higher than GAT+Deep Graph Infomax and 2.1x higher than PyTorch-BigGraph, with +0.96% CTR and +2.75% CVR in 14-day A/B tests across 20+ launches.
