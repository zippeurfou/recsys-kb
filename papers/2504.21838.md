## Technical note

This Snapchat paper describes Universal User Modeling (UUM), which learns general-purpose user representations encoding collaborative filtering signals across multiple in-app surfaces rather than sharing per-surface embeddings only post-hoc. Formulated as a cross-domain sequential recommendation retrieval task over stitched cross-domain interaction sequences, UUM embeddings enrich existing domain-specific representations with platform-wide cross-domain trends. After A/B testing, UUM launched in production powering Long-form Video EBR (+2.78% Open Rate) and L2 ranking (+19.2% View Time sum), Lens L2 ranking (+1.76% play time), and Notification L2 ranking (+0.87% Open Rate).
