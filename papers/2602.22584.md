## Summary

Industrial advertising QA, a high-stakes task, suffers from frequent "hallucinations" like fabricated URLs, risking financial loss and legal issues. Existing RAG systems often fail due to the relational and dynamic nature of advertising knowledge and misalignment between retrieval and generation. To address these challenges, the paper introduces a reinforced co-adaptation framework featuring GraphRAG for relational evidence retrieval and GRPO for evidence-constrained reinforcement learning, significantly improving accuracy, safety, and reducing hallucinations, with strong real-world validation in production.

## Technical note

The paper proposes a reinforced co-adaptation framework for industrial advertising QA that jointly optimizes retrieval and generation to eliminate URL hallucinations. Retrieval combines a GraphRAG channel—building an entity-relation knowledge graph over a traffic-selected high-citation subgraph with community detection and multi-hop traversal—run in parallel with a traditional BGE+BM25 hybrid RAG channel using query rewriting, merged and deduplicated. Generation uses a GRPO-tuned Qwen3-32B with a multi-dimensional reward covering evidence faithfulness (LLM-as-judge), style compliance, safety, and URL validity (URL must appear in evidence or resolve to an approved prefix with a valid HTTP status). GraphRAG improves coverage while RL cuts hallucination, together reducing hallucination rate 72% offline and URL hallucination 92.7% in a two-week A/B test.
