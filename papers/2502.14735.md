## Technical note

EAGER-LLM is a decoder-only LLM generative recommendation framework that non-intrusively integrates endogenous and exogenous behavioral/semantic signals. It uses dual-source knowledge-rich item indices to compress exogenous collaborative signals into few tokens, non-invasive multiscale alignment reconstruction tasks to teach both collaborative and semantic understanding, and an annealing adapter to balance recommendation performance against comprehension. It frames recommendation as next-token item generation and demonstrates superiority over prior LLM-based and traditional recommenders on three public benchmarks.
