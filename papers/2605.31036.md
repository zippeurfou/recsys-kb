## Technical note

This Uber paper formalizes when better pCTR/pCVR prediction models actually improve platform-level metrics (revenue, welfare, liquid welfare) given auctions and autobidders, defining model refinement via a filtration-inspired cluster-refinement relation where a finer model partitions users more granularly. It systematically characterizes ECM monotonicity across bidder types (tCPA, MAX-CPA), auction formats (first-price, second-price/VCG), and budget constraints, finding monotonicity is rare: only three settings guarantee it (tCPA no-budget under FPA via Jensen's inequality, MAX-CPA no-budget under VCG for welfare, and LP fractional allocation with budgets). In all others—including VCG for tCPA and FPA with budgets—monotonicity fails, shown via explicit numerical counterexamples.
