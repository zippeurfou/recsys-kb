## Technical note

Text-only dense retrieval for e-commerce product search ignores product images, which are key signals for relevance. Amazon builds multimodal semantic retrieval by fusing a text-only BiBERT bi-encoder with CLIP's image/text encoders, comparing a 4-tower model (4tMM) and a 3-tower model (3tMM), trained with NT-Xent/InfoNCE loss over in-batch and 'seen' negatives and served with cosine similarity + FAISS KNN over 3.38M products. Multimodal representations improve either purchase recall@100 (concatenation fusion +40-50 bps over BiBERT) or relevance precision (joint fine-tuning of BiBERT+MLP fusion reaches 54% exact matches), with a recall/precision trade-off; 4-tower gives better language-visual alignment than 3-tower but the gap narrows with fine-tuning. Exclusive (net) matches unique to multimodal models still contribute 10-20% recall at ~50% exact/30% substitute precision, showing complementary high-quality retrieval.
