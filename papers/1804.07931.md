## Summary

ESMM models post-click CVR over the entire impression space via the identity pCTCVR = pCTR x pCVR, training only the CTR and CTCVR tasks over all impressions (pCVR is an intermediate variable) with a shared embedding, eliminating sample-selection bias and data sparsity simultaneously.

## Technical note

ESMM (Alibaba, Taobao) tackles post-click CVR estimation, which suffers from sample-selection bias (trained on clicked impressions, served on all impressions) and extreme data sparsity. It exploits the sequential pattern impression->click->conversion via the identity pCTCVR = pCTR x pCVR, and trains ONLY the CTR and CTCVR losses over the full impression space (both over all impressions), treating pCVR as an intermediate variable constrained by the product; embeddings are shared between the CTR and CVR sub-networks for transfer learning. On Taobao's 8.9B-sample logs ESMM gives +2.18% CVR AUC and +2.32% CTCVR AUC over the base model; on the public 38GB dataset +2.56% CVR AUC / +3.25% CTCVR AUC. The multiplication form (vs a division form) avoids numerical instability and keeps pCVR in [0,1].
