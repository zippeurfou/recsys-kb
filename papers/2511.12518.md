## Technical note

DualGR is a generative retrieval framework for short-video feeds that explicitly models long- and short-term interests. It combines a Dual-Branch Long/Short-Term Router (DBR) with selective activation, Search-based SID Decoding (S2D) that constrains fine-level decoding within the current coarse bucket for efficiency and noise control, and an Exposure-aware Next-Token Prediction Loss (ENTP-Loss) that treats unclicked exposures as coarse-level hard negatives to fade out stale interests. On Kuaishou's double-column Explore feed (100M+ users, 1B video views/day) it beats retrieval baselines (ComiRec, PDN, KuaiFormer) and TIGER on online HR, with A/B lifts of +0.527% video views and +0.432% watch time.
