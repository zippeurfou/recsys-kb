## Technical note

TaoSR-AGRL is an adaptive guided reinforcement learning framework for LLM-based query-product relevance prediction in Taobao Search, addressing GRPO's sparse terminal rewards. It adds (1) Rule-aware Reward Shaping, decomposing the final relevance judgment into dense structured rewards aligned with domain relevance criteria, and (2) Adaptive Guided Replay, which detects low-accuracy rollouts during training and injects ground-truth guidance to steer the policy toward compliant reasoning. It consistently outperforms DPO and GRPO baselines on relevance accuracy and rule adherence with stable training, and is deployed on Taobao serving hundreds of millions of users.
