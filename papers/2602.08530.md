## Technical note

PIT is a dynamic personalized item tokenizer for end-to-end generative recommendation that makes item tokenization and recommendation co-evolve rather than using static two-stage RQ-VAE tokenization. Its co-generative architecture couples an Item-to-Token model, a User-to-Token generative recommender, and a DIN-based collaborative signal alignment auxiliary task, with a co-evolution learning paradigm using a user-guided minimum-loss selection mechanism and a one-to-many beam index for robustness to distribution shift in streaming training. Deployed at Kuaishou, an online A/B test yielded a 0.402% uplift in App Stay Time at hundred-million scale.
