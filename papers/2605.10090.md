## Technical note

This paper optimizes in-memory vector ANNS serving on modern CCD-based (multi-chiplet) multi-core CPUs at Xiaohongshu/RedNote, where naively adding cores fails to scale due to per-CCD last-level-cache pollution and load imbalance. It proposes a CCD-level, load-aware thread orchestration framework with a uniform interface for inter-query HNSW and intra-query IVF search, cache-friendly workload-adaptive task dispatching, and CCD-aware task stealing. On real search/recommendation/advertising workloads it delivers 1.4x-3.7x higher throughput, 30-90% P50/P999 latency reductions, 6-30% lower cache-miss ratio, and 20-80% less total CPU stall. This is a systems/serving-infrastructure paper for recsys retrieval.
