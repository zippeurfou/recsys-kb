## Summary

This paper addresses challenges in multimodal retrieval for food delivery, where joint optimization often neglects some modalities and causes inconsistent training speeds (the "one-epoch" problem). To solve this, the authors propose a staged pretraining strategy guiding the model through specialized tasks sequentially, allowing flexible training control. This approach, combined with generative and discriminative tasks using semantic IDs, significantly improves retrieval performance on Meituan data and boosts online revenue and click-through rate.

## Technical note

SMGR is a multimodal generative retrieval model for Meituan food-delivery search, where dual-tower joint optimization lets easy modalities (text) dominate and starve others (images), triggering the one-epoch overfitting problem. It replaces joint training with a staged pretraining strategy that focuses on specialized tasks (image2text, query2text/image/item) per phase to balance modality learning. High-dimensional fused embeddings are compressed into semantic IDs via RQ-VAE, and both generative and discriminative tasks teach the model associations among SIDs, queries, and item features. On real Meituan data it improves Recall/NDCG by up to ~3.8%/5.1% over baselines, with +1.12% revenue and +1.02% CTR in online A/B tests.
