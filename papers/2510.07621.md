## Technical note

Retentive Relevance is a content-level survey measure that asks users their intent to return to the platform for similar content, capturing forward-looking retention rather than immediate satisfaction. Validated psychometrically (convergent, discriminant, behavioral validity), it significantly outperforms engagement signals and other survey measures at predicting next-day retention, especially for low-signal users. A production proxy model integrates it into the final stage of a multi-stage ranking system on a social media platform, and calibrated score adjustments in large-scale A/B experiments improve engagement and retention while reducing exposure to low-quality content.
