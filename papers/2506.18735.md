## Summary

This paper addresses computational advertising challenges within Spotify's unique, audio-centric platform where users primarily consume music. The authors propose CAMoE (Cross-modal Adaptive Mixture-of-Experts), a novel multi-task learning framework designed specifically for this environment by incorporating modality-aware task grouping and deep-cross networks along with adaptive loss masking.

CAMoE significantly enhances CTR prediction across different ad formats (audio, video, display) within Spotify's ecosystem. Extensive tests show it achieves near Pareto-optimal performance compared to traditional models.

When deployed at scale, CAMoE delivered substantial improvements: a 14.5% increase in click-through rate for audio ads and a significant reduction in expected cost-per-click (eCPC) by about 4.8%.

## Technical note

Spotify's audio-centric, multi-modal ad platform must predict CTR across audio, video, and display formats where engagement and CTR distributions differ sharply. CAMoE (Cross-modal Adaptive Mixture-of-Experts) extends MoE with modality-aware task grouping, adaptive loss masking for extreme class imbalance, and deep-cross networks (DCN) to capture feature interactions. Ablations show near Pareto-optimal performance across formats and improved AUC-PR over single-task and content-based multi-task baselines. Deployed at scale, CAMoE delivered a 14.5% CTR increase for audio ads, 1.3% for video ads, and a 4.8% reduction in expected cost-per-click for audio slots.
