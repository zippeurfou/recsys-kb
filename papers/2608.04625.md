## Summary

1.  Industrial A/B testing strategy iteration is traditionally manual and time-consuming due to fragmented historical knowledge making reuse difficult.
2.  The authors propose "A/B Agent," an automated system that organizes past experiments into a hierarchical structure (Experience Tree) and uses this, along with online feedback, to generate new target-aware strategies autonomously through sequential refinement.
3.  Evaluations show the A/B Agent improves recommendation strategy effectiveness significantly (e.g., +4.829% GMV), while consistently maintaining positive guardrail metrics compared to traditional methods or standard RAG approaches.

## Technical note

Industrial recommendation strategy iteration is manual -- hours per experiment to design a change, read core and guardrail metrics, and choose the next parameter setting -- while past experiment knowledge sits fragmented across docs, code, configs and metric logs, and flat RAG agents retrieve semantically similar but contextually wrong strategies. A/B Agent (Kuaishou) closes the loop in three parts: Historical Strategy Knowledge Organization normalizes each report into a schema and has a strategy agent split it into atomic chunks (motivation, mechanism, parameters, applicability, outcomes, risks) placed on a semantic path [domain, scenario, recommendation stage, objective] in a hierarchical experience tree, with transferable chunks attached to multiple paths; Autonomous Target-Aware Strategy Generation runs multi-path sparse+dense retrieval, adds a tree-path boost B_tree = sum_l beta_l * exp(-gamma_l * d_T(v_c^l, v_q^l)), reranks the top-K with Qwen-Reranker, and filters candidates with agent judges on evidence consistency, feasibility, expected gain and guardrail risk; Experiment-Guided Self-Evolution builds an A/B experiment tree whose nodes hold (strategy, params, confidence-weighted metric deltas, context), scores them with U(v) = sum_core w_j*dy_j - lambda * sum_guard w_k*max(0, -dy_k), and does local parameter search on winning branches or mechanism replacement when a branch saturates or breaks a guardrail. With GLM-5.1 as the shared backbone it scores 7.244 average Overall under a GPT-5.5 judge versus 7.151 for Claude-Sonnet-4.6 and 5.728 for the best RAG baseline (HyDE), i.e. +25.0%/+31.7%/+23.5% over the strongest RAG method per scenario; ablation says the knowledge base matters most (-3.3% overall when removed) and Tree-RAG over Flat-RAG only -0.2%. Deployed on Kuaishou short-video e-commerce, five successive auto-tuned strategies trace a Pareto path from +1.123% GMV with -0.405% average guardrail to +4.829% GMV and +4.677% GPM with +0.342% average guardrail, plus +0.841% OPM, +0.578% CVR, +0.370% clicks and +1.053% orders.
