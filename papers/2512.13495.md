## Summary

This paper introduces the **Soul** framework for high-fidelity digital human animation, capable of generating long videos from single images and text/audio inputs with precise lip sync and identity preservation. To achieve this, the authors created a large, annotated dataset called **Soul-1M** and established a benchmark (**Soul-Bench**) for fair evaluation. The model, built on the **Wan2.2-5B** backbone, incorporates specialized training techniques and optimizations to ensure consistency over long durations and achieve high inference speed.

## Technical note

Soul is a multimodal digital-human animation framework that generates long-term, identity-preserving 1080P video from a single portrait, text, and audio. Built on the Wan2.2-5B DiT backbone, it injects Whisper audio features via audio-attention layers and mitigates long-term identity/feature drift using pivotal reference frames, intra-clip latent overlap, and threshold-aware K-means codebook replacement. Inference is accelerated 11.4x via step and CFG distribution-matching distillation plus a lightweight eVAE with negligible quality loss. The authors build Soul-1M (1M annotated samples) and Soul-Bench, and show Soul outperforms open-source and commercial models on video-text alignment, identity consistency, video quality, and lip-sync.
