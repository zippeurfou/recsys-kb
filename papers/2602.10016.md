## Summary

1.  Recommendation systems struggle to establish predictable scaling laws due to poor efficiency, primarily stemming from inefficient modules with low Model FLOPs Utilization (MFU) and suboptimal resource allocation.
2.  To address this, the authors introduce Kunlun, a novel scalable architecture incorporating low-level optimizations (like GDPA, HSP, and Sliding Window Attention) and high-level innovations (like Computation Skip and Event-level Personalization).
3.  Kunlun significantly boosts MFU (from 17% to 37%) and doubles the scaling efficiency compared to existing methods, demonstrating substantial real-world impact through deployment in major Meta Ads models.

## Technical note

Kunlun establishes predictable scaling laws for massive-scale CTR recommenders that jointly model sequential user behavior and non-sequential context features, identifying low Model FLOPs Utilization (3-15% vs 40-60% for LLMs) and poor resource allocation as the barrier. Its multi-layer architecture pairs a Kunlun Transformer block (GDPA-enhanced personalized FFN plus multi-head self-attention) with a Kunlun Interaction block that generates personalized weights, summarizes sequences via Hierarchical Seed Pooling, and performs global sequence-nonsequence interaction. Low-level optimizations (Generalized Dot-Product Attention, Hierarchical Seed Pooling, Sliding Window Attention for linear-complexity sequence modeling) improve hardware efficiency, while high-level Computation Skip and Event-level Personalization reallocate compute adaptively. This codesign raises MFU from 17% to 37% on B200 GPUs, doubles scaling efficiency, and delivered 1.2% topline gains in Meta Ads models; performance is measured via Normalized Entropy.
