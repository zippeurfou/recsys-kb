## Technical note

Reason4Rec introduces a Deliberative Recommendation task that makes RecLLMs explicitly reason about user preferences before predicting feedback, countering pattern-based alignment that is biased and weak on cold-start/low-frequency items. Its Reasoning-powered Recommender framework decomposes reasoning into three collaborative step-wise experts—Preference Distillation, Preference Matching, and Feedback Prediction—trained with tailored strategies using verbalized user feedback (reviews/conversations) as reasoning supervision. Across three real-world datasets it improves both prediction accuracy and reasoning quality over direct-feedback SFT/DPO alignment.
