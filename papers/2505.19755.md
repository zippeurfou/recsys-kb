## Technical note

EGA-V1 (Meituan) is an end-to-end generative architecture that unifies online advertising ranking (recall, pre-ranking, ranking, auction) into a single model directly generating optimal ad sequences from the full candidate corpus in location-based services, addressing multi-stage cascade inconsistencies and ignored ad externalities. It uses an algorithm-engine co-designed hybrid feature service decoupling user/ad feature processing, a RecFormer with a novel cluster-attention mechanism to extract intra- and cross-sequence mutual information, and a bi-stage pre-training plus RL post-training strategy. Offline benchmarks and large-scale online A/B testing show EGA-V1 outperforms state-of-the-art multi-stage cascading architectures.
