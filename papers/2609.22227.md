## Summary

1.  **Problem:** Generative Semantic IDs (SID) systems use a learnable tokenizer to represent items with short codes, but these tokenizations aren't inherently aligned with downstream Large Language Models (LLMs) or the specific recommendation task.
2.  **Solution:** The paper introduces Guided SID, which directly injects predefined, task-relevant, and text-grounded categorical attributes (like genres or categories) into the coarse levels of the SID tokenizer by deterministic index assignment, overriding standard nearest-neighbor selection while keeping the codebooks learnable.
3.  **Result:** This approach, called "collision cost," makes the tokenized attributes more meaningful for the task without hindering the tokenizer's ability to reconstruct item embeddings. In experiments, Guided SID significantly outperformed the standard SID baseline, improving recommendation recall and mean reciprocal rank, and ensuring the predefined attribute is correctly predicted much more often.

## Technical note

RQ-VAE semantic IDs are trained to reconstruct item embeddings, so their codes are aligned with neither the downstream LLM nor the task. Guided SID forces the coarse RQ-VAE level to encode a predefined text-grounded, task-relevant categorical attribute by deterministic supervised index assignment (overriding nearest-neighbor code selection with the attribute label while keeping codebooks learnable), with a trie-merge that maps high-cardinality or set-valued attributes onto the fixed code budget. On Meta ads data (256x6 codebooks, level 0 forced to the targeting-country code) with a Qwen3-4B CPT-then-SFT generative retriever, a matched comparison differing only in SID encoding over a 654-741M-identifier production catalog improves recall@1 by 1.36x and recall@10 by 1.39x, raises MRR from 0.0260 to 0.0355 and predicts the attribute 4.2x more often, while reconstruction and collision match or beat the vanilla tokenizer. Prepending the attribute as an extra token recovers almost none of the gain, and the unsupervised residual levels also become 1.4-1.5x easier to generate.
