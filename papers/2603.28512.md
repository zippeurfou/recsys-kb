## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently and accurately embedding the large-scale WikiKG90Mv2 knowledge graph (containing over 90 million entities). The authors propose a "TIEG-Youpu" solution based on a retrieve-then-re-rank strategy. Their novel contributions include a priority infilling retrieval model for finding structurally/semantically similar candidates and an ensemble re-ranking model using neighbor-enhanced representations. Experimental results demonstrate that their method significantly outperforms baselines, improving the validation set MRR from 0.2342 to 0.2839.

## Technical note

The TIEG-Youpu solution tackles WikiKG90Mv2 (90M+ entities, ~600M triples) link prediction with a retrieve-then-re-rank pipeline borrowed from large-scale recommendation. Retrieval combines a priority-infilling entity (PIE) typing model over neighborhood/relation types, 11 hand-defined structural walking paths (entity-to-entity and relation-to-entity co-occurrence scores), and MPNet text-embedding kNN search accelerated with FAISS product quantization; candidates are merged via a priority-infilling ensemble weighted by per-model recall. Re-ranking trains three KGE models—TransE, ComplEx, and NOTE—over randomly initialized, text-feature, and graph-structure-enhanced node representations, aggregated by a two-step ensemble. The method raises validation MRR from 0.2342 to 0.2839 and achieves 0.2309 MRR on the test-challenge set.
