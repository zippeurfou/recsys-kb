## Summary

This paper investigates using **Riemannian gradient descent** to optimize deep learning models with large matrices represented as low-rank factorizations (parameters broken down into smaller rank-$r$ matrices). The authors explored ten different design choices within the Riemannian optimization framework, including various geometries for these factorized parameters. However, despite tuning the learning rates and comparing against AdamW, their specific Riemannian methods did not consistently outperform standard approaches in experiments targeting multihead attention layers of small language models.

## Technical note

This paper explores Riemannian optimization for weight matrices kept in low-rank factored form W=AB^T, treating rank-r matrices as a smooth submanifold and updating W directly rather than A and B independently. It systematically studies ten design points: two geometries for rank-r matrices, three for rank-r partial isometries, and block-matrix (weight-sharing) variants, using a schematic iteration with Riemannian gradient, momentum, geodesic exponential map, and parallel transport at per-iteration cost O(mr^2+nr^2). Applied to multi-head attention layers of small language models, the methods do not conclusively beat an AdamW baseline after learning-rate tuning. It is a general optimization study with no recommendation-systems content.
