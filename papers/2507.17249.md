## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Harnessing Large Language Models (LLMs) for recommendation systems has emerged as a prominent avenue, drawing substantial research interest. However, existing approaches primarily involve basic prompt techniques for knowledge acquisition, which resemble System-1 thinking. This makes these methods highly sensitive to errors in the reasoning path, where even a small mistake can lead to an incorrect inference. ## Technical note

R4ec (Kuaishou) evolves LLM-based recommendation from fast System-1 prompting toward deliberate System-2 thinking via an iterative reasoning, reflection, and refinement framework. It uses two small LLMs (Qwen-2.5 7B): an actor model that reasons about user preference and item factual knowledge and refines its outputs, and a reflection model that judges rationality and gives feedback, iterating until no errors remain; the refined knowledge is then fed into a recommendation backbone. On Amazon-Book and MovieLens-1M it outperforms baselines, deployment on a large online advertising platform yielded a 2.2% revenue increase, and the paper studies scaling properties of the actor and reflection models.
