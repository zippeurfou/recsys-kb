## Summary

Firing Strict Cold-Start Items with Frozen Heterogeneous and Homogeneous Graphs for Recommendation":

1.  **Problem:** Current recommendation models using user/item IDs struggle with strict cold-start items (those unseen in training or test) because existing methods often only improve warm-start recommendations by incorporating multi-modal side information, which can be insufficient or even detrimental.
2.  **Solution:** The paper introduces Firzen, a unified framework that effectively leverages both item multi-modal content and knowledge graphs by preserving them as frozen graphs (collaborative KG and semantic/behavioral homogeneous graphs). This allows the model to capture collaborative signals for cold-start items using their side information while also benefiting warm-start recommendations.
3.  **Results:** Firzen significantly improves strict cold-start recommendation performance, effectively handling previously unseen item-user interactions, without compromising or even sacrificing performance on common warm-start scenarios where some user/item IDs are seen during training.

## Technical note

ID-based recommenders excel at warm-start but fail on strict cold-start items that have no training interactions, while content/KG methods that help cold-start can degrade warm-start accuracy. Firzen proposes a unified framework with Side-information-Aware Heterogeneous Graph Learning over a frozen collaborative knowledge graph and Modality-Specific Homogeneous Graph Learning over frozen item-item and user-user graphs. Freezing the graphs (no dropout/updates during training) lets item representations be built from multi-modal content and KG structure so unseen items still get usable embeddings. On four new strict cold-start benchmarks from Amazon and a Weixin Channels industrial dataset, Firzen substantially improves strict cold-start while matching state-of-the-art warm-start performance.
