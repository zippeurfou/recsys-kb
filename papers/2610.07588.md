## Summary

This paper introduces **Personal-Agent Mediated Recommendation**, where a personal LLM ranks items using cross-platform user history, while a platform LLM provides initial rankings based on local data. They created **MediateRec**, a benchmark to study this interaction, and developed **PAMO**, an optimization method designed to effectively balance rescuing items from platform bias against the risk of harmful overrides. Experiments show PAMO significantly improves recommendation outcomes across different platforms and achieves a better balance between beneficial corrections and negative impacts compared to other methods.

## Technical note

The paper formalizes Personal-Agent Mediated Recommendation, where a platform ranks candidates from platform-local data and a user-authorized personal LLM agent that also sees the user's cross-platform history turns that ranking into the final top-10 slate, trading rescues of targets the platform missed against harmful overrides of targets it had right. The MediateRec benchmark uses four Amazon Reviews 2023 categories as proxy platforms (Movie and Toy for training, Grocery and Beauty unseen) plus a real cross-platform test on OpenPlay (Steam target, Nintendo and Xbox history, 645 users), with 50-candidate episodes ranked by a frozen platform built from a SASRec ranker followed by Claude Sonnet 4.6 reranking on within-platform history (HR@10 49.3% on Amazon, 40.8% on OpenPlay). The agent, Qwen3-4B-Instruct, starts from SFT on 2,000 Sonnet demonstrations, and PAMO then runs GRPO with NDCG@10 split into per-cutoff group-relative advantages, shifting the fixed advantage mass within rollouts that disagree with the platform toward responses whose rationale likelihood drops most when cross-platform history is masked, via a KL-regularized exponential tilt under a value floor that preserves average platform-relative NDCG magnitude; the authors prove mass preservation and local optimality of this reallocation. The untuned base model falls below the platform on all five test sets and Claude Sonnet and Opus 4.6 still make non-negligible harmful overrides, while PAMO beats matched outcome-only GRPO on HR@10 and NDCG@10 everywhere (OpenPlay NDCG@10 0.284 vs 0.267 for GRPO and 0.241 for the platform, harmful overrides 6.0% vs 7.1%) and is competitive with the proprietary models at 4B parameters. Masking cross-platform history lowers every model's accuracy, PAMO without the value floor does worse than uniform allocation, support concentration kappa=0.5 works best, and annotation of 900 rescues attributes 72% primarily to cross-platform context.
