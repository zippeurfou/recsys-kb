## Summary

This paper introduces Graph-GRPO, a method for improving credit assignment in generative e-commerce search relevance models using large language models (LLMs). Current reinforcement learning techniques struggle with assigning credit to specific reasoning steps because they either rely solely on outcome-level rewards or ignore dependencies between steps. Graph-GRPO models the reasoning chain as a graph with nodes and edges representing steps and their logical dependencies, allowing outcome-level rewards to be propagated to individual steps for more accurate credit assignment, leading to better performance.

## Technical note

Graph-GRPO is a graph-structured extension of GRPO for LLM-based e-commerce search relevance that addresses misaligned credit assignment in chain-of-thought reasoning. It models the relevance reasoning chain as a dependency graph with CoT steps as nodes and their logical dependencies as edges, then propagates sparse outcome-level rewards along the graph into fine-grained node-level credit signals. A main-loss-driven controller parameterizes edge credit-propagation coefficients as learnable variables updated by policy-loss gradients, enabling task-aligned responsibility allocation. The framework adds CoT random masking for supervised policy initialization and graph-node-based multi-head distillation to a lightweight model, with offline evaluations and online A/B tests on JD showing gains in relevance classification and engagement metrics.
