## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of evaluating conversational AI systems due to the limitations of online A/B testing (scarce traffic and long readout times). It proposes a reusable framework where an offline proxy for user engagement is constructed through three critical alignments: mapping user behavior labels to product outcomes, linking a conversational AI's actions to a classifier predicting engagement, and ensuring the offline signal aligns with online experiment results. The framework's effectiveness is validated through 489 offline-online contrasts from 27 deployed experiments, showing high F1 scores and accurate directional predictions compared to a standard classifier, supporting its use for prioritizing system changes before committing to costly online tests.

## Technical note

Online A/B tests are the decision standard for conversational-assistant engagement but traffic and readout time limit how many changes can be tested, so Meta builds and audits an offline proxy computable without treatment-arm exposure. The proxy is treated as a chain of three alignments (behavioral label to product outcome, learned engagement classifier to candidate behavior, aggregated offline signal to experiment effect) and instantiated as a fixed multi-turn evaluation suite, a session/prompt-level engagement classifier and a calibration layer mapping sample-level score differences to model-level online engagement deltas, audited by interval-aware decision agreement (comparing confidence intervals rather than point estimates) and within-experiment ranking. Across 489 paired offline-online contrasts from 27 experiments, on the 113 post-freeze contrasts (8 experiments) the frozen composite reaches 81.1% F1 vs 34.3% for the raw classifier score, with zero wrong-direction calls vs 31, and beats five other deployed offline evaluators by 25.7 F1 points on a shared slice. Spending a fixed scoring budget on interaction depth beats more single-response cases, and the proxy is used to prioritize checkpoints and system prompts before scarce experiment traffic.
