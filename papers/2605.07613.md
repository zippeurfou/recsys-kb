## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of recommending news in conversational agents, where user intents are often implicit and hard to capture from keywords. To solve this, the authors introduce a novel Intent-Driven Semantic ID (SID) generation method, mapping diverse intents to hierarchical prefixes and matching them against news content. Their system, using a 7B LLM, achieves state-of-the-art results with high accuracy and strong cold-start performance, matching or exceeding GPT-4-based methods on key metrics at significantly lower cost.

## Technical note

This paper tackles conversational news recommendation over a rapidly expiring article corpus (most articles cycle out within 24h) with pervasive item and user cold-start and five implicit intent types that lack retrievable keywords. It proposes intent-driven Semantic ID generation under a Generate-then-Match paradigm: an LLM directly generates hierarchical RQ-VAE SID prefixes from intent, profile, and dialogue context, which are fuzzy-matched to the current news pool to guarantee grounded, hallucination-free recommendations. Two-stage training combines multi-task SID alignment with GPT-4 Chain-of-Thought distillation, and Profile-Aware Dual-Signal Reasoning routes cold/warm/hybrid paths so zero-history users still get recommendations. The 7B model reaches 0% hallucination, 12.4% L1 match in a 152K SID space, and sub-100ms latency.
