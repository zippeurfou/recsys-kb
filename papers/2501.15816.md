## Summary

Real-world recommendation systems often struggle with over-reliance on ID-based features (like user IDs) due to the long-tail data distribution problem caused by popularity bias, which hinders comprehensive learning of non-ID meta-features. To address this, a model-agnostic framework called AdaF^2M^2 was proposed, incorporating a feature-mask mechanism and an adapter module that utilizes multi-forward training and adaptive weights respectively. This approach has demonstrated significant improvements in user engagement metrics through online A/B tests and offline experiments, proving its effective enhancement of feature modeling capabilities across various applications.

## Technical note

Skewed long-tail data and popularity bias make industrial recommenders over-rely on ID features, weakening learning of non-ID meta features and hurting generalization, robustness to noise, and cold-start. AdaF2M2 is a model-agnostic framework combining a feature-mask mechanism (multi-forward training over feature-masked augmented samples for comprehensive feature learning) with a state-aware adapter that applies adaptive per-feature weights conditioned on user/item state signals. Deployed on both retrieval and ranking across Douyin Group, it delivered +1.37% user active days and +1.89% app duration in online A/B tests, with consistent offline gains across base models.
