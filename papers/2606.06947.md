## Technical note

DREAM identifies early static SID commitment (not model capacity) as the fundamental cold-start bottleneck in Semantic-ID-based generative recommendation: offline tokenizers assign each cold item one poorly discriminative SID path before feedback exists, and constrained decoding makes such items unreachable. DREAM is a three-stage progressive-refinement framework: an intent-aware tokenizer rebuilds the SID space via counterfactual contrastive learning to generate a diverse pool of behavior-aligned candidate SIDs per cold item; the frozen recommendation backbone acts as an evaluator selecting the most reliable candidate via multi-context user support without retraining; and a dynamic beam mechanism keeps multiple weighted SID hypotheses through training and inference. On three Amazon benchmarks DREAM substantially outperforms SOTA generative and sequential baselines on cold-start metrics.
