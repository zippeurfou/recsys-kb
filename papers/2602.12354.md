## Technical note

Feed SR is LinkedIn's transformer-based sequential ranking model that replaces a DCNv2 ranker for 1.2B+ Feed members. It interleaves post and action representations over T=1000 impressions into a decoder-only transformer (pre-LayerNorm, RoPE, scaled residuals), discards action-position outputs, and late-fuses context features into a parallel-DCNv2 multi-task head predicting Long Dwell and Contributions with BCE loss. Production techniques include late fusion, LLM-derived member profile embeddings, and within-session randomization to fight train-serve skew; serving uses a disaggregated CPU/GPU stack with a custom SRMIS Flash-Attention kernel (~2x over PyTorch SDPA). Feed SR reduced features ~80%, and in online A/B tests over three months lifted +2.10% time spent and +3.52% likes/comments/reshares. HSTU-block and fine-tuned LLM-Ranker alternatives underperformed on the quality-latency trade-off (HSTU -0.21% Long Dwell AUC at matched compute).
