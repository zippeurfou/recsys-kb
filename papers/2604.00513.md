## Summary

1.  The paper addresses the limitations of current multimodal large language models (MLLMs) for e-commerce product understanding, which struggle to capture fine-grained attributes due to implicit encoding and inherent model weaknesses.
2.  The authors propose MOON3.0, the first reasoning-aware MLLM model, tackling specific challenges like long-context reasoning dilution, limitations of supervised fine-tuning, and loss of fine-grained details by incorporating three key technical modules.
3.  MOON3.0 introduces mechanisms for adaptive modality fusion, reinforcement learning for reasoning strategy exploration, and residual enhancement for detail preservation, and it demonstrates state-of-the-art zero-shot performance on a new benchmark dataset.

## Technical note

MOON3.0 is a reasoning-aware MLLM for e-commerce product representation that explicitly deconstructs multimodal inputs into structured multi-dimensional attributes before generating an embedding, rather than treating the MLLM as a static feature extractor. It combines a multi-head modality fusion module (gating over raw image-text features to counter attention dilution) with a joint contrastive and reinforcement learning framework that uses a GRPO objective with composite rewards for attribute format, length, retrieval ranking, and quality. A fine-grained residual enhancement (FIRE) module preserves local details across visual encoding, cross-modal fusion, and language decoding via patch-level gating and long-range residual connections. The authors release the MBE3.0 benchmark and report state-of-the-art zero-shot performance across retrieval, classification, and attribute prediction tasks.
