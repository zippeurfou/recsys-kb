## Technical note

OptiRAG-Rec (Meta) addresses the efficiency-accuracy trade-off of RAG-augmented LLM CTR predictors by combining a lightweight GCN-Retriever (replacing expensive LLM embedders for similar-user retrieval over the user-item graph) with a multi-head early-exit mechanism that dynamically terminates Vicuna-7B inference once binary yes/no prediction confidence stabilizes across layers. Built on few-shot fine-tuning (<10% training data) with additional language heads mounted at layers 5/10/15/20/25/30, it treats CTR as constrained yes/no token generation. On five datasets it reaches AUC 82.11 (BookCrossing), 96.37 (Beauty), 97.86 (Video Games) and 98.46 (Movies and TV), beating TALLRec and traditional baselines (DeepFM, DIN, DCNv2) while boosting throughput; GCN retrieval outperforms LLM- and FM-based retrievers, and multi-head early exit beats SparseGPT pruning and TinyLLaMA compression on the accuracy-latency frontier.
