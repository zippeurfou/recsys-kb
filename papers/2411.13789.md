## Summary

Multi-Faceted Knowledge Enhanced LLM Empowered Display Advertisement Recommender System":

Traditional display ad systems often ignore content information during initial retrieval, limiting recommendation diversity. To overcome this and leverage Large Language Models (LLMs)' world knowledge effectively, researchers introduced LEADRE, which tackles three specific challenges inherent in using LLMs for ads: capturing user intent, bridging the gap between general knowledge and advertising specifics, and deploying LLMs efficiently.

LEADRE employs a multi-module framework: an Intent-Aware Prompt module generates personalized ad prompts; an Advertising-Specific Knowledge Alignment module fine-tunes the LLM using Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) to understand ad semantics and business value; and an Efficient Deployment system handles latency requirements for real-time recommendations. Extensive offline testing showed LEADRE's effectiveness, and online A/B tests demonstrated its commercial success with a 1.57% GMV lift on WeChat platforms, where it now serves billions of daily requests.

## Technical note

Introduces LEADRE, an LLM-empowered generative retrieval system for display advertising retrieval, replacing ID-based learning-to-rank that underuses ad content. It has three parts: Intent-Aware Prompt Engineering (builds intent-aware prompt/response pairs mixing long- and short-term interests and cross-domain behaviors), Advertising-Specific Knowledge Alignment (auxiliary fine-tuning tasks plus Direct Preference Optimization to align generated ads with business value), and Latency-Aware Deployment (hybrid latency-tolerant/sensitive serving with TensorRT LLM). Built on a 1B Hunyuan model, online A/B tests on WeChat Channels and Moments raised GMV 1.57% and 1.17%, serving tens of billions of requests daily.
