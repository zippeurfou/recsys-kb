## Summary

This paper introduces UniSGR, a unified framework designed for semantic ID generation and ranking in recommendation systems, addressing their limitations with multi-stage cascade architectures.

UniSGR employs two stages: first, multi-scenario pre-training on diverse data to learn general skills; second, scenario-specific alignment optimizing Value-Aware Parallel Multi-Token Prediction (VA-PMTP) alongside a unified multi-objective ranking module during joint training.

To improve downstream ranking integration and inference efficiency, UniSGR utilizes Task-Aware Tokens guided by Funnel-Aware Contrastive Learning and an STARK attention mechanism with reorganized KV cache. Extensive offline experiments on a large e-commerce platform confirm its effectiveness and scalability compared to existing state-of-the-art methods.

## Technical note

UniSGR unifies semantic-ID generative retrieval and multi-objective ranking in one encoder-decoder framework to avoid the objective mismatch of cascade pipelines. Items are tokenized via RQ-VAE with Sinkhorn-Knopp into a 3-layer codebook (K=8192), a lightweight MemoryNet encoder feeds a sparse SwiGLU-MoE decoder (GQA, ~7% params active), and a two-stage recipe of multi-scenario NTP pre-training then scenario-specific alignment jointly optimizes Value-Aware Parallel Multi-Token Prediction (VA-PMTP), a PLE-based multi-objective ranking head, and Task-Aware Tokens guided by Funnel-Aware Contrastive Learning. STARK, a semantic-tree-attention decoding scheme with reorganized KV cache, replaces batch-dimension beam expansion to give ~200% throughput improvement (219 vs 119 QPS). On Lazada 'Guess You Like', UniSGR-M beats TIGER/OneRec/OneRec-V2 on HR@K (HR@100 0.2195 vs 0.2151 for OneRec) and online A/B lifts IPV +3.36%, transactions +2.17%, GMV +5.68%.
