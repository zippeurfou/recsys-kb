## Technical note

HMAF (Hierarchical Multi-Slot Allocation Framework) unifies Guaranteed Delivery (GD) contract optimization with Real-Time Bidding (RTB) auctions in multi-slot advertising, avoiding the decoupling or heuristic priority rules of prior work. It follows a Plan-Calibrate-Execute paradigm combining offline GD resource planning under impression constraints, dynamic calibration of GD-RTB competitiveness, and online real-time listwise rank decisions across multi-slot page views to balance short-term revenue with long-term delivery guarantees. Deployed across marketing scenarios at Meituan, HMAF increases GD delivery rate by 3.72% and total advertising revenue by 1.59%.
