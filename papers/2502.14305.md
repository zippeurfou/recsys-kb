## Technical note

This LinkedIn paper shares insights for training and deploying small language models (SLMs) for industrial recommendation/search use cases, focusing on knowledge distillation and model compression via structured pruning and quantization. The internal foundation model is a Mixtral-style Mixture-of-Experts (16 experts, 4 active, each initialized from Llama 3.1 8B Instruct) trained purely on text to generalize across ranking and generative tasks. It reports deployment lessons and hardware optimizations that let SLMs retain most large-model quality (AUC for predictive tasks) while cutting serving cost and latency.
