## Summary

Large-scale e-commerce retrieval faces a challenge due to a mismatch in objectives between dual-encoder retrievers (optimized for contrastive similarity) and downstream rerankers (capturing finer relevance). Standard reinforcement learning (RL) fine-tuning for the retriever, even when the document index is frozen, can degrade the embedding geometry and damage pre-trained semantic manifolds. To overcome this, the authors propose PAO (Positive-Advantage-Only), a selective RL method that only updates retrieved items with positive advantages, preserving the global embedding structure while improving retrieval performance. Experiments show PAO significantly outperforms standard RL and distillation baselines.

## Technical note

Taobao/Alibaba address retriever-reranker objective mismatch in large-scale e-commerce semantic retrieval under the industrial constraint that the document index is frozen, so only the query encoder can be updated online. They cast it as an episodic MDP: the policy is the GTE-Base query encoder, the macro-action is a top-100 MIPS retrieval from the frozen index, the intra-list distribution is a temperature softmax over similarity scores, and the reward is an online fine-tuned BGE-Reranker score turned into a standardized advantage. The key empirical finding is geometry collapse: standard listwise REINFORCE over all samples (RL-All) catastrophically degrades the retriever on a proprietary 1M-train / 50k-test e-commerce search log, dropping Recall@5 from 0.7929 to 0.6547 (-13.8pt) and NDCG@5 from 0.7000 to 0.5635 (-13.6pt), because negative-advantage push updates in a frozen high-dimensional space have unbounded degrees of freedom and displace query vectors into semantic voids; t-SNE shows dispersed, overlapping category clusters. PAO (Positive-Advantage-Only) masks all A_i <= 0 terms so only pull updates toward above-baseline items survive, plus a KL penalty to the pretrained reference policy weighted by beta. Trained 2 epochs, batch 16, lr 2e-5, 100 warmup steps, beta=0.3. PAO reaches Recall@5 0.8618 (+6.9pt) and NDCG@5 0.7896 (+9.0pt) over the InfoNCE-finetuned baseline; an LLM judge (Qwen3-235b-a22b) gives Hits@20 +2.1pt and Matchment +1.4pt while RL-All degrades every judged metric. On MS MARCO passage ranking with the same frozen-index query-only recipe, PAO adds +2.03pt NDCG@5 and +4.23pt Recall@50 over the baseline and beats KL distillation from the same BGE teacher by +2.31pt Recall@50 and +0.56pt NDCG@50. Ablations put beta=0.3 and tau=1.0 at the optimum. Caveats: offline only with no online A/B, PAO cannot surface documents absent from the frozen index, and it can efficiently propagate reward-model bias (for example click-bait preference) from the reranker into retrieval.
