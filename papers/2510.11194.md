Personalized alignment is crucial for enabling Large Language Models (LLMs) to engage effectively in user-centric interactions. However, current methods face a dual challenge: they fail to infer users' deep implicit preferences (including unstated goals, semantic context and risk tolerances), and they lack the defensive reasoning required to navigate real-world ambiguity. ## Technical note

CDRA (Critique-Driven Reasoning Alignment) reframes personalized LLM alignment from scalar reward-matching into a structured reasoning process to close a 'preference gap' (inferring implicit user goals/risk tolerances) and a 'process gap' (defensive reasoning under ambiguity). It introduces the DeepPref benchmark (3000 preference-query pairs over 20 topics with critique-annotated reasoning chains from a simulated cognitive council), a Personalized Generative Process Reward Model (Pers-GenPRM) that emits a critique chain before scoring, and Critique-Driven Policy Alignment, a process-level online RL algorithm fusing numerical and natural-language feedback that addresses the 'zero advantage' problem. Experiments show state-of-the-art results on deep preference understanding and robust reasoning.
