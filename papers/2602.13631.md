## Technical note

GEMs is a generative recommendation framework with a multi-stream decoder that breaks the long-sequence barrier by processing users' lifelong interactions (over 100,000 items) as a multi-timescale memory-reading problem. It partitions behaviors into Recent, Mid-term, and Lifecycle streams with tailored inference: a one-stage real-time extractor for recent dynamics, a lightweight cross-attention indexer for mid-term, and a two-stage offline-online compression module for lifelong history, fused parameter-free before the unified GR decoder. This counters attention's recency bias and sub-linear scaling; GEMs is the first lifelong GR framework deployed in a high-concurrency industrial environment, outperforming SOTA in accuracy with superior inference efficiency.
