## Technical note

FITMM is a frequency-aware, information-theoretic framework for multimodal recommendation that replaces spatial-domain modality fusion (which obscures frequency structure and amplifies misalignment/redundancy). It shows that under an orthogonal transform block-diagonalizing bandwise covariances, the Gaussian Information Bottleneck decouples across frequency bands, motivating a separate-then-fuse pipeline: graph-enhanced ID/visual/textual representations undergo modality-wise spectral decomposition into orthogonal bands, lightweight within-band multimodal components are formed, and a residual task-adaptive gate aggregates bands. A frequency-domain IB term (Wiener-style shrinkage with weak-band shut-off) controls redundancy and a cross-modal spectral consistency loss aligns modalities; on three real-world datasets it consistently and significantly beats advanced baselines.
