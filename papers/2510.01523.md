## Technical note

MetaSynth is a multi-agent retrieval-augmented generation framework that optimizes search-result meta titles/descriptions by learning from implicit feedback in black-box ranking systems. It builds an exemplar library from top-ranked (query, metadata) pairs, generates candidate snippets conditioned on product content plus exemplars, and iteratively refines them via evaluator-generator loops enforcing relevance, promotional strength, and compliance. On proprietary e-commerce data and the Amazon Reviews corpus it beats strong baselines on NDCG, MRR, and rank metrics, and large-scale A/B tests show +10.26% CTR and +7.51% clicks, contributing a general paradigm for optimizing content in black-box systems.
