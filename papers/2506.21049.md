## Technical note

SSUF (Semi-supervised Scalable Unified Framework) from JD unifies e-commerce query classification subtasks (intent, category, brand) that are hampered by short ambiguous queries and a click-driven 'Matthew vicious cycle' of biased training data. It combines three pluggable modules: a knowledge-enhanced module injecting world knowledge into query representations, a label-enhanced module using label semantics and semi-supervised signals to reduce dependence on posterior click labels, and a structure-enhanced module exploiting complex label relations. Extensive offline and online A/B experiments show SSUF significantly outperforms state-of-the-art models and was deployed with commercial value.
