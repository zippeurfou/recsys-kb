## Technical note

A2DCDR (Adversarial Alignment and Disentanglement Cross-Domain Recommendation) tackles cold-start and data sparsity by capturing not only domain-invariant features but also 'valuable non-aligned' source-exclusive signals that benefit the target domain (e.g., gym-frequency signal from Sport helping Cloth recs). It refines MMD-based inter-domain adaptation with adversarial training to enrich source representations, uses an intra-domain disentangler with a mutual-information minimizer plus a feature reconstructor to preserve non-aligned features, and fuses domain-invariant + non-aligned + contextual features for prediction. Experiments on real-world datasets and online A/B testing show A2DCDR outperforms SOTA CDR methods.
