## Technical note

PTOFA (Predict-Then-Optimize Fund Allocation) reformulates online fund recommendation as a constrained allocation problem rather than top-k recommendation, to avoid the Matthew effect and revenue-agnostic conversion maximization. In the prediction stage it uses a counterfactual entire-space multi-task module to estimate each customer-fund pair's expected revenue (the first attempt to predict fund expected revenue), and in the optimization stage a solver maximizes total revenue under exposure and risk-matching constraints. Offline and online A/B experiments on LiCaiTong, one of China's largest online investment platforms, validate its effectiveness and efficiency.
