## Technical note

MBCN (Multi-Branch Collaborative learning Network) tackles video quality assessment in industrial video search, categorizing low-quality videos into four types: visual (mosaics/black boxes), textual (title/OCR issues), and semantic frame-incoherence/frame-text-mismatch from AI-generated videos. It uses four dedicated assessment branches whose scores are dynamically fused via a squeeze-and-excitation mechanism, and combines point-wise and pair-wise objectives for stable, well-ranged scores. Offline and online experiments in a world-level video search engine show significantly improved ranking and notably better recognition of AI-generated low-quality videos.
