## Technical note

REVISION (Taobao/Alibaba) addresses User-SearchSys Intent Discrepancy in Taobao's Pai Li Tao visual search, where many no-click queries reflect diverse implicit intents. Offline, a periodic pipeline aggregates no-click queries and uses VLMs (Qwen2.5VL-72B, Qwen3-30B-A3B) to mine implicit intents and generate actionable optimization suggestions from query images and product metadata. Online, REVISION-R1-3B (Qwen2.5VL-3B trained on the offline data) reasons over query images and historical products to predict and dispatch strategy-optimization plans across the search pipeline. Online A/B tests reduced no-click queries by 13.91% on the trigger subset and lifted CTR +10.73%, orders +13.60%, and GMV +10.73%.
