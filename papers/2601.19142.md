## Technical note

LAIN (Length-Adaptive Interest Network) is a plug-and-play CTR framework addressing a counterintuitive phenomenon: increasing max input sequence length improves long-sequence users but degrades short-sequence users (who are the majority: 57% of users contribute only 14.3% of samples), due to attention polarization and length-signal deficiency. LAIN explicitly conditions on sequence length via three lightweight components: a Spectral Length Encoder (Fourier bases mapping length to embeddings), Length-Conditioned Prompting (injecting global cues into long/short branches), and Length-Modulated Attention (adjusting softmax temperature/sharpness by length). Across three benchmarks and five CTR backbones it gives up to 1.15% AUC gain and 2.25% logloss reduction, notably boosting short-sequence users without hurting long-sequence ones.
