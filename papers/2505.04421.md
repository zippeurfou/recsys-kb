## Technical note

LONGER is ByteDance's GPU-efficient transformer for end-to-end ultra-long user behavior sequence modeling (scaling to length 10,000) in industrial recommenders, avoiding the upstream-downstream inconsistency of two-stage retrieval, pre-trained user embeddings, or memory-augmented approaches. It uses a global token mechanism to stabilize attention over long contexts, an InnerTransformer-based token-merge module plus hybrid attention to cut ~50% FLOPs near-losslessly, and engineering optimizations (mixed precision, activation recomputation, KV cache serving, synchronous training/serving). LONGER outperforms strong baselines offline and in online A/B tests for advertising and e-commerce, and is deployed across dozens of scenarios at ByteDance serving billions of users.
