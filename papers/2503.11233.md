## Technical note

This Lazada/Alibaba paper proposes Dual Enhanced Attention to address two failure modes of Transformer-based feature interaction in CTR prediction: information loss from compressing pairwise relationships via inner products, and interaction collapse where low-information-abundance feature fields cause collapsed embedding matrices under long-tail feature distributions. The framework combines a Combo-ID attention mechanism that directly retains feature interaction pairs to mitigate information loss, and a collapse-avoiding attention mechanism that adaptively filters out low-information-abundance interaction pairs. Extensive experiments on industrial datasets demonstrate its effectiveness for CTR prediction.
