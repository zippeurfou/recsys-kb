## Technical note

Retrieval-GRPO (Taobao) is a multi-objective reinforcement learning framework for dense retrieval in e-commerce search that eliminates offline hard negative mining by dynamically retrieving top-K candidate products per query during training and using a relevance LLM as a real-time reward model. Rewards combine LLM relevance scores, product quality scores, and multi-way exclusivity metrics for multi-objective preference alignment, mitigating the seesaw effect of multi-task learning between relevance and non-relevance objectives. This removes complex offline negative-construction pipelines, improves semantic generalization on long-tail queries, and is deployed on China's largest e-commerce platform with validated offline and online gains.
