## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) are a new research frontier with various applications and successes. The end-to-end inference for all nodes, is common for GNN embedding models, which are widely adopted in applications like recommendation and advertising. While sharing opportunities arise in GNN tasks (i.e., inference for a few nodes and training), the potential for sharing in full graph end-to-end inference is largely underutilized because traditional efforts fail to fully extract sharing benefits ...

## Technical note

Deal is a distributed GNN inference system dedicated to end-to-end inference for all nodes on graphs with multi-billion edges, used in applications like recommendation and advertising. It exploits an untapped sharing opportunity by sampling k separate 1-layer ego networks per node and collecting same-layer ego networks into 1-hop graphs, eliminating pointer-chasing and maximizing computation sharing, and adds a lightweight 1-D graph/feature collaborative partition with memory-saving distributed primitives (GEMM, SPMM, SDDMM) plus pipelined communication. On real-world benchmarks Deal reduces end-to-end inference time up to 7.70x and graph construction time up to 21.05x versus state-of-the-art.
