## Technical note

This Google paper analyzes whether LLMs can reason over collaborative information in recommendation, comparing them to matrix factorization (MF) baselines. It proposes RAG over the user-item interaction matrix with four prompting strategies to supply similar users'/items' interactions at inference. Results show the LLM outperforms MF whenever relevant collaborative information is provided in a clear, easy-to-follow format and the LLM is prompted to reason over it, and in nearly all cases more provided information yields better LLM performance.
