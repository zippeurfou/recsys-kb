## Summary

PinEqualizer is a comprehensive system developed by Pinterest specifically to tackle the content cold-start problem in both search and recommendation stages across their entire funnel, covering all types of exploration surfaces from initial discovery to final ranking.

Key innovations include its ability to span multiple user interaction paths (likepins, savepins) seamlessly while debiasing algorithms towards newer or less popular items, thereby reducing over-reliance on established content and improving long-term accuracy in diverse contexts.

The system's effectiveness was validated through scalable evaluation methods, allowing rapid testing against historical data, and its successful deployment has demonstrably increased fresh content exploration, overall user engagement, and the health of their content ecosystem.

## Technical note

PinEqualizer tackles content cold-start across Pinterest's full multi-stage search and recommendation funnel, where a rich-get-richer bias favors established content. It spans corpus selection (Thompson Sampling plus a model-based engagement prior), retrieval (a dedicated exploration corpus for token/graph/two-tower embedding retrieval, weighted random walk, and a debiased query tower), and ranking (engagement-feature dropout, feature imputation, DCNv2 feature crossing, content-type-aware calibration, and UCB / Neural Linear UCB exploration in the utility layer), plus a 3-layer measurement framework (fresh-content holdout north star, content-graduation proxy, and an under-explored engagement A/B metric). Since 2024 it delivered a 350% increase in fresh content impressions, +24%/+49% successful sessions (NA/i18n), +41% graduated corpus, +99% successful content providers, and an 18.5% shopping-session gain. A multi-stage debiasing and exploration system, not a generative recommender.
