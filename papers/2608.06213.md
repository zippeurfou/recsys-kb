## Summary

Traditional recommender systems often use complex multi-stage cascades requiring separate components and repeated user history processing.

Gryphon-v2 introduces a simpler end-to-end architecture by combining generation (Semantic-ID) and ranking into one unified model, processing user history only once during inference.

This model is trained using 'Rollout Distillation', where it learns to rank items by distilling knowledge from a separate high-capacity Teacher Ranker that simulates the production cascade's ranking preferences.

## Technical note

Yandex Music replaces a production cascade of >15 candidate generators plus pre-ranker and ranker with one served Gryphon-v2 model: a bidirectional Transformer encodes the user history once, an autoregressive decoder emits Semantic IDs under catalogue-trie-constrained beam search, and a lightweight item-level Ranking Module cross-attends to the same encoder states to produce the final order (SIDs come from residual K-means over Qwen2.5-Omni text/audio features aligned to collaborative signals QARM-style, Independent Code Rate 0.98). Rollout Distillation supervises the Ranking Module solely with a training-only Teacher Ranker (ARGUS-style autoregressive training, up to 8,000-event context), scoring candidates from current-decoder beam-search rollouts (beam 32, >90% of distinct distillation candidates) plus logged impressions, each as an equally weighted per-task MAE term added to the next-token-prediction loss. Distillation raises TeacherRecall@10 from <=0.04 (beam order, or Gryphon's identically-architected next-item-trained ranker) to 0.5654 and weighted pair accuracy from 0.5528 to 0.5892 -- closing 54% of the gap to the Teacher Ranker (0.6199) and 59% to the production ranker (0.6141) -- with Recall@1000 unchanged at 0.8615. In an 8%/8% user-level online A/B, active users +1.41%, total listening time +1.62%, likes +7.12%, Repeat commands +15.25%, unfinished-track ratio -9.65%, at latency comparable to the cascade while ranking <=1,200 resolved items instead of 10,000->3,000 and sustaining ~4x the throughput of the same backbone followed online by the Teacher Ranker. Ablations: rollout-only loses WPA, impression-only halves TeacherRecall@10 (0.2983); beam 64/128 and MSE/Huber/KL losses give only modest offline shifts.
