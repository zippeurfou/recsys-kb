## Technical note

TripCraft is a spatio-temporally fine-grained travel-planning benchmark built entirely from real-world data (public transit stops/schedules, event availability, diverse attraction categories, user personas), fixing spatial inconsistencies and missing constraints in TravelPlanner/TravelPlanner+. It has 1000 queries across 140 U.S. cities for 3/5/7-day itineraries with gold plans from 25 annotators, and proposes five continuous evaluation metrics (Temporal Meal, Temporal Attraction, Spatial, Ordering, Persona) beyond binary constraint checks. A parameter-informed setting raises the 7-day Temporal Meal Score from 61% to 80%, establishing a realistic constraint-aware LLM travel-planning benchmark.
