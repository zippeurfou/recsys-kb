## Summary

This paper introduces the Riemannian Nyström approximation, a low-rank method for efficiently approximating tangent operators on manifolds. It replaces Gaussian sketching with a Haar-Grassmann sketching condition to preserve key properties like positive semidefiniteness while handling the curved geometry. The resulting approximation enables faster optimization algorithms, demonstrated to reduce computation time while maintaining accuracy on various manifolds.

## Technical note

The paper develops the Riemannian Nystrom approximation, a low-rank approximation of self-adjoint positive-semidefinite tangent-space operators on manifolds via subspace projections onto the tangent space. It replaces Gaussian sketching with a coordinate-free Haar-Grassmann sketching condition that stays compatible under isometric vector transport across tangent spaces, while inheriting classical Nystrom properties such as positive semidefiniteness and spectral/Frobenius error bounds. A randomized Newton-type optimization method is proposed in which the linear system is built from this approximation. Experiments on SPD and Grassmann manifolds and principal geodesic analysis on real data show reduced computational cost of operator inversion with comparable accuracy.
