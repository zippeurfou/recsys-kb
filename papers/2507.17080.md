## Technical note

VL-CLIP (Walmart) enhances CLIP embeddings for e-commerce multimodal recommendation and search, tackling CLIP's weak object-level alignment, ambiguous text, and domain mismatch. It integrates Visual Grounding to localize key products for fine-grained visual embeddings and an LLM agent to disambiguate and enrich product descriptions for better text embeddings. Deployed over tens of millions of items, it increased CTR by 18.6%, add-to-cart by 15.5%, and GMV by 4.0%, outperforming CLIP, FashionCLIP, and GCL.
