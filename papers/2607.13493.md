## Summary

This paper introduces a system for personalizing incremental video search on Apple TV by combining text-based semantic (TextEmb) and ID-based collaborative (IdEmb) item embeddings learned from co-engagement data and user interactions, respectively. At ranking time, these item embeddings are combined with user representations derived from recent watch history in an XGBoost model to inject personalized signals via cosine similarity. The approach demonstrated significant offline improvements (+2.99% NDCG@10, +3.30% MRR) and strong online results (+1.14% TTR, +1.23% conversion rate), particularly benefiting users with longer history on ambiguous queries where intent is less clear.

## Technical note

Apple TV incremental (per-keystroke) video search personalization that combines two item embedding spaces: TextEmb, a multilingual Siamese text encoder fine-tuned on co-engagement pairs with a pairwise hinge/contrastive loss and in-batch negatives, and IdEmb, an ID-based collaborative Siamese model over co-play pairs. Mean-pooled user watch-history embeddings yield text and ID user-item cosine features injected into a pairwise XGBoost learning-to-rank model, with TextEmb giving cold-start/long-tail coverage and IdEmb high-precision behavioral signal. Offline the hybrid ranker lifts NDCG@10 +2.99% and MRR +3.30% overall, with the largest gain on ambiguous 1-3 character prefix queries (+8.63% NDCG@10 vs +1.46% on longer queries), and gains rise with history length. A three-week online A/B improves tap-through rate +1.14% and conversion rate +1.23% with a 2.91% better converted-item position; a held-out LLM-judged triplet corpus assesses embedding quality independent of click bias.
