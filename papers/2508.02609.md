## Technical note

Pinterest builds a large-scale onsite-offsite heterogeneous graph from users' onsite ad interactions and opt-in offsite conversions to capture shopping intent, and introduces TransRA (TransR with Anchors), a knowledge-graph-embedding model that maps non-anchor entity spaces into a single anchor space for efficient downstream use. To integrate KGE into ranking (which initially gave only modest gains), they load embeddings into large ID embedding tables and apply an attention-based KGE fine-tuning with a self-attention layer mimicking graph interactions. Deployed in Pinterest's Ads Engagement Model, it delivered a 2.69% CTR lift and 1.34% CPC reduction.
