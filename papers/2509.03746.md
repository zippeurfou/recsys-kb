## Technical note

Challenges the prevailing multi-token item tokenization in LLM-based recommenders by treating item IDs as first-class single-token embeddings, enabling single-step decoding. A dedicated item embedding table is projected into the LLM space, and a Two-Level (hierarchical, cluster-based) softmax makes training sublinear in catalog size; inference uses hierarchical structure or ANN search. A training paradigm mixes textual metadata with ID-only examples (25% ID-only) so inference is ID-only. On Amazon shopping datasets the method improves Recall/NDCG over multi-token baselines while giving 5x-14x inference speedup.
