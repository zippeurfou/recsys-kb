## Summary

Existing Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs) often ignore the correlations between different time series channels, which are crucial for accurate multivariate forecasting. To address this, the authors propose CoRA, a lightweight adapter that plugs into existing TSFMs to learn and leverage both dynamic and static correlations between channels. CoRA uses a low-rank decomposition of the correlation matrix, learnable polynomials for dynamic trends, and a novel dual contrastive loss, demonstrating significant improvements in forecasting accuracy across multiple real-world datasets.

## Technical note

CoRA is a lightweight plug-and-play adapter that adds channel-correlation modeling to channel-independent time series foundation models (TSFMs) through fine-tuning only. It targets three correlation types: dynamic (DCorr), heterogeneous positive/negative (HCorr), and partial (PCorr). A Dynamic Correlation Estimation module decomposes the correlation matrix into low-rank Time-Varying and Time-Invariant components, with learnable polynomials capturing trend/periodic dynamics. Heterogeneous-Partial Contrastive Learning uses channel-aware projections into positive/negative correlation spaces regulated by a dual (Heterogeneous-Partial) contrastive loss, achieving linear complexity in the number of channels at inference and improving multivariate forecasting across 10 real-world datasets.
