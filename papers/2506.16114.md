## Technical note

GFlowGR from Alibaba is a GFlowNet-based fine-tuning framework for generative recommendation that fixes two mismatches of supervised fine-tuning: single-item point-wise targets vs deployment's need for diverse candidate sets, and treating all interactions as equally informative. It reformulates GR as sequential set-generation with three components: a trajectory sampler building training trajectories from candidate sets for set-wise learning, a behavior-aware reward model quantifying item utility, and a GFlowNet objective giving token-level supervision so generation probability is proportional to reward. It outperforms strong baselines (including DPO and GRPO-style methods) on three datasets and, deployed in Taobao search advertising since mid-2025, delivered a 0.4% relative annual revenue improvement.
