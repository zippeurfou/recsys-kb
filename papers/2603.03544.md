## Summary

PinCLIP addresses the challenge of integrating large multi-modal Visual Language Models (VLMs) into Pinterest' recommendation systems by proposing a new large-scale representation model. It introduces a hybrid Vision Transformer architecture with a novel fusion mechanism designed to effectively align image and text data at different levels. PinCLIP significantly improves retrieval performance and business metrics, including addressing the cold-start problem, through offline and online evaluations.

## Technical note

PinCLIP learns large-scale multimodal Pin representations at Pinterest using a hybrid Vision Transformer with a convolutional stem and Funnel ViT trunk plus SigLIP multilingual text encoders, fused via a small Transformer aggregator and attention pooler. Training combines an image-to-text sigmoid (SigLIP-style) alignment loss with a novel Pin-to-Pin neighbor alignment loss sampled from the Pin-Board bipartite graph, using a chunked implementation to reduce multi-node communication. Matryoshka Representation Learning shrinks embedding dimensionality, and Flash Attention, funneling, and activation checkpointing scale training to ~890M images. It beats Qwen baselines by ~20% offline and, in A/B tests, lifts fresh-content distribution (+15% organic Repins, +8.7% new-Ads clicks), addressing cold-start.
