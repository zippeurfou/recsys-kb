## Technical note

Kuaishou's INFNet (Information Flow Network) makes feature interaction in ranking models both expressive and linear-time by representing categorical features, behavior sequences, and task identifiers as tokens with a small set of per-group hub tokens. Interaction proceeds via an aggregate-and-broadcast flow: hub tokens gather global context across groups via cross-attention, then a gated broadcast unit injects refined context back, avoiding early sequence aggregation and late task fusion bottlenecks while preserving item-level signals. On a public benchmark and industrial data INFNet beats strong lightweight baselines (RankMixer, HSTU-style) with strong scaling; online deployment in a commercial advertising system lifted revenue +1.587% and CTR +1.155%.
