## Technical note

SCASRec (Self-Correcting and Auto-Stopping Recommendation, Alibaba/navigation) is a unified generative encoder-decoder framework for route-list recommendation that merges fine-ranking, re-ranking, and redundancy elimination into one end-to-end process, addressing offline-online metric misalignment, rigid handcrafted redundancy rules, and disjoint ranking stages. It introduces a Stepwise Corrective Reward (SCR) using list-level List Coverage Rate as sequence-level supervision (evaluating marginal coverage gain per step) to align training with online satisfaction, and a learnable End-of-Recommendation (EOR) token that adaptively terminates generation instead of fixed rules, with noise-aware training. On two large-scale open route datasets SCASRec sets offline and online SOTA and is fully deployed in a real navigation app.
