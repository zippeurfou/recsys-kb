## Summary

This paper introduces AutoModel, an agent-based architecture called AgenticRS-Architecture designed for the full lifecycle management of recommender systems. It replaces traditional pipelines with three core agents (AutoTrain, AutoFeature, AutoPerf) responsible for model design/training, data analysis/feature evolution, and performance/deployment, respectively. These agents interact via a shared knowledge layer, and the system facilitates automated evolution and adaptation, demonstrated through a case study showing AutoTrain's ability to automate model reproduction, thereby reducing manual effort for scaling recommender systems.

## Technical note

AutoModel reframes the industrial recommender lifecycle as three interacting evolution agents—AutoTrain (model design/training), AutoFeature (data analysis and feature evolution), and AutoPerf (deployment, resource allocation, and A/B experimentation)—coordinated by a shared orchestration and knowledge layer that persists decisions, configs, and outcomes. Online recall/ranking/reranking modules are treated as evolvable decision-agent instances managed by AutoTrain rather than fixed pipeline stages. A case study, paper_auto_train, closes the loop from LLM-based paper parsing to code adaptation, large-scale training-job submission/monitoring, and offline metric comparison (AUC, NDCG, recall). Negative results are logged to the knowledge layer to avoid redundant future attempts, and the agents exchange inner/outer reward signals for credit assignment.
