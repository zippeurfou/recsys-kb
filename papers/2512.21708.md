## Summary

1.  The paper addresses the lack of parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods for large language model (LLM) agents, proposing MoRAgent which breaks down agent capabilities into three distinct roles.
2.  MoRAgent introduces the Mixture-of-Roles (MoR) framework, using three specialized LoRA adapters, one for each role (reasoner, executor, summarizer), allowing them to collaborate effectively.
3.  To train this framework, the authors developed a specific data generation pipeline, and validated their approach through extensive experiments on LLMs and agent benchmarks, demonstrating its effectiveness.

## Technical note

MoRAgent introduces parameter-efficient fine-tuning for LLM agents by decomposing agent capability into three roles: reasoner, executor, and summarizer. Its Mixture-of-Roles (MoR) framework assigns each role a group of LoRA adapters (more LoRAs for harder roles), routed via a rule-based role-aware gate and learnable token-aware routers, with auxiliary balance and orthogonality losses. A multi-role data generation pipeline (GPT-4o content completion, DeepSeek-V3 quality filtering) supplies training data. On StableToolBench it improves DFS pass rates by 40.6% and 14.2% for Llama3.2-1B and Phi-3.5-mini while adding only 0.16B-0.36B trainable parameters.
