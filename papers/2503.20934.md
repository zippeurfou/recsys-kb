## Summary

MOVEMETHOD is a hallmark refactoring. Despite a plethora of research tools that recommend which methods to move and where, these recommendations do not align with how expert developers perform MOVEMETHOD. Given the extensive training of Large Language Models and their reliance upon naturalness of code, they should expertly recommend which methods are misplaced in a given class and which classes are better hosts. ## Technical note

MM-assist is the first LLM-fully-powered assistant automating the entire MoveMethod refactoring lifecycle, from recommendation to execution, addressing that up to 80% of raw LLM (GPT-4o) MoveMethod suggestions are hallucinations. It filters hallucinations using IDE static analysis and code-trained vector embeddings, applies a self-consistency, critique, and ranking workflow, and uses refactoring-aware retrieval-augmented generation for global project-level reasoning within limited context, with two-step retrieval combining mechanical feasibility and VoyageAI semantic relevance. On a standard benchmark it improves Recall@1/@3 by 1.7x and by at least 2.4x on 210 recent open-source refactorings, and 30 developers rated 82.8% of its recommendations positively in a week-long user study.
