## Technical note

DRL-PUT is a deep RL framework for personalized ranking-utility-function tuning in Pinterest's ad recommender, replacing manual tuning of the linear weights that combine business-goal predictions (CTR, CVR). It formulates the problem as an RL task predicting optimal per-request hyperparameters and learns an optimal policy directly from online serving logs via a DNN policy-gradient method with discretized action space, avoiding value-function estimation under high-variance rewards. In an online A/B test at Pinterest it improved CTR by 9.7% and long-CTR by 7.7% on the treated segment.
