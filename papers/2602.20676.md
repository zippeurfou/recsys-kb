## Summary

PRECTR-V2 addresses the challenge of integrating search relevance and CTR prediction by tackling three main issues: sparse data for low-active users, training data mismatch with candidate distribution, and limitations of the frozen BERT encoder. It solves these problems by mining global preferences for cold-start scenarios, creating hard negative samples to correct exposure bias, and replacing the frozen BERT encoder with a lightweight LLM-distilled transformer encoder optimized for adaptation.

## Technical note

PRECTR-V2 is a unified relevance-CTR framework for e-commerce search (Xianyu) that decomposes click probability into a relevance-score-level distribution and a within-level conditional click model. It tackles cold-start personalization by mining query-category-conditioned relevance preferences from ~50 globally sampled similar users to augment sparse behavior sequences. Exposure bias is corrected with synthetic hard negatives (embedding noise injection plus relevance-label reconstruction) optimized via pairwise loss, regularized by a critical-distance penalty and dynamic truncation weighting to preserve PCOC calibration. It replaces the frozen BERT encoder with a lightweight transformer distilled from an LLM and SFT-tuned on text relevance classification, enabling joint representation and CTR fine-tuning. Gains are validated on industrial datasets and online A/B tests.
