## Summary

1.  The paper addresses the challenge of adapting general LLMs into effective biomedical-specific retrievers for RAG systems, proposing the STM framework.
2.  STM uses synthetic hard negatives and model merging to significantly improve domain-specific retrieval performance without extensive pretraining, achieving up to 23.5% improvement on biomedical tasks.
3.  The results demonstrate an efficient way to transform general LLMs into specialized retrievers that outperform strong baselines, while retaining general capabilities.

## Technical note

STM adapts general LLMs into biomedical-domain retrievers for RAG without expensive continued pretraining. It generates synthetic hard negatives to strengthen contrastive training signal and uses model merging (task-arithmetic / TIES-style weight averaging) to combine domain-adapted and general models while retaining broad capabilities. This lightweight recipe transforms general LLMs into specialized biomedical retrievers that beat strong baselines by up to 23.5% on domain retrieval tasks. The results argue that less fine-tuning plus merging is a more efficient adaptation path than heavy domain pretraining, preserving general retrieval quality.
