## Summary

Diversity-Aware Self-Correcting Sequential Recommendation Networks":

1.  The paper addresses the challenge in recommendation systems where complex interactions between sequentially recommended items hinder effective optimization for goals like diversity and overall relevance (collective recommendation).
2.  It proposes DivNet, a novel system designed to specifically capture these intricate dynamics and simultaneously estimate their utility while making recommendations diverse.
3.  Experiments show that DivNet significantly outperforms existing baseline methods using both offline metrics and online settings by effectively handling the complexity of item interactions for better diversity-aware results.

## Technical note

Proposes DivNet for collective (whole-page) recommendation, the final stage that optimizes item layout for diversity and whole-page relevance where point-wise scoring ignores inter-item interaction dynamics. DivNet projects items and user/context features into a hidden space with self-attention, then sequentially selects items, propagating the influence of previously selected items to estimate utility and compute candidate-to-selected similarity for diversification. A self-correcting module jointly maximizes total utility and diversity, and a supervised regularization keeps selections near exposed sequences to reduce exposure bias. Offline and online deployment show gains over baselines with and without collective recommendation at large query scale.
