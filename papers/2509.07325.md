## Summary

This paper introduces CancerGUIDE, an LLM-based system designed to automatically generate accurate treatment recommendations for non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) patients following NCCE guidelines. The authors first created a unique dataset annotated by experts and demonstrated that existing LLMs can produce high-quality, guideline-compliant benchmark predictions with strong correlation to expert annotations. Finally, they developed a hybrid model combining human and LLM assessments with confidence scoring to predict treatments and verify accuracy, providing a scalable, clinically viable approach with regulatory support capabilities.

## Technical note

CancerGUIDE builds an LLM-agent framework that generates NCCN-guideline-concordant treatment trajectories for non-small-cell lung cancer and verifies them with calibrated confidence. To address the annotation bottleneck, the authors construct the first expert-annotated benchmark (121 NSCLC patient pathways from 13 oncologists) and evaluate six proxy-benchmarking methods (synthetic notes plus consistency-based pseudo-labels) that predict clinical accuracy without ground-truth labels, reaching Spearman r=0.88 and RMSE=0.08. A logistic-regression meta-classifier combining self- and cross-model consistency features accepts/rejects predictions at 0.804 AUROC, enabling ROC-based reporting for regulatory compliance while minimizing expert annotation.
