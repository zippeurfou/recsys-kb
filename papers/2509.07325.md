## Technical note

CancerGUIDE is an LLM agent framework that generates NCCN guideline-concordant treatment trajectories for non-small cell lung cancer, addressing the scarcity of expert-annotated data. It builds a dataset of 121 oncologist-annotated NSCLC patient pathways and shows LLM-generated proxy benchmarks (synthetic + consistency-derived) correlate strongly with expert labels (Spearman r=0.88, RMSE=0.08). A logistic-regression meta-classifier over self/cross-model consistency features predicts prediction correctness with calibrated confidence (0.804 AUROC), enabling ROC-based clinical validation with minimal expert annotation. Not a recsys paper (clinical treatment recommendation via LLMs); best-effort from fulltext.
