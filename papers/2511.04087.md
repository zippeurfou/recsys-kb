## Technical note

E-CARE (Huawei) is an efficient commonsense-augmented recommendation enhancer for e-commerce query-product relevance that leverages LLM commonsense reasoning without SFT or human annotation and without costly real-time LLM decoding at inference. It encodes most of the reasoning schema from powerful LLMs into a reasoning factor graph that augmented recommendation models can query offline, bridging the lexical/semantic gap for vague queries (e.g., 'phone case for the elderly' implying protection and grip). Building on bi-encoder (two-tower) and cross-encoder relevance baselines, experiments on two downstream tasks show improvements of up to 12.1% in precision@5.
