## Technical note

CoEA (Co-Evolutionary Alignment, Meituan) tackles serendipitous recommendation by escaping feedback loops in LLM-enhanced dual-model frameworks. Its Dual-Stable Interest Exploration (DSIE) module jointly models long-term group identity (via clustered Collaborative Semantic IDs) and short-term individual interests in parallel; its Periodic Collaborative Optimization (PCO) forms a closed loop where a Relevance LLM verifies preferences and incrementally fine-tunes a Novelty LLM with anti-forgetting constraints. Online and offline experiments verify effectiveness for balancing novelty exploration and preference alignment.
