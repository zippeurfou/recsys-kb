## Technical note

This work optimizes DLRM inference, whose bottleneck is the embedding layers performing many random memory accesses to look up small vectors from tables of varying sizes (DLRM inference is >79% of Meta's data-center AI workload). It proposes four strategies to look up an embedding table efficiently on one core plus a framework that automatically maps tables asymmetrically across the multiple cores of an SoC. Evaluated on Huawei Ascend accelerators (with high-level comparison to Nvidia A100), it achieves 1.5x-6.5x speedup on real workloads and over 20x on extremely unbalanced distributions, while being far less sensitive to query distribution than the baseline.
