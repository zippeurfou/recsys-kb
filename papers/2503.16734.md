## Technical note

This perspective paper by the authors introduces LLM-based Agentic Recommender Systems (LLM-ARS) powered by (multimodal) LLMs, positioning them as the next evolution beyond static, one-directional recommenders. It clarifies core concepts and architectures (single-agent and multi-agent), formalizes a task with components user profiling, planning, memory, and action, and argues agentic capabilities such as planning, memory, and multimodal reasoning can enhance recommendation quality. It outlines key research questions around safety, efficiency, controllability, and lifelong personalization, and surveys open problems and future directions rather than proposing a specific system.
