## Technical note

SCoTER (Tencent) integrates structured LLM chain-of-thought reasoning into recommenders while jointly solving 'what to transfer' and 'how to transfer'. A Generate-Validate-Mine (GVM) pipeline automatically discovers effective data-driven reasoning patterns instead of brittle manual templates, and a structure-preserving integration architecture transfers stepwise CoT logic into an efficient model rather than collapsing reasoning chains into a single vector, avoiding online LLM inference costs. Across four benchmarks it consistently improves diverse backbones, and in production on the Tencent Advertising Platform it achieved +2.14% GMV while eliminating online LLM inference cost.
