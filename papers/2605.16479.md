## Technical note

This LinkedIn paper introduces Dynamic Facet Suggestion (DFS) for job search, where over 80% of job queries have three or fewer keywords, causing underspecified intent. DFS is an interactive query-refinement mechanism that surfaces personalized semantic facet attributes conditioned on the joint user-query context in real time to disambiguate intent and improve job retrieval. It uses a policy-grounded, retrieval-augmented approach (with LLM distillation) to generate relevant facets, aiming to improve relevance for short, ambiguous queries.
