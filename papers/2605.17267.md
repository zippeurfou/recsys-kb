## Technical note

RAGR (Review-Augmented Generative Recommendation) argues that generative recommendation inherits an item-only modeling assumption—using semantic IDs, autoregressive decoding, and unified token spaces—that ignores why users choose items. It augments sequential/generative recommendation with user and item review text to inject reasons behind decisions rather than modeling only behavioral item interactions. By bridging behavioral signals with review-derived rationale, RAGR aims to overcome the structural bottleneck of purely item-based generative recommenders on standard sequential recommendation benchmarks.
