## Technical note

Kuaishou's UniECS is a unified multimodal e-commerce search framework that handles image, text, and combined queries via a gated multimodal encoder with adaptive fusion that also handles missing modalities. Training combines cross-modal alignment loss (CMAL), cohesive local alignment loss (CLAL), intra-modal contrastive loss (IMCL), and adaptive loss weighting; they also release M-BEER, a 50K product-pair benchmark. With only 0.2B parameters, UniECS beats larger models (GME-Qwen2VL 2B, MM-Embed 8B), achieving up to +28% R@10 on text-to-image retrieval; deployed at Kuaishou it lifted CTR +2.74% and revenue +8.33%.
