## Technical note

Pinterest shares learnings on embedding-table optimization and multi-epoch training for ads conversion prediction, where high-cardinality categorical embeddings suffer gradient sparsity, slow convergence, and multi-epoch (one-epoch phenomenon) overfitting. They introduce a Sparse Optimizer that applies a higher layer-specific learning rate to embedding tables on top of Adam to speed convergence, and a Frequency-Adaptive Learning Rate (FAL) that scales row-wise learning rates by relative log frequency to slow convergence for infrequent rows and mitigate overfitting. FAL achieves comparable results to embedding re-initialization (MEDA) on all but the sparsest objective, and the authors show solving multi-epoch overfitting may be unnecessary when fresh data is continually available.
