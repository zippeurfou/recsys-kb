## Summary

Baidu Search developed QDET, a system for creating focused, query-driven event timelines from vast document collections to explain specific user queries. QDET innovates with multi-task fine-tuning (for ordering, causality, and completion) and reinforcement learning-based summarization, enabling a small 7B model to match larger models' specialized performance and efficiency. Online tests confirmed QDET improves user engagement by 5.5%, increases dwell time by 4.6%, and demonstrates effective knowledge transfer to related tasks like heat prediction.

## Technical note

QDET is a query-driven event timeline summarization system deployed on Baidu Search that reconstructs the temporally and causally relevant sub-events explaining a specific query event from millions of daily noisy documents. Documents are clustered into events via a lightweight 1024-dim dense encoder (BGE/E5-style) with ANN over a 30-day sliding window; an RL-based concise summarizer enforces 5-15 character length constraints (88.2% compliance, beating 671B models by 7.7 points). Multi-task supervised fine-tuning with three auxiliary tasks (temporal ordering, causal judgment, timeline completion) lets a 7B model reach 76.2% F1, slightly exceeding zero-shot DeepSeek-R1-671B at ~1% of the parameters. Online A/B shows +5.5% CTR, +4.6% dwell time, +4.4% exploration, and timeline understanding transfers to intra-timeline heat prediction.
