## Technical note

Modular enhancements to Transformer sequential recommenders (loss functions, training objectives, layer architectures, negative sampling) have never been systematically benchmarked for additivity, so SOTA claims over vanilla SASRec/BERT4Rec are often overstated. Under a realistic global-temporal-split, accuracy-plus-coverage (Pareto) evaluation on ML-20M, Kion, and BeerAdvocate, the authors sweep objectives (Shifted Sequence, MLM, Next/All/Dense All Action), LiGR vs Post-LN layers, gBCE vs Sampled Softmax, and uniform/in-batch/mixed negatives with optional logQ. The winning combination, eSASRec = SASRec Shifted-Sequence objective + LiGR layers + Sampled Softmax (optional mixed negatives), sits on the Pareto frontier alongside HSTU and FuXi-alpha and is +23% NDCG@10 over ActionPiece (0.0523 vs 0.0424) on the common Amazon-Beauty academic benchmark. Notably, that academic advantage over HSTU did NOT hold under realistic time-based evaluation, underscoring evaluation-protocol sensitivity.
