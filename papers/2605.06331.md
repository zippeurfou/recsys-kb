## Technical note

This Snap paper analyzes the expressiveness limits of autoregressive semantic-ID generation in generative recommendation, viewing token-by-token decoding as traversing a decoding tree whose leaves are items. It shows items sharing long SID prefixes (short tree distance) get strongly correlated probabilities across users, violating personalization, and proves theoretically that GR cannot represent simple preference and item-item similarity patterns that collaborative filtering captures. The fix, Latte, injects a randomly sampled latent token before each semantic ID, reshaping one tree into multiple latent-token-conditioned trees with varied inter-item distances to relax probability coupling, yielding an average 3.45% relative NDCG@10 improvement.
