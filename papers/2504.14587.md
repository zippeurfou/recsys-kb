## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Auto-bidding, with its strong capability to optimize bidding decisions within dynamic and competitive online environments, has become a pivotal strategy for advertising platforms. Existing approaches typically employ rule-based strategies or Reinforcement Learning (RL) techniques. ## Technical note

GAVE (Generative Auto-bidding with Value-Guided Explorations) is an offline Decision-Transformer-based auto-bidding framework overcoming rule-based inflexibility and RL's neglect of temporal dependencies, plus offline-training issues of behavioral collapse and OOD. It uses a score-based Return-To-Go (RTG) module with customizable score functions (via differentiable programming) to handle diverse advertising objectives like CPA/CPC constraints, an action-exploration mechanism with RTG-based evaluation for stability-preserving updates beyond the fixed dataset, and a learnable value function to anchor exploration to plausible regions and mitigate OOD. On two offline datasets and real-world deployments it beats state-of-the-art bidding baselines in offline evaluation and online A/B tests, and won first place in the NeurIPS 2024 AIGB auto-bidding competition.
