Retrievers, which form one of the most important recommendation stages, are responsible for efficiently selecting possible positive samples to the later stages under strict latency limitations. Because of this, large-scale systems always rely on approximate calculations and indexes to roughly shrink candidate scale, with a simple ranking model. ## Technical note

Large-scale retrieval indexes (HNSW two-tower, Deep Retrieval) are rebuilt periodically, missing emerging trends and suffering index imbalance where hot items concentrate in few clusters. ByteDance proposes streaming Vector Quantization (streaming VQ), a VQ-VAE-derived retriever that assigns items to learnable clusters (16K single-task/32K multi-task) in real time via EMA updates written to a parameter server, achieving index immediacy, reparability (replacing L_sim with an in-batch softmax auxiliary loss to avoid degradation), and balancing (k-way merge-sort serving). It supports complicated cross-feature ranking heads and multi-task learning, cutting the model update period to 5-10 minutes. Deployed to replace all major retrievers in Douyin and Douyin Lite, it beat SOTA NANN on Watch Time (+0.0941% vs +0.0782% over HNSW baseline) and AAH while delivering fewer hot items, yielding significant user-engagement gains.
