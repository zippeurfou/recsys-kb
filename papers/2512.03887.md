## Summary

This paper introduces Static-DRA, a novel Deep Research Agent based on a configurable hierarchical tree workflow designed to handle complex, multi-turn research tasks effectively. Its core innovation lies in user-tunable Depth and Breadth parameters, allowing granular control over research intensity to balance output quality against computational cost. The agent's architecture and configurable settings were validated through benchmark testing, demonstrating that increased Depth and Breadth parameters lead to deeper analysis and higher performance scores.

## Technical note

Static-DRA is a Deep Research Agent built on a configurable hierarchical tree-based static workflow with Supervisor, Independent, and Worker agents that decompose a topic into sub-topics for parallel multi-hop investigation and synthesis into a markdown report. Two user-tunable parameters, Depth and Breadth, give explicit control over research intensity versus LLM cost, with breadth halved at each deeper level to focus detail on the most relevant sub-topics. Evaluated on DeepResearch Bench with the RACE framework using gemini-2.5-pro at depth 2 and breadth 5, it scores 34.72 overall, and experiments confirm higher depth/breadth yield deeper research and higher scores.
