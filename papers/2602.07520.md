## Technical note

MDL (Multi-Distribution Learning, ByteDance/Douyin Search) is a unified framework for multi-scenario (MSL) and multi-task learning (MTL) inspired by LLM prompting: it treats scenario and task information as specialized tokens rather than auxiliary gating signals. A unified information tokenization module maps features, scenarios, and tasks into a shared tokenized space, and three mechanisms drive deep interaction — feature-token self-attention, domain-feature attention (scenario/task tokens as queries adaptively activating features), and domain-fused aggregation for joint distribution prediction — letting distribution priors 'prompt' the model's large parameter space layer-wise, unlike prior shared-specific/MoE designs that only touch shallow layers. MDL beats SOTA MSL/MTL baselines and, in a month-long Douyin Search A/B test, improved LT30 by +0.0626% and reduced change-query rate by 0.3267%; it is fully deployed serving hundreds of millions of daily users.
