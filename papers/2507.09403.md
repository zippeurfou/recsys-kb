## Technical note

This Meta paper improves related-video recommendation, where collaborative-filtering on co-engagement signals produces topically incoherent results with strong popularity bias. It proposes a multi-objective retrieval framework extending two-tower models that (a) uses multi-task learning to jointly optimize co-engagement and semantic relevance while prioritizing topical coherence, (b) fuses multimodal textual and visual content embeddings, and (c) applies off-policy correction via inverse propensity weighting to mitigate popularity bias. Industrial-scale evaluation and a two-week live A/B test show significant gains in both semantic relevance and engagement.
