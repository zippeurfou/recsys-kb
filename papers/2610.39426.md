## Summary

Existing generative ranking methods for e-commerce recommendations struggle with handling diverse candidate types (organic, ads, other businesses) and effectively utilizing feedback beyond initial exposures, often focusing only on single-business candidates. The paper introduces UGR (Unified Generative Ranking), a model that performs generative ranking over the entire multi-business candidate set (thousands of items) at once and directly generates full ranking lists. UGR incorporates complementary feedback supervision and an ad debiasing mechanism, leading to offline quality gains and significant online improvements in key metrics like Core IPV, Transaction Volume, and GMV compared to the production baseline.

## Technical note

Taobao's Guess You Like must build a list from about 1,000 request-level candidates spanning organic items, ads and other businesses, but prior generative rankers work on truncated single-business sets, imitate logged exposure sequences, and leave cross-business blending (mixrank) to a later step that changes positions and list context. UGR encodes the request into one context token, maps all businesses' candidates into a shared space, contextualizes the full set with low-rank candidate attention (candidate-axis-compressed keys and values), and a candidate-aware autoregressive decoder with cross-attention to the request token emits prefix-conditioned cosine-similarity scores over the remaining legal candidates. Training combines next-token prediction on exposure lists, weighted SFT that upweights exposed items by observed engagement and transaction feedback, and PermPO, a pairwise log-sigmoid preference loss over permutations of the same exposed items ordered by feedback-based DCG (loss weights 1 and 15); a training-only advertising debiasing tower absorbs historical ad-exposure effects via eCPM-driven logit offsets so serving-time scores combine additively with current eCPM under the unchanged GSP allocation and pricing, and a beam-style multi-value sequence retrieval (width 4, length 4) yields lists for the existing downstream evaluator. Offline UGR beats PRM, NAR4Rec, GReF, Dual-Rerank, UniRank and DEGR on all six feedback-based ranking metrics in both scenarios (e.g. +4.86% IPV NDCG, +7.53% IPV MAP); a 14+ day online A/B at 5%+ traffic gave Core IPV +1.76%, Transaction Volume +3.29% and GMV +2.10% (all p<0.05) with +12 ms latency and ad guardrails met, and unified generation beat the staged UGR-Single+Mix variant (+1.13%, +2.84%, +1.38%).
