## Summary

1.  Current parallelized multi-retrieval systems for recommenders often waste potential by ignoring that cross-scenario samples retrieved in one step are already available, limiting incremental performance gains.
2.  This paper introduces IncRec, a novel framework that specifically targets unused cross-scenario samples and designs an incremental learning approach to capture their unique value, aiming for better overall retrieval.
3.  IncRec further improves results by prioritizing samples likely to be exposed, and its effectiveness is validated through extensive tests and a real-world deployment on Taobao that increased online transactions by 1%.

## Technical note

IncRec is a retrieval framework for cross-scenario incremental sample learning in large-scale multi-retrieval recommender systems, motivated by the diminishing marginal utility of cross-scenario samples already retrieved by existing models. It partitions cross-scenario target items into a Retrieved Target Group and an Incremental Target Group, then uses a basic dual-tower model plus an incremental user tower trained by explicit contrast (ITG positives vs RTG negatives) to capture incremental user representations. A consistency-aware alignment tower adaptively weights incremental items by their ranking-stage exposure probability; deployed on the Taobao homepage it achieved a 1% increase in online transaction count.
