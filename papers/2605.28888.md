## Technical note

GPlan targets Generative Spatiotemporal Intent Sequence Recommendation (GSISR) in Amap, generating logically coherent, physically executable intent (tool-invocation) sequences while avoiding LLM latency and spatiotemporal hallucination. It uses Progressive Implicit CoT Distillation (PICD) to compress explicit teacher chain-of-thought into fixed-length latent think tokens with a compression-aware learning-rate schedule, and Spatiotemporal Counterfactual DPO (SC-DPO), a reference-anchored DPO on bidirectional matched vs. counterfactually-perturbed context-plan pairs to make plans change with context. Offline experiments and online A/B testing show improved sequence coherence and context responsiveness; the GSISR dataset is released.
