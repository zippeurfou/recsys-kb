## Summary

This paper introduces DV365, an embedding designed to model extremely long user histories (up to 70,000 items) cost-effectively by using offline generation instead of computationally expensive sequence optimization methods.

The authors propose a multi-slicing and summarization technique for creating these embeddings, capturing users' stable long-term interests from vast interaction sequences.

DV365 has been shown to be highly effective as an incremental feature atop existing recommendation models at Instagram, powering 15 different models with significant impact across Instagram and Threads since its production launch over a year ago.

## Technical note

Scaling attentive user-history encoders (like HSTU, capped near O(500)) is cost-prohibitive at Instagram scale, so DV365 instead pre-computes an offline user embedding from extremely long histories (up to 70K, avg 40K interactions). It uses a Multi-Slicing and Summarize strategy: raw UniTi timelines are rule-sliced by action type, dwell/watch-ratio, and time buckets into ~200 pooled (mean/weighted-mean) categorical embeddings, then compressed by a token-wise Funnel Summarization Arch plus linear compression encoder and 4-bit quantization (50x compression to 58x17 longs). A DLRM backbone simulation network with a 24h-gap 'distant interest' objective trains the user encoder to produce embeddings capturing stable long-term interest robust to staleness (cosine similarity >90% over 7 days). Launched in 15 Instagram/Threads models for over a year, DV365 gives ~0.4% average NE gains on ranking, 2-8% retrieval hit-rate gains, and +0.7% app time spent, while saving ~100 MW feature-extraction and 11 PB storage versus end-to-end long-sequence serving.
