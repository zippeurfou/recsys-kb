## Technical note

DV365 is Meta/Instagram's user embedding for extremely long user history modeling, choosing offline embedding over end-to-end sequence-length scaling to be cost-effective on GPU/power budgets. A single upstream foundational model uses a multi-slicing and summarization strategy to encode stable long-term interest from histories up to 70,000 (average 40,000) events, far beyond the ~500-length HSTU attentive encoders in production. The embedding proved highly incremental on top of advanced attentive sequence models and was launched across 15 models on Instagram and Threads, production-proven for over a year with significant impact.
