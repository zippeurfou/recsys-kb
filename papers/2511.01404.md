## Summary

1.  The paper addresses challenges in **multi-domain recommendation (MDR)** caused by imbalanced data across domains.
2.  The proposed solution, **Soft-partitioned Semi-supervised Collaborative Transfer Learning (SSCTL)**, tackles these issues by using dynamic parameters to counter data dominance and pseudo-labels from dominant domains to improve sparse data in smaller domains.
3.  Extensive experiments, including online tests, show SSCTL significantly improves performance, boosting overall GMV by 0.54-2.90% and CTR by 0.22-1.69%.

## Technical note

SSCTL addresses multi-domain recommendation where a dominant domain (e.g., a homepage with over 80% of traffic) skews shared-specific architectures, causing shared parameters to overfit to the dominant domain (overwhelming) and specific parameters of sparse non-dominant domains to overfit. Rejecting hard partitioning by domain indicator, it uses two modules on a CGC-style backbone: Instance Soft-partitioned Collaborative Training (ISCT) treats dominant-domain samples as unlabeled data and generates weighted pseudo-labels to enrich non-dominant domains, and the Soft-partitioned Domain Differentiation Network (SDDN) produces dynamic parameters that shift focus toward non-dominant samples. Online tests showed GMV lifts of 0.54-2.90% and CTR lifts of 0.22-1.69% across domains.
