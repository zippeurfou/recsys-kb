## Technical note

PLUM (Google/YouTube) adapts pre-trained LLMs for industrial-scale generative recommendation via three stages: item tokenization into Semantic IDs (SID-v2, building on RQ-VAE with multi-resolution codebooks, progressive masking, and multimodal/behavioral signals), continued pre-training that expands the LLM vocabulary with SID tokens over domain data, and task-specific fine-tuning for generative retrieval that autoregressively generates next-item SIDs. Generative retrieval avoids a separate item index and the dot-product limitation of embedding-based retrieval. On large internal video datasets PLUM substantially beats a heavily-optimized production model with large embedding tables, with a positive retrieval scaling study, and is launched to billions of YouTube users.
