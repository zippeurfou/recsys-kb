## Summary

This paper introduces AliBoost, an ecological boosting framework designed for Alibaba's billion-scale platforms. It addresses the "rich-get-richer" challenge where users favor popular items, hindering growth of less known ones.

The core idea is to combine user-oriented recommendations with a boosted delivery mechanism specifically for high-potential cold-start items (items needing exposure). AliBoost uses a Stacking Fine-Tuning Cold Predictor and an Item-oriented Bidding Boosting system.

Over six months in deployment across Alibaba's platforms, AliBoost successfully increased clicks and Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) of cold-start items by over 60%, demonstrating its effectiveness in fostering item discovery within large-scale recommendation systems.

## Technical note

On billion-scale platforms users gravitate to popular items, so 41.1% of cold items fail to reach 10 daily exposures even after 30 days, and existing cold-start/debiasing models don't counter this 'rich-get-richer' effect. AliBoost is an item-oriented ecological boosting framework that runs alongside natural recommendation with a tiered boosting structure (stage-based exposure budgets with CTR-vs-category promotion/exit thresholds), a Stacking Fine-Tuning Cold Predictor that augments the foundation CTR model with cold-item features to estimate CTR and item potential (P40 of the predicted CTR distribution), and an Item-Oriented Bidding Boosting mechanism where an item's cold CTR bid is compared to a user ideal price modulated by a PID-style boosting-speed factor and a user-preference (fatigue/activity) factor. Deployed for six months across Alibaba, it cold-started over a billion items and lifted cold-item clicks and GMV by over 60% within 180 days, increased 7-day-item traffic share ~250%, and mitigated the Matthew effect (Top-100 retention down 71.6% at 7 days). Ablations show removing item-oriented bidding drops CTR 45.31%.
