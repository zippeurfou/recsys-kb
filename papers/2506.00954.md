## Technical note

AliBoost is Alibaba's ecological boosting framework to counter the 'rich-get-richer' phenomenon where users gravitate to popular items and cold items stagnate (41.1% of new items fail 10 daily exposures after 30 days). It uses a tiered boosting structure with boosting principles to quickly expose high-potential cold items while minimizing disruption to low-potential ones, a Stacking Fine-Tuning Cold Predictor to improve the foundation CTR model's accuracy and potential prediction on cold items, and an Item-oriented Bidding Boosting mechanism to deliver cold items to suitable users while balancing boosting speed with personalization. Deployed across Alibaba platforms over six months, it cold-started over a billion new items and increased cold-item clicks and GMV by over 60% within 180 days.
