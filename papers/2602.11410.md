## Summary

CADET is an end-to-end decoder-only transformer model specifically designed for predicting ad click-through rates (CTR) at LinkedIn, addressing challenges in adapting transformer architectures to this task. Key innovations include a context-conditioned architecture incorporating post-scoring signals, a self-gated attention mechanism for stable training, a specialized RoPE variant for temporal data, session masking for deployment, and efficient engineering techniques. The system demonstrates significant improvement, achieving an 11.04% CTR lift compared to the existing production model and has been successfully deployed for LinkedIn's main ad feed.

## Technical note

CADET is an end-to-end decoder-only transformer for ads CTR prediction deployed at LinkedIn, adapting HSTU-style generative recommenders to advertising. A context-conditioned decoding block emits K multi-tower predictions over post-scoring signals like ad position, resolving the chicken-and-egg loop between predicted CTR and ranking without re-scoring. It adds a self-gated attention mechanism at representation and interaction levels for training stability, a timestamp-based RoPE variant capturing time gaps from seconds to months, and session masking to prevent train-serve skew from unavailable in-session events. Production optimizations include tensor packing, sequence chunking, and custom Flash Attention kernels, yielding an 11.04% online CTR lift over a LiRank DCNv2 ensemble baseline.
