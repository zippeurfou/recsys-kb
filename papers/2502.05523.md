## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of modeling complex user behaviors across diverse business scenarios within large-scale applications like Douyin. It introduces the Adaptive Domain Scaling (ADS) model, which enhances personalization by dynamically adapting the understanding of both user sequences and candidate items to different domains using two core modules. Extensive experiments on public and industrial datasets demonstrate ADS's effectiveness and compatibility, with online trials confirming significant business improvements for ByteDance platforms, leading to its full deployment in recommendation services serving billions of users.

## Technical note

Multi-domain recommenders in super-apps share a single embedding table, so identical items and candidate queries have the same representation across domains, hurting target-aware attention. Adaptive Domain Scaling (ADS) adds two modules: Personalized Sequence Representation Generation (PSRG) dynamically generates per-domain weight/bias to personalize behavior-item embeddings, and Personalized Candidate Representation Generation (PCRG) produces multiple domain-aware queries per sequence item via a shared-and-private structure. Both are plug-and-play into DIN/CAN/MHA attention. Deployed across ByteDance/Douyin, ADS raised total revenue by 1.00% (ads) and 0.79% (e-commerce).
