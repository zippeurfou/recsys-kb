## Technical note

Adaptive Domain Scaling (ADS) enhances personalized target-aware sequence modeling across multiple domains, which prior multi-domain work addressed mainly via network structure rather than sequence understanding. ADS has two modules: Personalized Sequence Representation Generation and Personalized Candidate Representation Generation, which dynamically learn domain-specific representations of behavior-sequence items and candidate items to adapt user-intention modeling per domain. Validated on a public dataset and two billion-scale ByteDance datasets, plus online experiments on Douyin Ads and E-commerce platforms; ADS is fully deployed serving billions of users.
