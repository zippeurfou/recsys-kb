## Summary

The paper addresses the limitation of existing generative POI recommendation systems that treat geography mainly as a textual attribute, often ignoring reachability from the user's location. It proposes SPAR, a novel framework designed to explicitly integrate real-world spatial knowledge into the recommendation process. SPAR achieves this through three synergistic stages: a tokenization stage fusing geographic and semantic information, a cognition stage pre-training the model on diverse geospatial data, and an adaptation stage preserving spatial knowledge during fine-tuning, demonstrating improved effectiveness on large-scale datasets.

## Technical note

SPAR is Alibaba AMap's three-stage framework for industrial generative next-POI recommendation, arguing that real urban spatial knowledge must be injected into every stage rather than inferred from behavioral co-occurrence, because a POI two kilometers away in a straight line may sit across a river. Stage 1, SI-SID, lifts raw longitude/latitude through a NeRF-style sinusoidal encoder with K exponentially spaced frequency bands f_k = 10000^(-k/K) into e_geo (proved shift-invariant and Lipschitz, so nearby coordinates share prefixes), concatenates it with a Qwen3-Embedding-4B textual embedding, L2-normalizes onto the unit hypersphere, and quantizes with 4-level RQ-KMeans (N_c = 512 industrial, 32 public) where the fourth level is a collision-disambiguation index. Stage 2, MG-CPT, continually pre-trains the backbone LLM under plain causal LM loss on 25 self-constructed per-city geospatial datasets in three tiers: basic POI and road attributes, POI-POI and POI-road relations including direction and Euclidean distance, and systemic navigation with route distance, travel time and ordered road sequences. Stage 3, TV-SFT, isolates tau_MG-CPT = W_MG-CPT - W_base as a frozen parameter-space task vector and trains W_TV-SFT = W_Base + alpha*(tau_MG-CPT + Delta_LoRA), running full-parameter behavioral SFT in one branch while a trainable LoRA adapter refines the frozen spatial branch, so massive behavioral data cannot overwrite the acquired geography; alpha = 1 is optimal and LoRA rank 128-192 works best. On four AMap city datasets (Beijing, Shanghai, Tianjin, Zhejiang; up to 11.25M users and 3.28M POIs) SPAR-8B beats PLUM-8B by 38.32% average over R@5/N@5/M@5 (Beijing R@5 0.6176 vs 0.4514), SPAR-4B by 28.54%, and even SPAR-0.6B beats every 8B baseline on all twelve metrics; on public NYC and TKY it improves 11.91-19.29% over PLUM. Ablation decomposition on industrial data: TextOnlyGR to SPAR-NoCPT is +10.76% (SI-SID), adding MG-CPT another +15.38%, and TV-SFT over plain full SFT adds 2.15-17.90%, while Dis@5 (mean haversine distance from the user's real-time location to recommended POIs) drops from 3.51-3.89 km for text-SID baselines to 3.13 km with SI-SID and 27.80% cumulative reduction on Shanghai. An 18-task spatial-cognition benchmark shows MG-CPT raises Qwen3-4B average accuracy from 36.8% to 76.1%, beating a general Qwen3-32B (45.3%) by 30.8 points and staying strong without coordinates supplied at inference; cold-start users (<8 interactions) gain disproportionately, +47.6% R@5 on Shanghai versus +30.7% warm-start. Caveat: all results are offline on public benchmarks, four industrial datasets and a case study, with no online A/B test reported despite the production AMap setting.
