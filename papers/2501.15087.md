## Technical note

Enables LLM-based sequential recommenders (LLM4Rec) to model long user histories despite context-length limits by compressing item titles into single-token item patches (average pooling of title token embeddings) and adjacent items into denser session patches. PatchRec uses two-stage training: Patch Pre-training pairs uncompressed and randomly-compressed sequences (compression probability scheduled 0->1) to teach the LLM the patch-to-token correspondence, and time-aware Patch Fine-tuning (PFT-I on items, PFT-S on sessions) compresses earlier interactions more heavily while keeping recent ones as text. On a Llama-3.2-1B backbone with constrained beam search over item titles, PatchRec beats TALLRec, SASRec, GRU4Rec, Mamba4Rec: PatchRec-I raises HR@20 by ~9%/26.75%/3.45% at 3.44x/6.81x/3.38x compression on MovieLens-1M/Goodreads/ML-100K, and for long (500+) sequences gives +25.11% HR@20 at 12.99% of TALLRec's inference time.
