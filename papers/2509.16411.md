## Technical note

This Google paper studies dual encoders for hierarchical retrieval (HR), where matching documents are all ancestors of a query in a hidden DAG (motivated by ad Phrase Match). It proves DEs are feasible for HR when embedding dimension is linear in hierarchy depth and logarithmic in document count, then identifies a 'lost-in-the-long-distance' phenomenon where accuracy degrades for distant ancestors. A proposed pretrain-finetune recipe boosts long-distance recall on a WordNet hierarchy from 19% to 76% without sacrificing near-document performance, and also improves retrieval on a shopping-queries dataset.
