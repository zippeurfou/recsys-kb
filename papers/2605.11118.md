## Summary

Current e-commerce systems use rigid, independent components for personalization, limiting dynamic objectives and merchandising. To overcome this, the paper introduces a cascaded generative framework composed of two tasks: placement theme generation and constrained keyword generation for product retrieval. This approach, enhanced with fine-tuning and content evaluation tools, improves scalability and personalization, demonstrating a significant 2.7% lift in cart adds in online tests.

## Technical note

This grocery e-commerce work replaces modular storefront construction with a top-down cascaded generative merchandising framework: Phase 1 generates ordered placement themes and Phase 2 generates constrained retrieval keywords per placement using embedding-based RAG over a curated taxonomy. Teacher-student fine-tuning distills closed-weight LLM quality into smaller models to meet production latency and cost, with ablations approaching closed-weight performance. Multi-level LLM-as-a-judge evaluators (page/placement/product) plus fine-tuned cross-encoders enable automated quality filtering and safe deployment. Generative output is fused with traditional rankers, yielding an estimated +2.7% lift in cart adds per page view in online experiments.
