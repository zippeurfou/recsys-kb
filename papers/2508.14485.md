## Technical note

DMAE (Distribution-Guided Multimodal-Interest Auto-Encoder) fuses users' multimodal interests at the behavioral level, unlike early-fusion methods that ignore behavior context. Its Multimodal Interest Encoding Unit encodes per-modality similarity scores between the target and historically clicked items into interest vectors; the Multimodal Interest Fusion Unit does intra- and inter-modal cross-fusion conditioned on the behavior sequence; and the Interest-Distribution Decoding Unit reconstructs per-modality similarity distributions as a guiding label to minimize multimodal information loss during training. It addresses item-ID data sparsity and captures explicit semantic content beyond co-occurrence, improving recommendation accuracy.
