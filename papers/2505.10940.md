## Technical note

TagCF (Kuaishou) augments the learning-to-rank/collaborative-filtering paradigm by explicitly modeling user social roles and the logical relations between user roles and item topics, arguing roles are latent confounders behind co-occurrence correlations (e.g. the diaper-beer example). It uses an MLLM (M3) to extract user-role and item-topic tags, then a second LLM (Qwen2.5-7B) to infer virtual U2I and I2U logic graphs, made scalable via cover-set reduction and tag-logic distillation. The tag knowledge and logic graph are integrated through tag-based user/item encoders, a contrastive learning objective, and an inference-time tag-logic score. Online A/B and offline experiments show user-role modeling can outperform item-topic modeling and that the logic graphs transfer across recommendation tasks.
