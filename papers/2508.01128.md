Textual reviews enrich recommender systems with fine-grained preference signals and enhanced explainability. However, in real-world scenarios, users rarely leave reviews, resulting in severe sparsity that undermines the effectiveness of existing models. ## Technical note

TWISTER (Adobe) imputes missing textual reviews to alleviate review sparsity in recommendation by jointly modeling semantic and structural signals. It represents user-item interactions as a Textual-Edge Graph with reviews as edge attributes, constructs line-graph views, and uses an LLM as a graph-aware aggregator that emulates GNN message passing to generate coherent personalized reviews from neighborhood text. On Amazon and Goodreads datasets it outperforms numeric, graph-based, and LLM baselines on imputed-review quality and downstream recommendation performance.
