## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of recommending photos based on aesthetic style, common on platforms like Flickr and Behance where users seek specific visual appeal rather than just content. The authors propose two methods for extracting aesthetic features: one based on color space and the other using deep style transfer embeddings. They found that using the deep style transfer embeddings significantly improved the performance of collaborative filtering recommendations compared to the color space method.

## Technical note

The paper studies personalized aesthetic-based photo recommendation for photography sites (500px), where users seek a visual style rather than content. It proposes two annotation-free aesthetic feature extractors: HSV/RGB color-channel histograms and deep style embeddings derived from the Gram matrix of a VGG-19 layer (neural style transfer). These features are used as side information in item-based nearest-neighbor collaborative filtering, blending style similarity with interaction similarity via a tuned weight. On a 500px dataset, color and explicit metadata features do not help, but style embeddings from VGG-19 layer 8 (with Euclidean similarity) substantially outperform baselines on Precision@10, R-Precision, and Average Precision.
