## Technical note

QuaSID (Qualification-Aware Semantic ID Learning) is an end-to-end framework that learns collision-qualified Semantic IDs by selectively repelling only genuinely harmful conflict pairs and scaling repulsion by collision severity, rather than uniformly suppressing all RQ-VAE token collisions. Hamming-guided Margin Repulsion (HaMR) turns low-Hamming SID overlaps into severity-scaled geometric margin constraints in the encoder space, while Conflict-Aware Valid Pair Masking (CVPM) masks benign protocol-induced overlaps; a dual-tower contrastive objective injects collaborative signals. On public benchmarks it improves top-K ranking quality by 5.9%; in a Kuaishou e-commerce A/B test it raises ranking GMV-S2 by 2.38% and cold-start completed orders by up to 6.42%, and the loss is plug-and-play.
