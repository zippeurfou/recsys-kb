## Summary

1.  Existing Group-Ranking Policy Optimization (GRPO) methods for Flow Matching models in text-to-image generation suffer from sparse, outcome-based rewards and ignore within-trajectory dependencies, hindering effective learning.
2.  To address this, the paper introduces TurningPoint-GRPO (TP-GRPO), which provides dense step-level rewards isolating each denoising action's effect and identifies "turning points" to capture delayed, long-term impacts of actions.
3.  TP-GRPO uses incremental reward sign changes to efficiently detect turning points, assigning aggregated long-term rewards, and experiments show it effectively utilizes reward signals to consistently improve generation quality.

## Technical note

The paper targets reward sparsity in GRPO-based RL fine-tuning of flow-matching text-to-image models, where a single outcome reward is copied to every denoising step and within-trajectory dependencies are ignored. It proposes TurningPoint-GRPO (TP-GRPO), which replaces outcome rewards with step-level incremental rewards (difference in reward before/after one SDE step, estimated via ODE completion) to isolate each step's pure effect, and detects 'turning points'—steps that flip the local reward trend to match the overall trajectory—assigning them aggregated long-term rewards. Turning points are identified purely by sign changes in incremental rewards, making the method efficient and hyperparameter-free. Experiments show TP-GRPO exploits reward signals more effectively and consistently improves generation quality over Flow-GRPO and DanceGRPO.
