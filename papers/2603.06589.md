## Technical note

The Isotonic Layer (LinkedIn) is a differentiable piecewise-linear module that unifies calibration and debiasing in one lightweight architectural component, replacing separate post-hoc calibration pipelines, propensity estimation, and per-segment model farms. It partitions the input space into segments with non-negative learnable bucket weights (via ReLU/Softplus) guaranteeing monotonic non-decreasing outputs, and parameterizes those weights as context-aware embeddings so swapping an embedding (position, device, advertiser, or combination) instantly yields sub-segment calibration/debiasing learned end-to-end from standard observational data with no IPS or preprocessing. First deployed globally in LiRank, this paper formalizes its monotonicity guarantees, context-conditioned and dual-tower debiasing extensions, and multi-task heterogeneous bias correction; production A/B tests confirm gains in predictive accuracy, calibration fidelity, and ranking consistency.
