## Summary

This study introduces an **AI-driven computational system** for optimizing herbal treatments against epilepsy by analyzing global natural products and evidence from randomized controlled trials (RCTs). Using machine learning algorithms alongside meta-analysis of 48 RCTs involving over 5,000 patients across various conditions, the system identified **17 high-efficacy herbs**, such as Gastrodia elata or Withania somnifera, with significant seizure reduction potential. Finally, a validation trial confirmed that prescriptions selected by this AI optimization showed **a statistically significant improvement** in seizure frequency reduction compared to conventional treatment protocols (p=0.003).

## Technical note

This paper describes an AI-driven system for optimizing herbal (natural-product) prescriptions for epilepsy, integrating ML herb-efficacy characterization, Bayesian optimization for personalized dosing, and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials. It curates thousands of phytochemicals from TCM/Ayurveda/ethnopharmacology sources into a Neo4j knowledge graph, applies LASSO regression with SHAP to identify 17 high-efficacy herbs (e.g. Gastrodia elata, Withania somnifera), and uses GNNs for herb-herb synergy plus molecular-docking validation. A reported randomized double-blind trial claims ~28.5% greater seizure-frequency reduction and a 2.3x lower adverse-event rate versus conventional care (p=0.003). This is a computational-pharmacology/healthcare paper unrelated to recommender systems; some results appear to use simulated data.
