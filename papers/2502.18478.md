## Technical note

This paper reports the first successful application of perturbation-based regularization in billion-scale industrial ads ranking and proposes Loss-Balanced Small Perturbation Regularization (LSPR), a self-supervised auxiliary-loss technique enforcing prediction consistency/invariance under small input perturbations (noise injection, feature dropout). It shows both Self-Consistency Regularization (SCR) and LSPR are scalable and improve ads delivery, with LSPR consistently outperforming SCR across groups and signal-availability setups. LSPR is deployed in a billion-scale ranking system spanning many surfaces, locations, and clients.
