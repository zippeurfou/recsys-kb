## Technical note

MuonRec is the first framework to bring the Muon optimizer to recommender-system training, challenging the near-universal default of Adam/AdamW for scalable RecSys. Muon performs orthogonalized momentum updates on 2D weight matrices via Newton-Schulz iteration, promoting diverse update directions. Across traditional sequential recommenders and modern generative recommenders (e.g. TIGER), MuonRec reduces converged training steps by an average of 32.4% while improving NDCG@10 by an average of 12.6% relative, with the largest gains in generative recommendation, positioning matrix-structured optimization as a new axis in RecSys scaling laws.
