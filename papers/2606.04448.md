## Technical note

RGCD-Rep transfers user interests from short videos to sparse, cold-start-prone live-streaming recommendation using MLLM reasoning in a resource-efficient two-stage design. Stage one, reasoning-aware distillation, has a frozen teacher MLLM generate structured cross-domain reasoning knowledge distilled into a lightweight student MLLM; stage two, transferability-guided cross-domain representation learning, decomposes item representations into transferable and domain-residual parts guided by behavioral collaboration. Representations are computed offline and injected into downstream retrieval for low-cost deployment. Deployed in Kuaishou's live-streaming recommendation serving over 400 million daily users, offline experiments and online A/B tests show significant gains across core business metrics.
