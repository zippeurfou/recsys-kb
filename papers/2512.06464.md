## Summary

This paper addresses multihop Question Answering by improving the selection of relevant passages. It proposes a lightweight model that predicts a passage's utility score based on its context-dependent relationship with other passages, unlike existing methods that only consider standalone relevance. Experiments show that using these utility scores for reranking retrieved passages leads to better overall QA performance compared to traditional relevance-based methods.

## Technical note

This paper models context-dependent passage utility for multi-hop QA, arguing that relevance-based reranking overlooks how a passage's usefulness depends on other passages in a reasoning chain. It distills reasoning traces from an advanced reasoning model (o1) to capture the order passages are used, then has GPT-4o assign 1-5 ordinal utility scores conditioned on the trace, producing synthetic (question, passage, utility) triplets. A lightweight RoBERTa-large regressor is fine-tuned via MSE on these annotations, and utility-based reranking outperforms relevance-based methods on downstream multi-hop QA across three benchmarks.
