## Technical note

AlignPxtr proposes bias-free video recommendation by hypothesizing that biases and true user interest are independent, then aligning predicted behavior distributions across bias conditions (duration, demographic, content-category) via quantile mapping — proving this yields zero mutual information between bias variables and the user-interest score. It handles both continuous signals (watch time) and discrete signals (likes, comments, follows) and multiple bias dimensions simultaneously, with a computationally efficient mean-alignment variant for real-time inference. Online A/B tests on Kuaishou and Kuaishou Lite gave cumulative active-days lifts of 0.267% and 0.115% and app-usage-time gains of 1.102% and 0.131%.
