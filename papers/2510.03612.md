## Technical note

This paper introduces Cross-Modal Preference Steering (CPS), the first black-box attack that jointly manipulates a listing's images and text to bias VLM-based web agents in selection tasks like content recommendation and product ranking. Under a realistic threat model where a non-privileged adversary edits only its own item, CPS uses PGD-based perturbations against an ensemble of CLIP architectures (transferable, resolution-preserving) plus RLHF-induced linguistic biases in descriptions. Evaluated on agents built on GPT-4.1, Qwen-2.5VL, and Pixtral-Large across movie and e-commerce tasks, CPS achieves >50% success on black-box commercial models and outperforms baselines while maintaining ~70% lower detection rates.
