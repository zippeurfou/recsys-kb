## Technical note

VLM-based web agents increasingly make selection decisions (content recommendation, product ranking), creating a manipulation surface. Cross-Modal Preference Steering (CPS) is a realistic black-box attack where a non-privileged content publisher edits only its own item's image and text to steer agent choices, with no access to model internals, memory, webpage structure, or user queries. It combines PGD-based transferable adversarial image perturbations optimized over an ensemble of CLIP architectures (>50% success on black-box GPT-4.1 while preserving resolution and imperceptibility) with RLHF-induced linguistic biases in the textual description, and shows the two channels synergize. Evaluated on GPT-4.1, Qwen-2.5VL, and Pixtral-Large across movie-selection and e-commerce tasks, CPS outperforms baselines while maintaining 70% lower detection rates, highlighting the need for robust agent defenses.
