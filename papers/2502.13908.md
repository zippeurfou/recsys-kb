## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Using Large Language Models (LLMs) for relevance assessments offers promising opportunities to improve Information Retrieval (IR), Natural Language Processing (NLP), and related fields. Indeed, LLMs hold the promise of allowing IR experimenters to build evaluation collections with a fraction of the manual human labor currently required. ## Technical note

This resource paper releases and benchmarks 42 sets of LLM-generated relevance labels for the TREC 2023 Deep Learning track, produced by eight teams in the SIGIR 2024 LLMJudge challenge. It analyzes how relevance-judgment pipeline components (prompt, LLM choice) affect quality, enabling study of systematic LLM biases, ensemble effectiveness, and trade-offs between models and human assessors. The released collection supports advancing automated IR evaluation methodologies where human relevance judgments are expensive or infeasible.
