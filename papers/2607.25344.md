## Summary

This paper introduces **Reward Guided Decoding (RGD)**, a new decoding framework for generative recommendation systems. The core issue addressed is that standard generation-focused decoding methods often prioritize likelihood over business value during the search process, potentially missing high-value items with lower probability scores early on.

The proposed solution formulates RGD as a KL-regularized reward maximization problem at test time, creating a closed-form distribution that combines base generator probabilities with learned reward signals. This allows injecting business preferences directly into each decoding step without retraining the underlying generative model by treating it as a reference policy and using an external reward model.

Extensive offline and online experiments confirm RGD effectively aligns recommendation generation with industrial business objectives like value maximization, demonstrating consistent performance gains even when user/business preferences change. The approach has been successfully deployed on platforms like Kuaishou to enhance real-world recommendations.

## Technical note

Generative recommendation autoregressively generates an item's semantic-ID sequence end-to-end from user history (a single-stage paradigm replacing the retrieve-then-rank cascade), but likelihood-dominated beam search prunes high-value low-probability candidates early, misaligning generation probability with business value. RGD formulates value-guided decoding as a KL-regularized reward maximization problem whose closed-form Boltzmann solution reweights the base generator distribution by exp(R/beta), folding a business-value ranking signal directly into the single generative stage instead of a separate reranking cascade or retraining. It attaches a lightweight chain-structured reward model (sharing the frozen generator decoder) as a test-time controller that injects reward at each SID beam-search step (pre-merge/post-merge/hybrid modes), supporting flexible single- and multi-objective (CTR/LVTR/WTR/GTR/LTR) switching without retraining. RGD beats TIGER, HSTU, RPG, and PROMISE on Amazon benchmarks (up to +11.49% Recall@10) and, deployed on Kuaishou live-streaming, lifted page-level CTR +0.392%, watch time +0.689%, and watch counts +0.349%.
