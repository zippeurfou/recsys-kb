## Technical note

GPSD (Generative Pretraining for Scalable Discriminative Recommendation) from Alibaba addresses the severe overfitting that worsens with model size when training Transformer-based discriminative CTR/CVR models on sparse data. Inspired by generative training showing no overfitting, GPSD initializes the discriminative model with parameters from a pretrained generative model and applies a sparse parameter-freezing strategy. It narrows the generalization gap and unlocks Transformer scalability, showing consistent gains following power laws as dense parameters scale from 13K to 0.3B; extensive industrial and public experiments plus online A/B tests confirm superior performance, pointing toward unifying recommendation and language-model architectures.
