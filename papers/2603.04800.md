## Summary

Existing post-training quantization (PTQ) techniques, which reduce Large Language Model (LLM) size while preserving accuracy, face significant challenges when applied to Multimodal LLMs (MLLMs). This paper identifies two specific issues with the SmoothQuant method: Smoothing Misalignment and Cross-Modal Computational Invariance. To resolve these, the authors introduce MASQuant, a novel framework with modality-specific smoothing and cross-modal compensation techniques, demonstrating stable quantization across different MLLM architectures and showing competitive results compared to state-of-the-art PTQ methods.

## Technical note

MASQuant is a post-training quantization framework for multimodal LLMs that diagnoses two failures of channel-wise smoothing (SmoothQuant): smoothing misalignment, where a single per-channel scale is dominated by the high-magnitude modality (visual tokens 10-100x larger than text), and cross-modal computational invariance, which would otherwise require separate quantized weights per modality. Modality-Aware Smoothing (MAS) learns modality-specific smoothing factors to eliminate misalignment. Cross-Modal Compensation (CMC) proves inter-modal activation differences are low-rank and uses SVD-based whitening to encode them as lightweight low-rank corrections to a shared text-smoothed base weight, preserving a single quantized weight for inference. It gives stable W8A8/W4A8-style quantization across dual- and tri-modal MLLMs, competitive with SOTA PTQ. Not a recommender-systems paper.
