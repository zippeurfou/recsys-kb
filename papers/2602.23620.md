## Technical note

This Taobao/Alibaba paper tackles retrieval for long-tail, knowledge-intensive e-commerce queries by distilling a powerful offline query-rewriting model into the online dense retriever via synthetic query-product training pairs. A multi-candidate rewriting model (initialized from Qwen3-30B-A3B) is optimized with REINFORCE++ under three rewards: Query Semantic Relevance, Product-side Distribution Alignment (perplexity under a product-domain LM), and n-gram Diversity; rewrites feed an offline pipeline (3B dense retriever + 42B MoE relevance model + LLM filters) that produces ~20M query-product pairs from 400K queries. Adding this synthetic data to training lifted Item Goodrate by up to +12.95pt (Negative queries) and +10.73pt (Knowledge), and online Side-By-Side human eval showed Query Goodrate gains up to +8.62pt over four weeks of Taobao A/B testing.
