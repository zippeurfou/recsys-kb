## Technical note

Generative retrievers trained with supervised next-item prediction optimize immediate feedback, not long-term satisfaction. This work frames music recommendation as a session-level MDP and trains a dense-embedding autoregressive generative retriever with off-policy REINFORCE, replacing prior one-step off-policy correction (Chen et al.) with a multi-step importance-weight approximation (up to K=10 clipped policy-ratio factors) computed in a single forward pass. A causal-transformer user feedback model (like + listened-ratio heads) enables adapting doubly-robust off-policy evaluation (Step-IS/DM/DR) to sequential recommendation and a multi-step test-time-scaling procedure that rolls out N future trajectories and picks the highest predicted discounted return. On Yambda-5B the REINFORCE agent beats NIP-CE and Positive-CE on all OPE metrics (DR 0.6306 vs 0.6046) while preserving recall@100, and more inference-time roll-outs monotonically raise the DM long-term-return estimate without updating the policy.
