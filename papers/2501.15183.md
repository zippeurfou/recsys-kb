## Technical note

Tackles negative sampling in multi-modal recommender systems (MMRS), where naive MLLM-generated negatives underperform uniform sampling and the textual modality dominates the visual one (shown via a BPR gradient-magnitude analysis of the NDCG lower bound). NegGen uses a multi-modal LLM (Llama-3.2-11B-Vision) through Description Generation, Attribute Masking, and Attribute Completion prompts to produce balanced, contrastive negatives, plus a causal-learning module (structural causal model with self-attention estimating total causal effect) to disentangle key from spurious attributes, trained with a BPR loss and an InfoNCE-style multi-modal alignment loss over a LightGCN base. On Amazon Baby/Beauty/Clothing/Sports it beats FREEDOM, DRAGON, DiffMM, LGMRec and hard-negative baselines (AHNS, DNS, MixGCF), e.g. +9.02% Recall@10 on Baby and +8.70% NDCG@10 on Clothing.
