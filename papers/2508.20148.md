## Summary

This paper introduces a Personal Health Agent (PHA), a multi-agent system designed for personalized health recommendations based on non-clinical data from wearables and personal records. The authors identified three major user needs – data analysis, personalized insights generation, and tailored coaching – which they addressed by creating specialized sub-agents: a Data Science agent, an Expert Agent, and a Coach Agent. They evaluated the PHA's performance comprehensively on 10 benchmark tasks involving extensive annotation effort from health experts and users to demonstrate its effectiveness.

## Technical note

Google Research proposes the Personal Health Agent (PHA), an LLM-based multi-agent framework that reasons over multimodal personal health data from wearables and medical records to give personalized recommendations. A user-centered design study of web-search and health-forum queries identified three skill categories, each served by a specialist sub-agent: a data-science agent for numerical time-series and population-context analysis, a domain-expert agent for personalized knowledge-grounded insights, and a coach agent for multi-turn interaction, goal setting, and progress tracking using a psychological strategy. The system is evaluated across 10 benchmark tasks with over 7,000 annotations and 1,100 hours of expert/user effort, described as the most comprehensive health-agent evaluation to date.
