## Summary

1.  Current recommendation system retrieval models are limited by strict latency requirements and cannot effectively model very long (thousand+ interaction) user behavior sequences, which contain richer interest signals.
2.  To overcome these limitations, researchers propose ULIM, a novel framework enabling efficient modeling of ultra-long user histories in the retrieval stage for capturing multiple distinct interests simultaneously.
3.  ULIM consists of two key components: Category-Aware Hierarchical Dual-Interest Learning to partition and represent long sequences with multi-interest clusters, and Pointer-Enhanced Cascaded Retrieval using PGIN to predict relevant categories before retrieving items from them, demonstrating significant improvements in click-through, order, and sales metrics on a Taobao dataset.

## Technical note

ULIM enables thousand-scale user behavior modeling in the retrieval stage, which is normally limited to hundreds of behaviors by latency and lack of target-aware mechanisms. It introduces Category-Aware Hierarchical Dual-Interest Learning that partitions long sequences into category subsequences and jointly optimizes long- and short-term multi-interest embeddings with sampled-softmax losses over category-homogeneous batches. At serving, a Pointer-Generator Interest Network predicts top-K interest categories, then category-constrained parallel ANN searches retrieve items per category to keep computation bounded. On Taobao it improves HR@K over YouTubeDNN and MIND variants and lifts +5.54% clicks, +11.01% orders, +4.03% GMV in online A/B on Taobaomiaosha.
