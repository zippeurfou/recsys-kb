## Technical note

ULIM (User Long-term multi-Interest retrieval Model) brings thousand-scale user-behavior modeling to the retrieval stage, where models are usually limited to hundreds of behaviors due to latency and the lack of target-aware cross-interaction. It introduces Category-Aware Hierarchical Dual-Interest Learning, which partitions long sequences into category-aware subsequences for multi-interest and jointly optimizes long- and short-term interests, plus Pointer-Enhanced Cascaded Category-to-Item Retrieval using a Pointer-Generator Interest Network (PGIN) for next-category prediction then next-item retrieval over top-K categories. On Taobao it beats SOTA and delivers +5.54% clicks, +11.01% orders, and +4.03% GMV for the Taobaomiaosha mini-app.
