## Summary

First-order Dataset Distillation for Text-based Sequential Recommendation":

Text-based sequential recommendation systems are accurate but expensive to train due to large datasets and complex models. The paper introduces FOSTER, a novel dataset distillation method designed to create compact synthetic datasets for cheaper, efficient training. FOSTER improves both effectiveness and efficiency through three key components: stochastic sampling, first-order optimization, and regularization promoting item co-occurrence, significantly outperforming baselines using fewer synthetic sequences.

## Technical note

FOSTER is a first-order dataset distillation method for text-based sequential recommenders, which are accurate but expensive to train due to language-model item encoders and periodic retraining. It combines three components: stochastic item-subset sampling (via Tucker decomposition) that replaces full-corpus embedding extraction each step, analogous to negative sampling; first-order optimization with trajectory-anchored parameter reset to avoid costly bi-level gradient computation; and regularization promoting co-occurrence of semantically similar items to preserve semantics under embedding tying. Across three benchmarks FOSTER outperforms dataset-distillation and coreset-selection baselines, approximating full-dataset performance with as few as 20 synthetic interaction sequences.
