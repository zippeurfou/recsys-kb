## Technical note

LLMIA is an out-of-the-box, tuning-free database index advisor that uses LLMs via in-context learning to emulate expert DBAs, avoiding the inefficient exhaustive search and inaccurate cost estimation of heuristic and learning-based methods. It injects database expertise through a high-quality demonstration pool (synthesized by GPT-4-Turbo and labeled by an ensemble of heuristics, retrieved by similarity), comprehensive workload feature extraction (row counts, NDV, predicates, key operations), and iterative refinement guided by actual DBMS feedback. On five OLAP benchmarks (TPC-H, JOB, TPC-DS, SSB) it matches or outperforms 12 baselines with minimal database interactions and generalizes to two real commercial workloads without retraining.
