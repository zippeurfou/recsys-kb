## Summary

This paper introduces KuaiLive-M3, an advanced dataset for live streaming recommendations designed to overcome major gaps in previous benchmarks. It addresses the lack of dynamic content evolution data by including segment-level multi-modal embeddings (text, audio, video features) across over three decades of user interactions on Kuaishou's platform. Furthermore, it incorporates explicit user feedback via questionnaires alongside implicit behaviors, enabling new research directions like cross-domain recommendation and connecting perception with satisfaction metrics.

## Technical note

KuaiLive-M3 is a large public benchmark dataset from Kuaishou for live-streaming recommendation, spanning 21,938 users with 35.8M live-stream and 111M short-video interactions, about 88M timestamped segment-level multimodal (MLLM-derived) embeddings, and 25,403 questionnaire responses, addressing three gaps: temporally evolving multimodal content, short-video-to-live cross-domain behavior, and explicit feedback. It defines three benchmark tasks: cross-domain recommendation (best: MGCCDR, with LightGCN a strong single-domain baseline), next-segment highlight prediction (GRU and hierarchical causal Transformers over segment embeddings), and questionnaire-enhanced sequential recommendation (SASRec/FMLP-Rec plus questionnaire behavior gains, while the specialized SAQRec underperforms due to explicit-feedback sparsity). Analyses reveal heavy-tailed live-room popularity (60.4% watched by one user, a cold-start challenge), front-loaded viewer arrival, and authors bridging the two domains. It is a resource/benchmark paper enabling multimodal, cross-domain, and implicit-explicit feedback research rather than a new model.
