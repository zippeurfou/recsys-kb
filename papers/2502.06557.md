Live-streaming, as a new-generation media to connect users and authors, has attracted a lot of attention and experienced rapid growth in recent years. Compared with the content-static short-video recommendation, the live-streaming recommendation faces more challenges in giving our users a satisfactory experience: (1) Live-streaming content is dynamically ever-changing along time. ## Technical note

LiveForesighter is a generative model for live-streaming recommendation at Kuaishou that forecasts future live-stream information, motivated by two observations: users have better experiences at a stream's 'high-light moment', and authors keep a 'consistent style' making future content predictable. It uses statistic sequences of users' positive behaviors (click, buy) to detect and forecast high-light-moment trends, and uses past sold-product sequences to predict future products for online-shopping streams. Deployed since July 2024 across various Kuaishou services supporting 400 million daily active users, with offline and online experiments demonstrating effectiveness.
