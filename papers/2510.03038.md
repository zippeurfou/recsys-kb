## Technical note

CHORD is a device-cloud collaborative framework for on-device sequential recommendation using channel-wise mixed-precision quantization to achieve both personalization and resource-adaptive deployment. The cloud identifies user-specific critical parameters via auxiliary hypernetworks and multi-granularity (layer/filter/element) sensitivity analysis, mapping user profiles to per-channel quantization strategies encoded in just 2 bits per channel. Devices apply this to randomly-initialized models for single-pass inference without backpropagation, avoiding costly local retraining. Experiments on three datasets with SASRec and Caser backbones demonstrate accuracy, efficiency, and fast adaptivity.
