## Technical note

PinFM (Pinterest) is a 20B+ parameter transformer foundation model for user activity sequences, pretrained on years of user data and fine-tuned coupled with existing downstream ranking models across a billion-scale visual discovery platform. To meet strict latency/cost constraints while scoring millions of items per second, it introduces the Deduplicated Cross-Attention Transformer (DCAT) and quantized embedding tables, improving throughput by 600% on internal data. It learns interactions between user sequences and candidate items, yielding a 20% increase in engagement with new items, and is deployed to over half a billion users.
