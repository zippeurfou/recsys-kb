## Summary

This paper introduces PinFM, a large transformer-based foundational model specifically designed for understanding user activity sequences at a billion-scale level on platforms like Pinterest. It addresses the challenges of scalability (processing millions of items per second) and handling novel items by developing infrastructure and algorithmic techniques such as DCAT. The resulting model learns item interactions from sequences and is now deployed to enhance engagement for hundreds of millions of users across various applications.

## Technical note

PinFM is a 20B+ parameter transformer foundation model pretrained on years of user activity sequences at Pinterest, then fine-tuned and coupled with existing downstream ranking models for each application. The central challenge is industrial serving: scoring millions of items per second under tight latency and cost while modeling interactions between user activities and candidate items and handling items unseen during pretraining. Key techniques include the Deduplicated Cross-Attention Transformer (DCAT), which raised throughput by 600%, and quantized large embedding tables to control serving cost. Altering input sequences lets PinFM learn user-candidate interactions, yielding a 20% increase in engagement with new items, and it is deployed to over half a billion users across multiple applications.
