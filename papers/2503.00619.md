## Summary

PinLanding is a novel system addressing the challenges of creating keyword landing pages (KLPs) for large online content collections, which are traditionally done manually or via user search logs but suffer from poor scalability or limited topic coverage.

The architecture uses a multi-stage pipeline combining a vision-language model to extract product attributes and a large language model to generate topics, along with a CLIP-based dual-encoder system for precise matching of content to KLPs.

PinLanding significantly improves both recall (99.7% on Fashion200K) and topic coverage/precision compared to traditional methods when deployed at scale, offering a generalizable AI-powered solution for scalable content discovery experiences.

## Technical note

Pinterest's PinLanding is a content-first pipeline for generating topical keyword landing pages at scale, replacing search-log-driven curation that suffers limited coverage and imprecise matching. Stage one extracts free-form product attributes with a VLM (GPT-4V), curates them via frequency filtering, BERT-based semantic deduplication, and human/LLM safety review, then trains a CLIP-style dual-encoder to match products (image+text) to attributes scalably. Stage two uses an LLM to synthesize SEO-optimized queries from attribute combinations and does distributed feed matching to populate collections. It reaches 99.7% Recall@10 on Fashion200K, and in production over 4.2M shopping pages it delivers a 4x increase in topic coverage and 14.29% higher attribute precision versus the search-log baseline.
