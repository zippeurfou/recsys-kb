## Technical note

TFRank is an efficient pointwise reasoning ranker built on small-scale LLMs that avoids the cost of large LLMs and explicit chain-of-thought at inference. Training integrates CoT data, fine-grained score supervision, and multi-task (pointwise/pairwise/listwise) objectives, with optional GRPO for reasoning. A 'think-mode switch' plus pointwise format constraints let the model use explicit reasoning during training but output direct relevance scores at inference without generating reasoning chains ('Think-Free' reasoning), which even improves accuracy over full reasoning. A 1.7B TFRank matches 7B baselines on the BRIGHT benchmark and stays competitive on BEIR with large efficiency gains.
