## Technical note

LAMO (Language-Assisted Medication recOmmendation) tailors LLMs for general medication recommendation, addressing severe overprescription observed in off-the-shelf models (GPT-4 recommends over 80 medications per patient, ~3x physicians). It fine-tunes LLaMA-2-7B via instruction tuning on restructured MIMIC-III clinical notes and uses a mixture-of-experts strategy with separate LoRA adapters per medication cluster to curb overprescription and control compute. LAMO surpasses SOTA by more than 10% in internal validation on MIMIC-III, generalizes across temporal (MIMIC-IV) and external (eICU, 200+ hospitals) validations, and maintains high accuracy on out-of-distribution medications.
