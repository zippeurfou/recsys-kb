## Technical note

SAQ (Huawei) is a vector quantization method for approximate nearest neighbor search used in recommenders, search, and RAG. It introduces dimension segmentation that partitions PCA-projected vectors and allocates more bits to leading high-magnitude segments via a dynamic-programming bit-allocation algorithm, plus a code-adjustment technique that quantizes dimensions independently then refines via coordinate descent to avoid exhaustive enumeration. SAQ achieves up to 80% reduction in quantization error and over 80x faster encoding versus Extended RabitQ, outperforming PQ, PCA, and LVQ.
