## Technical note

LiveUpdate is a systems paper that eliminates inter-cluster parameter synchronization for petabyte-scale DLRMs, where syncing multi-terabyte embedding tables over commodity networks causes multi-minute staleness (e.g., 26 min for 20TB). It exploits two observations: inference nodes' CPUs are underutilized (peak <=20%) and embedding gradients are intrinsically low-rank (>80% variance in <5% of PCA components). It co-locates LoRA trainers inside inference nodes with dynamic rank adaptation (memory overhead <2% of EMTs) and NUMA-aware QoS scheduling (P99 impact <20ms). It cuts update latency from >20 min to sub-second, halves update cost vs delta-update baselines, and improves accuracy by 0.04-0.24%.
