## Technical note

GMP-GR (GPU-accelerated Multi-relational Parallel Graph Retrieval) is Baidu's web-scale retrieval framework. It learns a multi-relational user-item relevance metric via multi-objective optimization with a self-covariant loss to unify diverse behaviors and improve graph pathfinding, and a hierarchical parallel graph-based ANNS (breadth-depth-balanced HNSW traversal with adaptive GPU aggregation via a queuing scheme) to raise throughput under high concurrency. Deployed across 20+ Baidu applications, it serves hundreds of millions of users at over one hundred million requests per second with improved retrieval accuracy and efficiency.
