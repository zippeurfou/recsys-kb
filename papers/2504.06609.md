## Technical note

InteractRank is Pinterest's production web-scale search pre-ranking model that augments a two-tower architecture with query-item cross-interaction features derived from two years of historical user engagement (the ItemQueryPerf/IQP framework), added to the two-tower dot product with minimal latency. It also incorporates real-time user-sequence modeling to capture in-session context that vanilla two-tower and BM25 methods miss. In A/B tests it improves online engagement by 6.5% over a BM25 baseline and 3.7% over a vanilla two-tower baseline, with the combined gain exceeding the sum of the individual contributions; code is open-sourced.
