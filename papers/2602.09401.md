Large-scale live-streaming recommendation requires precise modeling of non-stationary content semantics under strict real-time serving constraints. In industrial deployment, two common approaches exhibit fundamental limitations: discrete semantic abstractions sacrifice descriptive precision through clustering, while dense multimodal embeddings are extracted independently and remain weakly aligned with ranking optimization, limiting fine-grained content-aware ranking. ## Technical note

SARM is an end-to-end live-streaming ranking architecture that integrates natural-language semantic anchors directly into ranking optimization, avoiding the information bottleneck of discrete Semantic IDs and the misalignment of independently extracted dense multimodal embeddings. Each semantic anchor is a learnable text-token description (generated offline by a pretrained MLLM) jointly optimized with ranking features, with a lightweight dual-token gated design for live-streaming semantics and an asymmetric deployment strategy for low-latency online training/serving. Fully deployed serving over 400 million users daily, it shows consistent improvements over production baselines in offline evaluation and large-scale A/B tests.
