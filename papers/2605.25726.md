## Summary

1.  **Problem:** Existing recommender systems struggle to effectively integrate diverse multi-modal content and collaborative filtering data to model evolving user interests over time, due to misalignment between different data types.
2.  **Solution:** The paper introduces SIREN, a unified framework that simultaneously integrates multi-modal and collaborative features through two stages (General Search and Exact Search) within a transformer architecture, enabling fine-grained interaction and improved relevance modeling.
3.  **Results:** SIREN achieves state-of-the-art offline performance (higher GAUC) and significant online business impact (GMV gains like +2.28% to +3.87%), leading to its full deployment in Tencent's advertising platform.

## Technical note

SIREN is a unified framework for multi-modal lifelong user interest modeling that jointly integrates multi-modal content and collaborative-filtering signals, addressing misalignment between the two data types. It uses a transformer architecture with two stages, General Search and Exact Search, to enable multi-granularity semantic interaction and fine-grained relevance modeling over long user behavior sequences. It reports state-of-the-art offline GAUC and online GMV gains of roughly +2.28% to +3.87%, and is fully deployed in Tencent's advertising platform. The design connects lifelong sequence retrieval (TWIN-style two-stage) with multimodal semantic IDs.
