## Technical note

LORE (Large Generative Model for Search Relevance) is a three-iteration industrial blueprint for LLM-based e-commerce relevance at JD, achieving a cumulative +27% improvement on the online GoodRate metric. It deconstructs relevance into Path Construction and Path Following, identifying three core capabilities (knowledge & reasoning, multimodal matching, rule-awareness) across 18 attribute dimensions and an L1-L4 scale. A two-stage post-training paradigm uses progressive CoT synthesis + SFT (with distillation) to instill capabilities, then RL with verifiable rewards (RLVR) to align with human preferences; a query-based stratified deployment transfers offline ability online. LORE also released the RAIR benchmark.
