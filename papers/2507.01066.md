## Technical note

This TikTok paper introduces an Embedding-Based Retrieval (EBR) system for multimodal short-video content moderation to complement slow classification-based approaches for trend adaptation and urgent escalations. It trains single-modal and multi-modal foundation embedding models with a Supervised Contrastive Learning (SCL) framework, outperforming CLIP and MoCo, then builds a retrieval system over these embeddings for efficient trend handling. Across 25 emerging trends EBR raised ROC-AUC from 0.85 to 0.99 and PR-AUC from 0.35 to 0.95, and online it increased action rates by 10.32% while cutting operational costs by over 80%.
