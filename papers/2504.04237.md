## Technical note

This work models segment-level user dynamic interests in short-video recommendation, arguing that treating each video as a whole overlooks how preferences evolve across specific video segments. The proposed model integrates a hybrid representation module, a multi-modal user-video encoder, and a segment interest decoder to achieve precise segment-level interest prediction while addressing dynamic interest patterns, missing segment-level labels, and multi-modal fusion. It is evaluated on two downstream tasks — video-skip prediction and short-video recommendation — showing promising results on real-world short-video datasets, and the authors release a dataset with segment-level video data and diverse user behaviors.
