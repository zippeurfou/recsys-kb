## Technical note

MUFASA is a multimodal fusion and sparse-attention alignment model for long sequential recommendation across mixed genres. Its Multimodal Fusion Layer uses item titles as cross-genre semantic anchors with a four-fold objective (title-guided contrastive alignment, alignment to the collaborative-filtering space, title-guided consistency to prevent modality collapse, and distributional regularization). Its Sparse Attention-guided Alignment Layer scales to long behavior sequences via multi-granularity sparse attention (windowed, block-level, and selective) to model coherent interest blocks and fine-grained intra-block variation. It surpasses SOTA baselines and shows significant online A/B production gains.
