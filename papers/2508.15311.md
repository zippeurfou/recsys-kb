Nonetheless, the vast number of behaviors and complexity of noise interference pose challenges to prediction efficiency and effectiveness. ## Technical note

DiffuMIN (Diffusion-driven Multi-Interest Network) models long-term user behavior for CTR by first doing target-oriented multi-interest extraction: it orthogonally decomposes the target embedding into interest channels, routes behaviors to channels and aggregates top-p% to disentangle multiple interests. A diffusion module (first application of diffusion to user interests in CTR), guided by contextual interests and interest channels and started from perturbed interests rather than Gaussian noise, generates augmented interests, with contrastive learning aligning them to true preferences. On three datasets it reaches SOTA and online A/B testing gains +1.52% CTR and +1.10% CPM.
