## Technical note

SIEVE addresses filtered vector search - finding similar vectors that also satisfy hard predicates (e.g., videos tagged 'kids') - where graph-based ANN degenerates because filtered traversal loses the few-hops connectivity property. Instead of constraining traversal, SIEVE builds a collection of many indexes each serving different predicate forms, guided by a three-dimensional analytical cost model for effective index construction and selection. This preserves efficient just-a-few-hops search across a wide range of predicates, including selective ones where few items match, outperforming prior traversal-modification approaches.
