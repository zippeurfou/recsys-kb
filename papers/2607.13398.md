## Summary

This paper introduces TMallGS, a new scalable ranking architecture for e-commerce search (like Tmall) designed because existing Transformer models often ignore the different types of features needed for prediction.

TMallGS's key innovation is its hierarchical distribution-calibrated tokenization that splits input features into subspaces and processes them differently. It also includes other novel components like field-adaptive gating, late fusion to preserve signals, bias separation, and error-aware training.

Extensive offline experiments and online A/B tests demonstrate TMallGS significantly improves crucial ranking metrics (like UCTCVR) while also boosting overall business value (GMV).

## Technical note

TmallGS is a scalable Transformer ranking architecture for CTR prediction in Tmall e-commerce search that adapts LLM-style scaling to heterogeneous search features without all-in-one tokenization. Its five components are Hierarchical Distribution-Calibrated Tokenization (Field-wise Saliency Reweighting + Distribution-Calibrated Projection), a Field-Adaptive Gated Transformer backbone with Per-Field QKV projections and noise-adaptive gating, Decoupled FiLM (Feature-wise Linear Modulation) late fusion to preserve high-frequency explicit cross-features, a Context-Aware Bias Net that additively decouples systemic bias to boost GAUC, and Error-Aware Progressive Training for hard-sample curriculum learning; it trains with joint BCE + pairwise ranking loss over 1500-length behavior sequences. Offline on Tmall search logs it beats a DIN baseline by +1.12% AUC and +1.26% GAUC and follows scaling laws across width/depth/length. Online A/B over 30 days yields +1.38% UCTCVR and +1.52% GMV at only +6ms latency, using a two-stage warm-up to migrate legacy embeddings.
