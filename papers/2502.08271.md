## Technical note

RecCocktail is a generalizable, efficient LLM-based recommendation framework that unifies the breadth-oriented (multi-domain/multi-task instruction tuning for generalization and cold-start) and depth-oriented (domain-specific) paradigms via LoRA weight-space merging. It fine-tunes a domain-general 'base spirit' LoRA once, constructs a domain-specific 'ingredient' LoRA, then merges them with a linear arithmetic operation plus an entropy-guided adaptive test-time merging method — adding no inference-time overhead and enabling plug-and-play scaling to new domains. Experiments across multiple datasets in both warm and cold-start scenarios validate its effectiveness and generality.
