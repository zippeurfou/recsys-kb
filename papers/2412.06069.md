## Summary

Recommender systems struggle with information overload; traditional methods like Product Quantization (PQ) are efficient but often compromise recall rates when suggesting highly relevant items quickly. This paper introduces Fuzzy Norm-Explicit Product Quantization using Type-2 Fuzzy sets, which allows sub-vectors to be associated with multiple elements in the codebook and calculates norms via integration. The new method significantly boosts recall performance (up to +6% to +8%) compared to standard PQ approaches while maintaining computational efficiency similar to existing efficient methods on relevant datasets like Netflix and Cifar60k.

## Technical note

Targets improving recall of maximum inner-product search for recommendation retrieval without increasing the low complexity of product quantization. The method extends norm-explicit quantization (NEQ) by defining codebooks with Type-2 Fuzzy Sets so each sub-vector can be softly associated with multiple codewords, resolving the norm via Sugeno-integral fusion. This soft assignment reduces the hard-association error of discrete quantization. On Netflix, Audio, and Cifar60k it beats NEQ, PQ, and RQ by up to +6%/+5%/+8% recall (reaching 94%/69%/59%) at near-identical compute cost.
