## Summary

1.  Existing e-commerce search systems often suffer from the "Matthew effect," prioritizing popular items and neglecting new ones, hindering their growth despite the importance of new items for platform health.
2.  To address this, the authors propose GrowthGR, a novel retrieval framework designed to align search results with both immediate conversion goals and the long-term growth potential of items.
3.  GrowthGR incorporates a module to predict item long-term value and a generative retrieval module trained with multi-value policy optimization, successfully deployed on Taobao to significantly boost new item and overall search revenue.

## Technical note

GrowthGR is a multi-value-aware generative retrieval framework for e-commerce search that balances immediate conversion against long-term new-item growth to counter the Matthew effect in cold-start. Its ItemLTV module formulates long-term value estimation as a counterfactual causal inference problem, quantifying the uplift effect of a single user interaction on an item's future transaction increment. The MultiGR module builds on a semantic-ID (RQ-VAE) generative retrieval architecture and uses Multi-Value-Aware Policy Optimization (MoPO), a preference-alignment training paradigm that integrates cascaded search-funnel signals (candidates, impressions, clicks, conversions) with predicted long-term potential. Deployed on Taobao, GrowthGR achieved a 5.3% lift in new-item GMV and a 0.3% gain in overall search GMV in two-month online A/B tests.
