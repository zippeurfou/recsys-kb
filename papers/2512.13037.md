## Technical note

This eBay study progressively refines e-commerce search ranking using the buyer's short-term click activity, adapting results across the shopping journey and intent shifts. It moves from heuristic autoregressive contextual features to an intent-aware contextualization strategy that fuses relevant past clicks with the current query, then to attention-based sequence models over recent click history that emphasize clicks relevant to the evolving purchase intent. Integrated into the production ranker (MLR), it improves offline and online MRR: online A/B tests show autoregressive contextual features lift MRR Sale by 1.30% and intent-aware features add a further 0.96%.
