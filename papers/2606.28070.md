## Summary

This paper introduces the JD Oxygen AI Item Center (Oxygen AIIC), a large-scale platform developed by JD.com to manage its vast catalog of billions of SKUs more effectively using Large Language Models and Vision-Language Models. It addresses key industrial challenges like keeping up with new concepts, producing high-quality item knowledge efficiently, and meeting diverse operational needs through four core components: collaborative ontology building, a Semantic Search then Discrimination architecture for scalable AI Library generation, self-improving models for reliable knowledge creation, and a unified data hub.

The system dynamically expands its understanding of items via human-AI collaboration on an extensive ontology. It significantly automates item information management, resulting in high coverage rates (80.4%) across JD.com's search traffic, near elimination of manual effort needed to fill core attributes during listing updates (>80% automated), and a dramatic improvement in overall item data quality with very few errors or omissions.

Oxygen AIIC is now operational across multiple business areas like search, recommendation, and operations, delivering tangible benefits at JD.com's massive scale by improving consumer experience, lowering management costs through automation, and boosting operational efficiency.

## Technical note

JD Oxygen AIIC is an industrial LLM/VLM platform for item knowledge over tens of billions of SKUs, built on four pillars: human-AI collaborative ontology engineering, a 'Semantic Search then Discrimination' (S2D) architecture, self-evolving item-understanding models, and a unified item tunnel serving tiered-freshness knowledge. S2D decouples the ontology from model parameters so it can evolve without retraining and to mitigate hallucinations, while cache reuse and asynchronous pipeline parallelism deliver >10x throughput on Huawei Ascend NPUs. Self-evolving models reach 94.2% precision / 82.8% recall. Deployed across search, recommendation, and operations, it reaches 80.4% search-traffic coverage, >80% automated core-attribute fill, and +9% creative CTR.
