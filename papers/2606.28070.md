## Summary

This paper introduces Oxygen AIIC (JD Oxygen AI Item Center), an industrial-scale platform for managing JD.com's vast e-commerce catalog of tens of billions of SKUs. It addresses three major challenges: keeping up with new product concepts, efficiently structuring knowledge for massive inventory, and serving diverse business needs by leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs). The system uses four core pillars—ontology engineering via human-AI collaboration, a Semantic Search then Discrimination architecture, self-evolving AI models, and a unified item tunnel—to significantly improve item knowledge quality while enabling high-throughput processing and deployment across JD's search, recommendation, operations, and planning systems.

## Technical note

JD Oxygen AI Item Center (Oxygen AIIC) is an industrial LLM/VLM platform for item-knowledge production over tens of billions of SKUs. Four pillars: human-AI ontology engineering, a 'Semantic Search then Discrimination' knowledge-identification architecture, self-evolving item-understanding LLMs/VLMs (94.2% precision, 82.8% recall on an 8B base after SFT), and a unified item tunnel with tiered freshness. Deployed on Huawei Ascend NPUs across search/recommendation/operations, it raises item-information richness 3.35x, reaches 80.4% search-traffic coverage, and lifts creative CTR by 9%.
