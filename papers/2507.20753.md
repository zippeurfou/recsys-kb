## Technical note

This industry study from OTTO systematically benchmarks deep neural networks against a production-grade LambdaMART (GBDT) baseline for e-commerce learning-to-rank, evaluating multiple DNN architectures and loss functions on proprietary interaction-log data. Validated through an 8-week online A/B test, a simple DNN architecture outperformed the strong tree-based baseline in total clicks and revenue while achieving parity in total units sold. The work gives practitioners actionable evidence that DNNs can beat GBDTs for real-world e-commerce ranking, addressing prior inconclusive comparisons.
