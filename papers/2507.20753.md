## Summary

1.  E-commerce companies currently rely heavily on tree-based Learning-to-Rank models like LambdaMART, but there's ongoing discussion about whether deep learning models might be better.
2.  This study systematically compared various DNN architectures and loss functions against production-grade LambdaMART models using a large proprietary dataset from OTTO, validated by an online A/B test lasting eight weeks.
3.  The results indicate that even relatively simple DNN designs can surpass the strong tree-based baseline in key business metrics like total clicks and revenue generation, while performing comparably to LambdaMART on total units sold.

## Technical note

This paper benchmarks deep neural rankers against a production-grade LambdaMART (LightGBM) baseline for e-commerce learning-to-rank at OTTO. It evaluates three DNN architectures (Two-Tower, Cross-Encoder, and a Transformer with multi-head self-attention over the candidate list) and two loss functions on a proprietary interaction-log dataset, embedding numerical, categorical, and bag-of-words textual features. Offline, DNNs match or exceed the tree baseline across NDCG metrics, with a simple Two-Tower model competitive with heavier architectures. An 8-week online A/B test showed a simple DNN beating the LGBM baseline on total clicks and revenue while reaching parity on units sold.
