## Technical note

LASER is a full-stack framework for end-to-end ultra-long user sequence modeling deployed at Xiaohongshu (RedNote), breaking the 'Latency Wall' of I/O and quadratic attention. SeqVault, a schema-aware serving infrastructure with hybrid DRAM-SSD indexing, cuts retrieval latency by 50% and CPU usage by 75% for full life-cycle histories. Segmented Target Attention (STA) uses sigmoid gating as a silence mechanism to compress noisy items within fixed segments, and Global Stacked Target Attention (GSTA) refines cross-segment dependencies cheaply. In online A/B tests over 100M+ DAU it achieved a 2.36% lift in ADVV and 2.08% lift in revenue.
