## Technical note

RARE (Real-time Ad REtrieval) is Tencent's LLM-generative sponsored-search framework that generates LLM-produced 'Commercial Intentions' (CIs) as intermediate semantic representations mapping queries to ads, replacing heavy numeric/content DocIDs whose one-to-few mapping is slow and hard to update. A base LLM is customized via commercial-knowledge injection and format fine-tuning, offline-generating CIs per ad into a one-to-many dynamic index; at query time it generates CIs in real time (with constrained beam search for new ads) and retrieves the mapped ads. Deployed serving tens of millions of users daily, online A/B tests show +5.04% consumption, +6.37% GMV, +1.28% CTR, +5.29% shallow and +24.77% deep conversions, beating ten baselines.
