## Technical note

ROS (Reasoning Over Space), from Alibaba/Amap, is an LLM generative next-POI recommender that treats geography as a first-class decision variable in the reasoning process rather than as text/numeric side features. It introduces a Hierarchical Spatial Semantic ID that discretizes coarse-to-fine locality plus POI semantics into compositional tokens, and a three-stage Mobility Chain-of-Thought paradigm (model user personality, construct an intent-aligned candidate space, perform locality-informed pruning), then aligns the model with real-world geography via spatial-guided RL. On three LBSN datasets ROS achieves over 10% relative hit-rate gains over the strongest LLM baselines and improves cross-city transfer, despite a smaller backbone.
