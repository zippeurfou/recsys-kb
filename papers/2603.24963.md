## Technical note

This paper studies large-scale ML-model ecosystem efficiency in ads ranking and proposes the Standard Model Template (SMT), a set of six reusable, composable templates (differentiated by paradigm such as two-tower vs single-tower and feature-interaction type) that instantiate across a model fleet. By decoupling Template Iteration (validate a technique once at template level, O(k)) from Model Iteration (instantiate models from templates, O(n)), SMT reduces technique-propagation complexity from O(n*2^k) to O(n+k). Across four global development cycles in Meta's production ads ranking, SMT delivers a 0.63% average cross-entropy improvement at neutral serving cost, 92% less per-model iteration engineering time, and a 6.3x increase in technique-model adoption throughput.
