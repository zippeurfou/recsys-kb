With the evolution of large language models (LLMs), there is growing interest in leveraging their rich semantic understanding to enhance industrial recommendation systems (RecSys). ## Technical note

QARM V2 bridges LLM semantic understanding with industrial RecSys business objectives for user sequence modeling in Kuaishou's GSU/ESU (General/Exact Search Unit) paradigm over 100k-length histories. On the GSU side it introduces a reasoning-based item alignment mechanism that generates business-aligned LLM embeddings for semantic-aware retrieval of relevant sub-sequences; on the ESU side it proposes Res-KmeansFSQ, a hybrid Residual K-means plus Finite Scalar Quantization method producing multi-level Semantic IDs with minimal code conflicts that are optimized end-to-end with the ranking model. Offline experiments and online A/B tests on Kuaishou short-video and live-streaming show significant gains.
