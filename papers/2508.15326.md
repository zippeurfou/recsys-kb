## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

CTR models play a vital role in improving user experience and boosting business revenue in many online personalized services. However, current CTR models generally encounter bottlenecks in performance improvement. Inspired by the scaling law phenomenon of LLMs, we propose a new paradigm for improving CTR predictions: first, constructing a CTR model with accuracy scalable to the model grade and data size, and then distilling the knowledge implied in this model into its lightweight model that can ...

## Technical note

SUAN (Stacked Unified Attention Network) builds a CTR model that follows scaling laws across three orders of magnitude in model grade (model size plus sequence length) and data size, using unified attention blocks that jointly model sequential and non-sequential features with RMSNorm and SwiGLU. Because high-grade SUAN is too slow, they distill it online into LightSUAN (sparse self-attention, parallel inference), where the distilled LightSUAN outperforms a SUAN one grade higher at the same inference time. Deployed online it raises CTR by 2.81% and CPM by 1.69%.
