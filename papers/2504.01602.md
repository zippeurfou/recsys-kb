## Technical note

LCU (LLM-enhanced Comment Understanding) predicts comment-section staytime for short-video platforms, a task existing watchtime methods handle poorly because they ignore individual comments and their interrelations. It is a two-stage framework: first fine-tune an LLM on domain-specific tasks (staytime bucketing, top-comment prediction, user-comment interaction) to bridge video and comments; then integrate LLM embeddings with traditional model features and add two auxiliary comment-ranking tasks — user-agnostic (comment popularity) and user-specific (individual preference). The authors release KuaiComt, a Kuaishou video+comment dataset. Offline experiments and online A/B testing show significant staytime-prediction improvements over strong baselines.
