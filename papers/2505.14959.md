## Technical note

This Pinterest paper proposes a privacy-preserving CVR-prediction training framework for data clean rooms where advertisers won't share sample-level conversion data. Borrowing from split learning but avoiding its leakage and full-model-inference drawbacks, it (1) exchanges batch-level aggregated gradients instead of sample-level gradients to cut privacy risk, (2) applies adapter-based parameter-efficient fine-tuning and gradient compression to reduce communication cost, and (3) uses de-biasing to train under label differential privacy despite label perturbation. On industrial datasets it achieves competitive ROC-AUC while significantly decreasing communication overhead and complying with advertiser and user privacy requirements.
