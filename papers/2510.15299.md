## Technical note

GRank is a structured-index-free industrial retrieval paradigm that unifies target-aware learning with user-centric retrieval, replacing decoupled dual-tower encoders and tree/graph/quantization indices. A target-aware Generator performs personalized candidate generation via GPU-accelerated MIPS, a lightweight Ranker does fine-grained candidate-specific scoring on small subsets, and an end-to-end multi-task framework keeps generation and ranking semantically consistent. On public benchmarks and a billion-item corpus it improves Recall@500 by over 30% (relative gain +32.8%) at 1.7x the P99 QPS of SOTA tree/graph retrievers. Deployed at Kuaishou since Q2 2025 serving 400M users at 99.95% availability, with online Total App Usage Time +0.160% (main) and +0.165% (Lite).
