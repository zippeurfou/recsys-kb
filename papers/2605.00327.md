## Technical note

DynamicPO addresses 'preference optimization collapse' in LLM-based recommendation with multi-negative DPO, where adding more negatives degrades performance even as training loss falls; it shows theoretically this stems from gradient suppression when easily-discriminable negatives dominate boundary-critical negatives. It is a lightweight, plug-and-play framework with two mechanisms: Dynamic Boundary Negative Selection, which identifies informative negatives near the decision boundary via real-time clustering, and Dual-Margin Dynamic β Adjustment, which sets a per-sample β by boundary ambiguity to focus gradients where discrimination is hard. On Goodreads, LastFM, and Steam across LLM backbones, DynamicPO prevents collapse and improves accuracy with negligible overhead across multi-negative DPO objectives.
