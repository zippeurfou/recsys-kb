## Technical note

BBDRec revisits diffusion-based sequential recommendation from a preference-centric view, arguing that the conventional 'item <-> noise' Gaussian-anchored formulation adds a noise-reconstruction burden that distracts from preference modeling. Instead it uses a Brownian bridge process to build an endpoint-constrained diffusion trajectory directly between target-item and user-history representations (an 'item <-> history' transition), and adds a progressive learning strategy that first builds a preference-aware representation space then initializes the diffusion model. Experiments on multiple public datasets show BBDRec consistently outperforms representative sequential and diffusion-based baselines.
