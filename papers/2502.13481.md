## Technical note

LLM4Tag is an industrial automatic tagging system for search/recommendation that addresses three limitations of LLM-enhanced tagging: incomplete candidate recall, poor adaptation to emerging domain knowledge, and unreliable confidence. It uses a graph-based tag recall module to build a small highly-relevant candidate set, a knowledge-enhanced tag generation module with long-term and short-term knowledge injection, and a tag confidence calibration module for reliable scores. Across three large industrial datasets it significantly outperforms SOTA baselines and is deployed online serving hundreds of millions of users.
