## Summary

Local life service is a vital scenario in Kuaishou App, where video recommendation is intrinsically linked with store's location information. Thus, recommendation in our scenario is challenging because we should take into account user's interest and real-time location at the same time. ## Technical note

Kuaishou's OneLoc is an end-to-end generative recommender for short-video local life service that must jointly consider user interest and real-time location. It leverages geographic information three ways: geo-aware semantic IDs (tokenizing video+geo), geo-aware self-attention combining content and location-context similarity, and neighbor-aware decoder prompts encoding surrounding locations. It balances objectives via reinforcement learning with geographic and GMV reward functions on an encoder-decoder pre-train/post-train pipeline. Deployed in Kuaishou local life service for 400M daily users, OneLoc achieved +21.016% GMV and +17.891% order-number improvements.
