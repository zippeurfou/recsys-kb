## Technical note

ARTER (Adaptive Routing and Targeted Entity Reasoning) is a structured entity-linking pipeline that avoids deep fine-tuning by combining candidate generation, context-based scoring, a lightweight router, and selective LLM reasoning. Complementary embedding- and LLM-based signals over retrieved candidates classify mentions as easy (handled by a frozen low-cost linker like ReFinED) or hard (routed to a targeted LLM reasoning prompt). On six benchmarks (AIDA, MSNBC, ACE2004, AQUAINT, CWEB, WIKI), ARTER beats ReFinED by up to +4.47% (avg +2.53% on 5/6 datasets) and matches full-LLM-reasoning pipelines while using roughly half the LLM tokens.
