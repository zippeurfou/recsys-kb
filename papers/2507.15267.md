## Technical note

GREAT (Kuaishou) addresses item-to-query (I2Q) recommendation for related search on short-video, where existing embedding-similarity methods lack deep semantic interaction between video and query. It is an LLM-based framework that guides query generation with a trie: high-exposure, high-CTR queries build a query-based trie that enhances the LLM's training and constrains token generation at inference, followed by a post-processing module refining item-query relevance and literal quality. The authors release the large-scale real-world KuaiRS dataset, and offline plus online experiments demonstrate the method's effectiveness.
