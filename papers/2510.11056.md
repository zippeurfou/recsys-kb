## Technical note

This Meituan paper proposes a two-stage reasoning-distillation framework to transfer LLM reasoning to a lightweight BERT student for latency-constrained query-service relevance. Stage 1 builds a domain-adapted teacher LLM via domain-adaptive pre-training, SFT to elicit reasoning, and preference optimization with a multi-dimensional reward model; this teacher auto-annotates search logs with relevance labels and reasoning chains. Stage 2 introduces Contrastive Reasoning Self-Distillation (CRSD), which treats the same student under standard vs reasoning-augmented inputs as a teacher-student pair and uses InfoNCE contrastive learning to internalize reasoning without needing the reasoning path at inference, overcoming decoder-vs-encoder architectural heterogeneity. Offline and Meituan online A/B tests show significant gains.
