## Summary

1.  The paper addresses the challenge of accurately determining product relevance in e-commerce search, particularly for complex queries where evidence can conflict.
2.  It introduces CADRE, a Verifier-Guided Multi-Agent Reasoning framework that uses three specialized expert agents and a trainable Verifier to analyze different aspects and debate interpretations before reaching a final judgment.
3.  CADRE improves relevance assessment by optimizing the Verifier through debate guidance and experience replay, and it demonstrates effectiveness through offline evaluations and online A/B tests.

## Technical note

Hard e-commerce query-product pairs mix conflicting evidence from attributes, intent and business rules, and a single chain of thought often lets an intent reading override a decisive attribute mismatch. CADRE (Taobao search ads) runs three frozen Qwen3-14B expert agents in separate contexts (Analyst with a 14-attribute comparison schema, Intent with the product's top-5 historical queries and the query's top-5 clicked titles, Ruler with the platform's relevance guidelines) and a trainable Verifier that adjudicates their reports into one of seven labels (strongly relevant, substitute, or five mismatch types); only when expert labels disagree does it ask each expert one targeted question and re-adjudicate after revisions. The Verifier is SFT-initialized on Qwen3.8-Max-synthesized adjudications, trained with multi-turn GRPO in verl (reward = format + hierarchical label accuracy + rationale consistency + gated debate-shaping terms with a policy mask on Verifier tokens), then retrained from the SFT checkpoint with Replay-GRPO that mixes 2 stored successful trajectories (16 sampled per input by the GRPO checkpoint, top-8 by reward kept) with 8 fresh rollouts per group. On 78,997 train / 18,710 test in-house pairs CADRE reaches 0.8334 accuracy and 0.7766 macro-F1, +3.73 and +13.64 points over a single LLM-Replay-GRPO model given the same context, and exceeds the expert oracle (0.8051); initial-stage report sharing among experts hurts, confirming independent analyses. An mBERT student trained with gold labels plus cosine alignment of its [CLS] projection to BGE-M3 embeddings of the expert and Verifier CoTs gains +3.53 accuracy and +6.65 macro-F1 over label-only training, and in a 7-day A/B test lifted RPM 1.69%, CTR 0.76% and relevance satisfaction +0.3pp over production.
