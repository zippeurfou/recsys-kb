## Summary

Content platforms face immense pressure due to content overload, requiring recommendation systems that better satisfy user demands for high engagement and retention.

To tackle this challenge, researchers propose HGenPush, an innovative end-to-end generative system designed specifically for industrial push notification services like short-video apps (e.g., Kuaishou).

HGenPush uniquely integrates video *and* author recommendations into a single unified model, using a more efficient generation process and user feedback alignment to simultaneously deliver high-quality content and trusted creators, significantly boosting daily active users.

## Technical note

HGenPush is an end-to-end heterogeneous generative recommender for Kuaishou push notifications that jointly generates videos AND trusted authors, addressing the single-type-content and slow-autoregressive-decoding limits of prior GenRec (TIGER, OneRec). It uses a decoder-only backbone over multi-scenario behavior (long/short feed, push click/send sequences), a Chained Multi-Token Prediction (Chained-MTP) that replaces autoregressive decoding with parallel FFNs conditioned on a stable interest anchor plus cumulative semantic-ID embeddings, author semantic IDs built via behavior-aligned RQ-Kmeans (InfoNCE alignment, SimVQ anti-collapse), and a User Consumption Preference Alignment module trained with a GSISPO RL objective (GSPO sequence-level + CISPO clipping) on session-feedback rewards. Offline it beats SASRec and TIGER on HitRate@100 (video 0.3915, author 0.4848), Chained-MTP gives ~34% higher QPS than DeepSeek-MTP at comparable quality, and online A/B yields +0.181% DAU, +1.577% CTR, and +7.57% forward rate.
