## Technical note

NLGR improves generator training/generation in the evaluator-generator reranking paradigm, addressing goal inconsistency (the generator fits exposure distribution instead of the combinatorial optimum) and suboptimal one-by-one autoregressive generation. It uses neighbor lists in the combinatorial space to teach the generator relative list scores and optimization direction, and a sampling-based non-autoregressive generation method that jumps flexibly between neighbor lists. On public and industrial datasets it outperforms prior generative reranking, and is deployed on the Meituan food-delivery platform.
