## Technical note

This Google paper compares fine-tuning versus Retrieval-Augmented Generation for keeping LLM-powered recommenders up to date as user interests and content shift, using a deployed user-interest-exploration system that predicts a user's next novel interest cluster. Analysis shows high temporal variability in interest transitions (mean Jaccard 0.17 month-over-month), motivating frequent updates. It proposes a hybrid strategy combining periodic (monthly) fine-tuning for long-term knowledge with low-cost frequent RAG for agility; live A/B experiments on a billion-user platform show statistically significant user-satisfaction gains, offering a cost-effective update framework.
