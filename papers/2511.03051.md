## Summary

This paper introduces ScalingEval, a benchmark that evaluates 36 large language models (LLMs) as judges for recommendation tasks without human involvement, using a consensus-driven, multi-agent framework based on majority voting. The study found that Gemini 1.5 Pro offers the best overall performance, while Claude 3.5 Sonnet has the highest decision confidence, GPT-4o provides the best latency-accuracy-cost balance, and GPT-OSS 20B leads among open-source models. Analysis revealed strong agreement in structured domains (like Electronics) but persistent disagreement in lifestyle categories (like Clothing and Food), establishing ScalingEval as a reproducible benchmark for LLM evaluation in recommendation systems.

## Technical note

ScalingEval is a multi-agent LLM-as-a-judge benchmarking framework for complementary-item recommendation (CIR) that replaces human annotation with consensus ground truth built via scalable majority voting across 36 LLMs. The pipeline decomposes evaluation into specialized CI pattern-audit and issue-audit agents, resolves conflicts with a strict prioritization policy, and aggregates judgments into agreement-scored labels on Walmart-scale product pairs. Findings: Claude-3.5-sonnet has the highest decision confidence (~99%), Gemini-1.5-pro the best overall accuracy/coverage, GPT-4o the best latency-accuracy-cost tradeoff, and GPT-OSS-20B leads open-source models. Structured categories (Electronics, Sports) show strong cross-model agreement while lifestyle categories (Clothing, Food) remain contentious.
