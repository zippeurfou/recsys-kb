## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Sequential recommendation (SR), which encodes user activity to predict the next action, has emerged as a widely adopted strategy in developing commercial personalized recommendation systems. Although Transformer-based models have proven effective for sequential recommendation, the complexity of the self-attention module in Transformers scales quadratically with the sequence length. ## Technical note

Hydra is a multi-head latent Mamba architecture for sequential recommendation that replaces quadratic Transformer self-attention with multiple low-dimensional Mamba-2 layers plus FC layers and positional encoding, mapping items into latent subspaces to jointly capture historical and item information from long, noisy user sequences (up to hundreds of thousands of interactions). It scales to large parameters and extends to multi-domain recommendation by integrating and fine-tuning a single LLM, showing that one fine-tuned LLM can improve performance across domains. Experiments show Hydra outperforms SOTA SR baselines with fewer parameters and reduced training time.
