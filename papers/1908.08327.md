## Summary

This paper introduces a new algorithm, ZSF-c, for personalized complementary item recommendations in fashion e-commerce. To address data challenges, it proposes a sampling strategy for building the training set. The algorithm significantly outperforms standard collaborative filtering (+8.2% offline Orders Recall@5 and +3.24% online conversion rate lift) in recommending items customers can buy together.

## Technical note

ZSF-c is a personalized session-based complementary-item recommender for fashion e-commerce, extending the STAMP attention model to replace a collaborative-filtering baseline (CF-c) that scored poorly on attributed purchases. Key contributions are a sampling strategy that builds training pairs from general user behavior (click then complementary purchase within a day, filtered by a category-hierarchy complementarity definition and co-occurrence) rather than baseline logs, plus separate short-term (views) and long-term (purchase) representations combined by summation, and item embeddings fused from category, event-type, and fashion-specific image features. Offline it lifts Order Recall@5 to 0.267 (+8.2% relative over CF-c), and an online A/B test at a 28M-customer platform shows +6.23% CTR and +3.24% ordered products; ablation shows category embeddings and order events drive most gains.
