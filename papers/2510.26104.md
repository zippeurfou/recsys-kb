## Technical note

OneTrans (ByteDance) is a unified Transformer backbone that jointly performs user-behavior sequence modeling and feature interaction for ranking, replacing the conventional encode-then-interaction pipeline. A unified tokenizer converts both sequential and non-sequential features into one token sequence; OneTrans blocks share parameters across sequential tokens but assign token-specific parameters to non-sequential tokens (HiFormer-style mixed parameterization), and use causal attention with cross-request KV caching (reducing per-session complexity from O(C) to O(1)). It shows near log-linear scaling and delivered a 5.68% per-user GMV lift in online A/B tests.
