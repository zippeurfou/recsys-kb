## Summary

Unified Feature Interaction and Sequence Modeling with One Transformer in Industrial Recommender":

1.  The paper introduces OneTrans, a novel Transformer architecture designed to unify two distinct tasks in recommendation systems: modeling user behavior sequences and capturing complex feature interactions.
2.  OneTrans achieves this by employing a single tokenizer to convert both sequential and non-sequential data into a uniform token sequence, and uses parameter sharing mechanisms (similar tokens share parameters, others have unique ones) for efficient learning.
3.  This unified approach allows for bidirectional information flow, efficient precomputation, and significantly reduced training/inference costs, demonstrated to be highly scalable and outperforming standard baselines in industrial settings, including online A/B tests.

## Technical note

OneTrans is a unified Transformer backbone that jointly performs user-behavior sequence modeling and feature interaction for industrial ranking, replacing the conventional encode-then-interaction pipeline that keeps the two on separate tracks. A unified tokenizer converts both sequential behaviors and non-sequential (user/item/context) features into a single token sequence, and stacked OneTrans blocks use mixed parameterization: sequential tokens share Q/K/V and FFN weights while each non-sequential token gets token-specific parameters. Causal attention plus cross-candidate and cross-request KV caching precompute and reuse user-side representations, cutting per-session complexity from O(C) to O(1) over C candidates, and it inherits LLM optimizations like FlashAttention and mixed precision. OneTrans shows near log-linear scaling and a 5.68% per-user GMV lift in online A/B tests at Bytedance.
