## Technical note

ChorusCVR performs debiased post-click conversion-rate (CVR) estimation in the entire exposure space, tackling the sample selection bias from training CVR only on clicked samples while inferring over all exposures. Unlike ESMM (CTCVR) and counterfactual/pseudo-label methods (DCMT, NISE, DDPO), it targets two requirements: discriminating whether a clicked sample converts, and separating factual negatives (clicked-but-unconverted) from ambiguous negatives (unclicked). It uses a Negative sample Discrimination Module (NDM) with a novel CTunCVR auxiliary task to produce robust soft labels, and a Soft Alignment Module (SAM) to supervise CVR learning. Offline experiments and online A/B testing at Kuaishou's e-commerce live service validate superiority over state-of-the-art CVR methods.
