## Technical note

RecLLM-R1 (Xiaohongshu) is an LLM recommendation framework inspired by DeepSeek R1 that converts user profiles, histories, and item attributes into natural-language prompts, then applies two-stage training: Supervised Fine-Tuning to instill recommendation ability, followed by Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) reinforcement learning with a Chain-of-Thought mechanism and a flexibly defined reward function optimizing accuracy, diversity, and business objectives. On real user-behavior data from a large social-media platform it surpasses baselines on accuracy, diversity, and novelty and mitigates the filter-bubble effect, offering to supplant separate diversity/strategy modules.
