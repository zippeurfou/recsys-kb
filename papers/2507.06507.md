## Technical note

This survey reviews the past year's advances in LLM-based Generative Recommendations (GRs), positioning them as a new paradigm distinct from discriminative recommendation and potentially replacing feature-engineered systems. It traces the paradigm evolution (MLR, DLR, GR) and covers key methods including SASRec, HSTU, TIGER (RQ-VAE semantic IDs), OneRec (MoE + DPO), MTGR (cross features in generative models), and URM as a universal recommendation learner. It outlines industrial-application considerations and future directions, highlighting benefits like explainability, novelty, unified design, and LLM scaling laws for GR.
