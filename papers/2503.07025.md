## Technical note

This paper presents a distributed, scalable weak supervision system deployed to improve precision of a large-scale job search Learning to Rank pipeline. Building on Snorkel, it has SMEs author labeling functions (LFs) whose votes are aggregated by a simple probabilistic generative model (equivalent to a weighted majority voter under LF independence) trained on a small binary-annotated seed set, then relabels the end-model's training data by replacing labels with expected values. Implemented on Apache Spark and TensorFlow/Horovod, it labels hundreds of millions of points; NDCG@k is evaluated on original, updated, and weak-labeler-predicted labels to show precision gains over engagement-only training.
