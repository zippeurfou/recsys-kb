## Technical note

This Meta paper introduces an exposure-aware retrieval scoring approach that explicitly models item exposure probability and adjusts retrieval-stage ranking at inference time to mitigate popularity bias in a closed feedback loop. By reframing candidate selection as P(positive label | item, exposed) rather than P(positive label & exposed | item), it decouples engagement likelihood from exposure bias, unlike IPS or off-policy correction which operate at ranking or training time. Deployed in a large-scale video recommendation system, online A/B tests show a 25% increase in uniquely retrieved items and a 40% reduction in over-popular content dominance while preserving overall engagement.
