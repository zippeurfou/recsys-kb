## Summary

Generative Recommendation models, while powerful, suffer from two major limitations related to discrete Semantic IDs: an "Input Bottleneck" due to quantization and popularity bias, and an "Output Bottleneck" due to imprecise targets. To solve this, the paper introduces AsymRec, an asymmetric framework using continuous inputs and structured discrete outputs. AsymRec employs Multi-expert Semantic Projection to enhance input semantics and Multi-faceted Hierarchical Quantization to create better output targets, resulting in consistently improved performance over existing methods.

## Technical note

AsymRec is an asymmetric continuous-discrete generative recommendation framework that decouples input and output representations to address a dual-stage information bottleneck in semantic-ID pipelines. Multi-expert Semantic Projection (MSP) replaces discrete ID lookup with a mixture-of-experts MLP that maps continuous item embeddings directly into the Transformer hidden space, preserving fine-grained semantics and improving generalization to cold items. Multi-faceted Hierarchical Quantization (MHQ) builds high-capacity discrete targets by partitioning a projected latent into orthogonal subspaces (product quantization) and applying depth-L EMA-stabilized residual quantization within each, preventing dimensional collapse. It reports a 15.8% average lift over SOTA generative recommenders and a deployed pCVR system gain of 1.4% consumption and 1.9% GMV in online A/B tests.
