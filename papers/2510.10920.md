## Technical note

CoCountER (Huawei) provides comparative counterfactual explanations for recommendations, fixing factual inaccuracies in prior counterfactual methods that push the summed reduced-aspect score to a decision-boundary rather than per-aspect. It introduces a soft 'swap operation' that manipulates aspect values between a pair of comparative items (an explaining item and a reference item), optimizing the minimal swaps needed to change their relative ranking, thereby identifying the truly influential aspect (e.g., price or comfort) for recommending one item over another. Aspect-sentiment pairs are extracted from reviews via Sentires; experiments validate the approach yields more faithful explanations for arbitrary item pairs.
