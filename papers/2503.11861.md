## Summary

Text Analysis of Mobile Banking iOS and Google App Reviews":

1. This study analyzes user reviews for mobile banking apps from five major Canadian banks across both Apple (iOS) and Google Play platforms, focusing on sentiment and specific concerns following their rapid growth post-COVID-19.
2. Using Natural Language Processing tools including NLTK, LDA topic modeling, and sentiment analysis algorithms (LSTM/NB), it classifies reviews to identify key user preferences like usability/reliability and major issues such as login problems or update dissatisfaction.
3. The findings provide unique insights into both app store feedback strengths/weaknesses by comparing results across platforms, suggesting banks should prioritize user-friendly design, stable updates, improved customer service, and more transparent feature changes.

## Technical note

The study mines mobile-banking app reviews from iOS App Store and Google Play for five Canadian banks, combining topic modeling (LDA over uni/bi/tri-grams selected by perplexity and coherence) with sentiment classification. Reviews were labeled relative to rating standard deviations and classified by TextBlob, VADER, multinomial Naive Bayes, and LSTM; LSTM reached 82% accuracy on iOS while MNB reached 77% on Google Play, both far above lexicon methods. Bi-gram models with 15 topics gave the most coherent topics. Negative reviews centered on login/2FA problems, crashes, and update-induced breakage, while positive reviews praised ease of use and customer service.
