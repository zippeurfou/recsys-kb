## Summary

This paper investigates using Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4o or LLama 3.2 as recommenders for free-form text requests, such as specific movie descriptions. The authors find that LLMs are highly effective at generating contextually relevant recommendations and significantly outperform traditional methods like doc2vec in this task. While larger closed-source models generally perform best, the research also shows medium-sized open-source models remain competitive due to their lower computational costs.

## Technical note

Investigates using LLMs (e.g., GPT-4o, LLaMA 3.2) as narrative-driven recommenders that answer free-form natural-language item requests such as detailed movie descriptions. Across a dataset of such requests, LLMs generate highly contextually relevant recommendations and significantly outperform traditional methods like doc2vec. Larger closed-source models generally perform best, but medium-sized open-source models remain competitive given their lower computational cost. The study characterizes the trade-off between recommendation quality and model size/cost for this task.
