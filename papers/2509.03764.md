## Technical note

Pinterest automates whole-page search-relevance evaluation for online A/B experiments by fine-tuning small open-source LLMs (cross-encoder architecture, pointwise multi-class classification loss) on human-annotated relevance labels. The fine-tuned LLM-as-a-judge closely aligns with human judgments (average query-level sDCG@K error within 0.02), replacing costly slow human annotation. This unlocks larger query sets and better sampling design, yielding higher-quality relevance metrics and substantially lowering the Minimum Detectable Effect for online experiments.
