## Technical note

CausalRec (Taobao/Alibaba) integrates causality into the attention mechanism for sequential recommendation to avoid spurious correlations from item co-occurrence (e.g., cable and phone-shell co-purchases both caused by buying a phone). It has a causal discovery block that learns a causal graph over user-behavior sequences with an identifiability guarantee (using a linear SCM with equal-variance assumption, estimated from the transformer's final-layer covariance via continuous optimization with acyclicity/sparsity constraints), and a CausalBooster that reweights attention toward causally significant behaviors. It reports average improvements around 4.71-7.21% in Hit Rate and 8.57-8.65% in NDCG over state-of-the-art baselines.
