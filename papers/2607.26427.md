## Summary

Pair-Space Generation for Efficient Generative Reranking":

1.  Modern recommender systems use Auto-Regressive (AR) generators to rerank candidate item sequences, but this approach becomes slow and inefficient with long lists, limiting exploration potential due to linear complexity.
2.  To overcome these limitations, the paper introduces Pair-Space Generation (PSG), which represents a sequence as an ordered list of pairs from its candidates, allowing generation through fewer tokens while retaining equivalent expressiveness theoretically.
3.  PSG achieves significant practical improvements: faster inference and better real-world performance (+0.178% user engagement) compared to standard AR item-space generation methods, despite being based on a quadratic-sized vocabulary optimized via pretraining.

## Technical note

PSG (Pair-Space Generation) is a generative reranking method (Kuaishou) for the Generator-Evaluator paradigm that raises the autoregressive generation atom from single items to ordered item pairs, so a length-L slate is produced in L/2 steps over an n(n-1) per-request pair vocabulary. A pretrained pair-token representation module computes pair embeddings on the fly (avoiding the sparsity of a static n-squared vocabulary), and training combines pair-token pretraining, next-token prediction, and GRPO reinforcement against the evaluator. The authors prove PSG is bijective and equally expressive to item-space generation, gives a near-4x worst-case suboptimality reduction and 2x-4x decode speedup; deployed on Kuaishou (400M+ DAU) it yields +0.178% stay time and a 1.83x generator speedup (+79.8% QPS). It optimizes only the reranking stage, not retrieval.
