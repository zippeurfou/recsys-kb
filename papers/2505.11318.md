## Technical note

This Snap paper investigates why weight decay is crucial in collaborative-filtering pipelines and finds, via theory and experiments, that its primary function is encoding popularity information into embedding-vector magnitudes, acting as a coarse non-linear knob for preference toward popular vs unpopular items even in angle-based losses (cosine/normalized) that ignore magnitude. Based on this, it proposes PRISM (Popularity-aware Initialization Strategy for embedding Magnitudes), which pre-encodes popularity information directly, eliminating the need for weight-decay tuning. PRISM improves performance by up to 4.77% and reduces training time by 38.48% versus SOTA training strategies, and is parameterized to modulate initialization strength for mitigating popularity bias.
