## Technical note

This paper presents the Tencent Advertising Algorithm Challenge 2025 on all-modality generative recommendation, releasing two datasets from de-identified Tencent Ads logs: TencentGR-1M (1M user sequences, up to 100 items, exposure/click labels) and TencentGR-10M (10M users, distinguishing click vs. conversion at sequence and target level). Each interaction carries collaborative IDs plus multi-modal (text/vision) embeddings, framing next-ad prediction as multi-modal sequence generation with weighted evaluation for high-value conversions. It provides task definition, feature schema, a baseline GR model, and findings from top solutions, filling the gap of large-scale all-modality GR benchmarks in advertising.
