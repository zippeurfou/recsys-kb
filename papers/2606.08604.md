## Technical note

Gryphon is an encoder-decoder generative-retrieval architecture that fixes the mismatch where beam search ranks Semantic-ID (SID) token sequences rather than concrete items, addressing SID likelihood miscalibration and SID collisions. It adds a jointly trained Item-Level Scoring Module (ILSM) - a cross-attention + MLP head reusing the shared user encoder states - that resolves each beam-generated SID to its items and re-scores them (trained with next-item prediction via LogQ-corrected sampled softmax), discarding beam likelihood from final ranking. On an industrial music platform Gryphon reaches item-level Recall@1000 of 0.8552, beating vanilla GR (0.8245, +3.7%), collision-resolved GR (0.8343, +2.5%), and ARGUS (0.6582), and its item-level scoring exceeds its own SID-level beam ceiling (0.8404). Deployed as the sole candidate source in a 7-day A/B test, it caused no significant total-listening-time change (+0.25%) while replacing 15+ candidate generators plus the preranking stage.
