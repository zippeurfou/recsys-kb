## Summary

Existing authoring tools struggle to maintain story consistency and align with user goals for storytelling infographics. To address this, the authors designed InfoAlign, a human-AI co-creation system based on a three-phase workflow (story, visual, layout) that transforms text into stories, recommends visuals, and generates layouts, allowing user intervention. Evaluations show InfoAlign preserves story coherence throughout the design process and effectively supports collaborative infographic creation.

## Technical note

InfoAlign is a human-AI co-creation system for authoring storytelling infographics that preserves story consistency and user intent across interdependent design stages. Grounded in formative interviews and a quantitative analysis of 70 real-world infographics, it proposes a narrative-centric workflow with three phases: story construction, visual encoding, and spatial composition. The system transforms long or unstructured text into a coherent story, recommends semantically aligned visual designs, and generates layout blueprints, while letting users intervene and refine at any stage via human-in-the-loop editing. A 12-participant user study shows it maintains coherent stories across authoring stages and effectively supports co-creation. This is an HCI/visualization system, not recommendation.
