## Summary

Pinterest's Homefeed ranking module that encodes a user's real-time action sequence with a transformer and fuses it with long-term PinnerFormer embeddings. Documents productionization issues including diversity loss (random time-window mask) and engagement decay when a real-time-feature model is not retrained, motivating a twice-weekly retrain.

## Technical note

TransAct is Pinterest's Homefeed ranking submodule that encodes the user's most recent ~100 real-time actions (PinSage embedding, action type, timestamp) with a small transformer, early-fuses the candidate pin embedding into each token, and feeds the first-K outputs plus a max-pool into the Pinnability DCNv2 crossing layers alongside long-term PinnerFormer embeddings. A random 0-24h time-window mask on recent actions during training counters the rabbit-hole diversity loss that pure recency modeling causes. Online A/B on Homefeed improved repin volume and reduced hides, but engagement gains decayed to baseline within about two weeks when the model was not retrained; because real-time features make the model time-sensitive, production retrains Pinnability from scratch twice a week. The paper also notes a positive feedback loop: full-traffic deployment changes the real-time sequences that become future training data, compounding the gain.
