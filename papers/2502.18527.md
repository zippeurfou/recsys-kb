## Summary

The GOD model is a privacy-preserving personal AI assistant framework designed to address data concerns from services like Apple Intelligence and Meta AI. It operates directly on users' devices within secure environments (TEEs) to evaluate how well assistants anticipate needs while protecting sensitive information, unlike traditional benchmarks focused purely on accuracy. A key innovation is its token-based incentive system rewarding user data sharing based on GOD's assessment of assistant understanding, fostering a "data flywheel" for responsible AI development through community collaboration and open-source tools.

## Technical note

The GOD (Guardian of Data) model is a privacy-preserving framework for training and evaluating proactive on-device personal AI assistants without raw user data leaving the device. Running inside a Trusted Execution Environment, it acts as an 'AI school' that generates curriculum-based synthetic queries of increasing difficulty to score and refine assistants via reinforcement and imitation learning, mitigating the cold-start problem through simulated users. A token-based incentive mechanism rewards secure data sharing, creating a data flywheel, and a data-value estimation method quantifies each data source's contribution to recommendation quality. Anti-gaming safeguards (cross-verification, identity checks) protect evaluation integrity.
