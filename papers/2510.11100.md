## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Click-through rate (CTR) prediction, which models behavior sequence and non-sequential features (e.g., user/item profiles or cross features) to infer user interest, underpins industrial recommender systems. However, most methods face three forms of heterogeneity that degrade predictive performance: (i) Feature Heterogeneity persists when limited sequence side features provide less granular interest representation compared to extensive non-sequential features, thereby impairing sequence modeling ...

## Technical note

HoMer (Meituan) is a Homogeneous-Oriented TransforMer for CTR prediction that tackles three heterogeneities: feature (aligning sparse sequence side-features with rich non-sequential features for fine-grained interest), context (shifting from point-wise to set-wise prediction to model cross-item interaction among co-exposed items), and architecture (a unified encoder-decoder replacing fragmented specialized modules for efficiency/scalability). It outperforms the industrial baseline by 0.0099 AUC, lifts online CTR/RPM by 1.99%/2.46%, and saves 27% of GPU resources through engineering optimization.
