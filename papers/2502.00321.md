## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Click-Through Rate (CTR) prediction is a crucial task in recommendation systems, online searches, and advertising platforms, where accurately capturing users' real interests in content is essential for performance. However, existing methods heavily rely on ID embeddings, which fail to reflect users' true preferences for content such as images and titles. ## Technical note

MIM (Multi-modal Content Interest Modeling) shifts user-behavior modeling for CTR from ID-based to content-based, since ID embeddings capture CF signals but not content preferences and fail in cold-start/long-tail. It has three stages: pre-training foundation models on domain data for multi-modal embeddings, Content-Interest-Aware Supervised Fine-Tuning (C-SFT) that uses behavior signals to align embeddings with interests, and CiUBM that fuses multi-modal embeddings with ID-based CF. Deployed on Taobao, MIM achieved +14.14% CTR and +4.12% RPM in online A/B tests.
