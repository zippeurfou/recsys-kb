## Technical note

FIM (Frequency-Aware Multi-View Interest Modeling) targets periodic user intent in Kuaishou local-life service recommendation, where diverse demands have mixed and dynamically shifting periodicities. It combines a Multi-View Search Strategy (MSS) that filters long behavior subsequences across four views (price, brand, category, author) with a Frequency-domain Perception and Evolution Module (FPEM) that uses FFT to move behaviors into the frequency domain, band-filters them, and evolves periodic interests; predictions use an MMoE head. FPEM is a plug-and-play module. On a 13M-scale Kuaishou industrial dataset plus public benchmarks it improves accuracy over DIN/DIEN/SIM-style baselines, and online A/B testing significantly increased transaction volume.
