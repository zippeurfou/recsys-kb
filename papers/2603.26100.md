## Technical note

This position/blueprint paper argues that static multi-stage industrial recommenders (recall, ranking, re-ranking) and 'One Model' designs should be reframed as multi-agent decision systems, proposing AgenticRS. Modules are promoted to agents only when they form a functional closed loop, are independently evaluable, and have an evolvable decision space; agents self-evolve either via RL-style optimization in well-defined action spaces or via LLM-based generation/selection of new architectures in open-ended design spaces. It distinguishes individual from compositional evolution and uses a layered Inner/Outer reward design to couple local optimization with global business objectives, providing a conceptual framework rather than a specific algorithm.
