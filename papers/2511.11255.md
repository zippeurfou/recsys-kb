## Technical note

Align3GR unifies token-, behavior-modeling-, and preference-level alignment for LLM-based generative recommendation to close semantic/behavioral misalignment. It uses dual tokenization fusing semantic and collaborative signals, bidirectional semantic alignment for behavior modeling, and a progressive DPO strategy combining self-play (SP-DPO) for adversarial bias correction and real-world-feedback (RF-DPO) for dynamic preference adaptation. On the Instruments dataset it beats the SOTA baseline by +17.8% Recall@10 and +20.2% NDCG@10, with significant online A/B gains and full-scale deployment on an industrial recommendation platform.
