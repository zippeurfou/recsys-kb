## Summary

GalaxyDiT addresses the computational inefficiency of video generation using diffusion models, specifically tackling the high cost of iterative steps and classifier-free guidance (CFG). It introduces a training-free method combining guidance alignment and adaptive proxy reuse to accelerate generation by identifying optimal proxy metrics for different models. This approach achieves significant speedups (1.87x to 2.37x) with minimal quality degradation and maintains superior fidelity compared to previous methods.

## Technical note

GalaxyDiT is a training-free method to accelerate diffusion-transformer (DiT) video generation, which is expensive because it needs dozens of denoising steps and classifier-free guidance (CFG) doubles the compute. Through rank-order correlation analysis it systematically selects the model-specific optimal proxy metric (via Spearman's rank correlation) for deciding when to reuse intermediate features across adjacent denoising steps, and introduces CFG-aligned reuse to remove artifacts from treating conditional/unconditional passes independently. It achieves 1.87x-2.37x speedups on Wan2.1-1.3B/14B with under 1% VBench-2.0 drop and 5-10 dB higher PSNR fidelity than prior work.
