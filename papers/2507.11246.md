## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Click-Through Rate (CTR) prediction models are integral to a myriad of industrial settings, such as personalized search advertising. Current methods typically involve feature extraction from users' historical behavior sequences combined with product information, feeding into a discriminative model that is trained on user feedback to estimate CTR. With the success of models such as GPT, the potential for generative models to enrich expressive power beyond discriminative models has become apparent...

## Technical note

This JD paper proposes generative CTR prediction for search advertising, arguing generative models can enrich expressive power beyond conventional discriminative CTR models that feed behavior-sequence and product features into a feedback-trained classifier. Inspired by GPT-style success, it reformulates CTR estimation with a generative modeling approach over user historical behavior sequences and product information. Experiments demonstrate improved CTR prediction with applications to personalized search advertising.
