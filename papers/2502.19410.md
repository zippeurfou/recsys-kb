## Summary

Large Language Models (LLMs) have shown remarkable potential in recommending everyday actions as personal AI assistants, while Explainable AI (XAI) techniques are being increasingly utilized to help users understand why a recommendation is given. Personal AI assistants today are often located on ultra-small devices such as smartwatches, which have limited screen space. ## Technical note

This HCI study designs glanceable explanations for LLM action recommendations on ultra-small devices (smartwatches) with limited screen space. It explores spatially structuring explanation text into defined contextual components during prompting (using Chain-of-Thought over vision-language-model summaries of Ego4D videos) and temporally adaptive, confidence-based presentation. A user study found structured explanations reduced time-to-action and cognitive load and, when always-on, increased acceptance of AI recommendations, though users found them less satisfying due to fewer readable details; adaptive timing was less effective than always-on structured explanations.
