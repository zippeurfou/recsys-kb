## Technical note

Reconceptualizes eBay advertiser keyphrase relevance as an interaction between Advertising (produces keyphrases) and Search (a middleman that decides which enter auctions), coining 'middleman bias' — a sample-selection bias where click data only contains keyphrases passing Search's relevance filter. Rather than training on biased click/sales signals, they curate 24M/3M item-keyphrase pairs labeled by Search's relevance judgment and compare contrastive bi-encoders (eBERT), softmax and in-batch-negative bi-encoders, and bert-mini/bert-tiny cross-encoders. Contrastive bi-encoder (F1 0.79) beat other bi-encoders; bert-mini cross-encoder was best (F1 0.81). The deployed bert-tiny model, in an A/B test vs a Jaccard baseline, raised bought items +26.04%, CVR +11.6%, clicks +13.01% with neutral CTR.
