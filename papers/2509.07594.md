Click-through rate (CTR) prediction plays an important role in online advertising systems. ## Technical note

ELEC (Alibaba) is an efficient LLM-empowered CTR prediction framework combining collaborative and semantic signals while keeping serving efficient. It adapts an LLM for CTR, then uses a pseudo-siamese network: a gain network injects the LLM's high-level representation into a collaborative CTR model (benefiting from both tabular and textual modeling), and knowledge is distilled at both score and representation levels to a vanilla network that takes only tabular input for online inference, eliminating LLM latency. The approach is model-agnostic (integrates various LLMs and collaborative models like DeepFM/DCN) and experiments on real-world datasets show effectiveness and efficiency.
