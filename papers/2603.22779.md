## Technical note

KARMA (Knowledge-Action Regularized Multimodal Alignment) addresses a Knowledge-Action Gap when fine-tuning LLMs for personalized search at Taobao: action-only next-item objectives cause 'Semantic Collapse' where attention degenerates into barcode-like sinks and the model exploits ID shortcuts. KARMA learns a next-interest embedding for retrieval/ranking (Action) while enforcing semantic decodability as a train-only regularizer (Knowledge) via history-conditioned semantic generation and embedding-conditioned semantic reconstruction, plus optional visual-feature reconstruction. Ablations show semantic decodability yields up to +22.5 HR@200; deployed online with low overhead it gives +0.25 CTR AUC in ranking, +1.86/+2.51 HR in pre-ranking/recall, and +0.9% GMV.
