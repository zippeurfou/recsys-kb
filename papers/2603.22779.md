## Summary

Knowledge-Action Regularized Multimodal Alignment for Personalized Search at Taobao":

1.  Personalized search systems face a challenge when using large language models (LLMs) for tasks like next-item prediction; fine-tuning LLMs directly often leads to "semantic collapse," where the model loses general semantic understanding.
2.  To solve this "Knowledge-Action Gap," the authors propose KARMA, a framework that balances specific action training (like predicting the next item) with preserving semantic knowledge, using history-conditioned generation and embedding-conditioned reconstruction objectives.
3.  KARMA improves both the action performance (click-through rates, relevance) and semantic fidelity of the model, achieving significant offline metric improvements and driving a small but measurable increase in online item click-through rates with low computational cost.

## Technical note

KARMA (Knowledge-Action Regularized Multimodal Alignment) tackles the Knowledge-Action Gap that arises when fine-tuning LLMs for personalized search: action-only objectives over a continuous-token interface (compressing each item into one embedding) induce Semantic Collapse, seen as barcode-like attention sinks and ID-shortcut solutions. KARMA learns a next-interest embedding for retrieval/ranking via a pairwise cross-entropy action loss over exposed-but-unclicked hard negatives and in-batch negatives, while adding a train-only semantic-decodability regularizer with two paths: history-conditioned semantic generation (anchored to the LLM's native next-token distribution) and embedding-conditioned semantic reconstruction (enforcing an embedding bottleneck). Decoding heads are removed at inference for zero online overhead. On Taobao search, ablations show semantic decodability yields up to +22.5 HR@200, with +0.25 CTR AUC in ranking and +0.9% GMV online.
