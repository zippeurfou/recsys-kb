## Summary

1.  KuaiShou's push recommendation system uses LLMs to generate semantic IDs (SIDs) for personalized content delivery but faces challenges related to their black-box nature hindering deployment and complex methods like OneRec-Thinking being too costly.
2.  To address this, they introduced PushDualGen, a lightweight generator that first produces an SID followed by an optional copy explanation derived from the user's potential preferences or context (like a CoT prompt but skippable).
3.  PushDualGen was successfully deployed and shown to significantly improve engagement metrics while reducing dissatisfaction rates, demonstrating its effectiveness for industrial-scale recommendation systems and benefiting long-tail content visibility in the future.

## Technical note

Kuaishou's PushDualGen is a lightweight generative recommender for push notifications serving nearly a billion users at ~100K QPS, designed to keep OneRec-Think's interpretability without its chain-of-thought decoding cost by generating the Semantic ID first and the push copy afterwards as a skippable explanation. Videos are encoded by Qwen2.5-Omni-3B with 8 learnable compression tokens into M=8 parallel embedding slots, each K-means quantized against its own K=512 codebook (parallel SIDs avoid RQ's residual error accumulation); the 4,096 codebook indices are registered as special tokens and a Qwen3-0.6B generator is aligned to them with symmetric Text2SID / SID2Text tasks while the original vocabulary embeddings stay frozen. The generator is then fine-tuned on user click histories with L_gen = L_SID + lambda * L_Copy, and at serving the Top-20 generated SIDs are encoded and fused into the user vector as e_u' = alpha*e_u + beta*e_s (alpha=beta=1) for ANN retrieval over candidate video embeddings. A 14-day A/B on 15% of traffic (~150M users, CUPED-adjusted) gives effective play rate 64.46% -> 69.94% (+8.50% relative), dislike rate 0.053% -> 0.033% (-37.70%), Click PV +0.43% and DAU +0.05%, all significant. Ablations on SID prediction Pass@1/4/20 show SID adaptation matters most (0.335/0.391/0.422 full vs 0.319/0.352/0.366 without, and 0.312/0.347/0.358 for vanilla Qwen3-0.6B), with parallel SIDs beating OneRec-style residual SIDs (0.326/0.379/0.401) and token freeze / multi-token binding giving small gains; exposure analysis shows the model shifts share toward torso and tail categories relative to the cascading online service.
