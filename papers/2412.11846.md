## Summary

Enhancing Session-based Recommendation with Single Positive Graph Learning" in three sentences:

1.  The paper addresses challenges in session-based recommendation (SBR), particularly data sparsity and high model complexity, due to limited user interaction sequences during short sessions.
2.  It introduces SPGL (Single Positive Graph Learning), a novel SBR model combining graph convolutional networks (GCNs) for global item relationships with batch session representations generated by an intent extractor.
3.  The key innovation is the use of single positive optimization loss, which simplifies training and improves recommendation accuracy across three benchmark datasets, effectively integrating spatial information from the directed global graph and temporal context through reverse positional awareness in sessions.

## Technical note

Session-based recommendation suffers from data sparsity and high model complexity given short interaction sequences. SPGL combines a graph convolutional network over a directed global item graph (spatial signal) with batch session representations produced by an intent extractor, and adds reverse positional awareness for temporal context. Its key contribution is a single positive optimization loss that simplifies training while improving accuracy. On three benchmark datasets SPGL improves recommendation accuracy over prior session-based methods.
