## Summary

ReaGeo presents an end-to-end geocoding framework using large language models (LLMs) to overcome the limitations of traditional geocoding methods. It converts coordinate prediction into a text generation task by using geohash sequences and enhances reasoning with a Chain-of-Thought mechanism and reinforcement learning. Experiments demonstrate that ReaGeo accurately handles precise and vague queries, and effectively predicts non-point regions, showcasing its versatility.

## Technical note

ReaGeo reformulates geocoding as text generation: a decoder-only LLM (Qwen2.5-3B) autoregressively decodes a length-9 Base32 geohash (~2.4m precision) from a query, taking the region centroid as coordinates, thereby avoiding multi-stage NER/entity-linking/retrieval pipelines and their error propagation. Training has two stages: CoT SFT that injects neighborhood/relative-position reasoning (using adjacent POIs as anchors, with thinking-mode tags), then GRPO reinforcement learning that computes group-relative advantages without a value model, optimized by a geospatial reward based on WGS-84 geodesic distance deviation between predicted and true coordinates. Anchor Offset training data adds '[distance] meters [direction] of' phrases with shifted labels to teach directional offsets. Evaluated on ~240k Amap Beijing samples, ReaGeo handles explicit and vague relative queries plus non-point line/polygon regions.
