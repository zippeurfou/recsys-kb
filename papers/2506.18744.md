## Technical note

Meta addresses the problem that tuning recommender scoring policies via A/B tests requires long-running experiments to measure long-term outcomes, which stalls sequential Bayesian optimization. The 'fast and slow' framework runs short biased experiments and off-policy proxies in parallel with slow long-running A/B tests, and introduces multi-task GPs and a novel target-aware GP that learns to map short-term proxies to long-term outcomes. Deployed at Meta, the parallel experimental design cut experimentation wall time by over 60% while achieving superior optimization, and works with online tests as short as a few hours.
