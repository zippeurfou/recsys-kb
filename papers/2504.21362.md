## Technical note

FairAgent is a reinforcement-learning framework for enhancing new-item fairness in dynamic recommender systems (DRSs), where new items continuously appear with limited exposure and evolving user preferences amplify bias toward old items through feedback loops. It uses knowledge distillation to inherit collaborative signals and pre-trained embeddings from traditional models (preserving old-item quality), and designs a three-part reward: a new-item exploration reward to promote exposure of newly introduced items, a fairness reward adapting to users' personalized fairness needs, and an accuracy reward leveraging dynamic feedback. Experiments on three public datasets and multiple backbones show FairAgent boosts new-item exposure and personalized fairness while maintaining accuracy.
