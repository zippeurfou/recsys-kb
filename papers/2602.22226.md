## Technical note

SEGB (Self-Evolved Generative Bidding) is an offline framework for automated ad bidding that plans proactively and self-improves without simulators or online interaction. It uses Local Autoregressive Diffusion (LAD) to synthesize causally-consistent short-horizon future states, feeds this foresight into a Next-State-Aware Decision Transformer to turn a reactive imitator into a forward-looking planner, and then evolves the policy entirely offline via GRPO guided by an IQL critic. On the AuctionNet benchmark SEGB outperforms SOTA baselines, and a large-scale online A/B deployment delivered a +10.19% increase in target cost.
