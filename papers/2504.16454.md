## Summary

This paper introduces UniGRF, a novel single generative framework unifying the retrieval and ranking stages in recommendation systems. It treats both tasks as sequence generation processes to allow seamless information sharing between them within one model, eliminating traditional inter-stage loss. Experiments show that UniGRF significantly outperforms existing models by effectively enhancing collaboration through specific modules like ranking-driven refinement and gradient-guided weighting, achieving synchronized optimization for both stages.

## Technical note

UniGRF unifies the retrieval and ranking stages of recommendation into a single generative model to eliminate information loss from training separate stage-specific models. Both stages are cast as sequence-generation tasks over user behavior sequences and distinguished by output position, giving a model-agnostic, plug-and-play framework. A ranking-driven enhancer module exploits the ranking stage's precision to generate high-quality samples for retrieval through parameter sharing, creating a mutually reinforcing loop, while a gradient-guided adaptive weighter monitors per-stage optimization speeds and dynamically balances the retrieval and ranking losses to synchronize convergence. Extensive experiments show UniGRF outperforms state-of-the-art baselines, with ablations confirming effective inter-stage collaboration, synchronized optimization, and strong scalability.
