## Summary

This paper provides a comprehensive overview of personalized news recommendation systems, highlighting their importance in addressing information overload and summarizing decades of research and its notable successes. Instead of a traditional taxonomy, it proposes a novel perspective based on core problems and the techniques used to solve them, while also discussing the associated challenges. The review covers techniques, public datasets, evaluation methods, the need for responsible AI implementation, and suggests future research directions.

## Technical note

This is a comprehensive survey of personalized news recommendation that departs from the conventional collaborative/content-based/hybrid taxonomy and instead organizes the field around core problems: news modeling, user modeling, personalized ranking, and model training. It reviews techniques and challenges for each, emphasizing news-specific difficulties such as severe cold-start from short article life cycles, reliance on textual content requiring NLP, and inference of interests from implicit click feedback. It also catalogs public datasets, benchmarks, and evaluation methods, and discusses responsibility concerns (bias, fairness, privacy). The paper closes with proposed future research directions to guide the field.
