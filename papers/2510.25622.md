## Technical note

ADC-SID (Taobao/Alibaba) adaptively denoises collaborative information when generating semantic IDs, addressing the quality gap between popular and long-tail items whose sparse interactions inject collaborative noise. It has two components: Adaptive Behavior-Content Alignment, which adjusts alignment strength based on the reliability of pre-trained item embeddings to prevent noise from corrupting content representations; and a Dynamic Behavioral Weighting Mechanism, which learns importance scores over multiple behavioral SIDs so downstream models suppress noisy ones instead of equal-weighting. Offline and online A/B experiments show gains on both generative retrieval and discriminative ranking tasks.
