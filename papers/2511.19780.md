## Summary

NOEM³A presents a neuro-symbolic method integrating a structured intent ontology with compact language models to improve multi-intent understanding in mobile AI agents, addressing the challenge of ambiguous user utterances. This approach uses techniques like retrieval-augmented prompting and logit biasing to incorporate symbolic intent structure, leading to more disambiguated and grounded interpretations, as shown qualitatively. Experiments demonstrate that NOEM³A significantly boosts accuracy (approaching GPT-4 levels) while drastically reducing energy and memory usage compared to larger models like GPT-4 or even smaller Llama models, validating its effectiveness for on-device use.

## Technical note

NOEM3A augments compact language models with an intent ontology for efficient multi-intent NLU on mobile devices. For each query it retrieves a small ontology neighborhood by cosine similarity, serializes candidate action labels into the prompt, and applies a token-level decoding prior that biases logits toward valid ontology labels. It also introduces Semantic Intent Similarity (SIS), a hierarchy-aware diagnostic based on ontology depth. On MultiWOZ 2.3, ontology augmentation consistently improves TinyLlama and Llama-3.2-3B on exact match and Slot-F1, letting a small model approach GPT-4-level accuracy while staying private and low-latency.
