## Technical note

Studies cross-attention in cross-domain sequential recommendation (CDSR) and identifies 'Orthogonal Alignment', a phenomenon where cross-attention output is orthogonal to (rather than a filtered version of) the query input, emerging without any explicit orthogonality regularization. Introduces a gated cross-attention (GCA) module inspired by Flamingo, inserted at multiple depths of three CDSR baselines (CDSRNP, ABXI, LLM4CDSR). Across 300+ experiments on Amazon Reviews cross-domain pairs, lower cosine similarity between query and cross-attended output correlates strongly with better ranking accuracy. GCA-augmented baselines beat parameter-matched baselines, showing orthogonal alignment improves accuracy-per-parameter and thus scaling.
