## Summary

This paper introduces Decoupled Temporal Encoding (DTE), a new framework for generative recommendation systems using Transformers. Existing methods struggle to effectively integrate complex temporal dynamics (like recency and seasonal shifts) into models that treat user item sequences as purely autoregressive. DTE addresses this by separating broad temporal signals and local sequence order through two distinct modules, offering a lightweight and parameter-efficient solution.

## Technical note

DTE (Decoupled Temporal Encoding) is a positional-encoding replacement for HSTU-style decoder-only generative recommendation, built at Rajax / Taobao Shangou (Alibaba food delivery and instant retail) for the ad ranking stage. The argument is that RoPE, sinusoidal and ALiBi encode only discrete order, while unified time-aware methods like BST, TiSASRec and HSTU relative attention bias conflate heterogeneous temporal effects into one representation. DTE splits them: a personalized macro-temporal module adds a scalar signal m(t) to each item embedding via a learnable projection vector, where m(t) is a softmax-gated mixture of recency decay exp(-lambda*dt), intra-day periodicity sin(2*pi*hour/24), weekly periodicity sin(2*pi*weekday/7), and a burst term log(1+ReLU((x_prev-x_avg)/x_avg)) over target-side traffic, with the four mixture weights produced by a 16-8-4 MLP over the average-pooled user history. A micro-sequential module adds a time-gated relative-order attention bias B_ij = -alpha*sigmoid((tau-|t_i-t_j|)/gamma)*|i-j|, so order priors only fire when interactions are temporally dense. Backbone is 1 block, 8 heads, hidden 64, 16-8-1 task tower, Adam, batch 1024, lr 1e-4, max sequence 150. Offline on a 400M-user / 5M-item / 2.5B-sample industrial impression log DTE reaches 0.7098 GAUC and 0.2578 logloss versus 0.6982 for the BST+ALiBi production baseline and 0.6978 for RAB, and on KuaiRand-1K 0.9243 versus 0.9110. It adds only 238 trainable parameters (0.47 KB FP16) against TiSASRec's 327,680, at 3.02 ms offline inference. A three-week 20 percent/20 percent user-randomized A/B gave +1.8 percent CTR and +3.0 percent RPM at p<0.01 with serving latency up only 0.3 percent (15.4 to 15.5 ms), and DTE went to 100 percent of Shangou ad traffic in January 2026. Ablations show removing macro costs the most (0.6852 GAUC), and the authors note gains shrink for users with sparse histories or weak periodicity and that the burst term is scenario-specific.
