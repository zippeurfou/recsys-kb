## Technical note

IU4Rec is a two-stage Interest Unit-based recommendation framework for Xianyu, China's largest C2C platform, where individual-seller items have limited stock and few interactions, hurting DIN-style methods. It clusters products into Interest Units (IUs) via DSI-based clustering on category/image/semantic attributes; stage one recommends IUs to capture broad interests and stage two ranks products within the chosen IU. User-IU interactions persist and accumulate better than sparse item-level interactions, mitigating the limited-stock/cold-start problem. Production offline experiments and online A/B testing demonstrate its effectiveness.
