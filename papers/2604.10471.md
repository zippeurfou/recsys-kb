## Technical note

SID-Coord is a lightweight Semantic ID framework that integrates discrete trainable SIDs directly into ID-based short-video search ranking models to fix the memorization-generalization trade-off, where hashed-ID (HID) models memorize frequent interactions but generalize poorly to long-tail items. It coordinates HID memorization with SID generalization via three components: attention-based fusion over hierarchical (three-level, 8192-size codebook, RQ-VAE/QARM-style) SIDs for coarse-to-fine semantics, a target-aware HID-SID gating mechanism that adapts to item popularity, and a SID-driven interest alignment module over target-history semantic similarity. It plugs into production rankers without backbone changes; online A/B tests show +0.664% long-play rate and +0.369% search playback duration.
