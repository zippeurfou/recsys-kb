## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of optimizing App Store search relevance by tackling the scarcity of textual relevance labels. To overcome this, the authors fine-tuned a Large Language Model (LLM) to generate millions of high-quality textual relevance judgments. By integrating these LLM-generated labels into their production ranking system, they achieved significant offline improvements in NDCG and confirmed these gains with a real-world A/B test showing a statistically significant increase in conversion rates, particularly for difficult "tail" queries.

## Technical note

This industrial paper augments the App Store search ranker by using a fine-tuned in-house LLM as an offline label generator to overcome scarcity of expert textual-relevance labels relative to abundant behavioral labels. It finds a specialized fine-tuned model produces higher-quality relevance labels than a much larger pretrained one, then generates millions of pointwise textual-relevance labels across storefronts and languages. Mixing these labels into multi-objective learning-to-rank training shifts the behavioral-vs-textual relevance Pareto frontier outward, improving offline NDCG on both objectives. A worldwide A/B test showed a statistically significant +0.24% conversion lift, with the largest gains on tail queries where behavioral signal is weakest.
