## Technical note

R1-Ranker from Meta is a reinforcement-learning framework that teaches LLM rankers to reason across diverse ranking tasks (recommendation, LLM routing, passage ranking) with a single model. It has two variants: DRanker generates a full ranking in one shot optimized with RL on task rewards, and IRanker decomposes ranking into an iterative worst-candidate-elimination process with step-wise rewards to reduce output space and enable deeper reasoning. Evaluated on nine datasets, IRanker-3B achieves state-of-the-art, surpasses some 7B models, and yields a 15.7% average relative improvement; it improves zero-shot out-of-domain performance by over 9%, and its reasoning traces boost other LLMs by up to 22.87%.
