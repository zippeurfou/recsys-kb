## Summary

This paper introduces **IRanker**, a unified foundation model designed for various ranking tasks like recommendation, routing, and search. IRanker employs an innovative approach combining reinforcement learning with iterative decoding, progressively eliminating the worst candidates from a pool.

The core idea of iterative decoding significantly reduces the output space during training, enhancing performance compared to models of similar size on standard benchmarks within its domain. Furthermore, IRanker demonstrates strong generalization capabilities, performing well (at least 5% better in-domain) and surprisingly excelling even on unrelated out-of-domain tasks like GSM8K and MathQA using its generated thoughts.

## Technical note

IRanker (part of the R1-Ranker framework) is a unified RL-trained LLM ranker spanning recommendation, LLM routing, and passage ranking. It contrasts DRanker (one-shot full-ranking generation optimized with PPO on an MRR-based reward plus F1 format penalty) with IRanker, which decomposes ranking into iterative worst-candidate elimination with step-wise exclusion rewards, shrinking the output/action space and enabling deeper reasoning within limited context. A single IRanker-3B reaches SOTA, beats some 7B models, averages 15.7% relative improvement, and its reasoning traces transfer to boost other LLMs on out-of-domain tasks (GSM8K, IFEval, MathQA) by up to ~23%.
