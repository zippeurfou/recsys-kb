## Summary

This paper proposes LLMD4Rec, a bidirectional knowledge distillation framework combining Large Language Models (LLMs) and conventional Recommendation Models (CRMs). It addresses LLMs' high inference costs by enabling iterative optimization where both models refine each other through dynamic knowledge exchange. Experiments demonstrate that this approach significantly improves recommendation accuracy without adding extra parameters or increasing inference expenses.

## Technical note

LLMD4Rec is a bidirectional mutual-distillation framework that couples an LLM-centric recommender with a conventional recommendation model (CRM) for sequential recommendation, overcoming the static, unidirectional nature of prior LLM<->CRM distillation. It alternates two steps: downward enhancement (CRM collaborative knowledge into the LLM via projection layers over LoRA-tuned modules) and upward semantic distillation (LLM knowledge back into the CRM), iterating in a CRM->LLM->CRM cycle for dynamic co-optimization. Both directions distill the prediction-layer output distribution (which shares a common space) rather than embeddings or top-K lists, avoiding feature transforms, and use a sample-wise adaptive weighting mechanism. It improves accuracy of both models across datasets without adding parameters or inference cost, and speeds LLM convergence.
