## Technical note

Fortress is a framework for stabilizing search/recommendation predictions by identifying and pruning features that cause temporal instability while retaining predictive power, useful in multi-stage systems where score fluctuations flip items in and out of results. Using temporally partitioned historical snapshots, it flags high-variance (top-quartile Coefficient of Variation) samples, computes per-feature CV, and greedily removes volatility-inducing features (often engagement signals) only when doing so yields a statistically significant PR-AUC lift on validation. Validated on an Apple app-marketplace query-to-app relevance model, Fortress lowers Coefficient of Variation (stability) and improves PR-AUC (accuracy).
