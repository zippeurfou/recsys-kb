## Summary

This paper addresses two major challenges in deploying large language models (LLMs) for e-commerce search: insufficient product knowledge leading to hallucinations, and security vulnerabilities from jailbreak attacks. To solve these, the authors introduce the SIA framework, combining a corpus synthesis stage (integrating structured data and logs), a parameter-efficient knowledge injection stage (preserving general capabilities), and a dual-path alignment stage (enhancing performance and safety). The framework was successfully deployed at JD.com, resulting in significant improvements in key business metrics across multiple search scenarios.

## Technical note

SIA (Synthesize-Inject-Align) is a framework for building knowledge-grounded and jailbreak-resistant e-commerce search LLMs, targeting product-knowledge hallucination and security/compliance risks. The Synthesize stage generates natural-language corpora by combining structured knowledge graphs with unstructured behavioral logs, augmented with chain-of-thought reasoning traces and safety-aware data. The Inject stage uses a parameter-efficient Depth Up-Scaling pre-training strategy (with layer initialization and tailored learning schedules) to add domain knowledge while mitigating catastrophic forgetting of general capabilities. The Align stage applies a dual-path strategy: multi-task instruction tuning for task performance plus adversarial training for safety robustness, balanced with general alignment data. Deployed at JD.com, A/B tests across five scenarios (search suggestion, product title generation, review summarization, query correction, safety moderation) showed significant business-metric gains.
