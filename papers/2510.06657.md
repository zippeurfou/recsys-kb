## Technical note

A Google/YouTube case study deploying 'LLM-as-annotators' to achieve nuanced content understanding (e.g., content 'vibe' like authentic, calming, energetic) at scale in a short-form video recommender. The end-to-end workflow covers iterative attribute definition and evaluation, scalable offline bulk annotation of video corpora using LLMs (Gemini) with multimodal features plus knowledge distillation for coverage, and online integration via personalized restricted retrieval. LLMs outperform human raters on offline annotation quality for nuanced attributes, and online A/B tests show significant gains in user participation and satisfied consumption, with much shorter development cycles than traditional ML classifiers.
