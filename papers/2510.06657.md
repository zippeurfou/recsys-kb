## Technical note

Traditional ML classifiers for content understanding have long development cycles and miss nuanced attributes like content 'vibe' (authentic, calming, energetic), limiting personalization in industrial short-video recommendation. This case study deploys an LLM-as-annotators pipeline with three stages: iteratively defining subtle attributes and validating them against an expert-aligned Golden Set plus online A/B feedback; scalable offline bulk annotation of 10^5-10^6 videos/day using multimodal LLMs (Gemini) with quantization, batching, and sharding for 2-3x throughput, then distilling into lightweight student DNNs to cover the full 10^7/day corpus; and integrating annotations online via personalized restricted retrieval. LLMs outperformed human raters on nuanced-attribute annotation quality, and online A/B tests showed significant gains in user participation and satisfied consumption.
