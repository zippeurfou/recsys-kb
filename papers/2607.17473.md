## Summary

1.  **Problem:** The use of personalized features (e.g., age, gender) for accurate ad recommendation via Click-Through Rate (CTR) and conversion rate (CVR) prediction is increasingly restricted by privacy regulations.
2.  **Solution:** To address this challenge in limited feature settings, the RAMP framework proposes two distinct pathways: a dual-tower personalized pathway with masked outputs, and a separate non-personalized pathway trained without those features.
3.  **Result:** An alignment mechanism between these pathways distills knowledge to improve predictions when personalized data is unavailable, consistently outperforming existing methods in this scenario according to extensive experimental results on various benchmarks and industrial datasets.

## Technical note

RAMP improves CTR/CVR prediction on non-personalized, consent-restricted ad traffic where user-linked personalized features are missing. It combines a personalization-gated dual-tower personalized pathway (shared embeddings, independent towers, output masking that routes personalized vs non-personalized traffic), a non-personalized pathway trained only on non-personalized features (used at training time only), and a distillation-inspired logit-level prediction-alignment loss between the two pathways. Model-agnostic over PNN/FCN/FINAL backbones, it beats the best baselines on Non-PER AUC by +0.10% to +0.87% (e.g., +0.87% on CriteoPrivateAd) and outperforms KD baselines (KD, PFD, HAPFD); an industrial CVR A/B test raised total advertiser value (TAV) by over 3%.
