## Technical note

UKDSL is a Unified Knowledge-Distillation and Semi-Supervised Learning framework for industrial ads ranking that lets early-stage models learn from large unlabeled data, reducing overfitting and training-serving distribution discrepancies inherent to multi-stage cascades. It provides formal analysis and numerical simulations of the inherent miscalibration and prediction bias of multi-stage ranking and empirical evidence that UKDSL mitigates them. Deployed at multi-billion user scale across surfaces, locations, clients, and multiple events for both CTR and CVR models across retrieval and pre-ranking stages, improving offline and online performance efficiently.
