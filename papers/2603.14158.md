## Technical note

This study evaluates how clinician-provided input steers frontier AI models in medical decision-making, finding that such input can push models toward both more accurate and more harmful decisions. Using clinical vignettes evaluated by a multi-institution physician team across specialties, the authors show that human context injected into the models can improve correctness yet also amplify harmful recommendations, highlighting a dual-edged effect of human-in-the-loop clinical AI.
