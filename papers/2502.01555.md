## Technical note

This paper addresses brand entity linking for short (avg 2.4-word) e-commerce search queries with a massive brand space. It compares a two-stage pipeline (named-entity recognition for brand mention detection using MetaTS-NER, followed by disambiguation/matching to a global brand entity) against a novel end-to-end solution using extreme multi-class classification that directly maps a query to its brand entity, unifying brand variants across languages, surface forms, and parent/sub-brand relationships. Large-scale offline benchmarks and an online A/B test show significant recall and customer-engagement gains.
