## Technical note

SIDReasoner is a two-stage framework that elicits reasoning over Semantic IDs (SIDs) for LLM-based generative recommendation without requiring large amounts of recommendation-specific reasoning traces. Stage one strengthens SID-language alignment via multi-task training on an enriched SID-centered corpus synthesized by a stronger teacher model, grounding itemic tokens in semantic and behavioral contexts; stage two applies outcome-driven reinforced optimization to guide effective reasoning trajectories without explicit rationale annotations. On three real-world datasets, SIDReasoner improves reasoning-augmented SID-based generative recommendation accuracy and yields better interpretability and cross-domain generalization.
