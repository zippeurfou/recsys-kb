In real applications, user interests evolve over time and exhibit nonstationary dynamics, where different preference factors change at different rates. ## Technical note

TimeMM is a time-conditioned spectral filtering framework for dynamic multimodal recommendation that injects temporal dynamics into the graph propagation operator rather than as an auxiliary feature. Its Time-as-Operator maps interaction recency to parametric temporal kernels that reweight user-item graph edges, forming a lightweight spectral filter bank without explicit eigendecomposition; Adaptive Spectral Filtering mixes the operator bank by temporal context, Spectral-Aware Modality Routing calibrates visual vs textual contributions, and a ranking-space Spectral Diversity Regularization prevents filter-bank collapse. On real-world benchmarks TimeMM consistently outperforms SOTA multimodal recommenders (AlignRec, SMORE, FITMM, etc.) while maintaining linear-time scalability.
