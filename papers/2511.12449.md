## Technical note

MOON2.0 (Taobao) is a dynamic modality-balanced multimodal representation learning framework for e-commerce product understanding addressing modality imbalance, under-used intra-product alignment, and multimodal noise. It uses a Modality-driven Mixture-of-Experts that adaptively processes samples by modality composition for Multimodal Joint Learning, a Dual-level Alignment method leveraging semantic alignment within individual products, and an MLLM-based Image-text Co-augmentation strategy with Dynamic Sample Filtering to improve data quality. It releases the MBE2.0 benchmark and achieves state-of-the-art zero-shot performance on MBE2.0 and multiple public datasets, with attention heatmaps showing improved multimodal alignment.
