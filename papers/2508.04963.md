## Technical note

This Xiaohongshu (RedNote) paper tackles evaluating MLLM representation alignment for large-scale multimodal recommendation, where static benchmarks are inaccurate, online evaluation is too expensive, and the AUC Improvement Score (AIS) is costly and hard to diagnose. It proposes the Leakage Impact Score (LIS), which measures the ranking-performance gap between models trained with and without leaked preference information to assess the upper bound of preference-data quality without training or running MLLMs on billions of items. Online A/B tests on both Content Feed and Display Ads of Xiaohongshu's Explore Feed showed significant improvements in user time spent and advertiser value.
