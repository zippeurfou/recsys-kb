## Technical note

NAM (Normalization Attention Model) from Taobao/Fliggy improves personalized product search by addressing the item perspective: co-occurrence-based methods overestimate conversion for popular items and underestimate long-tail items, and personalization value depends on item popularity. NAM optimizes 'when to personalize' via a gating mechanism using the target item's Inverse Item Frequency (IIF), and 'how to personalize' by globally normalizing the attention mechanism over user-behavior items with IIF weighting. Data analysis (PCOC across popularity levels) confirms popular-item overestimation; NAM outperforms state-of-the-art baselines and achieved a 0.8% conversion-rate improvement in an online A/B test on Fliggy.
