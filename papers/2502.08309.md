## Technical note

LUM (Large User Model) is a production-aware three-step paradigm that brings LLM-style scaling to industrial recommendation without discarding DLRM strengths (feature engineering, modular architecture, production optimization). It bridges generative pre-training and discriminative application via a 'next-condition-item prediction' task, addressing E2E generative recommenders' weakness on discriminative tasks like CTR (calibration and ranking). LUM outperforms SOTA DLRMs and E2E-GR methods, scales consistently up to 7B parameters exhibiting a clear scaling law, and delivered statistically significant gains in a live A/B test.
