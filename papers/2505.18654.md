## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Scaling law has been extensively validated in many domains such as natural language processing and computer vision. In the recommendation system, recent work has adopted generative recommendations to achieve scalability, but their generative approaches require abandoning the carefully constructed cross features of traditional recommendation models. We found that this approach significantly degrades model performance, and scaling up cannot compensate for it at all. In this paper, we propose MTGR ...

## Technical note

MTGR (Meituan Generative Recommendation) is an HSTU-based generative ranking model that, unlike prior generative approaches, retains traditional DLRM cross features whose removal it shows severely degrades performance beyond what scaling can recover. It adds Group-Layer Normalization (GLN) for encoding across different semantic spaces, a dynamic masking strategy to prevent information leakage, and user-level compression plus training-framework optimization for efficient scaling supporting 10-100x DLRM compute. Achieving 65x FLOPs for single-sample forward inference versus DLRM, MTGR delivered the largest offline and online gains in nearly two years and is deployed handling main traffic on Meituan, the world's largest food-delivery platform.
