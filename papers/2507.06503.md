## Technical note

USD is a unified sampling and dual-debiasing framework for large-scale homepage recommendation, tackling pseudo-negative samples from exposure bias (non-clicks caused by inattention) and pseudo-positive samples (clicks merely to reach a marketing portal). It has a user-intent-aware negative sampling module that filters invalid exposure samples, plus an intent-driven dual-debiasing module that jointly corrects exposure bias and click bias. Online experiments on the Taobao homepage improved user click-through rate (UCTR) by 35.4% and 14.5% on two marketing-block variants (Baiyibutie and Taobaomiaosha).
