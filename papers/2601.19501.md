## Technical note

MDGR (Masked Diffusion Generative Recommendation) reshapes semantic-ID generative recommendation from autoregressive decoding to a masked diffusion (masking-denoising) process, giving order-agnostic, multi-step-refined, parallel generation. It uses a parallel codebook, and during training applies dynamic global curriculum noise scheduling along the temporal dimension plus history-aware masking allocation along the sample dimension to build hard supervision matching heterogeneous user interests; inference uses a warm-up-based two-stage parallel decoding combinable with beam search. MDGR beats ten SOTA baselines (e.g., TIGER, Cobra) by 7.17%-10.78%, and online A/B testing raised advertising revenue by 1.20% and GMV by 3.69%.
