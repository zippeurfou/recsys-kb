## Technical note

SEC (Stratified Expert Cloning) is an imitation-learning framework that optimizes long-term user retention by cloning behavior from high-retention users, avoiding RL's delayed-credit-assignment and sample-inefficiency issues. It combines multi-level expert stratification (segmenting expert users by retention level and learning specialized policies), adaptive expert selection (dynamically matching users to policies by state embedding and retention history), and action entropy regularization via nuclear-norm maximization to boost recommendation diversity and prevent policy collapse. Offline evaluations and online A/B tests on Kuaishou and Kuaishou Lite (hundreds of millions of users) gave cumulative active-days lifts of 0.098% and 0.122%, each translating to over 200,000 additional daily active users.
