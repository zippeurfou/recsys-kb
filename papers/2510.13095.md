## Technical note

R4R (Reason-for-Retrieval) (Alibaba) is a reasoning-augmented framework for generative retrieval that harnesses LLMs' reasoning, not just generation, to produce docids. A preliminary study shows free-form CoT before constrained docid decoding helps but is verbose and poorly aligned with the docid space. R4R converts free-form CoT into a compact structured format and iteratively refines it during retrieval: a single instruction-tuned LLM alternates between constrained decoding to produce candidate docids and updating the structured reasoning from retrieval results, requiring no extra models or training. Experiments on Natural Questions, MS MARCO, and a real-world item-search benchmark validate its effectiveness.
