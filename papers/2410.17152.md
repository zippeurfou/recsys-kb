## Summary

This paper details how Pinterest improves search relevance by incorporating Large Language Models (LLMs). Specifically, it uses text representations from LLMs generating Pin captions alongside other rich data sources like user boards and queries to train its model effectively. The system enhances training data volume and diversity through semi-supervised learning and distills the improved performance into operational models for real-time use.

## Technical note

Describes Pinterest Search's LLM-based relevance system that fine-tunes an LLM as a cross-encoder to predict 5-level query-Pin relevance from enriched text representations (Pin titles/descriptions, VLM-generated image captions, link text, user-curated boards, and high-quality engaged queries). Semi-supervised learning with multilingual LLMs scales training data beyond expensive human labels and extends coverage to unseen languages and domains. The powerful LLM teacher is distilled into a real-time servable student model to meet latency and cost budgets. Offline experiments and online A/B tests validate gains from the enriched features and distillation at production scale.
