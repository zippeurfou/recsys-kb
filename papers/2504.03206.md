## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Effective conversational agents like large language models (LLMs) must personalize their interactions to adapt to user preferences, personalities, and attributes across diverse domains like education and healthcare. Current methods like Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), often prioritize helpfulness and safety but fall short in fostering truly empathetic, adaptive, and personalized dialogues. ## Technical note

CURIO (Google) adds a curiosity-based intrinsic reward to multi-turn RLHF so an LLM agent actively infers user traits by optimizing conversations to improve its user model's accuracy, enabling online personalization without extensive pre-collected user history. It formulates personalized dialogue as a POMDP with belief-state updates and shows the intrinsic reward is a Potential-based Reward Shaping term that preserves the optimal policy while improving sample efficiency, the first application of PBRS to LLM training. On Education Dialogue and Exercise (conversational) Recommendation tasks CURIO outperforms multi-turn RLHF baselines, adapting quickly even to unseen users while maintaining conversation quality.
