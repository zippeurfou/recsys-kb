## Technical note

PROSPER (Alibaba) uses LLMs as sparse retrievers for first-stage product search, tackling vocabulary mismatch while retaining sparse retrieval's interpretability and storage efficiency. It addresses two LLM-specific issues: a literal residual network (LRN) that reinforces underweighted literal terms (brand/model names) to curb lexical-expansion hallucination, and a lexical focusing window (LFW) with FLOPS regularization implementing coarse-to-fine sparsification for stable training in the ultra-high-dimensional LLM vocabulary. On Multi-CPR and Taobao logs it improves target-product recall by 10.2% over BM25 and 4.3% over SPLADE, matching dense retrievers, with a 0.64% online GMV gain.
