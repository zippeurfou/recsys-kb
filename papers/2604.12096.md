## Summary

1.  Online advertising faces a "cold-start" problem where new ads lack sufficient user data, making it difficult to predict their performance (Click-Through Rate - CTR).
2.  The proposed solution, LLM-HYPER, uses large language models (LLMs) as hypernetworks to automatically generate the parameters for a CTR prediction model by analyzing the new ad's text and images, without requiring traditional training.
3.  By leveraging few-shot prompting and CLIP embeddings of similar past campaigns, the LLM infers relevant feature weights for the CTR model, and extensive testing shows LLM-HYPER significantly improves ad performance and drastically shortens the cold-start period, leading to its production deployment.

## Technical note

LLM-HYPER treats an LLM (e.g., Gemini-2.5) as a hypernetwork that directly generates the feature-wise weights of a linear CTR estimator for cold-start ads in a training-free manner, decoupling costly LLM inference from low-latency serving. It retrieves semantically similar warm campaigns via CLIP multimodal (text+image) embeddings using Faiss KNN and formats their content plus trained weights as few-shot chain-of-thought demonstrations. Generated weights are L2-normalized and calibrated via an intercept shift so predicted CTR distributions align with production models. Offline it beats cold-start baselines by 55.9% NDCG@10, serves at 0.14-0.17 ms latency, and was deployed on a top U.S. e-commerce homepage ads ranker.
