## Technical note

TGA (Transition-Aware Graph Attention Network), from Taobao/Alibaba, models multi-behavior sequential recommendation (clicks, favorites, add-to-cart, purchases and the transitions between them) with linear time complexity, avoiding the quadratic cost of transformer-based multi-behavior models on long sequences. Instead of a fully-connected attention graph, TGA builds a structured sparse graph from three views (item-level, category-level, and neighbor-level transitions) and applies a transition-aware graph attention mechanism that jointly models user-item interactions and behavior-transition types, stacking layers for high-order dependencies. TGA outperforms SOTA models at significantly lower cost and is deployed in a large-scale industrial production environment with impressive business-metric gains.
