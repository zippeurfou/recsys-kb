## Summary

1.  Existing task-oriented dialogue benchmarks often oversimplify complex natural language instructions, ignoring intricate constraints crucial for real-world agent performance.
2.  To address this, the paper introduces TOD-ProcBench, a new benchmark featuring complex, multi-level process instructions with fine-grained constraints for evaluating LLMs' instruction-following capabilities in multi-turn conversations.
3.  The benchmark includes three tasks testing relevance, error detection, and conditional response generation, along with studies on multilingual settings and instruction format, and is released under a specific license.

## Technical note

Amazon introduces TOD-ProcBench, a benchmark for complex instruction-following in multi-turn task-oriented dialogues, addressing that existing TOD benchmarks oversimplify instructions into intents, slots, and API configs. Instruction documents derived from the ABCD dataset are formulated as multi-level condition-action statements with intricate, fine-grained constraints, paired with human-quality-controlled conversations. Three tasks evaluate LLMs: retrieving the most relevant instruction statement and predicting the next action, detecting instruction-violating responses synthesized by injecting inconsistencies, and conditional generation of instruction-following responses. Results reveal significant gaps across all tasks, with additional studies on multilingual settings and instruction text formats; released under the Llama 3.3 Community License.
