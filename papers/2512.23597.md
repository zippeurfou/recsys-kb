## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of early pancreatic tumor detection in CT scans, which is difficult due to subtle tumor margins and significant anatomical variations. The proposed solution is the Scalable Residual Feature Aggregation (SRFA) framework, combining multimodal imaging preprocessing, a specialized MAGRes-UNet for segmentation, DenseNet-121 for hierarchical feature extraction, hybrid metaheuristic optimization for feature selection, and a hybrid attention-based Vision Transformer (ViT) with EfficientNet-B3 for classification. Experimental results demonstrate that this SRFA framework significantly outperforms traditional CNNs and transformer models, achieving high accuracy (96.23%), F1-score (95.58%), and specificity (94.83%).

## Technical note

The paper proposes the Scalable Residual Feature Aggregation (SRFA) framework for early pancreatic neoplasm detection in multimodal CT imaging, addressing subtle tumor margins and high inter-patient anatomical variation. The pipeline chains preprocessing, segmentation via a Multi-Attention Gated Residual U-Net (MAGRes-UNet) to isolate regions of interest, and hierarchical feature extraction with DenseNet-121 using residual feature storage. A hybrid Harris Hawks Optimization and Bat Algorithm metaheuristic performs feature selection, and classification uses an attention-based Vision Transformer fused with EfficientNet-B3, with Sparrow Search and Grey Wolf Optimization tuning hyperparameters. It reports 96.32% accuracy, 95.58% F1, and 94.83% specificity, outperforming conventional CNNs and transformer baselines.
