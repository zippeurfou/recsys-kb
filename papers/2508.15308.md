## Technical note

REG4Rec is a reasoning-enhanced generative recommender that overcomes the rigidity of fixed per-item semantic tokens. An MoE-based Parallel Quantization codebook (MPQ) produces multiple unordered semantic tokens per item, creating a large diverse reasoning space; at each step the model generates parallel predictions and selects the highest-confidence token. Reliability comes from Preference Alignment for Reasoning (PARS, RL with recommendation rewards), Multi-Step Reward Augmentation (MSRA) over future actions, and inference-time Consistency-Oriented Self-Reflection for Pruning (CORP), plus a LADQ dynamic-precision controller for efficient training. Gains up to 16.59% offline; Alibaba A/B test yields +5.60% ad revenue, +1.81% CTR, +3.29% GMV.
