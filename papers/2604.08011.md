## Technical note

SSR (Explicit Sparsity for Scalable Recommendation) shows industrial CTR backbones exhibit implicit connection sparsity—92% of weights suppressed near-zero and 80% of weight mass in 4% of dimensions—so dense MLPs waste capacity on sparse recommendation data. It shifts from implicit weight suppression to explicit signal filtering via a multi-view filter-then-fuse mechanism, realized by a zero-FLOP Static Random Filter (SSR-S) and Iterative Competitive Sparse (ICS/SSR-D), a differentiable input-adaptive dynamic filter. On three public datasets and a billion-scale AliExpress dataset, SSR outperforms SOTA baselines under comparable compute and keeps improving where dense models saturate.
