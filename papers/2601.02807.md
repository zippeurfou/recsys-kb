## Summary

This paper introduces the COFFEE framework, designed to enhance user and ad representations in recommendation systems by incorporating richer data sources without increasing operational complexity. The framework considers three dimensions: diverse event sources, longer user histories, and enriched event attributes/multimodal data. By comparing organic and ad-related engagement events, the study demonstrates that COFFEE significantly boosts metrics like AUC and CTR, particularly when using enriched ad impression data, leading to better model scaling and performance.

## Technical note

Studies how to enrich user-ad representations for commercial ads-ranking without increasing inference or serving complexity. It proposes a three-dimensional enrichment framework spanning more diverse event sources, longer user-engagement histories, and richer per-event attributes with multi-modal content embeddings, assembled into event-based feature (EBF) sequences. Comparing organic-usage against ad-impression sources shows ad-impression event sources boost AUC and the scaling-curve slope by 1.56-2x even at short sequence lengths. Enriched ad-impression sources improve CTR-prediction AUC by 0.56% over the production baseline.
