## Summary

This paper introduces the Reinforcement Infused Agentic RAG framework, which combines autonomous agents, large language models, and reinforcement learning (using PPO/DQN) to automatically improve software test case generation from business requirements. It addresses the limitation of conventional static-knowledge-base systems by enabling continuous learning from Quality Engineering (QE) feedback, defect discovery, and execution results. Experimental results on real projects showed significant improvements in test generation accuracy and defect detection rates, establishing a continuous improvement loop driven by expert QE input.

## Technical note

The paper proposes a Reinforcement Infused Agentic RAG framework that combines autonomous agents, LLMs, and reinforcement learning to author software test cases from business requirement documents in Quality Engineering (QE) workflows. It addresses the static-knowledge-base limitation of prior agentic RAG by having agents learn from QE feedback, assessments, and defect-discovery outcomes to refine generation strategies. Agents are optimized with PPO and DQN against signals such as test effectiveness, defect detection rates, and workflow metrics, over a hybrid vector-graph knowledge base storing testing knowledge. On enterprise Apple projects it raised test-generation accuracy from 94.8% to 97.2% and improved defect detection by 10.8%, establishing a continuous refinement loop that augments human testers.
