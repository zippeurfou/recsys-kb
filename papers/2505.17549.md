## Summary

1.  Traditional industrial ad systems are limited by multi-stage cascades, leading to premature candidate rejection and disconnected components, while existing generative models lack critical functionalities needed for real-world deployment like bidding or payment calculation.
2.  This paper introduces EGA-V2 (End-to-End Generative Advertising), the first unified framework that holistically models everything from user interests and POI recommendation/creative generation to ad allocation and payment optimization within a single generative model.
3.  By employing hierarchical tokenization, multi-token prediction, permutation-aware rewards, differentiable ex-post regret minimization for bidding/payment separation, EGA-V2 ensures advertiser objectives are met while maintaining good user experience, significantly outperforming traditional systems in both performance and practicality.

## Technical note

EGA-V2 is an end-to-end generative advertising framework that replaces the traditional multi-stage cascade (recall, pre-rank, rank, creative selection, auction) with a single generative model, avoiding premature candidate pruning. It encodes user behavior and item features into hierarchical semantic tokens with RQ-VAE and uses an encoder-decoder with multi-token prediction to jointly generate POI recommendations and ad creatives. A token-level bidding and generative allocation mechanism with a permutation-aware reward model decouples allocation from payment, using a differentiable ex-post regret minimization network to approximately guarantee incentive compatibility at the POI level. A multi-phase strategy pretrains on exposure sequences then applies auction-based post-training; offline experiments show it outperforms cascaded systems.
