## Summary

Existing generative models for CTR prediction work during training but switch to a simpler discriminative method during inference, missing out on potential accuracy gains. The paper introduces SGCTR, a novel symmetric framework that uses generative capabilities learned during training to iteratively refine input features during online inference itself. This approach effectively tackles noisy features and significantly improves prediction accuracy, demonstrating the power of consistently applying a generative paradigm throughout both phases.

## Technical note

SGCTR (Symmetric Masked Generative Paradigm for CTR prediction) fixes the asymmetry in generative CTR models that use a generative objective only during training but revert to a discriminative pass over noisy inputs at inference. In training it applies a discrete diffusion process that masks feature fields (mask ratio scheduled with time-step) and predicts them with a generative scoring network optimized via sampled softmax, learning feature dependencies. Its core contribution is an inference-time iterative refinement: it fixes stable user-side features, masks item/cross features, and iteratively regenerates them, using cosine similarity to the original as a confidence weight to redefine features and re-mask the lowest-confidence ones, denoising the input before the final prediction. Offline datasets and online A/B tests confirm its effectiveness.
