## Summary

Large-scale ad recommendation systems require efficient retrieval, but current dual-tower network models limit feature interaction between user and ad data. This paper proposes an efficient solution using GPU acceleration and compressed inverted lists to enable early feature interaction within the dual-tower structure, significantly improving retrieval accuracy while drastically reducing computational costs. The method, successfully deployed at Tencent, demonstrates superior offline performance and significant online business gains, offering a practical new approach for large-scale ad retrieval systems.

## Technical note

This paper adds early user-ad feature interaction to the dual-tower embedding-based retrieval (EBR) model used for large-scale ad retrieval, where standard two-tower models only interact via a final inner product. Implicit interactions are added by concatenating IPNN-style expressions into the tower embeddings (keeping it dual-tower), while explicit Wide & Deep interactions are computed via a novel GPU-friendly compressed inverted list that exploits the binary feature-interaction matrix. Trained with a LambdaRank pairwise-logistic + delta-NDCG loss, it is claimed to be the first industrial Wide & Deep in retrieval, cutting query latency up to 7x versus cuSPARSE. Deployed at Tencent Advertising with offline gains and online business improvements.
