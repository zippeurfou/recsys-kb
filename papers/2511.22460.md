## Technical note

A Tencent Advertising retrieval method overcomes the dual-tower EBR limitation where user and ad embeddings only interact at the final inner product. It brings early-stage feature interaction into the retrieval stage via a GPU-accelerated operator, introducing a novel compressed inverted list for efficient feature-interaction computation at scale, effectively implementing the Wide & Deep architecture in retrieval (claimed as the industry's first). It investigates IPNN (implicit interactions) and Wide & Deep (explicit) for retrieval, improves retrieval accuracy while reducing compute, and delivers significant online gains after deployment in Tencent's advertising system.
