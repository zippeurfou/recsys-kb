## Technical note

SAGE (Sequence-level Adaptive Gradient Evolution) is a unified RL preference optimizer for list-wise generative recommendation that fixes failure modes of Gradient-Bounded Policy Optimization (GBPO): Symmetric Conservatism suppressing rare positive/cold-start signals, static negative constraints causing diversity collapse, and low-resolution group-normalized multi-objective rewards. It introduces a geometric-mean importance ratio and a decoupled multi-objective advantage estimator to cut token-level variance and reward collapse, plus asymmetric adaptive bounding with a positive Boost factor for high-potential cold-start items and an entropy-aware penalty against low-diversity failures. On Amazon Product Reviews and RecIF-Bench it improves top-K accuracy, cold-start recall, and diversity (e.g. cold-start recall gains up to ~+90-101%) across Semantic-ID and native-text action spaces while staying numerically stable.
