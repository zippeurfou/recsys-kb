## Summary

TacticAI is an AI system developed in collaboration with Liverpool FC experts specifically to analyze corner kicks and help coaches devise effective tactical responses against rivals.

It combines predictive capabilities (e.g., identifying receivers) with generative tools, allowing users to explore alternative player setups for corners by sampling likely outcomes and adjusting positions based on predicted success likelihoods.

Despite data limitations, TacticAI's suggestions were validated through user studies as indistinguishable from real tactics 90% of the time compared to existing methods, proving its effectiveness using geometric deep learning.

## Technical note

TacticAI is an AI football tactics assistant, developed with Liverpool FC, that analyzes corner kicks by representing each situation as a fully-connected graph of players (nodes = players with position/velocity/height/weight features, edges = same/opposing team) and applying graph neural networks with message passing. It uses geometric deep learning to bake in the pitch's reflection symmetries, gaining data efficiency given scarce gold-standard tracking data (~7,176 Premier League corners). The system has three components: receiver prediction (node classification), shot prediction (graph classification), and tactic recommendation via guided generation (node regression suggesting player position/velocity adjustments), plus a corner-kick retrieval system over player representations. In a qualitative study, expert coaches favored TacticAI's suggestions over real tactics 90% of the time and found them indistinguishable from genuine tactics.
