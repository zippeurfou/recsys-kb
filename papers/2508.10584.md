However, their lack of collaborative signals results in a misalignment with downstream discriminative and generative recommendation objectives. ## Technical note

Kuaishou's DAS learns Dual-Aligned Semantic IDs for advertising by jointly optimizing quantization and CF alignment in one stage, avoiding the information loss of two-stage (LETTER, QARM) pipelines. It combines a User/Item Semantic Model (RQ-VAE with k-means codebook init), an ID-based CF Debias Model that disentangles popularity/conformity bias, and a Multi-view Dual-Aligned Mechanism doing multi-view contrastive alignment (dual u2i, i2i/u2u, co-occurrence) to maximize mutual information between SIDs and CF signals. Deployed across Kuaishou advertising scenarios serving 400M+ daily users, DAS shows significant A/B gains with larger improvements in cold-start situations.
