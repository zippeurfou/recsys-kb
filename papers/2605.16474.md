## Technical note

LERA is an LLM-enhanced RAG framework for inserting ads into generative chatbots, building on the retrieve-then-generate paradigm that decouples ad retrieval from generation for lightweight ad insertion and payment determination. It improves on prior retrieve-then-generate ad-auction methods by strengthening retrieval quality and integrating auction mechanisms while balancing relevance, efficiency, and user experience in LLM-based chatbot advertising. The work targets commercialization of LLM chatbots through better-aligned ad retrieval and auction design.
