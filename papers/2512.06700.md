## Technical note

This paper enhances live-streaming recommendation with foresight prediction to handle the real-time content and time dynamics that distinguish live streams from short videos or TV. It discretizes live-streaming content into semantic IDs via quantization, encodes the historical segment sequence for overall understanding, and decodes to predict future content, improving the model's perception of content changes. Offline ranking experiments show AUC/GAUC gains (e.g. foresight prediction adds up to +0.65% GAUC on some slots), and online A/B testing on a live-streaming platform confirms commercial benefits.
