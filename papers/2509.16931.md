## Technical note

Industrial pre-ranking must be cheap, so it relies on vector-product two-tower models that cannot capture the fine-grained interactions of Target Attention (TA) used in ranking. TARQ approximates TA at pre-ranking latency by using multi-level Residual Quantization: item embeddings are quantized into semantic-ID codebooks, personalized per user via attention over the user's history, and only 128 codebook vectors are processed per request regardless of candidate count. A teacher-student setup distills a full offline TA net into the online RQ-Attention student, and a KL-based Codebook Alignment objective raises codebook utilization from 59% to 98% while mitigating collapse. On Taobao data (14.2B interactions) TARQ reaches 0.799 AUC vs 0.785 for the two-tower baseline, and an online A/B test showed +0.57% CTR, +4.59% CVR, and +7.57% GMV, leading to full production deployment.
