## Summary

Current recommendation systems using Large Language Models (LLMs) often rely on passive information processing, leading to suboptimal recommendations due to insufficient or fragmented data. To address this, the paper introduces RecThinker, an agentic framework that shifts towards active investigation by dynamically planning reasoning paths and proactively using specialized tools to fill information gaps. RecThinker employs an Analyze-Plan-Act paradigm, supported by a self-augmented training pipeline, and was shown through extensive experiments to significantly outperform existing methods.

## Technical note

RecThinker is an agentic recommendation framework that shifts LLM agents from passive information processing to active investigation via tool-augmented reasoning. It follows an Analyze-Plan-Act workflow: first assess sufficiency of available user/item information, then plan and execute tool-calling sequences to bridge information gaps before recommending. The authors position it in the Agent-as-Investigator paradigm (versus Agent-as-Simulator and Agent-as-Assistant) and build specialized tools for user-side, item-side, and collaborative information acquisition to support deeper reasoning and user-item matching. A self-augmented two-stage training pipeline first uses supervised fine-tuning on high-quality reasoning trajectories, then reinforcement learning to optimize decision accuracy and tool-use efficiency (reducing redundant tool calls). Experiments across multiple benchmark datasets show consistent improvement over strong baselines.
