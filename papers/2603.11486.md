## Technical note

This work applies low-precision quantized inference to generative recommendation, specifically OneRec-V2, showing that unlike traditional recommenders (high-magnitude, high-variance weights/activations, low hardware utilization) OneRec-V2's weight and activation statistics are more controlled and closer to LLMs, with a more compute-intensive inference pattern. Leveraging this, the authors build an FP8 post-training quantization framework integrated with optimized inference infrastructure, achieving a 49% reduction in end-to-end inference latency and a 92% increase in throughput. Online A/B tests confirm FP8 inference introduces no degradation in core metrics, arguing LLM-domain quantization techniques transfer to LLM-like generative recommenders.
