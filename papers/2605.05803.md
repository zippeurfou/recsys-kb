## Technical note

UniVA is a unified value-alignment framework for generative recommendation in Tencent advertising, addressing 'value inconsistency' where commercial signals are modeled fragmentedly across tokenization, decoding, and serving. It introduces a Commercial SID tokenizer that injects value attributes (via classify-then-bin and bid discretization) into the final SID level while upper levels preserve RQ-based semantic locality, a dual-head Generation-as-Ranking SID Decoder trained with supervised learning plus eCPM-aware reinforcement learning to fuse value into next-token generation, and a value-guided personalized beam search using a per-request trie for valid paths. On WeChat Channels it achieves 37.04% relative offline HR@100 improvement and a 1.5% GMV lift online.
