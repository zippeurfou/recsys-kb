Generative models have shown great promise in collaborative filtering by capturing the underlying distribution of user interests and preferences. However, existing approaches struggle with inaccurate posterior approximations and misalignment with the discrete nature of recommendation data, limiting their expressiveness and real-world performance. ## Technical note

FlowCF applies flow matching to generative collaborative filtering, addressing VAE posterior-approximation errors and diffusion models' slow multi-step sampling and continuous-Gaussian mismatch with discrete interactions. It introduces a behavior-guided prior aligned with user behavior patterns for sparse/heterogeneous interactions and a discrete flow framework preserving binary implicit feedback, learning deterministic straight flow trajectories for stable training and few-step inference. Across datasets FlowCF achieves state-of-the-art accuracy with the fastest inference among generative CF methods (VAE, DiffRec).
