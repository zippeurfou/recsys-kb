## Technical note

LiDDA is LinkedIn's industry-deployed data-driven attribution system that uses a single-layer transformer self-attention over sequences of (channel, action) marketing touchpoints to assign conversion credit, framed as binary conversion classification. It adds temporal-aware positional embeddings (discretized day-gap and day-of-week embeddings plus tAPE) and probabilistically imputes missing paid-media impressions to handle GDPR/CCPA gaps. DDA credits are reconciled with an aggregate media-mix model (MMM) via training-time calibration to unify member-level and channel-level attribution. Attention weights are used directly as percent-contribution credits and shown to closely match incremental-propensity estimates.
