## Summary

Standard generative recommendation models primarily use fast direct prediction, which is efficient but may not provide sufficiently detailed scoring needed for ranking tasks. This paper introduces "Refiner," a novel latent residual reasoning framework designed to improve ranking quality by adding cumulative scoring adjustments and user history queries to existing generative models, achieved through parameter-efficient fine-tuning. Experiments on various benchmarks show that Refiner consistently outperforms direct prediction, offering a more effective way to enhance recommendation ranking, especially in industrial settings.

## Technical note

Generative rankers such as MTGR score all candidates in one forward pass (a candidate mask stops candidates attending to each other) but use one-shot direct prediction, leaving open whether extra inference-time computation can refine fine-grained scores. Refiner (Meituan) adds latent reasoning on top of a frozen deployed ranker: each reasoning step appends K candidate-specific reasoning tokens (the previous step's candidate hidden states) plus N_HQ history-query tokens taken from the user-history positions, runs the LoRA-augmented Transformer under an extended leakage-free mask, and a new reasoning head predicts additive residual corrections to the base scores, trained with MSE on the cumulative score at every step; only LoRA and the head are trained. On Steam and MovieLens10M MTGR+Refiner lifts AUC/GAUC by 2.3-4.3% and beats the adapted ReaRec latent-reasoning baseline; ablations show history queries, residual (vs per-step BCE) supervision and the frozen base all matter and end-to-end training is worst, with the largest gains on long-history users and the least popular items. On a Meituan industrial dataset (70M users, 2B interactions) it adds +1.8 permille CTR AUC and +2.5 permille CTCVR AUC, and a 7-day 1% online A/B on the 15-layer production MTGR with one reasoning step and a daily incremental update (reload frozen MTGR, warm-start Refiner parameters) gave PV-CTR +0.75%, UV-CTCVR +0.21% and GMV +0.46%. One or two steps suffice since residual magnitudes shrink with depth.
