## Technical note

PEARL (Google) is the first large-scale four-arm RCT (13,463 Fitbit users) testing a reinforcement-learning algorithm, informed by health behavior-change theory, that personalizes the content and timing of physical-activity nudges via a Fitbit app. Arms were control, random, fixed, and RL, drawing from a bank of 155 behaviorally-designed nudges. The RL group significantly increased average daily step count at 1 month versus control (+296 steps, p=0.0002), random (+218), and fixed (+238), with a sustained +210 steps versus control at 2 months, demonstrating scalable RL personalization for digital health.
