## Technical note

Tackles session-level dynamic ad load optimization (balancing ad monetization vs user engagement in real time) where causal meta-learners suffer confounding bias and distribution shift. The authors formulate it as a robust MDP and propose an offline DQN that mitigates confounding by encoding previous ad-load actions in the state, then an offline robust dueling DQN using an IPM uncertainty set that reduces to a cheap weight-norm regularizer. Offline DQN (gamma=0.8) lifts test AUCC by 80%+ over a T-learner baseline (1.14 vs 0.60); robust dueling DQN adds ~5% and ~25% over non-robust dueling DQN and T-learner on shifted data. Deployed at Meta via a CART student model distilled from the RL teacher, online A/B tests showed double-digit engagement-ad score trade-off efficiency gains.
