## Technical note

Criteo closes the gap between generative recommenders and well-tuned ID-based baselines like SASRec by fixing two limitations: non-collaborative item tokenization and inefficient encoder-decoder architectures. COSETTE is a contrastive tokenization method that injects collaborative signals directly into RQ-VAE quantization via a latent contrastive loss (no pretrained SASRec needed), and MARIUS is a lightweight RQ-Transformer-inspired (audio modeling) generative model that decouples timeline modeling from item decoding for KV-caching and cheaper top-K generation. On standard sequential recommendation benchmarks the approach narrows or eliminates the generative-vs-ID performance gap while retaining generative-paradigm benefits.
