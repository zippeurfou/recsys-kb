## Summary

This paper introduces HiGenTrans, a framework for enhancing generative recommendation systems by leveraging large language models (LLMs). It tackles two main issues: insufficient use of semantic knowledge from LLMs in current methods (which rely solely on user interactions) and the beam search method's indirect ranking of item sequences. HiGenTrans proposes three complementary stages to integrate LLM knowledge effectively, improving recommendation metrics while ensuring the use of only observable interaction data to prevent leakage. Experiments show it significantly boosts recall and NDCG compared to existing baselines.

## Technical note

Kuaishou. Problem: SID-based generative recommenders learn only from interaction supervision, so pretrained-LLM semantics enter at most once through a prefix or BOS embedding, and beam-search likelihood ranks token sequences rather than item relevance, so relevant items sit in the beam at poor ranks. Method: HiGenTrans transfers frozen Qwen3-Embedding-8B knowledge at three levels on top of a GRLM backbone with 3x8192 residual-KMeans SIDs: Global Semantic Priming projects an LLM representation of the observable history prefix into the BOS embedding; Hierarchical Representation Transfer mean-centers, projects and injects intermediate LLM-layer representations into depth-matched GenRec layers through learned residual gates; Multi-signal Decision Calibration trains, with the generator frozen, a small mixture-of-experts scorer (BCE on beam candidates) over LLM semantic similarity, sequence likelihood / beam rank / token-confidence statistics and within-beam standardized context features to rerank and truncate the beam (it cannot recover items the beam missed). All features are prefix-only to avoid target leakage and, for deployment, the multi-layer LLM features are vector-quantized into SIDs with shared codebooks so online inference needs only codebook lookups and no LLM call. Results on Amazon Beauty / Sports / Toys (leave-one-out, up to 50 recent items): best on every metric against GRU4Rec, SASRec, HSTU, BERT4Rec, TIGER, RPG and GRLM, Toys R@10 0.1047 / N@10 0.0674 vs 0.0842 / 0.0504 for GRLM (+12.9% / +33.7%), Beauty R@10 +2.7%; MDC is the strongest single module (Toys R@10 0.0803 -> 0.1010) and promotes 43-55% of ground-truth items ranked 11-20 into the top 10, deeper LLM layers give higher candidate-ranking AUC until saturation, gains are largest for items with 3-5 interactions (+30% Hit@10), and in two industrial cross-domain scenarios SID level-1 accuracy rises +48 and +62 points.
