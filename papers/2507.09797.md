## Technical note

LinkedIn's STAR (Signal integration for Talent And Recruiters) system tackles job-matching challenges - cold-start, filter bubbles, and candidate-job matching biases - by combining LLMs and GNNs. LLMs supply semantic understanding of jobs and members while GNNs propagate collaborative and relational signals across the candidate-job graph, integrating multiple signals into a scalable recommendation system. The paper details the modeling and system design for large-scale, low-latency job matching on the LinkedIn platform.
