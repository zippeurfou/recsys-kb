## Technical note

This Netflix economics study builds a discrete-choice model embedding recommendation-induced utility, low-rank user/good heterogeneity, and flexible state dependence, applied to Netflix viewership to separate the value of recommendations from underlying demand for goods. Recommendation-induced engagement is identified from observational variation in algorithmic exposure (treated as conditionally exogenous), and model-free diversion ratios from a randomized experiment validate the structural model. Counterfactuals show replacing the current recommender with matrix factorization or popularity-based algorithms would reduce engagement by 4% and 12% respectively and lower consumption diversity; most of the recommendation gain comes from effective targeting (not mechanical exposure), largest for mid-popularity goods.
