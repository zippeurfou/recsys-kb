## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of transforming casual selfies into professional-looking portraits by synthesizing them in chosen poses, standard clothing, and ideal lighting, while preserving the person's unique identity. To overcome the lack of paired datasets needed for direct training, the authors introduce a method using canonical UV maps, which represent the person's body geometry, allowing transformation leveraging existing unpaired data. Furthermore, they enhance the realism and personalization of the generated images through multi-image fine-tuning, resulting in high-quality synthesized portraits.

## Technical note

Pro-Pose canonicalizes an in-the-wild selfie into a controllable, reposed full-body avatar in standardized clothing and lighting while preserving whole-body identity, without any paired training data. It operates in canonical UV texture space to decouple pose from appearance and introduces Donor-based UV Reposing, which applies occlusion masks from unrelated donor images to break the correlation between texture boundaries and source pose, preventing pose leakage and enabling joint training on 30K paired and ~470K unpaired images. The generator is a Latent Rectified Flow (Flow Matching) model on a Flux.1 DiT backbone, with test-time LoRA few-shot personalization; it achieves state-of-the-art identity-faithful synthesis and improves downstream virtual try-on.
