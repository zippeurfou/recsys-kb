## Summary

This paper introduces EvoReason as an improved framework for generative recommendation that uses latent reasoning for efficiency. It addresses the limitations of standard CoT distillation by identifying that existing methods struggle with diverse and unstable reasoning paths found in recommendations. The key innovation is a self-evolving on-policy distillation approach guided by essential "reasoning primitives" extracted from high-quality trajectories, enabling more effective transfer of structured reasoning into compact latent representations through continuous refinement based on student model outcomes.

## Technical note

Latent reasoning removes the inference-latency cost of explicit chain-of-thought in generative recommenders, but distilling raw CoT into latent tokens entangles reusable reasoning behavior with instance-specific phrasing, and the teacher supervision stays static while the student policy keeps changing. EvoReason runs three stages on an OneRec-Think-SFT'd Qwen3-1.7B backbone with 4-level, 256-token-per-level semantic IDs: (1) induce a library of reusable reasoning primitives from ReAct-style agentic trajectories generated by QwQ-32B; (2) SFT a primitive-aware teacher that emits structured CoT by invoking those primitives as pseudo-tools; (3) self-evolving on-policy distillation where the student iteratively refines M latent slots, is optimized by GRPO with a token-level SID-match reward and group-relative advantage, while a stop-gradient teacher branch sharing the same backbone (given extra refinement context, so no new parameters) supplies a confidence-gated OPD loss g_t = sigmoid(beta*(logp_T - logp_S)) plus an L1 latent-KV alignment loss against R-KV-compressed teacher KV; total loss L_RL + lambda_OPD*L_OPD + lambda_KV*L_KV, with the primitive library re-induced from student outcomes each round. On Amazon Beauty/Sports and an industrial dataset it beats the strongest latent-reasoning baseline LASAR by +17.9% R@5 and +27.4% N@5 on Beauty (0.0724 R@5 / 0.0557 N@5 vs 0.0613 / 0.0437) and +15.4% R@5 industrially, ahead of TIGER, HSTU, ReaRec, OneRec-Think, FLR and LatentR3. Ablations on Beauty rank component importance as primitive library (R@5 drops 0.0724 -> 0.0636) > self-evolution (0.0675) > OPD vs plain KD (0.0689); gains are largest for inactive/cold-start users, and a one-week 20%-traffic A/B replacing LASAR in a production ad recall stage yields ADVV +8.11%, revenue +6.23%, show ratio +4.56%, avg watch time +5.39%.
