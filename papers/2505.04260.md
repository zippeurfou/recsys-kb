## Technical note

This Google paper personalizes LLM chatbots to users' soft preferences via activation steering—adding pre-computed contrastive steering vectors to the residual stream at inference—which is lightweight, needs no retraining or long user history (works cold-start), and is controllable by a linear strength factor, unlike memory-based personalization. Computational experiments across five open-source LLMs show steering induces preference expressions (e.g., cost, ambiance, culture) at varying intensities, composes with prompting and multiple preferences, and can learn hidden preferences in simulated conversations. A within-subjects user study (n=14) across three chatbot interfaces confirms steered chatbots align better with users' true latent preferences than a prompting-only baseline.
