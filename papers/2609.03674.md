## Summary

1.  Traditional AI-generated guidance queries (AIGQ) for e-commerce suffer from semantic drift and poorly capture user multi-interests, primarily relying on "user-item" co-occurrence relationships for derivation.
2.  To overcome these limitations, the paper introduces LLM4AIGQ, a framework using Large Language Models (LLMs) trained with SFT, RL, and DPO, along with a multi-level reward system, to generate more relevant AIGQs by directly segmenting user interests from their profiles and interaction history.
3.  LLM4AIGQ first infers specific consumption intents for each user sub-interest, then generates guidance queries tailored to them, demonstrating strong performance in both offline and online A/B tests.

## Technical note

LLM4AIGQ generates AI guidance queries (AIGQ) for Taobao and Tmall shopping-agent entry points on homepage recommendation, search and item detail pages, replacing a two-stage Q2AIGQ pipeline that first retrieves a primary search query and then generalizes it by rules, which caused cascading information loss and semantic drift. The backbone is Qwen3-30B-A3B post-trained in three stages: SFT, GRPO reinforcement learning, then DPO. Inputs are a user profile plus the most recent 50 behaviors (like, favorite, add-to-cart, purchase, search); item titles are compressed into LLM-generated short titles fine-tuned against DeepSeek-V4-Pro labels, cutting input tokens 26.8% and end-to-end response time 10.7% (3.18s to 2.84s). SFT teaches AIGQ style on single-interest subsequences produced by a teacher-model decomposition (11,259 instances from 5,000 raw records); RL makes the model segment up to three interests itself under a four-granularity reward: think-level (optimal ordered matching of predicted versus reference interest subsequences, action times logic score, plus three-step format), query-level (at most 15 Chinese characters plus an S/A/B judge model for shopping-guidance value), interest-level (confidence-weighted semantic similarity to the reference query, type-token-ratio style diversity, count format) and global-level (1 minus mean pairwise similarity across interest clusters). DPO then distills think-mode answers into non-think mode, dropping the top 30% most semantically similar preference pairs to avoid ineffective training. On 1,000 Taobao test samples the full single-interest SFT+RL+DPO variant reaches Recall@10 0.2101, NDCG@10 0.2377 and S-ratio 0.8616, versus 0.1513/0.1754/0.6405 for zero-shot Qwen3-30B-A3B and 0.1363/0.1582/0.8152 for DeepSeek-V4-Pro; Gemini-3.5-Flash pairwise judging puts the RL checkpoint at a 57.53% win rate over the teacher (DPO 54.95%). Serving is nearline generation triggered after N accumulated interactions, writing into a user_id to guidance-query mapping table read online by vector retrieval. Online A/B on Tmall gave +4.46% uCTR at 10% traffic and a sustained +2.53% uCTR over six days at 40% traffic. Caveat: the relevance metric is proxy semantic similarity against 10 masked future items plus 500 random negatives, not real engagement labels.
