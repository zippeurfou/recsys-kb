## Summary

Current generative recommendation systems focus solely on semantic relevance, ignoring monetization via advertising. This paper introduces GEM-Rec, a novel framework that integrates commercial relevance and ad bidding directly into the generative sequence model. GEM-Rec uses control tokens and a Bid-Aware Decoding mechanism during generation to prioritize higher-value items based on bids, ensuring ad allocation monotonicity without retraining, and allows platforms to dynamically balance relevance and revenue.

## Technical note

GEM-Rec extends the TIGER semantic-ID generative recommender to jointly serve organic and sponsored items within one autoregressive sequence. It augments the semantic vocabulary with <ORG>/<AD> control tokens, factorizing the slot decision P(f|H) (ad satisfaction modeling) from mode-conditional item retrieval P(c|f,H) over RQ-VAE codes. A Bid-Aware Decoding mechanism injects live auction bids into logits at both slot level (boosting by log(1+b_max)) and item level (via prefix-aggregated max-bid lookup on hierarchical SIDs), steering generation toward high-value ads at inference without retraining. The authors prove allocative monotonicity (higher bids weakly increase exposure) and organic integrity, using a first-price payment rule and beam search over the modulated decoder.
