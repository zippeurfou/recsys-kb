## Summary

This paper introduces HEALTHDIAL, a large-scale dataset designed for developing multilingual spoken dialogue systems, specifically addressing the challenge of creating such datasets at scale. HEALTHDIAL includes 6,000 dialogues across four WHO languages (Arabic, Chinese, English, Spanish), featuring real user speech, demographic, and sociolinguistic annotations, all grounded in WHO information. The dataset reveals significant performance differences across languages and is released along with tools to aid future research in multilingual, knowledge-grounded dialogue.

## Technical note

HealthDial is a large-scale multilingual, multi-parallel spoken dialogue dataset for RAG-based dialogue systems, with 6,000 information-seeking dialogues (1,500 per language) across Arabic, Chinese, English, and Spanish grounded in WHO health snippets, plus 163 hours of native-speaker speech with demographic and sociolinguistic annotations. Construction uses a bottom-up outline-based pipeline: WHO knowledge-base crawling, a pilot study yielding an 11-act dialogue schema, first-order Markov-chain sampling of dialogue schemata to prompt gpt-4o for hypothetical English dialogues (with 10% Out-of-Knowledge turns verified by BM25 retrieval), and native-speaker surface realisation from improvisational prompts to avoid translationese. Benchmarks over ASR, TTS, knowledge retrieval, and knowledge filtering reveal consistent cross-language performance disparities even among high-resource languages.
