## Technical note

This paper proposes a curiosity-driven LLM-as-a-judge that personalizes creativity evaluation to individual annotators on the TTCW (Torrance Test of Creative Thinking) benchmark. It trains an Intrinsic Curiosity Model that measures the judge's 'surprise' (belief shift) at an expert's explanation while predicting which annotator wrote it, then appends the resulting curiosity score as an auxiliary self-supervised signal to a supervised fine-tuning classifier. Across model sizes and 5-fold cross-validation, adding the curiosity signal significantly improves Pearson correlation, Cohen's kappa, and F1 over the SFT baseline, particularly useful when annotators disagree.
