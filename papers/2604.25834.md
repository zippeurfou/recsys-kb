## Technical note

A2Gen (Action-Aware Generative Sequence Network) models short-video preferences at segment level by chaining user actions (Like, Follow, Comment, etc.) with their timing into sequences rather than treating a video as one binary label. It combines a Context-aware Attention Module (CAM) for item-context-enriched action sequences, a Hierarchical Sequence Encoder (HSE) for historical action patterns, and an Action-seq Autoregressive Generator (AAG) that jointly predicts action occurrence and timing. Statistical analysis motivated the design (e.g. Follow-Like sequences show 1.28x longer watch time). Deployed at Kuaishou, online A/B tests gave +0.34% watch time, +8.1% interaction rate, and +0.162% LifeTime-7 retention.
