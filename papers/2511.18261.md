## Technical note

A Netflix study develops LLM reasoning strategies for cold-start item recommendation, where sparse interactions make classic collaborative filtering underperform. It systematically evaluates supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement-learning-based fine-tuning, and hybrid approaches on a re-ranking task (top-40 production titles plus sampled cold-start titles forming 50 candidates), using the LLM's world knowledge and reasoning to infer preferences for newly launched items. Reasoning-based fine-tuned models outperform Netflix's production ranking model by up to 8% in certain cases, with cold-start items defined to postdate both training interactions and the base LLM's knowledge cutoff.
