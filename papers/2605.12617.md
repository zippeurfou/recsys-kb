## Summary

This paper addresses the high inference latency of beam-search decoding in generative recommendation models using Semantic IDs (SIDs). The authors argue that standard attention-heavy Transformers are inefficient for SIDs, as later tokens have little prediction difficulty after the first. They propose SID-MLP, a lightweight MLP-centric distillation framework that replaces the autoregressive decoder, capturing the global context in a single step and eliminating dense attention overhead, thereby matching teacher model accuracy while accelerating inference by 8.74x. They further extend this approach with SID-MLP++ to replace the entire Transformer encoder for additional latency reduction.

## Technical note

Sid-Mlp accelerates generative recommendation over Semantic IDs by observing that SID prediction difficulty drops sharply after the first token, making attention-heavy autoregressive decoders structurally redundant. It retains a lightweight one-layer attention module for a user-aware summary, then distills the heavy autoregressive T5-style teacher into position-specific MLP heads whose inputs concatenate previously generated prefix-token embeddings. This preserves prefix and context dependencies while eliminating dense attention overhead, matching teacher accuracy (e.g. TIGER) with an average 8.74x inference speedup as a plug-and-play accelerator across backbones and tokenizers. Sid-Mlp++ further distills the encoder side, reaching 10.25x speedup with competitive accuracy.
