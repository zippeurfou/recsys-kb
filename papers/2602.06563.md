## Summary

1.  The paper introduces TokenMixer-Large, a significantly scaled-up version of the TokenMixer architecture for recommendation systems, addressing design limitations in its predecessor.
2.  TokenMixer-Large incorporates specific improvements like a mixing-and-reverting operation, inter-layer residuals, auxiliary loss, and a Sparse-Pertoken MoE structure, allowing it to reach 7B and 15B parameters effectively.
3.  This large model, currently deployed by ByteDance, achieves substantial performance improvements both offline and online, demonstrating the successful scaling of the TokenMixer architecture.

## Technical note

TokenMixer-Large scales the TokenMixer ranking architecture (from RankMixer) for extreme-scale industrial recommendation, fixing predecessor bottlenecks: sub-optimal residual paths, vanishing gradients in deep models, incomplete MoE sparsification, and limited scalability. It introduces a symmetric mixing-and-reverting operation for dimension-consistent stable residuals, interval residuals plus an auxiliary loss and small down-matrix initialization for deep-model convergence, and a Sparse-Pertoken MoE with a unified 'Sparse Train, Sparse Infer' paradigm (FP8, token parallel). It scales to 7B (online) and 15B (offline) parameters and, deployed across ByteDance scenarios, delivers +1.66% orders and +2.98% GMV in e-commerce, +2.0% ADSS in ads, and +1.4% live-streaming revenue.
