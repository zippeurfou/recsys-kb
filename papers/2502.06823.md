## Technical note

CAIG generates advertising images with Multimodal LLMs optimized directly for Click-Through Rate rather than only aesthetics. It builds targeted pre-training tasks on a large e-commerce multimodal dataset, then fine-tunes via reinforcement learning with a novel multimodal reward model that reflects user click preferences, plus a product-centric preference-optimization strategy ensuring generated backgrounds match product characteristics. Using Pair Accuracy for the reward model and both online and offline metrics, CAIG achieves state-of-the-art CTR-driven advertising image generation on commercial and public datasets.
