## Technical note

RAD proposes relative-advantage debiasing for watch-time in short-video recommendation, correcting confounders beyond duration (popularity, individual viewing habits) that distort watch time as a satisfaction proxy. It maps each observed watch time to its empirical quantile within reference distributions conditioned on video ID and on user ID, yielding a uniform bias-corrected preference signal, and fuses user- and item-side signals via Bayesian evidence fusion. A two-stage architecture decouples distribution estimation from preference learning, and distributional embeddings parameterize watch-time quantiles without online sampling or historical storage, avoiding the single-observation problem of CQE/AlignPxtr. Offline and online experiments show accuracy and robustness gains.
