## Technical note

FAIR is the first generative-recommendation framework with focused attention, addressing attention noise that arises when items are tokenized into long (L>>4) semantic-ID code sequences. Its focused attention mechanism learns two separate Q/K weight sets and subtracts their attention scores (like a differential amplifier) to suppress irrelevant context; a noise-robustness objective enforces consistency under stochastic perturbations; and a mutual-information-maximization objective keeps context most informative for next-item prediction. Using non-autoregressive multi-token prediction, FAIR outperforms both generative and traditional baselines across four public benchmarks.
