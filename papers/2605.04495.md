## Summary

Traditional RAG systems often use relevance-based reranking, which doesn't optimally select documents that best help the AI generator. CAR (Confidence-Aware Reranking) addresses this by using changes in the generator's confidence, estimated via multiple answer samples, to determine document usefulness and rank them accordingly. Experiments show CAR consistently improves downstream retrieval performance and strongly correlates with improved generation quality across different RAG setups.

## Technical note

CAR (Confidence-Aware Reranking) is a training-free, plug-and-play RAG reranker that reorders documents by generation usefulness rather than query-document relevance, using generator confidence change as the signal. Confidence is estimated from the semantic consistency of multiple sampled answers under query-only versus query-document conditions, with a query-level gate skipping intervention on already-confident queries. Documents that raise confidence are promoted and those that lower it demoted, via order-preserving binning that respects the baseline ranking as a prior. On four BEIR datasets it improves NDCG@5 across sparse/dense retrievers and rerankers, with ranking gains strongly correlated (Spearman rho=0.964) to downstream generation F1.
