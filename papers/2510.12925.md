## Technical note

This paper presents the first systematic evaluation of LLM robustness to 'inquiry personas' -- first-person user attributes (identity, expertise, credulity, reading level) prepended to factual questions whose ground-truth answers should be invariant to the asker. Prepending plausible persona cues via an 'I am <persona>, <question>' template meaningfully alters QA accuracy on datasets like SimpleQA and triggers failure modes such as refusals, hallucinated limitations, and role confusion. The authors propose inquiry-persona testing as a push-button robustness tool and analyze how asker characteristics affect both correctness and stereotyping risk.
