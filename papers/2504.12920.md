## Technical note

CSMF (Cascaded Selective Mask Fine-Tuning) is a multi-objective embedding-based retrieval method for e-commerce that, unlike MoE-based two-tower models, adds no network parameters or ANN vector-dimension overhead. It exploits the cascaded exposure->click->conversion hierarchy by pre-training a backbone on exposure data then sequentially fine-tuning on click then conversion data, using cumulative-percentile pruning (CPP) to selectively mask/free parameters per objective plus a cross-stage adaptive margin loss (AML) to reduce negative transfer. Trained with softmax contrastive loss over in-batch negatives, CSMF supports flexible linear fusion of objective weights and shows superior offline and online (Tencent advertising) performance versus MoE-EBR baselines.
