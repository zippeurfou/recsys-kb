## Summary

This paper introduces a Riemannian geometric framework for low-rank optimal transport (OT) to overcome the limitations of existing first-order solvers. It models positive factorizations as smooth submanifolds and develops efficient second-order optimization techniques specific to these manifolds, including cost-agnostic algorithms covering various OT problems.

The key advantages are faster convergence without regularization or careful hyperparameter tuning for both balanced and unbalanced transport scenarios, achieved through a combination of conjugate-gradient updates and closed-form operations. This approach provides a rank-sufficiency certificate for verifying optimality conditions.

Overall, extensive experiments show that this geometric solver significantly outperforms previous state-of-the-art low-rank OT methods across different problem sizes.

## Technical note

This paper proposes a unified Riemannian geometric framework for low-rank optimal transport that avoids the first-order mirror-descent updates and hyperparameter tuning of solvers like LOT, LR-GW, and UB-LOT. It shows the set of rank-r positive factored couplings forms a smooth embedded submanifold of the positive orthant for both balanced and unbalanced regimes, and equips it with the Fisher-Rao product metric to derive tractable Riemannian projectors, retractions, and Hessian-vector products enabling conjugate-gradient and trust-region solvers. For unbalanced OT the operations reduce to closed-form scalings, eliminating inner Dykstra/Bregman loops; per-iteration cost scales linearly with dataset size. The cost-agnostic construction extends to linear OT, Gromov-Wasserstein, and fused GW by only changing the Euclidean gradient/Hessian, and for convex costs a rank-sufficiency certificate with a computable duality-gap bound certifies global optimality. Experiments to n=50,000 converge in an order-of-magnitude fewer iterations without step-size tuning.
