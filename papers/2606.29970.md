## Technical note

IID-Nav reframes large-scale retrieval from static similarity extraction to stateful goal-oriented graph navigation, addressing the 'interest tunnel' effect (i2i heuristics confined to local behavioral clusters) and 'search drift' (static entry points and rigid topologies like TDM/NANN deviating from real-time intent at depth). It introduces a goal-oriented navigational policy with a target-aware discriminator (via cross-attention) for intent-driven routing, a recursive state evolution mechanism with cross-request state persistence achieving Indirectly Infinite Depth (IID) without linear latency growth, and trajectory-aware learning with graph-based hard negative sampling over semantic (multimodal-LLM-built) and collaborative (Swing) graphs. Billion-scale industrial experiments show IID-Nav significantly outperforms SOTA baselines in retrieval effectiveness under latency constraints while mitigating search drift at extended depths.
