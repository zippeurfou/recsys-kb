## Technical note

Systematically compares data-splitting strategies for offline evaluation of sequential recommenders, formalizing leave-one-out (LOO) versus Global Temporal Split (GTS) with target options (All, First, Last, Random, Successive) and validation schemes (Global Temporal, Last-Training-Item, User-Based). Across 8 datasets and SASRec+/BERT4Rec/GRU4Rec, GTS-Successive is treated as the production-realistic reference: GTS-Random (Kendall 0.91) and GTS-Last (0.83) correlate strongly with it, while LOO (0.71), GTS-First (0.62), and GTS-All (0.43) diverge and reorder models. It finds a survey where 77.3% of 2022-24 SRS papers used LOO despite its temporal leakage, shows split choice destabilizes model rankings, and recommends matching global-temporal validation plus retraining on combined train+val data (up to +81.8% NDCG@10 on Beauty).
