## Technical note

Meta tackles the 'Click A, Buy B' (CABB) phenomenon in e-commerce ads, where users click product A but purchase a different product B, so training on raw click-conversion pairs biases learning. It reframes conversion prediction as a multi-task problem with separate heads for Click A->Buy A (CABA) and Click A->Buy B (CABB), and introduces a taxonomy-aware collaborative-filtering weighting scheme that learns a category-to-category similarity matrix from co-engagement logs to amplify genuine substitutable/complementary pairs and down-weight coincidental cross-category purchases. Offline it reduced normalized entropy by 13.9% versus last-click attribution, and an online A/B test gave +0.25% on the primary business metric.
