## Summary

This paper presents the Inventory-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation (InvAwr-RAG) model, designed to optimize sponsored search query generation on platforms like Walmart. It addresses the challenge that many user queries don't match available ads, leading to missed revenue opportunities. By combining semantic understanding with real-time inventory data and historical success, InvAwr-RAG significantly increases ad fill rates by 68% while improving relevance for users and advertisers.

## Technical note

InvAwr-RAG tackles the ~13% of Walmart sponsored-search queries that retrieve zero ads by rewriting them to align with real-time inventory and ad budgets. It combines two-tower BERT query/product encoders (cosine similarity) for dense retrieval of top-N=20 inventory items, a Llama2-7B query-rewrite LLM fine-tuned with LoRA to generate K=5 diverse rewrites, blending of historically successful popular queries, and a cross-encoder BERT relevance filter. On 10,000 historically 0%-fill queries it reaches a 68% fill rate with NDCG@8=0.6847, beating GPT-4 (53% fill, NDCG@8=0.6458) and the 0% baseline, projecting up to $1B incremental ad revenue over five years.
