## Technical note

BAR (Bidding-Aware Retrieval) tackles multi-stage inconsistency in Alibaba display advertising where the retrieval stage cannot access real-time bids used by ranking (eCPM = pCTR x Bid). It adopts a learning-to-rank paradigm for eCPM ordering with monotonicity-constrained bidding-aware modeling, multi-task distillation, an Asynchronous Near-Line Inference service for real-time ad-embedding updates, and a Task-Attentive Refinement module disentangling user interest from commercial value. Full deployment yielded a 4.32% platform revenue increase, 3.78% RPM lift, and 22.2% impression improvement for positively-operated ads.

- Hard Pairs: These pairs consist of a positive sample from the impression set $\mathcal{I}_{u}$ and a negative sample from our sampled ranking set $\mathcal{R}_{u}$. These negatives are considered “hard” as they were deemed relevant enough by the pre-ranking stage
- Easy Pairs: For each positive example drawn from the union of $\mathcal{I}_{u}$ and $\mathcal{R}_{u}$, we generate five “easy” pairs. The negatives  for these pairs are ads randomly sampled from the entire ad corpus $\mathcal{A}$, excluding those already present in the user’s session ($\mathcal{I}_{u}\cup\mathcal{R}_{u}$). These are considered “easy” as they are overwhelmingly likely to be irrelevant to the specific user request (arXiv:2508.05206)
