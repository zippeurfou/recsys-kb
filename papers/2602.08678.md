## Technical note

SA-CAISR (Stage-Adaptive and Conflict-Aware Incremental Sequential Recommendation, Tencent) is a buffer-free incremental learning framework for sequential recommenders that avoids replay-based memory/privacy costs and the rigidity of old-model-only regularization. Its core Fisher-weighted knowledge-screening mechanism computes Fisher information on new data to quantify parameter-level conflicts between the old model and new interactions, selectively masking outdated/conflicting parameters while preserving compatible historical patterns; an InfoNCE-based consistency loss retains valid historical signals alongside cross-entropy for new knowledge. On four public datasets SA-CAISR improves Recall@20 by 2.0% (MRR@20 +2.1%, NDCG@20 +2.1%) on average over the best baseline while cutting memory usage 97.5% and training time 46.9%.
