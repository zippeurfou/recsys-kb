## Technical note

This Adobe paper studies position-based click models for carousel (multi-swipeable-list) recommender interfaces, proposing three carousel PBMs including the Observed Examination Position-Based Model (OEPBM) — the first position-based click model with no latent variables, using eye-tracking fixations as an observable examination signal. Evaluated on the RecGaze dataset (87 users, ~2,387 clicks), gradient ascent consistently beats classic EM and MLE for fitting, and PBM variants outperform the prior Cascade/Carousel Click Model baselines on click log-likelihood. OEPBM achieves the best click prediction and learns examination patterns matching the F-pattern (and mirrored right-to-left after swiping) seen in eye tracking, but the key finding is that strong click-likelihood fit does not imply realistic examination modeling, exposing a fundamental limit of click-only models.
