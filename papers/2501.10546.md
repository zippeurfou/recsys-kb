## Technical note

This Google systems paper describes the centralized TPU training infrastructure for large-scale Ads recommendation and auction-scoring models (billions of parameters, hundreds of embedding tables, petabytes of data). It contributes: (1) a Shared Input Generation (SIG) service that memoizes and amortizes raw-feature transformation across models (>95% cache hit rate, each memoized component reused by ~22 models on average) plus horizontally scalable input readers; (2) hybrid table/column/row embedding partitioning (ILP-solved), TensorCore/SparseCore pipelining with one-step-stale gradients, feedback-directed partitioning, and RPC fetch/update coalescing with table stacking for parameter-server (caught-up) training; and (3) preemption-notice and training-hold mechanisms to cut wasted TPU time in shared datacenters. Together these deliver a 116% performance boost and 18% training-cost reduction across five representative production models on 128 TPUv4 chips.
