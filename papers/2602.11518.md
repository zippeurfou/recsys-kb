## Technical note

KuaiSearch is a large-scale e-commerce search dataset built from real Kuaishou user interactions, covering ~330k users, ~18.6M products and ~2.5M real queries, and spanning three pipeline stages: recall, ranking, and relevance judgment. Unlike prior datasets (Amazon, JDsearch, Qilin) it preserves authentic queries and natural-language product texts without popularity filtering and retains cold-start users and long-tail products, making it ~15x larger in queries than JDsearch. The authors provide multi-perspective analysis and benchmark representative recall/ranking/relevance models to support LLM-based e-commerce search research.
