## Summary

Unified Generation and Ranking with One Model in Industrial Advertising Recommendation":

1.  The paper addresses challenges in advertising recommendation systems caused by the shift towards generative models, specifically the misalignment between user interest and business value (GMV).
2.  To solve this, OneRanker proposes a unified model that deeply integrates generation and ranking, using a value-aware architecture to separate interest coverage from value optimization, and a collaborative target awareness mechanism to align value during generation and ranking.
3.  Finally, OneRanker ensures end-to-end optimization with consistency guarantees, and its deployment on a major advertising platform resulted in a significant increase in GMV (+1.34%), demonstrating its industrial viability.

## Technical note

OneRanker deeply integrates generation and ranking in one model for industrial advertising recommendation, addressing misalignment between user-interest objectives and business value (GMV/eCPM). A value-aware multi-task decoupling architecture uses task tokens plus a causal mask to separate interest-coverage and value-optimization spaces over shared representations, with task-ordering priors (impression -> click -> conversion -> value). A coarse-to-fine target-awareness mechanism adds Fake Item Tokens (K-means item cluster centers) for implicit awareness during HSTU multi-token generation and a cross-attention ranking decoder for explicit value alignment. Key/Value pass-through and a Distribution Consistency loss enforce end-to-end generation-ranking consistency; Tencent WeiXin Channels deployment gave GMV-Normal +1.34%.
