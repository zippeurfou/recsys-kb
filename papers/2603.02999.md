## Technical note

OneRanker unifies generation and ranking in one model for industrial advertising recommendation, resolving misalignment between interest objectives and business value, target-agnostic generation, and generation-ranking disconnection. It uses a value-aware multi-task decoupling architecture (task tokens + causal mask separating interest coverage and value spaces over shared representations), a coarse-to-fine collaborative target-awareness mechanism (Fake Item Tokens via K-means for implicit awareness plus a ranking decoder for explicit eCPM alignment), and input/output dual-side consistency via Key/Value pass-through and a Distribution Consistency constraint loss. Fully deployed on Tencent's WeiXin channels advertising system, it improved GMV-Normal by +1.34%.
