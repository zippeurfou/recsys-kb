## Summary

1.  Fun-Audio-Chat addresses limitations in large audio-language models (LALMs) by tackling the challenge of efficiently processing high-resolution speech (25Hz) while retaining knowledge from large text LLMs.
2.  It introduces two key innovations: Dual-Resolution Speech Representations (grouping speech tokens for efficiency while refining them for quality) and Core-Cocktail Training (a two-stage fine-tuning process preventing catastrophic forgetting).
3.  Leveraging pre-trained models and extensive post-training, Fun-Audio-Chat achieves competitive performance on various audio tasks, offers a full-duplex variant, and is open-sourced with code and demos.

## Technical note

Fun-Audio-Chat is a large audio-language model that tackles the temporal resolution mismatch between 25Hz speech tokens and ~3Hz text tokens, high compute cost, and catastrophic forgetting during multimodal training. It uses Dual-Resolution Speech Representations (a shared LLM backbone runs at 5Hz via token grouping while a Speech Refined Head generates 25Hz speech tokens) and Core-Cocktail Training (two-stage SFT with intermediate model merging), followed by Multi-Task DPO. The 5Hz backbone cuts training GPU hours by ~50% while preserving speech quality. Dense 8B and MoE 30B-A3B models rank top among similar-scale open models on speech-to-text/speech-to-speech QA, with a full-duplex variant and open-sourced 8B checkpoint.
