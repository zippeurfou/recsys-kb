## Summary

Large-scale recommender systems often use sequential models, but standard approaches face a computational bottleneck with long sequences. The paper introduces ULTRA-HSTU, a novel model designed through system and model co-design that specifically addresses this bottleneck. ULTRA-HSTU achieves significant improvements in both training and inference speed (over 5x and 21x faster scaling respectively) while enhancing recommendation quality, and it has been successfully deployed at scale with measurable real-world benefits.

## Technical note

ULTRA-HSTU is a next-generation HSTU sequential recommender that preserves self-attention (rather than switching to cross-attention) for ultra-long user histories via model-system co-design inspired by DeepSeek-V2. Modeling contributions: merging item and action embeddings with heterogeneous action encodings to halve sequence length; Semi-Local Attention (SLA), a sparse mask with linear O((K1+K2)*L) complexity combining local and global windows; and dynamic topological designs (Attention Truncation and Mixture of Transducers) for depth scaling. System side adds Load-Balanced Stochastic Length to cut stragglers, recsys-tailored 16/8/4-bit mixed precision (BF16 stability, FP8 GEMM, INT4 embedding quantization), and custom SLA kernels extending FlashAttention V3 for SiLU attention and non-standard masks. Deployed with 18 self-attention layers over 16k-length sequences, yielding ~5x training and ~21x inference scaling efficiency and 4-8% engagement lifts.
