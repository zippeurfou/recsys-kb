## Summary

This paper introduces E-GenRM, a novel generative reward model designed specifically for e-commerce shopping agents to handle open-ended tasks, moving beyond traditional simple queries. The model addresses limitations in existing approaches by using real user behavior data to create diverse and long interactions, and employing a two-stage training process involving supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning with specific criteria. E-GenRM significantly improves agent performance, achieving high preference accuracy and demonstrating a substantial increase in effective shopping assistance and user satisfaction in real-world tests.

## Technical note

Reward-guided post-training of e-commerce shopping agents lacks verifiable rewards, logged agent trajectories only cover what the agent already does, and scalar rewards collapse per-requirement judgments. E-GenRM (Taobao) mines one month of 120+ behavior types into purchase-terminated shopping trajectories (3.8 sessions, 162 events and 4.6 decision steps each), renders them as agent interaction histories with candidate-response pairs labelled by the user's subsequent behavior plus a single controlled perturbation, and has a GPT-5.6-Sol teacher derive a weighted task-specific rubric and criterion-level, evidence-cited evaluation chains (94.2% annotator pass rate). Training is SFT on the chains (LoRA r=64), then GRPO with reward = outcome correctness + 0.25 x criterion-level factual-support score from a fact verifier, then continued RL on personalized hard negatives chosen by teacher disagreement and small utility margin; a ReAct-style extension with a tool-use reward learns when to fetch product-encyclopedia or review evidence. E-GenRM-27B reaches 82.57% preference accuracy on the expert-labelled E-GenRM-Bench (87.40% with autonomous retrieval at a 22% call rate), beating Bradley-Terry RMs on the same data (66.22%), Skywork-Reward-V2, RM-R1, GRAM-R2 and prompted judges including Gemini-3.8-Flash, and transfers to rubric generation on the external SRB benchmark; ablations show the outcome reward matters most (-19 points without it) and hard negatives add +5.09 points. Used as the reward for GRPO/DPO post-training of the Taobao Omni-Shop Agent (100k+ queries/day), it beat an expert-calibrated Gemini judge offline and in a 14-day A/B test lifted effective-assistance rate +1.39pp, satisfaction +2.33pp and card coverage +7.47pp with 17.9% lower P99 latency.
