## Technical note

This validation study tests whether interview-informed LLM generative agents can simulate user responses in early-stage product-discovery concept tests. From workflow interviews with 51 knowledge workers, personalized agents were built and their evaluations of four novel AI document-workflow concepts (scalar TAM/NPS ratings plus open-ended feedback, 3,060 responses) compared to the same participants' real responses. Agents were found to be distribution-calibrated but identity-imprecise: they approximate population-level response distributions and outperform baselines but fail to reliably reproduce the specific individual, so simulation may help early concept screening but not substitute for individual-level insight.
