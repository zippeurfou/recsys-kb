## Technical note

This Zalando perspective paper maps the challenges of industrial fashion recommender systems (rapid trend shifts, item-item compatibility, high return rates, multi-stakeholder ecosystem) and charts a research agenda around five scenarios spanning static queries, outfit completion, and multi-turn chat. It argues mixed-modality refinement—combining image anchors with textual constraints—is critical and proposes AMMR (Agentic Mixed-Modality Refinement), a pipeline fusing multimodal encoders, dynamic query composition, and an LLM-based agentic planner. The work is a positioning/agenda paper rather than an empirical benchmark, advocating a shift from static retrieval toward adaptive, generative, stakeholder-aware systems.
