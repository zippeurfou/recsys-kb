## Technical note

HarmonRank rethinks multi-objective ensemble (ME) ranking in recommenders, arguing scores should be rank-consistent with each objective's labels and align with the commonality shared across objectives, unlike classification/regression-based, relation-agnostic prior ME methods. It formulates rank consistency (AUC) as a rank-sum problem optimized end-to-end differentiably, and shifts to a relation-aware ensemble by capturing objective commonality via self-attention and fusing relation-aware objective representations per user. On two industrial datasets and online it beats SOTA with robustness to label skew; fully deployed on Kuaishou's live-streaming e-commerce platform (400M DAU) contributing +2.6% purchase gain (e.g. +2.635% core purchases, +0.451% follows).
