## Summary

The paper addresses the challenge of portfolio optimization by using Conditional Autoencoders (CAEs) to estimate many latent asset-pricing factors, which existing methods limited to K=5 due to performance concerns. To overcome this, the authors propose a scalable framework combining high-dimensional CAEs with an uncertainty-aware factor selection process. This selection ranks factors by forecast uncertainty and retains the top-k most predictable ones, significantly boosting risk-adjusted portfolio performance across different forecasting models. Furthermore, an uncorrelated ensemble of these models consistently outperformed individual models by achieving higher Sharpe, Sortino, and Omega ratios.

## Technical note

This asset-pricing paper couples a high-dimensional Conditional Autoencoder (up to K=50 latent factors) with uncertainty-aware factor selection to overcome the usual K~5 degradation limit. Three quantile forecasters rank latent factors by predictive uncertainty: zero-shot Chronos (a pretrained time-series foundation model), XGBoost quantile regression trees (Q-Boost via RAPIDS), and an IID bootstrap sample-mean model. Retaining the most predictable factors for tangency-portfolio construction improves risk-adjusted returns across all models, and a performance-weighted ensemble of the uncorrelated forecasts yields the best Sharpe/Sortino/Omega ratios. An adaptive log-sum-exp scheme selects the factor subset online, and an ablation rules out Chronos look-ahead bias.
