## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Sequential recommendation has rapidly advanced in click-through rate prediction due to its ability to model dynamic user interests. A key challenge, however, lies in modeling long sequences: users often exhibit significant interest shifts, introducing substantial irrelevant or misleading information. Our empirical analysis corroborates this challenge and uncovers a recurring behavioral pattern in long sequences (\textit{session hopping}): user interests remain stable within short temporal spans ...

## Technical note

This paper identifies 'session hopping' in long user behavior sequences -- interests are stable within a session but shift abruptly across sessions and later reappear -- which injects misleading signal into CTR prediction. It proposes Mixture of Sequence (MoS), a model-agnostic MoE framework with a theme-aware routing mechanism (a router with a codebook of user-behavior themes) that extracts coherent theme-specific subsequences, plus a multi-scale fusion mechanism using three expert types for global, short-term, and theme-specific patterns to counter information loss. MoS consistently improves long-sequence recommendation accuracy while using fewer FLOPs than other MoE counterparts.
