## Technical note

T-REX is an encoder-decoder transformer from Amazon Grocery that generates next-basket category-level recommendations, mapping ~29K products to 35 (later 280) categories to handle grocery's repetitive purchase patterns and sparse baskets. Its innovations are dynamic sequence splitting (pivot-based train instance generation), adaptive positional encoding (positions as days-before/after pivot), and causal masking in the decoder — the authors show BERT4Rec-style masked modeling leaks future item occurrences and is unsuitable for basket generation. The ~50.4M-param model (6-layer enc/dec) beats the deployed Personal Top-Frequency baseline on precision/recall@k (median customer recovers >90% of basket items from top-10) and, in production A/B tests on Consumables, delivered top widget CTR and ~23% higher sales lift than typical Amazon recommendation systems (validated with double-machine-learning causal analysis).
