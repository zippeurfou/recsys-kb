Trained on various human-authored corpora, Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated a certain capability of reflecting specific human-like traits (e.g., personality or values) by prompting, benefiting applications like personalized LLMs and social simulations. ## Technical note

IROTE (Microsoft) is an in-context method for stable, transferable human-like trait elicitation in LLMs, addressing the 'superficial elicitation' problem where LLMs mimic only shallow stylistic patterns. Drawing on Self-Reflective Identity Processing theory, it automatically generates and optimizes a textual self-reflection within prompts by iteratively maximizing an information-bottleneck-like objective—enhancing behavior-trait connection while cutting redundancy, without fine-tuning. Across three human trait systems, a single IROTE-generated self-reflection induces stable trait impersonation across diverse downstream tasks, outperforming strong baselines; recsys relevance is only tangential (personalized LLMs).
