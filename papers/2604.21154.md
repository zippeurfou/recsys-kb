## Summary

Personalized physiotherapy compliance is low due to a lack of dynamic feedback, which current digital solutions often fail to provide effectively. To address this, the paper introduces a novel Multi-Agent System leveraging Generative AI and computer vision. This system consists of four specialized agents designed to create patient-specific exercise videos and provide real-time pose correction and feedback during at-home sessions.

## Technical note

This paper proposes a four-agent system for at-home physiotherapy that closes the tele-rehabilitation loop with generative media and computer vision. A Clinical Extraction Agent parses unstructured physician notes into a structured JSON of kinematic constraints (e.g., max shoulder abduction 90 degrees); a Video Synthesis Agent turns those constraints into prompts for a foundational video model to generate patient-specific 'physio-twin' exercise demos that stop at safe ranges of motion. A Vision Processing Agent runs lightweight local pose estimation (MediaPipe) at >=30 FPS to compute joint angles, and a Diagnostic Feedback Agent applies a hybrid deterministic-generative decision matrix to issue corrective, empathetic feedback. Deterministic safety rules ensure LLM hallucinations cannot override physiological limits. The contribution is a prototype architecture and clinical evaluation plan rather than large-scale empirical results.
