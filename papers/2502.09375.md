## Technical note

FARM (Frequency-Aware Model for Cross-Domain Live-Streaming Recommendation) tackles data sparsity at Kuaishou where valuable live behaviors (like, comment, gift) are rare and exposed live-streaming is ~9x less than short-video. An intra-domain frequency-aware module uses the Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) to perceive users' sparse yet valuable high-frequency behaviors. A 'preference align before fuse' strategy transfers preferences across short-video and live-streaming domains: a cross-domain preference align module uses contrastive learning to align representations, and a fuse module applies tailored attention mechanisms. Online A/B testing shows up to 0.41% Click improvement; FARM is deployed serving hundreds of millions of Kuaishou users.
