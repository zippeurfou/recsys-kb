## Technical note

DMF (Decoupled Multimodal Fusion) (Taobao/Alibaba) fuses frozen multimodal representations with ID-based CTR models via modality-enriched modeling, enabling fine-grained interaction between behavioral signals and content semantics during user-interest encoding. Decoupled Target Attention (DTA) integrates target-aware multimodal similarity features without recomputing key/value per candidate by reusing target-agnostic ID-derived components and discretized embedding lookup, tripling throughput vs early fusion at 1200 candidates. Complementary Modality Modeling (CMM) unifies modality-centric and modality-enriched pathways. Deployed on Lazada, DMF improved CTCVR +5.30% and GMV +7.43%.
