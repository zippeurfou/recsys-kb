## Summary

FineViT addresses the bottleneck in multimodal large language models (MLLMs) caused by their visual encoders. The solution involves replacing standard web data with dense recaptions and employing a progressive training strategy: first, training at high resolution from scratch on billions of recaptioned pairs, then enhancing local perception via LLM alignment using the FineCap-450M dataset. This approach establishes FineViT as a state-of-the-art vision encoder, significantly improving fine-grained perception for tasks like recognition and retrieval, especially long-context retrieval, when integrated into MLLMs.

## Technical note

FineViT is a from-scratch 0.86B vision encoder (28 layers, patch 14, 2D RoPE) targeting fine-grained perception to remove the visual bottleneck in MLLMs, replacing noisy web captions with dense recaptions across a three-stage curriculum. Stage I is masked image modeling distilling DINOv3 features at 256x256; Stage II is native-resolution SigLIP-style contrastive learning on 1.56B recaptioned pairs, scaling resolution to 448 and text context 64->256 tokens; Stage III aligns to an LLM with autoregressive multi-granularity QA (bbox<->string, bbox<->OCR) at 1K resolution. The authors build FineCap-450M, claimed the largest fine-grained caption dataset, with 63M global captions, 226M region captions over 600k+ categories, plus rich-text and document OCR via PaddleOCR. FineViT matches SigLIP2/Seed-ViT on zero-shot classification and retrieval, excels at long-context retrieval, and outperforms Qwen-ViT/InternViT when integrated into MLLMs. Retrieval here is image-text retrieval, not recommendation.
