## Technical note

MixDec Sampling is a soft-link-based sampling method for GNN recommenders, replacing binary hard positive/negative pairs that lose structural information and undersample low-degree nodes. Its Mixup Sampling synthesizes new nodes and soft links (Beta-distribution feature interpolation) to supply pairs for nodes with few neighbors, and its Decay Sampling generates soft links whose weights decay with BFS distance to inject graph structure. It is the first to model node sampling relationships via soft links; on Amazon-Book, Yelp2018, and Last-FM it consistently improves GraphSAGE/GCN/GAT (e.g., up to +44.6% MRR).
