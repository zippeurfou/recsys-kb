## Technical note

Proposes conditional retrieval on top of standard two-tower user-to-item models, where retrieved items must satisfy a condition (e.g. topic) without needing condition-specific training data. It samples item-side features (e.g. item-to-topic) as artificial conditions, feeds the condition into the user tower's embedding and feature-crossing layers to encourage user-condition feature interactions, and trains with in-batch negatives via sampled softmax on generic engagement data. Deployed for Pinterest's topic-based notification feed, it beats two-tower-with-filter baselines on relevance and engagement and lifts weekly active users by +0.26%.
