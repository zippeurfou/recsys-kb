## Summary

MASS-DPO addresses the problem of optimizing DPO over large, costly negative pools where many candidates provide redundant gradients. It introduces a novel active sample selection method that uses a Fisher-information objective to choose compact negative subsets, specifically designed for the Plackett-Luce model, ensuring they contain complementary information. This selection significantly improves model alignment and performance metrics like accuracy, Recall, and NDCG, using far fewer negatives than required by the full preference pool.

## Technical note

MASS-DPO is a within-prompt active negative selection method for multi-negative Direct Preference Optimization under the Plackett-Luce model, addressing the redundancy of large negative pools whose near-duplicate candidates contribute overlapping gradients. It derives a PL-specific Fisher-information objective and casts negative selection as a D-optimal experimental design problem, maximizing the log-determinant of the information matrix so selected negatives span complementary directions in parameter space. Unlike prior instance-level optimal-design work, MASS-DPO selects within each prompt using an incremental rank-one greedy procedure with Sherman-Morrison updates to avoid recomputing determinants, and operates in a fixed-budget preprocessing regime rather than per-step mining. Across four recommendation and multiple-choice QA benchmarks and three model families, it matches or exceeds baselines on Recall/NDCG with substantially fewer negatives.
