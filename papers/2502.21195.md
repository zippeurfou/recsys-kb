## Technical note

This is a survey of joint modeling in recommendation, defining its scope across four dimensions: multi-task, multi-scenario, multi-modal, and multi-behavior modeling. It reviews methods and underlying paradigms in each dimension, arguing that conventional single-task/single-scenario/single-modality/single-behavior deep recommender systems inadequately capture complex, changing user preferences. It summarizes recent advances and outlines future research directions for integrating these dimensions to improve recommendation precision, efficiency, and personalization.
