## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently computing optimal transport (OT) and Gromov-Wasserstein (GW) alignment for complex Gaussian datasets, which are common in machine learning. It provides comprehensive, closed-form solutions for Gaussian OT and inner product GW alignment, including cases with uncentered Gaussians and the calculation of barycenters for centered Gaussians. Furthermore, the work introduces an efficient algorithm for a specific type of multimarginal OT problem and demonstrates the practical utility of these advancements on real-world tasks like knowledge distillation and clustering.

## Technical note

This work provides a comprehensive treatment of Gaussian optimal transport (OT) under quadratic cost and inner-product Gromov-Wasserstein (IGW) alignment, closing gaps in closed-form solutions used for large-scale applications. It gives a closed-form IGW expression up to a quadratic optimization over unitary operators for uncentered Gaussians on Hilbert spaces with tight analytic upper/lower bounds, reduces to fully closed form when a measure is centered, and extends to IGW barycenters and multimarginal OT with pairwise quadratic costs via a rank-deficiency-constrained algorithm. Utility is demonstrated on knowledge distillation and heterogeneous clustering over synthetic and real datasets.
