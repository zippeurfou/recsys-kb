## Technical note

UniScale is a co-design framework for industrial e-commerce search ranking that jointly scales data and architecture, motivated by the observation that pure parameter scaling shows diminishing returns under limited data capacity. Its ES3 (Entire-Space Sample System) expands high-quality training data via hierarchical label attribution over exposed and unexposed items, delayed cross-domain conversions, and 'searchification' of non-search interactions to mitigate selection/exposure bias and negative transfer; its HHSFT transformer uses heterogeneous feature tokenization, hierarchical feature interaction, and a domain-routed expert fusion layer. Online A/B tests on an e-commerce search platform show +1.70% user purchases and +2.04% GMV over strong production baselines.
