## Technical note

This Netflix paper systematically studies weighting schemes and matrix-factorization algorithms for top-N recommendation on implicit feedback, challenging the conventional wisdom that upweighting observed interactions helps. It finds, surprisingly, that training with unweighted data performs comparably to or better than weighted data, especially for large-capacity models, while weighting benefits mainly small/low-capacity models under specific regularization. It also derives efficient algorithms to exactly minimize several weighted MF objectives previously considered intractable, giving a comprehensive analysis of the interplay between weighting, regularization, and model capacity.
