## Technical note

RSIR (Recursive Self-Improving Recommendation, Huawei) is a model-agnostic framework that lets a sequential recommender bootstrap its own training data without external teachers, addressing extreme interaction sparsity. In a closed loop it (1) generates synthetic interaction sequences via bounded exploration from a hybrid candidate pool (user-history vs global items), (2) filters them with fidelity-based quality control that keeps a step only if held-out true items remain highly ranked, and (3) retrains a successor model. Theory frames RSIR as a data-driven implicit regularizer (Manifold Tangential Gradient Penalty) that smooths the loss landscape, with a proven contraction/breakdown-point stability bound. Applied to SASRec, CL4SRec, and HSTU on Amazon Beauty/Sports/Toys and Yelp, one RSIR iteration gives consistent gains (e.g. HSTU +7.71% Recall@10 on Sports), compounding to +13.92% over three iterations; weak teachers can even train stronger students.
