## Summary

E-commerce recommendation systems often use multimodal representations (like images and text) to improve Click-Through Rate (CTR) prediction compared to traditional ID-based models. However, separate pre-training of the multimodal encoder followed by fine-tuning for CTR faces challenges due to differing objectives and data distributions. This paper proposes the "Mine-Then-Train" method, which first selects highly relevant training samples from CTR data based on multimodal understanding, then uses these samples to fine-tune the pre-trained encoder, effectively aligning it with user click preferences.

## Technical note

Alibaba studies whether end-to-end multimodal training (E2EM) of the multimodal encoder jointly with the CTR ranker beats the standard two-stage frozen-encoder paradigm in Taobao display advertising; data is 84M users, 88M products, 1.9B samples over one week, the ranker is the production MUSE model, and the encoder is Chinese CLIP ViT-B/16 pretrained with Semantic-aware Contrastive Learning (SCL, a MoCo-style objective) that already gave a percentage-level GAUC lift. E2EM consistently loses: on the Taobao-MM benchmark GAUC falls from 0.6154 (frozen MUSE) to 0.6139 target-side-only, 0.6128 with a LEMUR memory bank, and 0.6124 for target plus sequence, and it loses under every combination of SGD/Adam/AdamW and lr 2e-3/2e-4/2e-5; adding SCL loss as an anchor costs a further 0.17pt GAUC and 5pt SCL top-1 accuracy. The diagnosis is that CTR labels are only partly explainable by multimodal semantics (MultiModal-Mean GAUC 0.541, MultiModal-Max 0.536, barely above 0.5 random) and that co-training yields near-orthogonal encoder gradients on the last Transformer layer (intra-batch cosine 0.016 vs 0.144 for SCL, adjacent-batch 0.006 vs 0.849), while E2EM on target plus sequence costs 25.09x peak GPU memory (84.56 GB vs 3.37 GB) and 7.18x step time. The proposed Mine-Then-Train decouples this: a TaskRes-style residual-tuning annotation model (frozen SCL encoder, RK-Means semantic IDs indexing a zero-initialized 3x8192 SID decode codebook) is trained end-to-end through the CTR loss via SA-TA and SimTier to score click relevance, then used to mine 30M triplets under thresholds tau_m=0.3 pair similarity, tau_s=0.05 SCL similarity gap and tau_a=0.15 annotation-score margin, with human raters validating the margin filter (85 percent win rate on high-confidence triplets vs 42 percent low-confidence). The SCL encoder is then fine-tuned with a triplet margin loss (m=0.1) plus the SCL InfoNCE loss as a forgetting regularizer. Offline, adding the annotation score gives +0.13 percent GAUC / +0.08 percent AUC and full NMRL gives +0.22 percent GAUC / +0.11 percent AUC over a baseline that already contains SCL features; online A/B in Taobao display advertising in early 2026 delivered +1.5 percent CTR and +0.5 percent RPM. Caveat: the negative E2EM result is conditioned on an already strongly in-domain pretrained encoder, and all offline numbers are relative deltas on proprietary data.
