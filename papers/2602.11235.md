## Technical note

MTFM (Meituan Foundation Model) is an alignment-free transformer foundation model for multi-scenario/cross-domain recommendation that abstracts disparate domain inputs into heterogeneous tokens rather than forcing a fixed feature template, giving extensibility, scalability, and efficiency. For efficiency it introduces Hybrid Target Attention (full attention on a few critical layers, target attention elsewhere) plus Grouped-Query Attention, multi-scenario user-level sample aggregation, and system optimizations (kernel fusion, CPU-GPU pipelining) that nearly double training/inference throughput. On Meituan data MTFM improves CTR by avg +0.36pp GAUC (max +0.76pp) and CTCVR by +0.29pp (max +0.53pp), with gains that scale with model capacity and multi-scenario data; online A/B tests gave +2.98% orders (SQS coupon-package rec) and +1.45% (PHF food rec).
