## Summary

This paper introduces a new algorithm called PUB (Parameter Update Balancing) for optimizing multi-task ranking models in recommendation systems. It addresses challenges arising from training inconsistencies caused by momentum-based optimizers like Adam, which can lead to imbalanced parameter updates across tasks. Comprehensive experiments on benchmark datasets and deployment on the Huawei AppGallery platform show that PUB consistently improves model performance and achieves state-of-the-art results.

## Technical note

Multi-task ranking models suffer the seesaw problem where conflicting per-task gradients on shared parameters make joint training inequitable. The authors observe that with momentum-based optimizers like Adam, the actual parameter update deviates substantially from the joint gradient, so balancing gradients is not the same as balancing updates. They propose PUB (Parameter Update Balancing), the first method to fuse tasks at the parameter-update level rather than the gradient or loss level, seeking an optimal joint update from per-task updates. PUB improves multiple multi-task backbones to state-of-the-art on benchmark ranking (and vision) datasets and boosted the deployed ranking model on HUAWEI AppGallery.
