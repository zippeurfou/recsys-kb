## Technical note

P-GRPO (Personalized Group Relative Policy Optimization, Apple) modifies GRPO for heterogeneous preference alignment, where standard group-based reward normalization implicitly assumes all samples in a generation batch are exchangeable and thus biases learning toward dominant preferences while suppressing minority signals. P-GRPO decouples advantage estimation from immediate batch statistics by normalizing each output against preference-group-specific reward histories (per-cluster mu, sigma maintained via Welford's online algorithm) rather than the concurrent group, preserving the contrastive signal for distinct preferences. Evaluated on a content-recommendation task and preference-conditioned generation tasks with Qwen3 and Gemma models, P-GRPO achieves faster convergence and higher reward than GRPO, higher LLM-as-judge win rates, and no degradation on MMLU.
