## Summary

SIDInspector is presented as a diagnostic tool specifically for semantic-ID (\sid) tokenizers used in generative recommendation systems, focusing on their exported item-to-code mappings. Its core goal is to inspect these mapping artifacts early and thoroughly, identifying critical issues like aliasing before downstream sequence generator training begins. By validating the mapping contract and analyzing prefix alignment, SIDInspector aims to catch problems that would otherwise only be discovered late during model development or deployment.

## Technical note

SIDInspector is a mapping-first diagnostic resource for Semantic-ID (SID) tokenizer artifacts used in generative recommendation, treating the exported item-to-code mapping as an inspectable interface before downstream generator training. It defines a small adapter contract over item mappings, metadata, interactions, and optional generator traces, validates it, and computes D1-D6 probes for utilization, aliasing, neighborhood alignment, popularity allocation, structural cost, and temporal churn. Across GRID/RQ-KMeans, ReSID/GAOQ, LETTER, and LC-Rec artifacts on 23,742 Musical items, the GRID feature-text export shows a 0.977 full-code aliasing rate while ReSID is aliasing-free. The strongest prefix-co-occurrence alignment comes from a deterministic category-prefix control (0.447 vs 0.154 and 0.055-0.080), showing addressability and behaviorally meaningful prefixes diverge and must be inspected separately.
