## Technical note

MOON is the first generative MLLM-based model for e-commerce product representation learning, addressing many-to-one image-text alignment that discriminative dual-flow encoders struggle with. It uses a guided Mixture-of-Experts module to target multimodal and aspect-specific content, detects core semantic regions to suppress background-noise distraction in product images, and adds a specialized negative sampling strategy for harder, more diverse negatives. The authors release the large-scale MBE benchmark. MOON shows competitive zero-shot performance on their benchmark and a public dataset across cross-modal retrieval, product classification, and attribute prediction.
