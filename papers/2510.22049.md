## Technical note

VISTA (VIrtual Sequential Target Attention) (Meta) scales sequential transducer generative recommenders to lifelong user histories (up to one million items) with hundreds of trillions of parameters while keeping downstream cost fixed. It decomposes traditional candidate-to-history target attention into two stages: (1) summarize the ultra-long user history into a few hundred summarization tokens (done only during foundational-model training and cached), and (2) compute candidate-item attention over those cached tokens for downstream training/inference. It also introduces a quasi-linear attention formulation and a generative sequential reconstruction loss, trading GPU compute for cheaper storage, and is deployed serving billions of users.
