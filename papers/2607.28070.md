## Summary

CCFormer is an efficient Transformer model designed for industrial recommendation systems that face challenges scaling sequential models due to computational constraints when handling long user interaction histories.

The key innovation combines two techniques: feature-field separated cross attention to effectively capture complex interactions between different types of features, and hierarchical sequence compression using progressively expanding receptive fields to manage long sequences efficiently with minimal information loss.

Experiments on public benchmarks and Tencent's large-scale datasets show CCFormer consistently outperforms existing methods, while online A/B tests demonstrate significant gains in Click-Through Rate (CTR) and advertising revenue. It has been successfully deployed in production systems at Tencent handling main traffic for video recommendations and ad ranking.

## Technical note

Industrial CTR ranking at Tencent benefits from longer user-behavior sequences, but full self-attention is O(L^2) and too costly under production latency budgets. CCFormer decouples inputs into user-profile, behavior-sequence, and target-item fields and runs feature-field separated cross attention with three directed flows (user to sequence, target to sequence, target to user) using RMSNorm and SwiGLU FFNs; intra-sequence self-attention is replaced by subspace token mixing that reshapes S to B x L/m x d/n x mn and applies a per-channel gated PFFN (m=8, n=16), plus group-local relative temporal-positional encoding with a learnable time-decay term, and Conv1D hierarchical compression (k=3, s=2) across 8 blocks to progressively expand the receptive field. Offline it hits 93.67% AUC on Taobao and 83.35% on KuaiRec, beating STCA and OneTrans by 0.86 and 0.59 points, and on a >4B-sample Tencent dataset gains +0.28 AUC / +0.50 GAUC over HSTU (1.01% / 2.40% relative) at roughly half the GFLOPs per sample and 2.21x training speedup. Online A/B at Tencent yields +3.57% CTR and +1.64% ad revenue in video recommendation and +1.71% ad revenue in ad ranking, with +30% peak QPS because target tokens never attend to each other so all candidates in a request are scored in one shared forward pass. Ablations show sequence compression is the efficiency driver (speedup drops 2.21x to 1.29x when removed) while removing subspace token mixing causes the largest accuracy loss (AUC 77.94% to 77.73%).
