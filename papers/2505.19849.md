## Technical note

HIT (Hierarchical Interaction-enhanced Two-Tower) is Tencent's display-advertising pre-ranking model that overcomes vanilla two-tower limitations (no cross-tower interaction, coarse dot-product similarity) while keeping inference efficiency. It adds generators that pre-generate holistic vectors capturing coarse-grained user-ad interactions via a dual-generator framework with a cosine-similarity generation loss, plus multi-head representers projecting embeddings into multiple latent subspaces for fine-grained multi-faceted user interests and multi-dimensional ad attributes. Deployed at Tencent serving billions of impressions daily with two-tower-like latency, online A/B tests show +1.66% GMV and +1.55% ROI; code is released.
