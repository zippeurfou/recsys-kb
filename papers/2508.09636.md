## Technical note

Presents a multi-task learning architecture for personalized product search ranking that integrates tabular and non-tabular data, using a fine-tuned TinyBERT for query-product semantic embeddings plus a sampling technique for diverse customer behaviors. It also proposes a scalable relevance-labeling mechanism combining click-through rate, click position, and semantic similarity via a voting scheme as an alternative to human labels. Evaluated against XGBoost, TabNet, FT-Transformer, DCN-V2, and MMoE, combining non-tabular data with embeddings in the MTL paradigm significantly improves ranking; ablations confirm benefits of the relevance labels, TinyBERT fine-tuning, and query-product embedding interactions (Walmart).
