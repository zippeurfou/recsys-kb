## Technical note

GRLM (Generative Recommendation Language Model) introduces Term IDs (TIDs) - sets of semantically rich, standardized textual keywords - as item identifiers to avoid the hallucination-prone huge output space of text-based IDs and the costly vocabulary-expansion/semantic-gap problems of Semantic IDs. It uses Context-aware Term Generation to convert item metadata into standardized TIDs, Integrative Instruction Fine-tuning to jointly optimize term internalization and sequential recommendation, and Elastic Identifier Grounding for robust item mapping. On real-world datasets GRLM significantly outperforms baselines across multiple scenarios, pointing toward generalizable LLM-based generative recommendation.
