## Technical note

COPF frames multi-behavior recommendation as combinatorial optimization over user behavior patterns. Its COGCN (Combinatorial Optimization Graph Convolution Network) imposes pre-behavior, in-behavior, and post-behavior constraints on graph convolution to restrict the solution space during multi-behavior fusion, avoiding both unconstrained aggregation and overly-strict cascading. Its DFME (Distributed Fitting Multi-Expert Networks) mitigates negative transfer in the MTL prediction step by using contrastive learning to align target/auxiliary feature distributions and decoupling gradients between target and auxiliary behaviors. Experiments on three real-world datasets show COPF outperforms multi-behavior baselines, with ablations validating COGCN and DFME.
