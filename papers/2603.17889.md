## Summary

1.  Existing methods lack a framework for precisely controlling both facial appearance and vocal timbre across different identities in synthesized audio-visual content.
2.  This paper introduces a unified, scalable framework for identity-aware joint audio-video generation, featuring a data pipeline for paired annotations and a flexible identity injection mechanism allowing control over both visual and auditory identity aspects.
3.  To address challenges in cross-modal consistency, the framework employs a multi-stage training strategy, and experimental results demonstrate its ability to produce high-fidelity, personalized audio-visual output.

## Technical note

Identity-as-Presence is a unified framework for personalized joint audio-video generation controlling both facial appearance and vocal timbre across single- and multi-subject scenes. It contributes an automated data-curation pipeline that extracts identity-labeled audio-visual pairs (YOLOv11/MOTRv2 tracking, FLAME/SMIRK face params, Demucs+3D-Speaker diarization, SyncNet alignment, ArcFace/ERes2Net cross-clip matching) without manual annotation. Identity is injected by treating reference face images and reference audio as extra input tokens with shared learnable per-identity embeddings that bind each voice to its face, processed by a dual-tower DiT with asymmetric self-attention and structured spatiotemporal position embeddings, trained with a weighted two-modality flow-matching objective. A three-stage curriculum (unimodal injection, joint multimodal injection, multi-view fine-tuning) mitigates cross-modal optimization instability, and experiments claim state-of-the-art audio quality, video fidelity, and cross-modal alignment. This is a generative multimodal paper with no recommendation content.
