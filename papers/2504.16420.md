RS techniques have traditionally relied on modeling interactions between users and items as well as the features of content using models specific to each task. ## Technical note

This Adobe survey provides a comprehensive overview of Foundation Models for Recommender Systems (FM4RecSys), organizing the field into three paradigms: feature-based augmentation of representations, generative recommendation, and agentic interactive systems. It reviews RS data foundations (explicit/implicit feedback to multimodal content), FM capabilities for representation learning, language understanding, and multimodal reasoning, and how FMs enhance tasks including top-N, sequential, zero/few-shot, conversational recommendation, and novel item/content generation. It critically analyzes trade-offs among the three paradigms and outlines open challenges such as cross-domain generality, interpretability, fairness, multimodal integration, and lifelong learning for long user sequences.
