Cross-Domain Sequential Recommendation (CDSR) predicts user behavior by leveraging historical interactions across multiple domains, focusing on modeling cross-domain preferences and capturing both intra- and inter-sequence item relationships. We propose LLM-Enhanced Multimodal Fusion for Cross-Domain Sequential Recommendation (LLM-EMF), a novel and advanced approach that enhances textual information with Large Language Models (LLM) knowledge and significantly improves recommendation performance ...

## Technical note

LLM-EMF (LLM-Enhanced Multimodal Fusion) from Microsoft addresses cross-domain sequential recommendation (CDSR) by enriching item textual data with LLM-generated domain-agnostic semantic attributes via prompts and fusing visual, textual, and ID embeddings. A domain-balanced hierarchical multi-attention mechanism jointly learns single-domain and cross-domain preferences while preventing high-frequency domains from dominating recommendations. Claimed as the first CDSR framework to systematically combine LLM-generated textual knowledge, multimodal fusion, and explicit domain balance; evaluations on four e-commerce datasets show it consistently outperforms state-of-the-art baselines across all metrics.
