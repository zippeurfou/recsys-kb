In a multi-stage recommendation system, reranking plays a crucial role in modeling intra-list correlations among items. A key challenge lies in exploring optimal sequences within the combinatorial space of permutations. Recent research follows a two-stage (generator-evaluator) paradigm, where a generator produces multiple feasible sequences, and an evaluator selects the best one. ## Technical note

GReF is a unified generative reranking framework that removes the separate evaluator of the two-stage generator-evaluator paradigm. Gen-Reranker uses a bidirectional encoder and a dynamic autoregressive decoder pre-trained on item exposure order, is post-trained with Rerank-DPO to inject sequence-level evaluation end-to-end, and uses Ordered Multi-Token Prediction (OMTP) to generate multiple ordered future items per step for low latency. On Avito and Kuaishou datasets it beats SOTA rerankers (e.g. AUC ~1.4-1.5% higher, NDCG higher than PIER) at 12.97ms latency, near non-autoregressive NAR4Rec and over 4x faster than Seq2Slate; deployed at Kuaishou (300M+ DAU).
