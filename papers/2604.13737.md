## Summary

TokenFormer addresses the challenge of combining multi-field and sequential recommendation systems by proposing a novel unified architecture. It overcomes the Sequential Collapse Propagation issue caused by merging non-sequence fields by introducing a Bottom-Full-Top-Sliding attention mechanism and a Non-Linear Interaction Representation layer. This approach achieves state-of-the-art performance and significantly improves dimensional robustness and representation quality.

## Technical note

TokenFormer is a homogeneous decoder-only architecture that unifies non-sequential multi-field features, behavior tokens, and target attributes into a single token stream, subsuming feature interaction, self-attention, and target attention under one projection/weighting/multiplicative-interaction template. It identifies Sequential Collapse Propagation (SCP): low-information static fields prone to dimensional collapse drag sequential representations into a low-rank subspace when unified via shared operators (steeper singular-value decay, lower effective rank). Two fixes mitigate SCP: a Bottom-Full-Top-Sliding (BFTS) attention schedule using full attention in lower layers and shrinking sliding-window attention in upper layers, and a Non-Linear Interaction Representation (NLIR) gating that applies one-sided nonlinear multiplicative transforms to preserve rank. Experiments on public benchmarks and Tencent Ads show SOTA accuracy with restored dimensional robustness and discriminability.
