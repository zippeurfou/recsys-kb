## Summary

1.  Industrial ad recommendation models face high costs due to sparse ID-list features requiring large embedding tables.
2.  To tackle this, LO-FAR introduces an efficient, CPU-only workflow ranking these features solely based on their standalone predictive signal using simple local estimators.
3.  This method is shown to complete the ranking task quickly (about two CPU-hours) and effectively preserve downstream performance gains across different retained feature budgets, making it a practical production choice under cost constraints despite being simpler than interaction-aware alternatives.

## Technical note

LO-FAR is a CPU-only, model-agnostic workflow for ranking sparse high-cardinality ID-list features in an industrial DLRM ad ranker, scoring each feature by its stand-alone held-out predictive signal (per-ID empirical positive rate with kNN back-off for rare IDs) rather than GPU retraining loops. On a production dataset of over 1M interactions and 475 sparse features it completes ranking in ~2 CPU-hours (under $100 vs over $4,000 and multi-GPU-days for shuffle-permutation importance and Binary Stochastic Neurons), preserving downstream Normalized Entropy gains on CTR and CVR across budgets of 100-400 retained features and yielding 40-75% deterministic sparse-embedding storage reduction. It is framed as a cheap, repeatable first-stage filter that trades interaction-awareness for turnaround and operational simplicity. Not a retrieval-plus-ranking model.
