## Technical note

This Amazon paper proposes a Whole-Page Experience Optimization framework for e-commerce search-results pages that have evolved into non-linear 2D layouts interleaving organic results and widgets. It formulates SRP optimization as a contextual bandit with a composite scalar reward (page non-abandonment, short-term revenue, and long-term satisfaction). The key contribution DV-WPX is a causal-inference framework using quasi-experimental variation to link whole-page quality to long-term (12-week) customer spend, modeling position-dependent sensitivity via page regions rather than a continuous decay. Online A/B testing showed +1.86% brand relevance and a statistically significant +0.05% revenue uplift, beating clicks-only and CTR-proxy reward variants.
