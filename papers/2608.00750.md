## Summary

Generative recommenders decode items using semantic identifiers (SIDs), but standard supervised training struggles with assigning credit effectively due to sparse feedback from only seeing item-level outcomes during decoding. This limits optimization towards higher downstream utility, so the authors propose Hierarchical Residual Policy Optimization (HRPO). HRPO converts item-level outcomes into dense token-wise learning signals by estimating prefix utilities and decomposing them into residual credits for each SID token, ultimately optimizing these tokens more effectively than traditional methods.

## Technical note

SID decoders are trained by supervised next-token prediction, so post-training with logged outcomes must map an item-level (terminal) reward onto every token of the SID path; uniform broadcasting gives undifferentiated supervision on shared prefixes and PPO-style advantages built from terminal feedback are non-comparable across positions. HRPO (Kuaishou) builds dense token-aligned signals in three steps: (a) deterministically map each user to a feature-defined cohort c (activity level, spend tier, region) and accumulate cohort-conditional counts/sums along the SID trie, shrinking toward the global mean with pseudo-count alpha to get a stable prefix utility mu_hat(c, y_1:t); (b) residualize along the path, r_hat_t = mu_hat(c,y_1:t) - mu_hat(c,y_1:t-1), and accumulate into a credit-to-go G_hat_t that telescopes to mu_hat(c,y_1:L) - mu_hat(c,y_1:t-1), making advantages comparable across candidates at the same position; (c) RRPO optimizes these with within-proposal position-wise normalized advantages, PPO clipping, and a KL trust region to a frozen reference policy (no logged propensities needed). On KuaiRand/KuaiSim, HRPO raises session total reward from 8.137 (SPRec) to 10.528 and EpisodeLen 14.14 -> 15.33 at comparable 56% coverage, and one-step reward 0.462 (GRPO) -> 0.548; it also beats interactive RL baselines (TD3/DDPG/HAC, 3-12% coverage) at 10.746 reward and 57% coverage. Ablations show cohorting is the biggest single contributor (10.528 -> 9.331 without it), then credit-to-go (9.872), KL (10.049), residual credit (10.132); online A/B in a large ads system gives Target Cost +0.168%/+0.186%/+3.490% across three IAA segments with exposure and cost roughly flat.
