## Technical note

This is a tutorial/survey on generative large recommendation models and emerging LLM-for-recommendation trends. It organizes two primary integration approaches: LLM-enhanced recommendation (using LLM knowledge to augment traditional recall/ranking) and generative/large recommendation models that reformulate recommendation generatively. The tutorial covers feature interaction and user-behavior modeling advances and situates recent works (HLLM, DeepFM, DCN v2, learnable tokenizers, LLM4Rec surveys) as background for practitioners moving toward LLM-based and generative recommenders.
