## Technical note

LifeAgentBench (from Google) is a large-scale QA benchmark of 22,573 questions for long-horizon, cross-dimensional, multi-user lifestyle-health reasoning over heterogeneous wearable/lifelog signals (activity, sleep, diet, stress), spanning basic retrieval to complex reasoning. It provides an extensible construction pipeline that aligns lifelog records into a relational database and derives executable SQL for verifiable ground truth, plus a standardized protocol. Evaluating 11 leading LLMs under Context Prompting and Database-augmented Prompting reveals major bottlenecks in long-horizon aggregation and cross-dimensional reasoning. The authors propose LifeAgent, a baseline agent integrating multi-step evidence retrieval with deterministic aggregation, achieving significant gains over two baselines. (Health-agent benchmark, tangential to recsys.)
