## Technical note

SparseCTR is an efficient long-term user-behavior model for CTR prediction that adapts sparse self-attention to the personalized, non-uniform temporal characteristics of recommendation. It first applies TimeChunking, a personalized time-aware method dividing each user's sequence into an equal number of variable-length chunks; then EvoAttention (Evolutionary Sparse Self-attention) uses three branches (global, transition, local) to jointly model long-term interests, interest transitions, and short-term interests; and RelTemporal encodes relative time (duration, hour, weekend) via learnable head-specific bias coefficients. SparseCTR exhibits a clear scaling law across three orders of magnitude of FLOPs, and online A/B tests increased CTR by 1.72% and CPM by 1.41%.
