## Summary

IntentRL addresses a challenge in deep research (DR) agents: their high autonomy often leads to unsatisfactory results on ambiguous queries due to the time-consuming nature of long-horizon tasks. To solve this, the framework trains agents to proactively clarify latent user intents before starting complex research. This is achieved through a scalable data generation pipeline and a two-stage reinforcement learning strategy, resulting in significantly improved task performance and intent accuracy compared to existing methods.

## Technical note

IntentRL trains proactive Deep Research agents to clarify latent user intent before launching expensive long-horizon research, addressing the autonomy-interaction dilemma where autonomously executing ambiguous queries wastes 30-minute research runs on off-target reports. It formulates intent clarification as a POMDP and tackles data scarcity with a Clarification Directed Acyclic Graph that expands few seed samples into thousands of shallow (query-fuzzification) and deep (rubric-derived) intent dialogue turns. A two-stage RL strategy first learns general interaction behavior via offline RL on C-DAG trajectories, then adapts online using an intent-aware user simulator that penalizes redundancy. Experiments show higher intent hit rate and downstream task performance, beating closed-source DR clarify modules and proactive LLM baselines, with gains scaling as the base DR agent improves.
