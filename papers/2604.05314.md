## Summary

Modern reranking in recommendation systems often uses generative models, but they struggle with finding the best items (lacking both global and local context) and have inconsistent training goals between the generator and evaluator. To solve these issues, the authors propose Next-Scale Generative Reranking (NSGR), a tree-based generative method that progressively builds recommendations from broad to specific, balancing different perspectives. NSGR uses a "next-scale generator" and a "multi-scale neighbor loss" for training, effectively guiding the generator at each step. This framework improves reranking quality and has been successfully deployed on the Meituan platform.

## Technical note

NSGR is a tree-based generative reranking framework deployed on Meituan food delivery that expands a recommendation list coarse-to-fine via a Next-Scale Generator using a 'one-generates-two' binary-split recursion over log2(m) steps. A Multi-Scale Evaluator built on HSTU and three-level semantic IDs predicts position-aware listwise CTR/CVR/GMV, and a multi-scale neighbor loss (GRPO-inspired) supplies scale-specific relative rewards to resolve generator-evaluator goal inconsistency. Offline it lifts AUC/GAUC over PRM, GRN, NAR4Rec, DCDR, NLGR, and YOLOR baselines, and reaches HR@1%=0.861 on the A_20^20 Meituan permutation space. Online A/B tests showed NSGR(20) improved CTR +2.89% and GMV +3.15% versus a YOLOR(8) baseline.
