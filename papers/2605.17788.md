## Technical note

This Bytedance work quantifies model uncertainty to balance reliability for Low-Active Users (LAUs) against diversity for High-Active Users (HAUs) on short-video and livestream platforms, where uncertainty approximates prediction-error risk and flags low-quality recommendations that could disengage low-active users. It uses uncertainty-calibrated recommendations to warn against risky suggestions for LAUs while identifying exploration opportunities for HAUs. The method draws on uncertainty-estimation and calibration techniques (e.g., MC dropout, conformal/selective prediction) to improve reliability for sparse-history users.
