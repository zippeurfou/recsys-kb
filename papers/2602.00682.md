## Technical note

RecGOAT addresses the semantic heterogeneity between generative LLM representations and ID-based collaborative signals in multimodal recommendation. It builds multimodal attentive graphs (item-item, user-item, user-user) enhanced with Qwen3-Embedding-8B, LLaVA-1.5-7B and QwQ-32B, then aligns LLM modality features to recommendation IDs at two granularities: instance-level cross-modal contrastive learning (CMCL) and distribution-level optimal adaptive transport (OAT) minimizing the 1-Wasserstein distance. It proves the unified representation has strictly lower target error, bounded by the Wasserstein distance and InfoNCE loss. On three public benchmarks it reaches SOTA (up to 59-70% gains over unaligned LM features) and is validated on a large-scale online advertising platform.
