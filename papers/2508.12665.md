## Technical note

Xiaohongshu addresses short-video watch time prediction by observing coarse-grained skewness (quick-skips) and fine-grained multimodal diversity across user/video granularities. They assume watch time follows an Exponential-Gaussian Mixture (EGM) distribution and propose EGMN, which uses a shared hidden encoder plus a mixture parameter generator with a gated network to parameterize the exponential (skewness) and Gaussian (diversity) components end-to-end, using the expected value as the prediction. Offline experiments on public datasets and online A/B tests in Xiaohongshu's short-video feed show EGMN outperforms state-of-the-art label-normalization and task-transformation methods with superior coarse-to-fine distribution fitting.
