## Summary

1.  Adobe Express faces a problem where LTR models trained primarily on US click data over-represent US content in other locales, suppressing local content discoverability and degrading ranking quality due to cross-locale exposure bias.
2.  Adding standard click feedback alone or VLM-derived relevance labels as auxiliary supervision improves semantic alignment but doesn't sufficiently boost the visibility of locally relevant content.
3.  The paper proposes a multi-objective framework combining clicks, VLM signals, and locale-specific boosting, which successfully improves overall ranking relevance while restoring stable localization across five different locales.

## Technical note

Adobe Express diagnoses cross-locale exposure bias in a click-trained linear SVM-rank system, where US-popular templates are over-served in growth locales and localization features are systematically underweighted. It adds VLM-generated graded relevance labels (0-3) as auxiliary listwise (ListNet top-1) supervision alongside the RankNet pairwise click loss to recover semantic localization signals. Multiplicative locale-aware boosting is applied to both objectives so locale-matching items get stronger training pressure only when relevant. Evaluated across US, JP, FR, DE, GB with offline metrics and GPT-4o LLM-as-a-judge, the combined LA-MO model improves relevance while restoring stable Local%@K.
