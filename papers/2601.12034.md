## Technical note

PUMA (Prompt-level User Migration Adapter) addresses the problem that user-specific soft prompts become obsolete whenever the foundation LLM in a '1+N' personalization system is upgraded, otherwise requiring full retraining of all prompts. PUMA decomposes migration into semantic incompatibility and migration efficiency, using a parameter-efficient adapter trained end-to-end to bridge the semantic gap between source and target models, plus a group-based user-selection strategy (grouping by prompt embeddings and output variance) to train on a small representative subset. Evaluated on personalized recommendation across model sizes and families (LLaMA 1B->3B, LLaMA->Qwen), it matches or surpasses from-scratch retraining while cutting computation by up to 98%, and supports chained and aggregated migration.
