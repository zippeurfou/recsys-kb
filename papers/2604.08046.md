## Summary

Current RAG systems often fail to effectively integrate retrieved documents due to conflicts with the LLM's internal knowledge, a problem termed the "integration bottleneck." To address this, the paper introduces GuarantRAG, a framework that decouples reasoning from evidence integration. It first generates an "Inner-Answer" using internal knowledge, then uses a novel Contrastive DPO objective to generate a "Refer-Answer" focused on suppressing hallucinations and extracting evidence from retrieved documents. Finally, a joint decoding mechanism fuses these two answers at the token level, improving accuracy by up to 12.1% and reducing hallucinations by 16.3% compared to standard baselines.

## Technical note

GuarantRAG addresses the RAG 'integration bottleneck' where LLMs ignore retrieved documents in favor of parametric priors (parametric override) or insert facts incoherently, which the authors measure at 67.3% integration failures on 500 knowledge-intensive queries. The framework decouples reasoning from evidence integration in three phases: generate an Inner-Answer from parametric knowledge, generate a Refer-Answer trained with a Contrastive DPO objective that treats retrieved documents as chosen and the model's own Inner-Answer as rejected to suppress internal hallucination, then fuse via a token-level Joint Decoding mechanism that keeps the Inner-Answer's reasoning skeleton while substituting factual tokens from the Refer-Answer on semantic divergence. A prior Knowledge Decision module filters unnecessary retrieval. Across five QA benchmarks it improves accuracy by up to 12.1% and reduces hallucinations by 16.3% versus static and dynamic RAG baselines.
