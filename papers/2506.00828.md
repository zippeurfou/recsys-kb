Therefore, only pointwise modeling solutions can be adopted, focusing solely on modeling the likelihood of clicks or conversions for items by users to learn user-item preferences, without the ability to capture the ranking information among different items directly. ## Technical note

Breaker is Meituan's single-slot recommendation model that removes shortcut bias, where DNNs with pointwise scoring exploit abundant user-side features to learn users' intrinsic click tendencies rather than the desired user-item preferences (their analysis shows adding item features only reduces log loss by 5e-5). Breaker adds an auxiliary deep-embedding user-clustering task combined with a multi-tower structure for cluster-specific preference modeling; clustering makes users within a cluster share similar intrinsic tendencies, increasing task difficulty and forcing the towers to learn user-item preferences. A delayed parameter update mechanism stabilizes end-to-end joint training of clustering and classification; offline and online experiments show it surpasses baselines and it is deployed serving tens of millions of Meituan users daily.
