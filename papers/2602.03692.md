## Summary

Generative Recommendation (GR), while efficient, suffers from a "bias amplification" issue where initial biases worsen during token generation, reducing diversity and harming user experience. To combat this, the paper introduces CARE, a cascaded reasoning framework designed to incorporate more diverse information progressively and allocate more computational resources during generation. Empirical results show that CARE significantly improves recommendation accuracy, diversity, and efficiency compared to existing GR models.

## Technical note

This paper addresses bias amplification in generative recommendation, where initial biases compound during autoregressive token generation, reducing diversity and hurting user experience. Viewing generation through the lens of cascaded ranking, it proposes CARE, a cascaded reasoning framework that progressively injects more diverse information and allocates more computation across generation stages. Empirically CARE improves recommendation accuracy, diversity, and efficiency over existing generative-recommendation models. The work bridges reasoning-style test-time compute with generative recommendation decoding.
