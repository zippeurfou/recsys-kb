## Summary

1.  Effective e-commerce search relevance modeling is crucial, and while Large Language Models (LLMs) using Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning offer advantages, they often rely on single-perspective reasoning and have high inference latency.
2.  To address these limitations, the paper introduces a novel framework combining Multi-Perspective CoT (MPCoT) for the teacher model and a robust training method (SFT + DPO), along with a new distillation technique called Latent Reasoning Knowledge Distillation (LRKD).
3.  LRKD allows a smaller "student" model to perform fast, low-latency inference while retaining the reasoning capabilities of the large teacher model, leading to significant improvements in both commercial performance and user experience in online A/B tests.

## Technical note

E-commerce relevance modeling with CoT-enhanced LLMs is limited by single-perspective reasoning and by high inference latency that forces distillation which discards the rationale at serving time. The teacher uses Multi-Perspective CoT (user intent, structured attribute matching, business rules) and combines SFT with DPO to build a robust reasoner. Latent Reasoning Knowledge Distillation (LRKD) gives the student a lightweight inference-time latent reasoning extractor so it internalizes the teacher's reasoning at low latency. Offline gains and a 1.42% online Revenue Per Mille improvement are reported on an ad-search platform serving tens of millions of users daily.
