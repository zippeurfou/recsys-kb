## Summary

Multimodal embedding models, derived from large multimodal language models (MLLMs), show promise but their embedding quality is often limited by standard contrastive learning methods, despite MLLMs being effective for generation tasks.

The paper introduces CoCoA (Collaborative Attention), a novel pre-training method designed to improve embedding quality by leveraging the MLLM architecture more effectively.

CoCoA restructures attention flow and uses an EOS-based reconstruction task, forcing the model to compress input semantics into the <EOS> token. Extensive experiments demonstrate that this approach significantly enhances embedding quality when applied to models like Qwen2-VL and Qwen2.5-VL.

## Technical note

CoCoA is a content-reconstruction pre-training paradigm that converts causal-attention MLLMs into strong multimodal embedding backbones. It runs three stages: warming up bidirectional attention with masked next token prediction (text) and masked autoencoding (images); an EOS-bridged reconstruction that uses attention truncation to force multimodal semantics into a single EOS token; and contrastive learning (InfoNCE with in-batch negatives) on the compressed EOS embeddings. On MMEB-V1 with Qwen2-VL/Qwen2.5-VL it reaches SOTA at small scale and competitive 7B results using far less pre-training data. Ablations indicate data quality, not quantity, drives the reconstruction gains.
