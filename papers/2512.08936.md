## Technical note

This paper presents a principle-based framework for developing and evaluating LLMs for personal health and wellness, centered on the SHARP principles (Safety, Helpfulness, Accuracy, Relevance, Personalization). It describes the Gemini-powered Fitbit Insights explorer, which routes user queries through query/date understanding, code generation, and explanation modules over wearable data (sleep, activity, heart metrics), and an end-to-end methodology combining generalist and clinical human evaluation, autoraters, and adversarial testing across an iterative lifecycle. Applied in a staged deployment to over 13,000 consented users, the framework surfaced issues invisible in isolated testing, providing a standardized approach for responsible health-LLM deployment.
