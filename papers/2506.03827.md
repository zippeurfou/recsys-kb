## Technical note

MoBGM (Multi-objective aligned Bidword Generation Model) from JD improves e-commerce search-advertising query rewriting for long-tail queries that fail to match merchant bidwords, jointly optimizing three objectives: query-bidword relevance, generated bidword authenticity, and advertising revenue. It comprises a generator (pre-trained and fine-tuned on e-commerce data), a discriminator that scores the three objectives, and a preference-alignment module that uses the discriminator's reward signal to align the generator with human preferences via multi-objective optimization. Extensive offline and online A/B experiments show MoBGM significantly outperforms the state of the art, and it is deployed handling hundreds of millions of requests daily with substantial commercial value.
