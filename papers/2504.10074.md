## Technical note

MMKB-RAG (Taobao/Alibaba) is a multi-modal knowledge-based RAG framework that leverages an MLLM's own knowledge boundaries to dynamically generate semantic tags for filtering retrieved documents, keeping only relevant/accurate references. Its three-stage token system decides whether retrieval is needed, evaluates per-document relevance, and verifies consistency across documents, replacing reliance on external annotation pipelines or auxiliary re-rankers. On knowledge-based VQA it improves over SOTA MLLM/RAG frameworks: +4.2% on E-VQA Single-Hop (+0.4% full) and +7.8%/+8.2%/+8.1% on InfoSeek Unseen-Q/Unseen-E/full.
