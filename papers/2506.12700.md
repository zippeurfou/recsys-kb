## Technical note

LinkedIn builds a cross-domain GNN for notification recommendation that unifies user, content, and activity signals from Feed, Email, and Notifications into a single heterogeneous graph (8.6B nodes, 100B+ edges, 15TB). The model adds a time-aware temporal component and adapts MMoE multi-task learning on top of GraphSAGE to jointly predict notification clicks and professional interactions. Deployed in production it beat single-domain baselines, yielding +0.62% CTR and +0.10% weekly active users in online A/B tests.
