## Summary

This paper introduces a large-scale Graph Neural Network system designed for LinkedIn's notification recommendations. It unifies signals from users, content (like articles), and activities into one cross-domain graph architecture. The model significantly outperforms single-domain baselines by incorporating temporal dynamics and multi-task learning, leading to measurable improvements in user engagement metrics like CTR and weekly active users during deployment.

## Technical note

Notification systems on professional platforms must fuse heterogeneous cross-domain signals, respect temporal sensitivity, and optimize competing objectives. The authors deploy a cross-domain GNN at LinkedIn that unifies user, content, and activity signals into a single large-scale graph, adding temporal modeling and multi-task learning. Training on the cross-domain structure significantly outperforms single-domain baselines on CTR prediction and professional engagement. In production it delivered a 0.10% lift in weekly active users and a 0.62% CTR improvement, showing cross-domain GNNs scale to high-impact real-world settings.
