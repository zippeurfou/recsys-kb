## Summary

1.  Existing benchmarks struggle to evaluate the complex, multi-shot, narrative-driven long-form videos being generated, failing to diagnose specific issues or account for user preferences.
2.  To address this, the authors introduce DirectorBench, a personalized multi-agent benchmark evaluating videos across five dimensions using structured metadata, user profiles, and specific checkpoints, identifying failures at the workflow level rather than just providing a single score.
3.  Human validation confirms DirectorBench effectively captures quality nuances and reveals profile-dependent failures hidden by aggregate scores, highlighting the need for diagnostic evaluation in long-form video generation.

## Technical note

DirectorBench is a personalized multi-agent diagnostic benchmark for long-form, multi-shot video generation, instantiated with 80 structured metadata entries, 7 user profiles, and 40 checkpoint criteria across five dimensions (script, visual, audio, cross-modal, stability). It separates objective intent (metadata: story arcs, shot design, camera control, consistency, audio) from subjective preference (profile priority weights and hard constraints), formalizing evaluation as f(m,u,G)->R. A multi-agent pipeline with dynamic checkpoint activation, tool-based evidence, confidence-aware aggregation, and profile-weighted scoring produces checkpoint-level diagnosis reports instead of a single aggregate score. Evaluating 4 workflows, 6 base LLMs, and 7 profiles reveals a between-unit bottleneck (transition quality averages 0.256, best 0.356) versus 0.71 prompt-level demand fulfillment, and human evaluation with 14 annotators validates dimension-level alignment.
