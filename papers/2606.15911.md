## Summary

This paper introduces **Interactor**, an innovative framework designed to automatically generate longer, more informative ad descriptions in sponsored search that incorporate relevant world knowledge beyond just clickbait optimization.

Unlike standard ad generation methods focusing on short titles for clicks, Interactor uses an iterative process guided by agentic Reinforcement Learning (RL), where a generative policy interacts continuously with specialized GenRMs acting as evaluators within its environment.

These **GenRMs** assess multi-dimensional qualities of the generated descriptions using both binary feedback and detailed reasoning, enabling the policy to refine and improve them iteratively until they effectively address user intents while maintaining accuracy regarding the landing page.

## Technical note

Interactor frames ad description generation in sponsored search as a multi-turn agentic RL problem where an LLM policy interacts with a customized environment of generative reward models (GenRMs). The GenRMs score two quality dimensions -- knowledge capacity and landing-page consistency -- returning both binary rubric-satisfaction signals and fine-grained reasoning feedback that the policy incorporates to iteratively refine descriptions across turns. A separate CTR estimation model trained on online logs (incrementally updated daily) supplies a scalar attractiveness reward, and actions are structured into think/retrieve/create/reward segments. Unlike single-pass, scalar-reward title generation, the reasoning feedback yields higher-quality rollouts and more efficient exploration; deployed since May 2026 in a search ads system with revenue and UX gains.
