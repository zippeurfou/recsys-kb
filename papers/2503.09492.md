## Technical note

LCRON (Learning Cascade Ranking as One Network) trains an entire multi-stage cascade ranking system (matching, pre-ranking, ranking, mix-ranking) as one unified network, addressing objective misalignment and lack of cross-stage collaboration in independently-trained stages. It introduces L_e2e, a surrogate loss that is the lower bound of the differentiable survival probability of ground-truth items through the whole cascade (aligning with end-to-end recall), plus a per-stage L_single (built on differentiable sorting, extending ARF's relaxed loss) that tightens the bound and supplies stage-level supervision, combined via uncertainty weighting. On the public RecFlow benchmark it outperforms all baselines under streaming evaluation; an online A/B test in a real advertising system gave +4.10% advertising revenue and +1.60% user conversions over FS-LTR.
