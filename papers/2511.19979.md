## Technical note

This is the proposal for the 2nd Workshop on Human-Centered Recommender Systems (HCRS), arguing that traditional accuracy/click/engagement metrics no longer capture what matters to humans and calling for a shift toward systems that understand, involve, and benefit people. It is organized around three thematic axes: Human Understanding (modeling intent, cognition, affect, and satisfaction/trust beyond clicks), Human Involvement (interactive, co-adaptive, controllable systems), and Human Impact (values like safety, fairness, transparency, well-being, and societal welfare). It convenes recsys, HCI, AI safety, and social computing researchers via keynotes, panels, and papers spanning LLM-based interactive recommenders to societal welfare optimization. (Workshop overview, not an empirical method paper.)
