Online Travel Platforms (OTPs) have been working on improving their hotel Search & Ranking (S&R) systems that facilitate efficient matching between consumers and hotels. ## Technical note

MERIT is Alibaba/Fliggy's merchant-incentive ranking model for hotel search and ranking that incorporates merchant objectives alongside consumer and platform interests to create an incentive loop. It defines a Merchant Competitiveness Index (MCI) for hotel quality and adds a Merchant Tower (with a monotonic neural network for interpretable, explicit quality-to-ranking relationships) alongside CTR and CVR towers, plus a Multi-objective Stratified Pairwise Loss to reconcile short-term vs long-term revenue and break the Matthew-effect feedback loop. Offline experiments show MERIT optimizes both consumer and merchant demands, and an online A/B test achieved a 3.02% MCI improvement; it is deployed on Fliggy serving tens of millions of consumers.
