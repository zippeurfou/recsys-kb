## Summary

This paper introduces Pailitao-MMSearch, a novel foundation model designed specifically for e-commerce multimodal search. Existing systems face challenges handling cross-modal queries or integrating general models lacking product domain knowledge effectively.

The authors present three key innovations to bridge this gap: the Hybrid Semantic ID approach (HybSID), a two-stage continual pre-training strategy, and a hybrid reasoning post-training pipeline.

Based on the Qwen model family and tested extensively on Taobao's platform, Pailitao-MMSearch demonstrates significant improvements in online performance compared to traditional multimodal search methods.

## Technical note

Pailitao-MMSearch is a native e-commerce multimodal (image+text) search foundation model on Taobao's Pailitao, built on Qwen3 plus Qwen2-VL that reformulates search as end-to-end autoregressive product generation. Its HybSID product tokenization combines three residual-quantized discrete semantic codes (L=3, K=8192) with a continuous multimodal embedding emitted via a special <|emb_token|>, giving coarse generalization plus fine-grained discrimination; a two-stage continual pre-training injects product and user-behavior knowledge then uses On-Policy Distillation (OPD) against the base model to counter catastrophic forgetting; and a hybrid-reasoning post-training adds multi-task SFT, difficulty-aware chain-of-thought, GRPO RL with verifiable product-grounded rewards, and multi-expert OPD fusion. Online A/B tests deliver cumulative +13.61% GMV and +8.21% transaction volume on the PeiWoGuang floor (generative retrieval +1.02% GMV overall; generative ranking +9.94% GMV on the floor).
