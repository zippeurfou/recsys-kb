## Summary

The paper addresses the challenge of predicting online ad click value using Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV), a continuous business metric, rather than just conversion probability. To tackle this, the authors introduce TRACE, a new benchmark dataset providing complete user click transaction sequences for delayed feedback modeling in an online setting. They found two key insights: GMV labels evolve rapidly requiring online training, and repurchase samples differ significantly from single-purchase samples, necessitating separate modeling. Motivated by these, they propose READER, a dual-branch model that selectively uses expert predictions based on repurchase likelihood and dynamically adjusts targets, which they show significantly outperforms baseline models on their benchmark.

## Technical note

The paper studies delayed-feedback modeling for post-click GMV (gross merchandise value) prediction in ad ranking, where a single click can yield multiple delayed purchases that cumulatively form a continuous label. It releases TRACE, a streaming benchmark of complete post-click transaction sequences from Taobao, and finds that online-updated models beat offline ones and that repurchase samples have a distinct label distribution from single-purchase samples. Their model READER uses a router to select single-purchase vs repurchase expert towers plus a label calibrator and partial-label unlearning to correct underestimation, improving accuracy by 2.19% and AUC by 0.86%.
