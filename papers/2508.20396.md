## Technical note

BiListing (Airbnb) aligns text and photos of a listing into single per-modality embeddings by adapting CLIP-style contrastive learning with LLMs and pretrained language-image models, overcoming CLIP's single photo:text pair and short context limits for listings with many images and long descriptions. It enables zero-shot semantic search, listing-to-listing search, and mitigates cold start. Deployed in Airbnb search ranking, it achieved a 0.425% NDCB gain and drove a statistically significant lift in bookings.
