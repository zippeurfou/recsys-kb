## Summary

Streaming recommendation systems face challenges with item indexing due to highly skewed and changing item distributions, which negatively impact existing Vector Quantization methods, causing poor assignments and imbalanced clusters. To address these issues, the paper introduces MERGE, a new item indexing paradigm that builds clusters from scratch, dynamically monitors usage, and forms hierarchical structures through merging. Experiments show MERGE significantly improves assignment accuracy, cluster balance, and separation compared to existing methods, with online A/B tests confirming substantial business metric improvements.

## Technical note

MERGE is an item-indexing (tokenization) paradigm for large-scale streaming recommendation that addresses VQ failures under skewed, non-stationary item distributions: poor assignment accuracy, imbalanced cluster occupancy, and weak cluster separation. Instead of a fixed-size codebook, MERGE builds clusters from scratch, matching items to clusters above a similarity threshold and updating centers via EMA, forming new clusters for unmatched items via a union-find algorithm, and dynamically monitoring/resetting occupancy. Fine-grained clusters are merged fine-to-coarse into a hierarchical index; offline experiments and online A/B tests show gains in assignment accuracy, cluster uniformity/separation, and business metrics.
