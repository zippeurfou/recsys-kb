## Technical note

Product Attribute Value Identification (PAVI) on second-hand e-commerce (Xianyu) suffers from cascading errors, poor out-of-distribution (OOD) handling, and weak generalization across classification, generation, and retrieval paradigms. MVP-RAG reframes PAVI as a retrieval-generation task: the product title/description is the query, and it retrieves both candidate attribute values (via the TACLR encoder) and similar same-category products (via BGE embeddings) as multi-level context. A Qwen2.5 LLM then generates standardized attribute values from this retrieved context, with OOD values injected during training to improve unknown-value discovery. Evaluated on Xianyu-PAVI (8,803 categories, 6.3M triples) with micro precision/recall/F1@1, MVP-RAG outperforms strong baselines including the SOTA retrieval method TACLR and is deployed in production.
