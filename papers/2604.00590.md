## Technical note

UniMixer proposes a unified scaling architecture for recommendation that theoretically bridges the three mainstream scaling paradigms: attention-based, TokenMixer-based (e.g. RankMixer), and factorization-machine-based (e.g. Wukong). By transforming the rule-based TokenMixer into an equivalent parameterized feature-mixing module, it lets token-mixing patterns be learned during training and removes the constraint that the number of heads equal the number of tokens. A lightweight UniMixing-Lite variant further compresses parameters and compute while improving scaling ROI. Extensive offline and online experiments demonstrate UniMixer's superior scaling efficiency and reduced GPU memory over the individual paradigms.
