## Technical note

CLSRec (Baidu) enriches behavior-based recommenders for cold users by fusing the user-user social graph, tackling social-relation noise and social/behavior interest inconsistency. It applies low-rank SVD to the user-item interaction matrix to reconstruct a denoised social graph, then uses contrastive learning (InfoNCE-style loss) to align original and reconstructed social-view embeddings as augmentation, plus a co-attention 'mutual distillation' step that disentangles aligned vs specific social/behavior interests fused by a gated routing (MoE-like) network, all trained with a BPR loss. On LastFM and Ciao it beats SocialLGN, LightGCN, DiffNet, GBSR and BPR (up to 1.7-3.4% overall gains), with much larger cold-user improvements exceeding 15% Precision, 13% Recall and 7% NDCG on LastFM.
