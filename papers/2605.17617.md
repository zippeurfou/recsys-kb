## Summary

1.  GraphMind addresses the challenge of automating complex, adaptive enterprise workflows by creating a self-evolving system from operational data.
2.  It operates in three phases: extracting structured workflow graphs from historical traces, executing tasks using a multi-agent graph traversal engine guided by LLMs, and refining the graph through an adaptation mechanism based on execution feedback.
3.  Deployed successfully in cloud services, GraphMind significantly outperformed baseline systems in automation and diagnostics, demonstrating that workflow graphs can learn and improve autonomously from operational experience.

## Technical note

GraphMind constructs, executes, and evolves action-centric workflow graphs from operational resolution traces to automate complex incident-investigation workflows with minimal human authoring. An offline pipeline extracts a fixed-schema graph of problems, actions, and causal relations from human traces; an online multi-agent traversal engine retrieves a relevant subgraph and interleaves graph-guided retrieval with LLM reasoning to exploit promising actions or explore new regions. Adaptive Traversal Reinforcement (ATR), inspired by Ant Colony Optimization, deposits reinforcement on successful trajectory edges and applies temporal decay to stale edges, letting the graph learn from execution feedback. Deployed across four production cloud database services and evaluated on 93 held-out incidents, it beats an Agentic Summary-RAG baseline in mitigation reach, hallucination rate, and throughput while using 8x less retrieval context; ATR cuts hallucination rate by 26%.
