## Summary

1.  Recommender systems relying on regression models face an often overlooked issue called "retransformation bias," which previous solutions have addressed externally with limited practical success in this domain.
2.  To overcome this, the authors introduce TranSUN and its generalization GTS, a novel "preemptive" paradigm that integrates bias learning directly into the model architecture from refinement during training.
3.  This approach provides theoretical guarantees for unbiasedness while maintaining strong empirical performance compared to existing post-hoc corrections, as demonstrated by successful real-world deployment in major e-commerce recommendation systems.

## Technical note

Regression models in recommenders apply target transformations (e.g. log, sqrt) to handle highly-skewed targets, but back-transforming predictions introduces retransformation bias (systematic under/over-estimation), which prior work only fixes post-hoc. The paper proposes TranSUN, a preemptive method that adds a jointly-learned auxiliary branch with a multiplicative (stop-gradient) bias-modeling loss to give theoretically guaranteed unbiasedness with superior convergence, and generalizes it into the GTS model family whose unbiasedness stems from conditional linearity rather than explicit bias learning. On synthetic and public data (CIKM16, DTMart) TranSUN keeps |SRE|<0.7% and cuts bias metrics (TRE/MRE) by >70% versus transformed MSE, and serves as a plug-and-play debiasing module for models like ZILN, MDME, WLR. Deployed as a pointwise GMV ranking model in Taobao's Guess-What-You-Like product and short-video scenarios (DAU>300M), online A/B tests show >+1.0% GMV gains.
