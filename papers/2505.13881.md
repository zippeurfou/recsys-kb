## Technical note

TranSUN (Alibaba/Taobao) is a preemptive method eliminating retransformation bias intrinsically from regression recommender models, where back-transforming a transformed-MSE prediction (used because skewed targets violate the Gaussian assumption) gives a biased estimate of the conditional mean. Via a joint bias-learning manner it offers theoretically guaranteed unbiasedness with empirically superior convergence, and is generalized into a Generalized TranSUN (GTS) model family serving as a generic framework for bias-free models. Deployed in Taobao's 'Guess What You Like' homepage for product and short-video recommendation (DAU >300M), experiments across domains demonstrate its superiority over MSE and LogMSE.
