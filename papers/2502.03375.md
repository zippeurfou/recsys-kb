Visualization recommendation aims to enable rapid visual analysis of massive datasets. In real-world scenarios, it is essential to quickly gather and comprehend user preferences to cover users from diverse backgrounds, including varying skill levels and analytical tasks. ## Technical note

This paper proposes an interactive personalized visualization recommendation (PVisRec) system that learns from users' feedback across rounds instead of relying on initial data for new users. Its core is Hier-SUCB, a contextual combinatorial semi-bandit with a learnable bias term modeling configuration-attribute relations and a hierarchical structure that reduces the effective action space. It proves an improved regret bound (same time rank, better action-space rank) and shows experimentally that Hier-SUCB matches offline methods and outperforms other bandit algorithms, handling cold-start via interactive exploration.
