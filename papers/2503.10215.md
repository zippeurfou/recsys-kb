## Technical note

This work introduces Adaptive Preference Aggregation (APA), a preference-aggregation strategy for AI alignment that overcomes RLHF's theoretical limitation of consolidating diverse users into a single entity. Drawing on social choice theory and a recently published balls-and-urn process that converges to a maximal lottery (a Condorcet-consistent solution concept), APA adapts to the user's context and inherits the maximal lottery's good properties. It ports the urn process to generative AI via function approximation, yielding an online algorithm that focuses annotator queries on important comparisons; it applies to both LLM fine-tuning (RLHF) and recommender systems, with soundness shown on generated datasets.
