## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Dense retrieval methods typically target unstructured text data represented as flat strings. However, e-commerce catalogs often include structured information across multiple fields, such as brand, title, and description, which contain important information potential for retrieval systems. We present Cascading Hierarchical Attention Retrieval Model (CHARM), a novel framework designed to encode structured product data into hierarchical field-level representations with progressively finer detail. ...

## Technical note

CHARM (Cascading Hierarchical Attention Retrieval Model) encodes structured multi-field e-commerce products (color, brand, category, title, description, bullet points) into a cascade of hierarchical field-level representations using a novel block-triangular attention mask over a shared-weight BiBERT dual-encoder, so each field token attends to its own and all higher-level fields. Field vectors are combined via learned softmax weights into an aggregated vector for a first-stage shortlist, then field-level vectors re-rank the shortlist, cutting cost from O(NM|F|) to O(N(M+k|F|)); training combines aggregated, per-field, and max InfoNCE losses with in-batch and hard negatives. On the Multi-Aspect Amazon Shopping Queries dataset (US/ES/JP) CHARM matches or beats BiBERT, MVR, and MURAL (e.g. US R@10 34.77, NDCG@50 45.20), and a shortlist of 50 preserves over 90% of the true top-10 matches.
