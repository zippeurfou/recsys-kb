## Summary

Pinterest developed the MTMD framework to address the challenge of optimizing different ad performance metrics (like CTR and CVR) across various domains (ad products like shopping, serving surfaces like search) within their lightweight ranking system. This system acts as an intermediate stage between retrieval and the final ranker in their ad recommendation cascade. The paper's key contributions include a unified architecture handling multiple tasks, integrating specialized domain experts with shared knowledge via novel components, leading to significant offline loss improvements (12-36%) that translated into measurable online cost reductions for Pinterest.

## Technical note

MTMD is a Multi-Task Multi-Domain two-tower lightweight ad ranker for Pinterest that unifies different surfaces (home feed, search, related pins), ad products, and prediction tasks (CTR, GCTR, OCTR, CVR) into one model. Its core Domain Expert block is an MoE combining task-specific deep/shallow experts, a domain-shared expert, and a task-shared expert with an expert-routing layer and a Squeeze-and-Excitation-based domain adaptation module (with per-domain batch norm) to handle missing domain features. Constrained modeling encodes task dependencies (e.g. long click conditioned on click), and using heavyweight-ranker scores as labels encourages funnel alignment; the shallow expert acts as a calibration term on the deep logits. A single MTMD model replaced 9 production models, improving offline loss 12-36% and delivering +2.41% CTR, +3.06% GCTR, and -1.96% CPC online.
