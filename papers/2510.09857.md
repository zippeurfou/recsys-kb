## Technical note

MTMD (Multi-Task Multi-Domain) (Pinterest) is a unified two-tower lightweight ad ranker that handles multiple prediction tasks (CTR for click ads, CVR for conversion ads), ad products, and serving surfaces (home feed, search, related items) in one model. Its core Domain Expert building block is a mixture-of-experts with fine-grained deep/shallow expert pairs per task, a domain-shared expert for common surface/product knowledge with domain adaptation for missing features, a task-shared expert, and an expert routing layer; it also uses heavyweight-ranker scores as labels for funnel alignment and constrains task dependencies (e.g., Good Click depends on Click). MTMD improved offline loss 12-36% and, deployed replacing 9 production models, lifted CTR +2.41%, GCTR +3.06%, and reduced CPC -1.96%.
