## Summary

Standard reranking in recommender systems struggles with optimizing item sequences due to vast search spaces and issues like heuristic label bias and sparse reward signals. To solve this, the paper introduces DeGRe (Dense-supervised Generative Reranking), which uses an offline Lookahead Evaluator with beam search to generate dense, cumulative rewards during training. These dense signals are then distilled into a lightweight Online Generator, enabling efficient, near-optimal sequence generation online. Experiments on benchmarks and a live deployment show DeGRe significantly improves recommendation quality.

## Technical note

DeGRe (Dense-supervised Generative Reranking) attacks the vast search space and sparse-reward, heuristic-label-bias problems of sequence-level reranking in recommenders. An offline Lookahead Evaluator uses beam search to generate dense, cumulative rewards over candidate item sequences during training. These dense signals are distilled into a lightweight Online Generator so near-optimal sequences can be produced efficiently at serving time. Benchmarks and a live deployment show DeGRe significantly improves recommendation quality, combining offline dense supervision with an online student generator.
