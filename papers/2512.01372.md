## Technical note

SSR (Structured Spectral Reasoning) is a frequency-aware multimodal graph recommendation framework that reasons over spectral structure instead of static frequency filtering. Its four-stage pipeline decomposes graph-based multimodal signals into spectral bands, modulates band reliability via Spectral Band Masking (training-time masking with a representation-consistency objective), fuses complementary bands with a graph-compatible hyperspectral operator (G-HSNO) using low-rank cross-band interaction, and aligns modality-specific bands with a spectral contrastive objective. On three multimodal Amazon datasets it consistently beats strong baselines, e.g. +5.0% Recall@10 over SMORE on Clothing and +10.0% Recall@20 over SMORE on Baby under user cold-start.
