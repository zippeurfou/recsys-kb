## Technical note

Kuaishou's Hierarchical Semantic RL (HSRL) resolves the vast, dynamic action-space bottleneck in RL-based recommendation by reframing RL over a fixed Semantic Action Space, encoding items as Semantic IDs for policy learning and mapping SIDs back to items via fixed lookup at execution. A coarse-to-fine Hierarchical Policy Network (HPN) generates SID tokens autoregressively with hierarchical residual state modeling to reduce representation-decision mismatch, while a Multi-level Critic (MLC) gives token-level value estimates for fine-grained credit assignment under sparse sequence-level rewards. Across public benchmarks and a large short-video advertising production dataset HSRL beats state-of-the-art; a 7-day online A/B test delivered +18.421% ADVV and +1.251% revenue.
