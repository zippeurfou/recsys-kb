## Technical note

MixFormer (ByteDance/Douyin) is a unified Transformer-style large recommender that jointly models user behavior sequences and dense feature interaction in a single shared parameter space, resolving the co-scaling trade-off of decoupled stacked/parallel designs. Each MixFormer block has a Query Mixer (a parameter-free HeadMixing plus per-head FFN replacing self-attention for heterogeneous non-sequential features), Cross Attention (feature-conditioned aggregation over the behavior sequence), and per-head Output Fusion. A User-Item Decoupling variant (UI-MixFormer) adds a mask enabling Request-Level Batching to reuse user-side computation, cutting ~36% FLOPs / >30% serving latency. On trillion-scale Douyin data MixFormer-medium beats stacked/parallel baselines (STCA->RankMixer, OneTrans) in AUC/UAUC and shows better dense- and sequence-length scaling; online A/B on Douyin and Douyin Lite lifted Active Days, Duration, and engagement (e.g. +0.39% Finish, +0.70% Comment on Douyin).
