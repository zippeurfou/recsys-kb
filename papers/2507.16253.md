## Technical note

RLIV-UA (Kuaishou) uses reinforcement learning to reinforce the Lifelong Interaction Value (LIV) of user-author pairs in short-video social recommendation, going beyond immediate CTR or user-side long-term engagement to model bidirectional, evolving user-author relationships. To handle long irregular intervals and the huge UA state space it proposes a Sparse Cross-Request Interaction MDP (SCRI-MDP) with an Adjacent State Approximation (ASA) method to build RL samples under storage limits, plus Multi-Task Critic Learning (MTCL) that exploits denser signals across the progressive click to follow to gift funnel, and an auxiliary supervised task for convergence. Offline experiments and online A/B tests show both higher user satisfaction and higher platform profits.
