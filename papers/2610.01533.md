## Summary

This paper argues that existing Semantic ID (SID) methods, which focus solely on item content, are incomplete. It proposes Graph-Informed Semantic IDs (GrIS), a framework that views SID construction as a hierarchical graph partitioning problem, where nodes represent items with semantic content and edges represent collaborative signals. By combining explicit graph construction and recursive partitioning, GrIS consistently improves recommendation performance, achieving gains of up to +52% Hit@10 over current state-of-the-art collaborative filtering-aware methods.

## Technical note

Huawei's GrIS recasts Semantic ID construction for generative recommendation as hierarchical partitioning of an item graph whose nodes carry text-embedding content and whose edges carry collaborative co-occurrence signal, which separates two design axes (graph construction and recursive partition algorithm) and makes graph-free tokenizers such as TIGER's RQ-VAE, RQ-KMeans and LETTER the empty-graph special case. RecDMoN recursively splits each cluster with DMoN differentiable graph pooling (relaxed modularity plus a collapse regularizer), so an item's SID is its root-to-leaf path, while RQ-GAE feeds APPNP-propagated item embeddings into RQ-VAE (4 codebooks of 256) and adds a mini-batch weighted neighborhood-softmax graph reconstruction loss on the quantized latents. With a fixed T5-based TIGER backbone, RecDMoN is best on Amazon Beauty, Sports and Toys (Toys H@10 8.16, +52% over LETTER) but cannot run on Books or Yelp because it materializes a dense adjacency matrix, while RQ-GAE scales and ranks top two on Books, Beauty, Sports and Yelp (Books H@10 4.60, +34.1% over LETTER and +16.5% over S2GR); HSTU still wins on MIND's long histories. Ablations show the graph contrastive loss is the more reliable RQ-GAE component, a deeper and narrower hierarchy (4 levels, branching 6) works best for RecDMoN, RecDMoN yields far more balanced clusters than spectral clustering (level-1 Gini 0.06 to 0.18 vs 0.51 to 0.74), and switching from sentence-t5 to Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B helps both.
