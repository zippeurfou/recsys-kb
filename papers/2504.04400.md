Generative recommendation autoregressively generates item identifiers to recommend potential items. Existing methods typically adopt a one-to-one mapping strategy, where each item is represented by a single identifier. However, this scheme poses issues, such as suboptimal semantic modeling for low-frequency items and limited diversity in token sequence data. ## Technical note

MTGRec (Huawei) improves generative recommender pre-training via Multi-identifier item Tokenization, breaking the one-to-one item-identifier mapping that hurts low-frequency items and token-sequence diversity. It treats RQ-VAE tokenizer checkpoints from adjacent training epochs as semantically relevant tokenizers, associating each item with multiple identifiers so one interaction sequence yields several token-sequence data groups, and applies curriculum recommender pre-training guided by first-order-gradient data-influence estimation to dynamically weight groups, fine-tuning with a single tokenizer at the end. On three public benchmarks MTGRec significantly outperforms traditional and generative baselines in effectiveness and scalability; code is released.
