## Technical note

Existing conversational recommender system (CRS) evaluation fragments recommendation (Recall) and dialogue (BLEU) into rule-based metrics that misalign with real user experience. The authors propose CoRE, an LLM-based user-centric framework combining an LLM-As-Evaluator that scores 12 experience factors (0-4 Likert) across four dimensions with a Multi-Agent Debater of four role-playing agents (common user, domain expert, linguist, HCI expert) that converge on a unified 0-100 overall score. Evaluating four CRSs (neural BARCOR/KBRD/UniCRS and GPT-based CHATCRS) on ReDial and OpenDialKG with simulated dialogues, CoRE (GPT-4o-mini evaluator/debater) aligns significantly better with 10 human annotators' overall judgments than rule-based metrics, and finds neural CRSs deficient in Semantic Relevance, Explainability, and Proactiveness.
