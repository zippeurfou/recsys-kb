## Technical note

Kuaishou's Congrats addresses the likelihood trap in generative reranking, where maximizing sequence likelihood yields repetitive high-frequency ('hot') item lists and low diversity. It introduces a non-autoregressive Graph-structured Model that replaces the linear decoder with a directed acyclic graph decoder plus a learnable graph transition module, expanding the decoding exploration space for diverse sequences while explicitly modeling item dependencies for accuracy. A Consistent Differentiable Training method integrates an evaluator into the generator's training loop via Gumbel-Softmax reparameterization so gradients flow from user-preference signals back to the generator. Offline experiments and deployment on Kuaishou (300M+ DAU) show significant gains in both recommendation quality and diversity.
