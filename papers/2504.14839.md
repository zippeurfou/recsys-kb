## Technical note

This paper explores l0-inspired sparsification for inference-free sparse retrievers, where documents are encoded offline and query-time reduces to lightweight term matching, arguing that FLOPS regularization (designed for symmetric Siamese encoders like SPLADE) is suboptimal for the asymmetric inference-free setting. It proposes two techniques, l0 mask FLOPS and l0 approximation activation, which apply regularization only to document-side representations exceeding a sparsity threshold while letting already-sparse representations optimize freely for ranking. Built on IDF-SPLADE-doc-distill and evaluated out-of-domain on BEIR, the method achieves state-of-the-art performance among inference-free sparse retrievers, comparable to leading Siamese sparse retrievers, with a favorable efficiency-effectiveness trade-off.
