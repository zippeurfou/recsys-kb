## Summary

Previous AI personas often provided unverifiable, persuasive but ungrounded advice for design decisions. PersonaCite addresses this by creating an "evidence-bounded" agentic system that retrieves and relies on actual voice-of-customer data during interactions, ensuring responses are verifiable and providing source attribution. This approach, validated through interviews and a deployment study, shows promise for more responsible AI use in design, proposing Persona Provenance Cards as a way to document the AI's evidence base.

## Technical note

PersonaCite reframes LLM-based synthetic personas as evidence-bounded research instruments to counter the tendency of prompt-based personas to give persuasive but unverifiable answers. Its agentic architecture ingests multimodal voice-of-customer (VoC) artifacts, vectorizes them, and at each conversation turn retrieves actual evidence, constrains responses to that evidence, explicitly abstains when data is missing, and provides response-level source attribution (building on agentic context engineering). A 3-month deployment study with 14 industry experts surfaced perceived benefits, validity concerns, and design tensions, and proposes Persona Provenance Cards as a documentation pattern.
