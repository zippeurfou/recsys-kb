## Summary

This paper introduces MILES-Rec, a novel framework for cross-domain POI (Points of Interest) recommendation. It addresses the challenge of inferring users' specific needs from map interactions by synthesizing their history and profile. MILES-Rec translates inferred implicit intents into prioritized natural language queries, using techniques like intent-aligned policy optimization and a credit assignment mechanism to ensure query relevance and coverage, and its effectiveness is validated on a large-scale dataset.

## Technical note

Map apps log mostly utility actions (destination searches, routes, navigation) that only indirectly reveal local-service demand, so MILES-Rec (Alibaba/Amap) recasts cross-domain POI recommendation as inferring implicit intents from profile plus 60-day map history and emitting a prioritized list of up to ten natural-language queries that drive a spatial hybrid retriever over a 2M-POI pool. Stage 1 SFTs Qwen3.5 (4B, 9B, 27B, 122B-A10B) on 19,006 Qwen-235B-generated, GPT-5.1/DeepSeek-refined, human-reviewed three-stage reasoning demonstrations (situation understanding, intent expansion, query prioritization). Stage 2 applies intent-aligned GRPO on 3,000 users whose subsequent target-domain interactions define the reward: a fine-tuned BGE-reranker query-to-service-category matcher times Qwen3-Embedding-8B query-POI cosine, aggregated by maximum-weight one-to-one matching with a log2 rank discount, minus duplicate-query and length penalties; two-level credit assignment gives every token the list-level advantage and query-text tokens an additional per-query local advantage, with a dual-clipped surrogate and KL to the SFT reference. On MILES-Bench (MapRec-Rich and MapRec-Sparse, 1,000 users each) GRPO beats SFT on Recall@50, NDCG@50, SIA@10 and SDA at all four scales (4B Hit@50 1.50% to 3.40%, NDCG@50 0.171% to 0.325% on Rich), MILES-Rec beats GLoSS, Rec-R1, PIGEON-history and Content-ComiRec-SA on every retrieval metric, and ablations show removing history zeroes NDCG@50, full 60-day history beats 7-day, and two-level credit beats list-only, shared-credit and Rank-GRPO.
