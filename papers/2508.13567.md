## Summary

Long-term user behavior sequences are a goldmine for businesses to explore users' interests to improve Click-Through Rate. However, it is very challenging to accurately capture users' long-term interests from their long-term behavior sequences and give quick responses from the online serving systems. ## Technical note

ENCODE is an efficient two-stage long-term behavior model for CTR that satisfies two requirements existing methods break: using the entire sequence (R1) and keeping learned interests relevant to the target (R2). Offline it clusters the whole behavior sequence to extract accurate interests, using a metric-learning-based dimension-reduction algorithm that preserves relative pairwise distances to cut clustering cost. Online it takes off-the-shelf user interests to score associations with target items, and it uses the same relevance metric consistently through the whole pipeline to avoid information loss. Experiments on industrial and public datasets show better precision-efficiency trade-off than SOTA.
