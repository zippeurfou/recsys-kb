## Technical note

Kuaishou's GRADE replaces static, manually-tuned Multi-Task Fusion weights with personalized RL, overcoming instability and sparse rewards of actor-critic methods. It uses a critic-free Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) paradigm comparing candidate weight groups, a Dirichlet distribution for structured exploration that inherently satisfies non-negative sum-to-one weight constraints, and a composite reward combining sparse user feedback, dense model priors, and rule-based constraints, bootstrapped from a supervised LambdaLoss learning-to-rank baseline. Deployed in Kuaishou's marketplace search for hundreds of millions of users, GRADE achieved +0.595% CTR, +1.193% CVR, +1.788% OPM, and +1.568% total order volume in large-scale A/B tests.
