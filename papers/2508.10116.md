## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Item information, such as titles and attributes, is essential for effective user engagement in e-commerce. However, manual or semi-manual entry of structured item specifics often produces inconsistent quality, errors, and slow turnaround, especially for Customer-to-Customer sellers. Generating accurate descriptions directly from item images offers a promising alternative. ## Technical note

eBay tackles generating schema-compliant item titles and aspects directly from product images by fine-tuning a multimodal LLM (OPAL). The pipeline pairs MLLM-Assisted Conformity Enhancement (using InternVL2.5-78B to strip non-visual text and reduce the modality gap) with LLM-Assisted Contextual Understanding (LLaMA 3.1-Instruct synthetic multi-turn dialogues), then applies visual instruction tuning followed by DPO on 20K preference pairs per backbone. Trained on ~890K instruction and ~800K conversation pairs, OPAL consistently outperforms in-house baselines on Rouge-L, aspect F1, and schema recall across MLLM backbones, improving generation quality and completion rates while mitigating hallucination.
