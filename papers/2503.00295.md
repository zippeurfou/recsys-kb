## Technical note

MO-ODPO (Multi-Objective Online DPO) is the first application of online Direct Preference Optimization to multi-objective LLM alignment, training a single preference-conditional policy that adapts to new objective-weight combinations at inference. It uses a prompt-conditioning mechanism (objective weights prefixed to input) with on-policy sampling to avoid offline distribution shift, avoiding the exponential cost of per-combination specialist policies and the inference overhead of parameter-based model souping. On Anthropic-HH and Reddit TL;DR benchmarks, MO-ODPO Pareto-dominates baselines like MORLHF, MODPO, and reward-soup methods while offering strong inference-time steerability between conflicting objectives.
