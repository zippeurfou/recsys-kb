## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

The two primary tasks in the search recommendation system are search relevance matching and click-through rate (CTR) prediction -- the former focuses on seeking relevant items for user queries whereas the latter forecasts which item may better match user interest. Prior research typically develops two models to predict the CTR and search relevance separately, then ranking candidate items based on the fusion of the two outputs. ## Technical note

PRECTR is a unified framework that integrates personalized search relevance matching and CTR prediction into a single model via a conditional probability fusion mechanism, replacing the divide-and-conquer paradigm of two separate models. To prevent non-convergence and over-exposure of high-CTR but irrelevant items, it adds two-stage training and a semantic consistency regularization, plus a personalized relevance incentive module that infers a user's relevance sensitivity from historical preferences on similar queries. Experiments on production data (Xianyu) and online A/B testing show PRECTR improves consistency and ranking performance over prior methods.
