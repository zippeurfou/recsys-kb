## Technical note

EGDB (Expert-Guided Diffusion planner for Auto-Bidding, Kuaishou) improves generative auto-bidding, which unlike MDP-based RL plans over long horizons but suffers from weak scalar-return conditioning and diffusion sampling latency. It uses a blended-forcing mechanism combining teacher-forcing stabilization with a VAE-enhanced decode forcing to imitate expert trajectories while exploring beyond demonstrations, a dual-conditioned planner that implicitly encodes expert behavioral semantics while explicitly enforcing budget constraints, and a step-skipping accelerated sampling strategy to meet real-time latency. Offline experiments and online A/B testing show an 11.29% increase in conversions and 12.36% revenue growth over the baseline.
