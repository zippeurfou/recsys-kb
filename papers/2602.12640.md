## Summary

Diffusion models, the current state-of-the-art for text-to-image generation, are often too slow due to their iterative nature, while attempts to speed them up compromise quality. The paper introduces ImageRAGTurbo, which addresses this by retrieving relevant text-image pairs for a given prompt and using them to condition the diffusion model's denoising process. This retrieval-augmented approach, combined with a trainable adapter blending the retrieved content with the prompt, significantly improves image quality and prompt alignment while maintaining fast generation speeds.

## Technical note

ImageRAGTurbo brings retrieval augmentation to few-step (one-to-four step) text-to-image diffusion to recover the prompt fidelity that distillation sacrifices while preserving low latency. Given a prompt it retrieves relevant text-image pairs and injects them into the UNet denoiser's H-space; a preliminary study shows that directly editing H-space with retrieved content already improves fidelity but needs costly inference-time hyperparameter search. To avoid that, it trains a lightweight H-space adapter that blends retrieved content with the target prompt via cross-attention, giving efficient finetuning versus full few-step model training. Experiments show it beats existing few-step approaches on image quality and text-to-image alignment at comparable inference speed, with one-step output rivaling 50-step Stable Diffusion.
