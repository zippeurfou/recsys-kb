## Technical note

Refine-POI from NVIDIA is a reinforcement-fine-tuning framework for next point-of-interest recommendation with LLMs that solves topology-blind semantic-ID indexing and single-answer supervised fine-tuning limitations. It introduces a hierarchical self-organizing-map quantization strategy to generate topology-aware semantic IDs where codebook coordinate proximity reflects semantic similarity, and uses a policy-gradient framework to optimize generation of top-k ranked lists and reasoning without requiring extra ground-truth labels, escaping SFT 'answer fixation.' On three real-world datasets Refine-POI significantly outperforms state-of-the-art baselines for accurate and explainable next-POI recommendation.
