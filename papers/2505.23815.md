Accommodating human preferences is essential for creating aligned LLM agents that deliver personalized and effective interactions. Recent work has shown the potential for LLMs acting as writing agents to infer a description of user preferences. Agent alignment then comes from conditioning on the inferred preference description. ## Technical note

PROSE (Preference Reasoning by Observing and Synthesizing Examples) improves the precision of user-preference descriptions inferred from writing samples for aligning LLM writing agents, building on CIPHER with two innovations: iterative refinement of the inferred preference description until generations align, and verification of inferred preferences across multiple user demonstrations. Evaluated on PRELUDE and a new benchmark PLUME with Qwen2.5 7B/72B Instruct, GPT-mini, and GPT-4o on summarization and email writing, PROSE outperforms CIPHER by 33%. PROSE and in-context learning are complementary, combining for up to 9% improvement over ICL alone and 47% over CIPHER.
