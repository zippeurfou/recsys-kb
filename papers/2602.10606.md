## Technical note

S-GRec (Tencent) is a semantic-aware generative recommendation framework that decouples an online lightweight SID-based generator from an offline LLM semantic judge, avoiding real-time LLM inference. Its Personalized Semantic Judge (PSJ) uses a two-stage pipeline (aspect-level scoring over four dimensions -> GRPO-learned user-conditional aggregation from pairwise feedback) to turn LLM semantics into stable scalar rewards. To prevent semantic signals from conflicting with business goals, Asymmetric Advantage Policy Optimization (A2PO) anchors optimization on business rewards (e.g. eCPM) and injects group-relative semantic advantages only when consistent, querying the PSJ on only a sparse subset of training instances. Experiments on public benchmarks and a large-scale ad system show CTR gains and a +1.19% GMV (+1.55% GMV-Normal) lift in online A/B tests without serving-time LLM cost.
