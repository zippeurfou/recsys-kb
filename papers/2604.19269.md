## Technical note

CS3 strengthens two-tower retrievers in online multi-stage recommenders via three plug-in modules: Cycle-Adaptive Structure (per-layer self-correction/denoising), Cross-Tower Synchronization (EMA-cached partner-tower vectors for embedding-space alignment), and Cascade-Model Sharing (reusing downstream ranker intermediate outputs). Applied to DSSM, IntTower, IHM-DAT, and RCG backbones, CS3 consistently lifts AUC (e.g. DSSM 0.6194->0.6855 on TaobaoAd, 0.6646->0.7484 on KuaiRand) and cuts LogLoss. Deployed in a large-scale advertising system it yielded up to 8.36% revenue improvement across three scenarios while keeping ms-level latency and online-learning compatibility.
