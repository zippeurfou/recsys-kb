## Technical note

Airbnb presents interleaving and online counterfactual evaluation methods to accelerate search-ranking experimentation where conversion-based A/B tests are slow due to low traffic and delayed booking outcomes. Competitive-pair-based interleaving is unbiased and gives a ~50x speedup versus A/B in production; online counterfactual evaluation (addressing selection bias via importance sampling) further improves sensitivity up to 100x. It is claimed the first work implementing both in production side by side, used daily to select A/B test candidates.
