## Summary

This paper addresses challenges in large-scale e-commerce search using embedding-based retrieval (EBR). Standard dual-encoders face a performance drop due to being trained on limited candidate pools versus needing to evaluate against hundreds of millions during inference. To overcome this, they introduce two innovations: Hybrid Hard Negative Mining combines online and offline methods for much better negative sampling diversity, and Legacy-Aware Distillation uses Warm-Start with DistilBERT knowledge transfer to upgrade the model backbone smoothly while preserving domain expertise.

These techniques form a unified pipeline deployed at Walmart. Offline experiments show significant improvements in key metrics like NDCG@5 through their enhanced Negative Mining approach alone (+3.42%). Online A/B testing confirmed these gains translate directly to business impact, achieving a +7.34% lift in NDCG@5 and a notable increase of +0.50% in gross revenue.

The paper presents the successful deployment of this pipeline at Walmart scale, demonstrating substantial improvements in both retrieval quality (NDCG@5) and real-world business metrics like gross revenue by effectively managing negative samples during training and ensuring a smooth transition to newer models with higher capacity.

## Technical note

Walmart's embedding-based retrieval (EBR) pipeline tackles the dual-encoder training-inference gap (trained on narrow pools but must discriminate against hundreds of millions of items) and the risk of upgrading encoder capacity. It scales negatives via Online Cross-Batch Sampling (differentiable all-gather across GPUs, expanding the pool from B to B x D) plus Hybrid Offline Hard Negative Mining that combines a BERT cross-encoder (distilled from a Mistral-7B teacher) with metadata guardrails, and stabilizes the DistilBERT to GTE-base upgrade with Legacy-Aware Warm-Start Distillation (KL divergence on the legacy model's score distribution). Offline, the full GTE-base + data-refresh + cross-batch + hybrid-negatives + warm-start stack reached +4.00% EM Recall@20 over the production baseline. Deployed live, it delivered +7.34% NDCG@5 (human eval) and a statistically significant +0.50% gross-revenue lift (p=0.03).
