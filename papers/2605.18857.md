## Technical note

This Meta paper introduces Bits-over-Random (BoR), a chance-corrected selectivity metric, BoR=log2(P_obs/P_rand) with a hypergeometric baseline, showing near-perfect retrieval success can equal random selection. On 20 Newsgroups, BM25 and SPLADE both report >99% coverage success at K=100 yet BoR≈0 (random-level), and selectivity collapses once the expected coverage ratio K·R_q/N exceeds 3-5. RAG accuracy degrades at large K consistent with the near-zero BoR ceiling, while BoR stays positive on BEIR/SciFact and MS MARCO (41 systems within 0.2 bits of the ceiling despite a 13-point recall gap); the collapse also afflicts LLM agent tool selection with small catalogs (N=50-500).
