## Technical note

AMEN (All-domain Moveline Evolution Network) (Taobao) proposes Next Interest Flow (NIF), a generative pre-training paradigm modeling user intent as a continuous evolutionary trajectory on a high-dimensional latent interest manifold, avoiding the discretization-induced information collapse of LLM/ID-based generative CTR methods. Kinematic constraints from differential geometry govern the flow: interest diversity via tangent-space decomposition and evolution velocity via geodesic regularization. A bidirectional alignment strategy bridges generative pre-training and discriminative CTR fine-tuning, and a Temporal Sequential Pairwise (TSP) mechanism instills temporal causality. On a 6.7-billion-instance industrial dataset and Taobao A/B tests it achieves +0.87pt AUC and +11.6% CTCVR.
