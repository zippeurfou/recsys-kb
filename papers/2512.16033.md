## Technical note

CCRec is a cascading category recommender for e-commerce that predicts users' category-level interests (as intention) rather than naively reusing item-level models, complementing item recommendations and helping users with little history. It uses a variational autoencoder to encode item-level information for category-level recommendation, plus a user-distinctive category encoder that builds personalized per-user category embeddings from item-level interactions (e.g. distinguishing high-end vs. budget preferences within Electronics) instead of one generic category representation. Experiments show advantages over methods designed for item-level recommendation.
