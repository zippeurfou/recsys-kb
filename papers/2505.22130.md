## Technical note

LISRec (Alibaba) captures stable user preferences for sequential recommendation by extracting personalized 'semantic shortcuts' from noisy interaction histories (e.g. incidental clicks). It learns task-agnostic semantic representations to estimate item similarities, builds a personalized semantic graph over all user-interacted items, and selects the Maximal Semantic Connectivity subset as representative shortcut items to guide user-preference modeling while preserving genuine-interest diversity. On Amazon Product and Yelp datasets LISRec achieves up to 13% improvement over baselines and generalizes across recommendation architectures.
