## Technical note

C2AL (Cohort-Contrastive Auxiliary Learning) mitigates representation bias in large-scale ads recommendation models where a single global objective causes attention weights to collapse onto majority cohorts (dead neurons / light-tailed attention). It discovers head/tail cohort pairs with maximal distributional divergence (KL, JS, Wasserstein) and adds partially conflicting auxiliary binary heads that regularize the shared factorization-machine attention layer, discarded at inference for zero serving cost. Evaluated on billions-scale production data across six SOTA models on the DHEN architecture, C2AL yields up to 0.16% normalized-entropy reduction overall and over 0.30% gains on targeted minority cohorts.
