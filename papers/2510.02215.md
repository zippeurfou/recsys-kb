## Technical note

Large-scale ad recommenders trained under a single global objective become dominated by majority cohorts, producing sparse, concentrated FM-attention weights and representation bias against head/tail minority segments. C2AL (Cohort-Contrastive Auxiliary Learning) discovers cohort pairs with maximal distributional divergence (via KL, JS, Wasserstein metrics) and builds two auxiliary binary heads whose partially conflicting labels regularize the shared encoder during training only, with no inference-time cost. A gradient analysis on the DHEN factorization-machine attention matrix shows the auxiliary term injects cohort-specific signals that yield a denser, less concentrated weight distribution and richer feature interactions. Validated on six production models with billions of examples, C2AL delivered up to 0.16% normalized-entropy reduction overall and over 0.30% gains on targeted minority cohorts.
