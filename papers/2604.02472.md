## Summary

B2B sales organizations struggle to identify high-value accounts ("whales") for costly sales efforts due to complex revenue distributions and standard uplift modeling limitations. To address this, the VALOR framework introduces a novel Treatment-Gated Sparse-Revenue Network combined with a specialized Cost-Sensitive Focal-ZILN objective function, designed to handle high-dimensional data and prioritize valuable accounts effectively. Evaluations demonstrate VALOR significantly outperforms existing methods, showing improved rankability of persuadable accounts and delivering a substantial, validated increase in incremental revenue during production testing.

## Technical note

VALOR is a value-aware revenue uplift framework for capacity-constrained B2B sales, where revenue is zero-inflated (>80% zeros) and heavy-tailed and standard uplift models suffer counterfactual gradient collapse and prognostic dominance. It uses a Treatment-Gated Sparse-Revenue Network with bilinear interaction to prevent causal signal collapse, optimized by a Cost-Sensitive Focal-ZILN objective that combines a focal mechanism for distributional robustness with a value-weighted ranking loss scaling penalties by financial magnitude. For interpretability it derives Robust ZILN-GBDT, a tree variant with a custom uplift-heterogeneity splitting criterion and Bayesian smoothing for sparse leaves. It reports a 20% Qini rankability gain over state-of-the-art baselines and a validated 2.7x increase in incremental revenue per account in a 4-month production RCT.
