## Technical note

MCNet (Monotonic Calibration Networks) is a post-hoc uncertainty calibration method for online advertising that produces expressive, context-aware, and field-balanced calibrated probabilities. It combines a monotonic calibration function (MCF) built from monotonic neural networks that universally approximates nonlinear relations between uncalibrated scores and posteriors while incorporating context features, an order-preserving regularizer enforcing monotonicity across adjacent bins, and a field-balance regularizer penalizing calibration-performance variance across data fields. Experiments on the public AliExpress dataset and a Huawei industrial advertising dataset for CTR and CVR prediction show MCNet outperforms binning, scaling, and hybrid baselines like Histogram Binning, Platt Scaling, NeuCalib, and AdaCalib.
