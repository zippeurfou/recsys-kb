## Technical note

This work proposes a context-aware, reasoning-enhanced generative search framework for e-commerce that unifies heterogeneous user/item contexts (spatiotemporal, history, query) into text or text-based semantic identifiers and aligns them. A self-evolving post-training paradigm iteratively combines supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning to build reasoning trajectories, and a debiased variant of GRPO corrects biases of standard RL in search ranking. On real e-commerce search logs it significantly outperforms lexical (BM25), dense/dual-tower (DSSM, DPR), and generative (GENRE, DSI, NCI) baselines, improving HR@1/HR@5 (e.g. HR@1 up to 0.0919). Positioned relative to industrially deployed generative search systems like OneSug and OneSearch.
