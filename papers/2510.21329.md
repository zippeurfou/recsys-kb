## Technical note

TripTide is the first benchmark for evaluating LLM travel-planning robustness under realistic disruptions (transit cancellations, weather closures, overbooked attractions). Built by augmenting 1,000 TripCraft queries across 3/5/7-day trips with disruption metadata (step/day/plan-level severity, Flexi-Venturer vs Plan-Bound tolerance) and human-annotated revised plans, it introduces novel metrics: Preservation of Intent, Responsiveness, and Adaptability (semantic/spatial/sequential divergence), evaluated via LLM-as-a-judge plus manual validation. Experiments show LLMs maintain strong sequentiality and stable semantic divergence but struggle with narrative coherence, and disruption-mitigation ability declines as plan horizon increases.
