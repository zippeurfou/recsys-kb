## Technical note

UniSpeaker (Alibaba) is a unified multimodality-driven speaker-generation model that turns face images, text descriptions, and reference speech into target voices. It introduces a KV-Former multimodal voice aggregator with learnable key-value vectors that maps modalities into a shared voice space, trained with a soft contrastive loss (InfoNCE inter-modal plus KL intra-modal soft labels) and a speech-anchoring mechanism for emergent alignment without full parallel data; CosyVoice is the backbone with self-distillation for voice disentanglement. On the newly built MVC benchmark across five tasks (FaceVC, FaceTTS, TextVC, TextTTS, AVE), UniSpeaker beats modality-specific experts, e.g., raising TextTTS speaker similarity SST to 23.09 (vs 17.39) and cutting FaceTTS SSD to 14.09 and WER to 4.01. Only loosely recsys — the transferable ideas are contrastive multimodal alignment and shared embedding spaces.
