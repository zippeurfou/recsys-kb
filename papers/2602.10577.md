## Technical note

Campaign-2-PT-RAG (Walmart) is a scalable label-generation framework that infers which product types (PTs) an e-commerce campaign promotes to construct user-campaign purchase labels for downstream campaign-ranking models. It reframes the ambiguous attribution of creative, thematic campaign language into a semantic-alignment task: an LLM first interprets the campaign into a natural-language description of intent, dense retrieval over the PT taxonomy pulls candidate PTs with a low threshold, then a structured LLM classifier labels each PT as strongly/weakly/irrelevant, and user purchases matching covered PTs generate positive labels. On internal and synthetic datasets validated against expert-annotated campaign-PT mappings, the approach yields 78-90% precision while maintaining over 99% recall, substantially beating lexical and embedding-only baselines.
