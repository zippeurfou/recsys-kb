## Summary

E-commerce campaign optimization needs to understand which campaigns drive purchases, but assigning credit directly is difficult due to vague campaign language. To solve this, the paper introduces Campaign-2-PT-RAG, a system that uses LLMs to infer the implicit product types a campaign promotes and then generates training labels by matching user purchases to these inferred types. This approach automates label generation with high accuracy (78-90% precision) and recall (>99%), enabling scalable and reliable supervision for campaign ranking models.

## Technical note

Campaign-2-PT-RAG is a label-generation framework for e-commerce campaign ranking that infers which product types (PTs) a creatively-worded campaign promotes to construct user-campaign purchase labels. The RAG pipeline uses an LLM to interpret the campaign into a semantic summary, retrieves candidate PTs from a 7,147-node taxonomy via cosine similarity (low threshold for recall), optionally reranks with a cross-encoder, and applies an LLM relevance classifier labeling each PT strong/weak/irrelevant. User purchases matching the resulting PT coverage yield positive training labels. On Walmart campaigns and synthetic data it achieves 78-90% precision and >99% recall, and LLM-based relevance reasoning is shown (via ablation) to be the primary driver over dense retrieval alone.
