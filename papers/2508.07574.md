## Technical note

Introduces an orthogonal low-rank transformation to stabilize user/item embedding spaces across recommender retraining cycles, so downstream consumers get consistent embedding dimensions without costly daily backfilling of hundreds of millions of embeddings. It combines an efficient low-rank SVD of the score space (computed via QR decomposition on a small e-by-e matrix, scaling linearly in users and items) with an orthogonal Procrustes transformation mapping embeddings into a fixed reference space. The transformation is fast, lossless (preserves the dot product and inference), lightweight, and works for factorization-style and softmax neural models without modifying training or embedding structure (Netflix).
