## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of unstable user/item embedding spaces during retraining cycles in recommendation systems. It introduces a novel technique combining low-rank singular value decomposition and orthogonal Procrustes transformation to standardize embeddings without altering primary model training. This approach stabilizes embeddings, preserves dot product quality crucial for recommendations, while being computationally efficient and easy to integrate with existing models.

## Technical note

The paper addresses instability of user/item embedding spaces across recommender retraining cycles, which breaks downstream consumers that use those embeddings as features and makes daily backfilling of hundreds of millions of embeddings infeasible. It proposes an orthogonal low-rank transformation: an efficient low-rank SVD (computed via QR on the factor matrices, scaling linearly in users/items) puts embeddings in a principal-components space, then an orthogonal Procrustes transform aligns each run to a fixed reference space. The transform is fast, lossless (preserves the dot product so inference is unchanged), and lightweight, needing no retraining-objective changes. It applies to factorization and softmax-style models and composes with other stabilization methods.
