## Summary

Standard offline evaluation metrics for ranking models often fail to predict downstream utility accurately because they don't account for potential "impression share shifts" where a model might change how impressions are distributed across different quality buckets. The paper introduces "impression share prediction" as a new offline evaluation task, using a structural causal model to estimate how a candidate model would allocate impressions by simulating its interaction with the system's delivery capacity. This counterfactual prediction task was shown to be identifiable from historical data, and while a Random Forest outperformed a baseline for models seen during training, a novel encoder-conditioned architecture significantly improved prediction accuracy even for unseen models in a simulated rollout.

## Technical note

Meta frames impression share prediction as a new offline evaluation task for ads ranking: given a candidate model, predict the distribution of impressions across objective buckets (click-, view-, conversion-optimized) it would induce online, because a model can improve accuracy metrics (NE, AUROC, GAUC) while shifting impressions in ways that hurt downstream utility. A structural causal model with model state A_t, shared delivery capacity D_t and share vector Y_t argues the effect is identified from observational A/B data because capacity does not feed back into a model's intrinsic score distribution, so no IPW is needed. Predictors map a 22-dim histogram of the candidate's prediction scores plus a coverage vector and 5xC capacity features to a 24h share vector; data are ~1.8M minute-level snapshots from 150 models over ~5 weeks (23 train / 15 test days; 83 seen and 20 unseen test models). A Random Forest cuts L1 error 48.6% vs a constant mean-share baseline on seen models (confidence features alone give 42.9%, capacity alone 25.3%), but on unseen models in the first hour after entry (the closest analog to pre-deployment offline evaluation) it is 20.5% worse than baseline. A PatchTST-style encoder over the last 2h of capacity dynamics plus a conditional MLP head trained with KL loss recovers +22.1% L1 in that first-hour regime; Spearman rank correlation is saturated (>0.98) everywhere.
