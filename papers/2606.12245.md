## Technical note

DiffCold is a diffusion-based generative framework for cold-start item recommendation that resolves the 'seesaw dilemma' (improving cold items degrades warm items) by attributing it to a distributional disparity between the warm behavioral manifold and the cold semantic manifold. Using conditional DDPM, it reconstructs warm item ID embeddings from content features, preserving manifold structure instead of forcing a rigid mapping. Two tailored designs address diffusion-specific issues: a Retrieval-enhanced Aggregator initializes denoising from k content-similar warm items (avoiding pure Gaussian noise), and a Simulation-based Representation Alignment module aligns simulated cold-generation with real warm embeddings via contrastive learning for distribution consistency. On three benchmarks DiffCold resolves the seesaw dilemma and achieves SOTA across all metrics for both cold and warm items.
