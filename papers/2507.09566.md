## Summary

This paper presents a strategy for linking offline metrics to online performance (like clicks and sales) in recommender systems. The approach leverages Pareto front approximation principles and is designed to be model-agnostic, meaning it works across different architectures, particularly neural networks. It validates this method through an experiment on the OTTO e-commerce platform, demonstrating that certain offline indicators can reliably predict real-world online impact for data-driven decision-making.

## Technical note

This work presents a pragmatic strategy to identify which offline metrics predict online KPIs using Pareto-front approximation, where a single preference-conditioned model serves multiple test groups each at a distinct point on the front. It defines a novel offline metric, order density (OD@20), estimating post-click conversion, and shows the product Recall@20*OD@20 proxies units sold. In a two-week ~26.5M-impression experiment on OTTO's session-based recommender (MultiTRON backbone), logistic regressions confirm Recall@20 positively predicts CTR, OD@20 predicts CVR, and their product predicts units sold; trading OD@20 for Recall@20 drives more units sold. A distortion loss is added to extend the approach to single-objective systems.
