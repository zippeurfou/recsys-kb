## Technical note

This work (OTTO) offers a pragmatic strategy to identify offline metrics that align with online impact, leveraging Pareto front approximation to serve multiple test groups - each optimizing a distinct offline metric - within a single scalable model in one online experiment. The approach is model-agnostic for neural-backbone systems, avoiding the cost of training and deploying many parallel model variants. A large-scale online experiment on the OTTO session-based recommender identified significant alignments between offline metrics and real-world click-through rate, post-click conversion rate, and units sold.
