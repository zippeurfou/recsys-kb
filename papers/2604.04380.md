## Summary

This paper introduces CPT, a system using a specialized language model to automatically generate variations of design templates while preserving their internal structure and style. The core innovation is the Creative Markup Language (CML), a compact format representing design elements and layout that the model learns from a large dataset of templates. By fine-tuning the model on this data, CPT can predict and modify visual attributes like color schemes and fonts, producing fully editable design documents for faster personalization and iteration.

## Technical note

CPT (Creative Pre-trained Transformer) fine-tunes a 7B decoder-only Mistral model on 220K designer-authored templates to generate editable design variations rather than pixel images. Templates are linearized into Creative Markup Language (CML), a flat XML-like format encoding canvas structure plus per-element content/style, where color/font/layout attributes are masked and the model predicts only the masked values (task-specific FIM-style infilling with LoRA). Three association modes (no/local/global) trade diversity against cross-element style consistency; global association improves alignment and contrast. A GPT-4o-based Design Scorer filters variations for color contrast and alignment and ranks for diversity; the CML-to-design mapping is a deterministic diff-and-apply pipeline, and human evaluation gave 90.7% thumbs-up on surfaced variations.
