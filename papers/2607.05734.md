## Summary

SCOReD addresses challenges in recommendation system chain-of-thought (CoT) distillation, where standard teacher traces are verbose or poorly fit student models due to high reasoning uncertainty and out-of-distribution issues.

It introduces a framework that parses teacher CoT responses into typed segments and evaluates each segment's importance using the attention scores of the smaller student LLM.

SCOReD dynamically selects edits (keep, rewrite, fuse, prune) for each segment based on answer improvement potential relative to the student model's constraints, leading to optimized traces that improve recommendation performance metrics while reducing reasoning length.

## Technical note

SCOReD (Student-Aware CoT Optimization for Recommendation Distillation) tackles the fact that large-teacher chain-of-thought traces for recommendation are verbose and out-of-distribution for small students: the teacher rechecks its answer 3.2x per trace with 79% of rechecks not changing the ranking. It parses each Gemma-4-26B teacher trace into six typed segments, scores each via the student's </think>-token attention (CRISP-style saliency), buckets them by importance, and selects a per-segment edit (KEEP/REWRITE/FUSE/PRUNE) maximizing a reward of student log-probability of the answer minus length and perplexity penalties. Training a Qwen3-0.6B student on SCOReD-optimized CoT beats raw-trace SFT by +1.56% NDCG and +1.9% Recall@5 while cutting reasoning length ~27.3% and parse failures ~46%, and even outperforms Qwen-3.6-35B. Post-SFT DAPO RL and On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD) gave no consistent gains, attributed to the small student-teacher gap and sparse reward.
