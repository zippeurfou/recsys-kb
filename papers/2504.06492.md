## Summary

1.  The paper examines adversarial attacks on graph link prediction models, specifically focusing on the vulnerability of the sophisticated Variational Graph Auto-Encoder (VGAE).
2.  It proposes an innovative unweighted poisoning attack using meta-learning techniques designed to significantly degrade VGAE's performance by strategically adding fake edges during training.
3.  Through experiments across multiple datasets, it demonstrates that this meta-learning-based approach effectively reduces link prediction accuracy and performs better than existing state-of-the-art methods for this type of attack on VGAE.

## Technical note

GNN link prediction underpins recommendation and social/biological networks but its adversarial robustness is far less studied than node classification. The paper proposes an unweighted graph poisoning attack that uses meta-learning with weighted-scheme strategies to strategically add fake edges during training and degrade link prediction, targeting the Variational Graph Auto-Encoder (VGAE). Across diverse datasets and link-prediction use cases, the meta-learning attack consistently reduces link-prediction accuracy. It outperforms existing state-of-the-art poisoning baselines under comparable conditions.
