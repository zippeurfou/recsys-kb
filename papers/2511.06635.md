## Technical note

This Baidu study investigates whether LLM-generated relevance annotations can replace user clicks for learning to rank. On TianGong-ST and industrial Baidu-Click datasets, click-supervised models do better on high-frequency queries and capture document-level signals like authority/quality, while LLM-annotation-supervised models excel on medium/low-frequency queries and at semantic query-document matching and distinguishing relevant from non-relevant documents. Motivated by this complementarity, it explores data scheduling and frequency-aware multi-objective learning to combine both supervision signals; both improve ranking across all query frequency levels, with the multi-objective approach more effective.
