## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of learning robust multimodal representations by improving upon existing noise injection techniques. The authors introduce FANoise, a novel noise modulation strategy that dynamically adapts noise based on feature distribution changes during training. Experiments demonstrate that FANoise consistently enhances performance across various multimodal tasks and underlying models.

## Technical note

FANoise studies active noise injection for multimodal contrastive representation learning, analyzing how noise affects InfoNCE gradient dynamics and feature distributions rather than relying on heuristic or static noise. Its central idea is a feature-adaptive strategy that performs SVD on the feature space and modulates Gaussian noise in the singular-value (V) directions before the contrastive objective, so perturbation magnitude tracks the evolving feature distribution during training. This preserves the robustness benefits of noise while mitigating its harm to alignment/uniformity. Across multiple VLM backbones and multimodal benchmarks FANoise consistently improves downstream performance, offering principled guidance on optimal noise strength and distribution.
