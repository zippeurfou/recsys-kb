## Summary

Multi-Agent Decomposition for Fine-Grained Table Attribution":

1.  TraceBack addresses the lack of transparency in table Question Answering (QA) by providing fine-grained cell-level attribution for answers, ensuring correct responses are verifiable.
2.  This modular multi-agent framework achieves this by pruning tables, decomposing questions into sub-questions, and linking answers back to their supporting cells, while introducing new benchmarks (CITEBench) and a reference-less evaluation metric (FairScore).
3.  Experiments demonstrate that TraceBack significantly outperforms existing methods, and the new FairScore metric effectively evaluates attribution quality without needing human-annotated cell labels.

## Technical note

TraceBack is a modular multi-agent LLM framework for fine-grained, cell-level attribution in single-table QA, addressing the lack of verifiable grounding in table answers. It sequentially prunes tables to relevant columns and rows, decomposes questions into semantically coherent sub-questions, and grounds each sub-answer in specific supporting cells, capturing both explicit and implicit intermediate-reasoning evidence unlike prior row-column methods such as MATSA. The authors release CITEBench, a benchmark of 1,500 phrase-to-cell annotations drawn from ToTTo, FetaQA, and AITQA covering final and intermediate evidence. They also propose FAIRScore, a reference-less metric comparing atomic facts from predicted cells and answers to estimate attribution precision and recall without human cell labels, which tracks human judgments closely.
