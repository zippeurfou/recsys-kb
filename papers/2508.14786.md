## Technical note

PNFRec incorporates negative feedback into sequential recommendation by processing a user's positive and negative interaction subsequences with two separate SASRec-style transformer encoders that share a single item embedding table (only the positive encoder is used at inference). It trains with a composite loss of positive cross-entropy, weighted negative cross-entropy (α), and a contrastive term (β) that pushes the predicted next positive item away from the user's negative items. Evaluated on ML-1M, ML-20M, Toys&Games, and the industrial Kion dataset with separate positive/negative ground-truth metrics (ΔHR@10, ΔNDCG@10), it improves the positive-vs-negative gap while maintaining true positives (e.g. +11% NDCG_p@10 on ML-1M, +33% ΔNDCG@10 on Toys&Games) and trains faster than the contrastive SASRec baseline; optimal α≈0.1-0.35.
