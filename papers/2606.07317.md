## Technical note

In encoder-decoder generative retrieval over long streaming user histories, the bidirectional encoder's quadratic softmax attention is the latency bottleneck, yet causal masking hurts quality (Recall@10 0.1878 vs 0.2800 bidirectional at L=2048). The paper introduces Gated Bidirectional Linear Attention (GBLA), a linear-time bidirectional layer extending kernelized linear attention with a Conv1D local mixer, per-token key gating for soft forgetting, and a gated RMSNorm output. A hybrid encoder interleaving self-attention and GBLA at a 1:2 [SA,LA,LA] ratio matches full bidirectional self-attention on a 400M-sample Yandex Music dataset (Recall@1000 0.8668 vs 0.8667 at L=2048) and preserves TIGER quality on Amazon Beauty/Toys/Sports. On H100 GPUs a single GBLA layer reaches up to 8.2x speedup over FlashAttention-v3 at length 32768, becoming faster from L=4096 onward.
