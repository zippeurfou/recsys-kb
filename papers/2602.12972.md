In online advertising, marketing interventions such as coupons introduce significant confounding bias into Click-Through Rate (CTR) prediction. Observed clicks reflect a mixture of users' intrinsic preferences and the uplift induced by these interventions. This causes conventional models to miscalibrate base CTRs, which distorts downstream ranking and billing decisions. ## Technical note

UniMVT (Unified Multi-Valued Treatment Network) jointly debiases CTR prediction and estimates coupon uplift in a single entire-space causal framework, correcting the calibration drift that arises when marketing interventions confound observed clicks. It uses a Deconfounded Causal Representation (DCR) layer to disentangle invariant confounders from treatment-sensitive features and a Counterfactual X-Network to reconstruct debiased base CTR and intensity-response curves, with a Latent Unit Sensitivity (uCATE) formulation enforcing a monotonic linear treatment shift in logit space for multi-valued (continuous coupon-value) treatments. Experiments on synthetic and industrial data show SOTA predictive accuracy and calibration, and real A/B tests improve business metrics via better coupon allocation.
