## Technical note

SUMMA is a multimodal LLM (fine-tuned from Qwen2-VL-2B) that summarizes short-video ads into commercially valuable, explainable text to replace compressed video embeddings in search-advertising retrieval and ranking. It uses two-stage training: multimodal SFT on video frames plus ASR/OCR transcripts (with rule-based LLM-as-a-judge filtering), followed by reinforcement learning with a mixed reward. Integrated into ByteDance's production pipeline for candidate retrieval and relevance ranking, it yields a statistically significant +1.5% advertising revenue online.
