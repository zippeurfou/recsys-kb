## Summary

A Fleet of User Embedding Specialists for Recommendation at Meta":

1.  Mosaic addresses the critical need in recommendation systems for better user representation by creating an online platform that generates diverse user embedding models instead of relying on single or task-adapted representations.
2.  It consists of a fleet deploying four distinct types of embedding specialists (memorization, dense-heavy, sequential, and collaborative) to capture different facets of user behavior simultaneously.
3.  To ensure each specialist contributes effectively, Mosaic uses techniques like multi-task relation mining and cosine redundancy loss during training, along with efficient evaluation methods that don't require logging, leading to significant offline and online performance improvements (NE gains).

## Technical note

Mosaic is Meta's user-representation platform that abandons the single-backbone paradigm for a fleet of architecturally diverse specialist embedding models: memorization-driven (large hash tables), dense-heavy (MHTA/SEDot/Wukong over aggregated counters), sequential (HSTU with MoE routing), and CoTrain (end-to-end gradients from a downstream-ranker copy). To sustain marginal value as the fleet grows it introduces Multi-Task Relation Mining (Cartesian-product composite labels over correlated tasks) and a Cosine Redundancy Loss enforcing embedding orthogonality, plus CoEval and User Tower Zero-Out for logging-free evaluation that speeds iteration 3-5x. A hybrid CPU/GPU, online-and-offline serving stack (AOTInductor plus memcache) cut GPU serving cost 79%. Mosaic delivers consistent downstream NE gains (dense-heavy -0.22% on Surface 1) and statistically significant online topline lifts up to +0.28%.
