## Technical note

Memento is a personalized retrieval-augmented framework for long-retention data scaling at Meta that treats a user's full engagement history as a document corpus and ad requests as queries, retrieving relevant interactions via Maximal Marginal Relevance (MMR) to balance similarity and diversity, addressing needle-in-haystack attention dilution, efficiency, and catastrophic forgetting versus LastN. It has two applications: Representation Memento retrieves historical embeddings for inference-time feature augmentation, and Data Memento retrieves forgotten training examples for second-pass rehearsal. With temporal chunking, INT8 quantization, and async serving it reaches sub-10ms latency and 5-10x efficiency, yielding 0.25-0.3% NE gain and a 1% CTR and 1.2% CVR lift on Facebook Feed and Reels over 365+ days of history.
