## Technical note

EASP (Environment-Aware Search Planning) resolves a 'blindness-latency dilemma' in LLM-based e-commerce search planning, where blind query rewriting produces invalid plans and ReAct-style deep agents incur seconds of latency. Its Probe-then-Plan mechanism uses a lightweight Retrieval Probe to expose a retrieval snapshot so the Planner can diagnose execution gaps and generate a grounded plan in one step; training uses SFT to distill a teacher agent's diagnostic capability followed by RL alignment to conversion rate, with complexity-aware routing that only activates full planning for complex queries. Online A/B tests on JD.com show +0.89% UCVR and +0.57% GMV with p75 planner latency under 20ms and p99 under 700ms, and EASP is fully deployed in JD.com's AI-Search.
