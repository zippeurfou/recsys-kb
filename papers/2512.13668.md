## Summary

This paper introduces QFANG, a scientific reasoning language model designed to automatically generate detailed experimental procedures for organic synthesis directly from reaction equations. QFANG was trained on a massive dataset derived from patent literature using LLMs and refined through supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning techniques grounded in chemical knowledge. The model significantly outperforms general models and baselines in predicting accurate synthesis procedures, demonstrating strong generalization capabilities.

## Technical note

QFANG tackles generating precise, machine-readable experimental synthesis procedures from reaction equations, bridging computational route design and laboratory execution. It curates 905,990 reaction-procedure pairs via an LLM annotation pipeline, applies a Chemistry-Guided Reasoning framework that fuses programmatic cheminformatics skeletons with LLM-written chain-of-thought, then does supervised fine-tuning on Qwen-3 (8B/32B) followed by RL with verifiable, step-wise rewards using PPO/GRPO. QFANG-8B (RL) reaches BLEU-4 61.3 and 78.2 on a GPT-5 chemically-aware LLM-as-judge, beating retrieval-augmented 3-shot GPT-5 and nearest-neighbor baselines. It generalizes to out-of-domain reactions, adapts to user constraints, and corrects flawed training procedures.
