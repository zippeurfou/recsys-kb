## Technical note

KuaiLive is the first real-time interactive dataset for live-streaming recommendation, collected from Kuaishou, recording interaction logs of 23,772 users and 452,621 streamers over 21 days. Unlike prior datasets it includes precise live-room start/end timestamps (enabling a time-dependent dynamic candidate pool), multiple real-time interaction types (click, comment, like, gift), and rich user/streamer side information. It supports top-K recommendation, CTR prediction, watch-time prediction, and gift-price prediction, plus research on multi-behavior modeling, multi-task learning, and fairness-aware recommendation; the paper benchmarks representative methods to establish baselines.
