## Technical note

SampleLLM is a two-stage framework for LLM-based tabular data synthesis in recommendation, targeting distribution misalignment and poor diversity of raw LLM output. Stage one uses an LLM (Llama-3-70B-instruct-awq / Qwen2.5) with Chain-of-Thought-refined instructions and K-means cluster-sampled exemplars for few-shot generation; stage two applies feature-attribution-based importance sampling that extracts significant feature interactions via a 2nd-order Taylor/Hessian attribution map, adopts a semi-independence assumption, and reweights synthetic samples using frequency-based joint distributions and a discriminant model to align with the real data. On CTR datasets (ML-1M, Amazon, Douban) plus HELOC and Covertype it beats CTGAN, TVAE, TabDDPM, GReaT, and REaLTabFormer on both augmentation and MLE utility (e.g. ML-1M AUC 0.8180); a one-week Huawei ad-platform A/B in a cold-start scenario delivered +2% RPM and +1.86% ECPM.
