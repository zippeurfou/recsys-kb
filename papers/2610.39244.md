## Summary

Current methods for advertiser subsidy allocation (ASA) struggle with large platforms due to tier-level policies being too coarse. The paper introduces CASH, a system pairing a knowledge-guided LLM with a data-driven response model. CASH learns reliable subsidy outcomes for individual accounts and defines "capability licenses" to guide budget allocation and account selection, aiming to expand reliable coverage by directing limited feedback towards high-value evidence. In a large-scale online experiment, CASH significantly increased revenue per account (15.35%) and ADVV (19.86%) while maintaining essentially the same overall subsidy rate.

## Technical note

Advertiser Subsidy Allocation picks a billing-discount level per advertiser account to maximize discounted revenue under a platform-wide subsidy-rate constraint, which Kuaishou previously set by tier-level operator rules. CASH pairs an LLM prediction agent (account history, operating info, business rules) with a numerical response model (Tool) trained on matured outcomes, projects both revenue curves to be monotone via isotonic regression, and issues per-account, per-level 'capability licenses' from a calibrated relative-error bound (conformal-style quantile on a later calibration set), kernel-weighted empirical support, and Beta-posterior regional admission; a learned judge plus an orchestrating LLM route contested accounts, licensed options feed a multiple-choice knapsack solved by dual search, and Expected Policy Coverage Gain spends a limited exploration quota where new feedback is expected to expand value-weighted licensed Tool coverage. Offline on 663K account-days, CASH cuts MAE 2.20% and RMSE 4.11% vs Tool using 6,119 LLM calls (15% of calling every account) and licenses block cross-account error propagation through the shared budget; EPCG expands licensed coverage by 2.06-2.75 points vs about 0 for random acquisition. In a one-month online experiment serving 60% of traffic, CASH raised revenue per account +15.35% and ADVV +19.86% over the tier-based Control (Tool alone: +8.50% / +14.32%) at an essentially unchanged subsidy rate, with 7-day LTV +15.98% and day-14 retention +1.57pp.
