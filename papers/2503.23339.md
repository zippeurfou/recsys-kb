## Technical note

This paper introduces Adaptive Precise Boolean rubrics, an evaluation framework that decomposes complex open-ended criteria (e.g., Likert scales) into a larger set of granular yes/no boolean questions, then uses an LLM (Gemini) as a zero-shot classifier to dynamically retain only rubric criteria relevant to each query-response pair. Validated in metabolic health (diabetes, cardiovascular disease, obesity) with expert and non-expert human and automated evaluators, it yields substantially higher inter-rater agreement (ICC) than Likert scales while roughly halving evaluation time, and achieves automated-evaluation parity with expert judgment plus better sensitivity to missing personalized health data. It targets scalable, reliable evaluation of personalized health LLM responses rather than deploying medical devices.
