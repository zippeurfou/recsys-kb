## Technical note

This Meta paper shows aggregation induces a Simpson's paradox in behavioral (engagement-versus-exposure) curves used for frequency capping and exploration scheduling: on Goodreads (3.3M users, 9 genres) individual users peak at n*≈11 exposures while the aggregate peaks at n*≈34, a 3x gap driven by survival bias (differential attrition), with Amazon Electronics showing a 5.3x distortion and MovieLens-25M (D≈1) as a negative control. It fits a Hill-exponential model and proves survival bias distorts fitted peak locations, proposes empirical Bayes shrinkage for hierarchical peak estimation, and introduces Synthetic Null Calibration to fix a 32% false-positive rate in per-user curve classification.
