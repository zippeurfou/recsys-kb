## Technical note

RAD-DPO adapts Direct Preference Optimization to generative retrieval over structured Semantic IDs at JD.com, fixing three failure modes of vanilla DPO on SIDs: gradient conflict on shared hierarchical prefixes (34.6% of pairs share the first SID token), noisy pseudo-negatives from implicit exposure feedback, and probability squeezing among multiple valid items. It introduces Token-Level Gradient Detachment (stop-gradient on shared prefixes), Representation-based Dynamic Reward Weighting (softening penalties on high-similarity pseudo-negatives), and a Multi-label Global Contrast objective combined with global SFT loss using a block-diagonal attention mask. Built on a Qwen-1.7B backbone with 130M SIDs from 3-level RQ-Kmeans codebooks, RAD-DPO beats SFT/DPO/SimPO/ORPO on Recall and MRR, scales cleanly from 0.6B to 8B, and delivers +0.34% User Conversion Rate in online A/B tests at <150ms latency.
