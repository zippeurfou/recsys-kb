## Technical note

CE-CDR reformulates cross-domain recommendation as a causal graph and directly models source-to-target causality to avoid negative transfer and causality sparsity that hamper multi-task and feature-based CDR. It heuristically builds a causality-aware dataset (Causality Labeling Module) and trains a Direct Causality Modeling Module with a theoretically unbiased Partial Label Causal Loss to learn a transferable cross-domain representation fed into the target model. It acts as a model-agnostic plugin over baselines like CoNet, MAN, and DICUR, improving HR and NDCG. A two-week A/B test on Rednote (Xiaohongshu) showed significant gains (e.g. +0.33% video, +0.28% note metrics) with a real-time embedding cache keeping serving latency unchanged; deployed in production since April 2025.
