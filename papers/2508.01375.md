## Technical note

SaviorRec (Taobao/Alibaba) improves CTR prediction for cold-start and long-tail items by keeping multimodal item representations aligned with the behavior space in a lightweight way. It trains a behavior-aware multimodal encoder (SaviorEnc), uses residual-quantized semantic IDs plus a trainable Modal-Behavior Alignment block for continuous semantic-behavior alignment, and adds a bi-directional target-attention mechanism between behavior and multimodal features. Online A/B tests on Taobao yielded +0.83% offline AUC, +13.21% clicks, and +13.44% orders.
