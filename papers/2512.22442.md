## Summary

Open-domain RAG systems often retrieve irrelevant information, leading to poor answer alignment. To address this, HiFi-RAG employs a two-stage process: it uses a fast model for initial retrieval and filtering, then relies on a powerful model for final generation. This hierarchical approach significantly improved performance on standard benchmarks and demonstrated strong capabilities even with recent knowledge cutoffs.

## Technical note

HiFi-RAG is a multi-stage open-domain RAG pipeline (the winning closed-source Text-to-Text system in the MMU-RAGent NeurIPS 2025 competition) that abandons vector-similarity filtering in favor of hierarchical LLM-based filtering. It uses the cheaper Gemini 2.5 Flash as a lightweight gatekeeper for query formulation, URL pre-fetch filtering (-33.5% URLs), hierarchical HTML section parsing, and LLM-as-a-reranker section filtering (-60.5% chunks), then reserves Gemini 2.5 Pro for two-pass drafting-and-refinement generation and decoupled post-hoc citation verification. This model-cascade design gates expensive reasoning on cheap relevance signals to cut cost and noise. It improved ROUGE-L by 19.6% and DeBERTaScore by 6.2% over baseline, with larger gains on a post-cutoff Test2025 set requiring non-parametric retrieval.
