The major task of any e-commerce search engine is to retrieve the most relevant inventory items, which best match the user intent reflected in a query. This task is non-trivial due to many reasons, including ambiguous queries, misaligned vocabulary between buyers, and sellers, over- or under-constrained queries by the presence of too many or too few tokens. ## Technical note

TagBERT (eBay) frames e-commerce query reformulation as a token-classification task for extracting important tokens, enabling token dropping to boost recall on ambiguous or over-constrained (especially tail) queries. It is a dependency-aware, tag-interaction transformer language model that leverages each token's semantic tags to learn superior query-phrase embeddings, capturing a token's role within the whole query. On large real-life e-commerce datasets TagBERT outperforms competing models including BERT, eBERT, and a sequence-to-sequence transformer on important-token classification.
