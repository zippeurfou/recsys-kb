## Summary

This paper presents a framework using Large Language Models (LLMs) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) on Reddit data to detect mental health disorders. It employs both rule-based labeling and fine-tuned pre-trained models derived from specialized LLM predictions, combining generalization with domain-specific insights. The research highlights the potential of this approach for early detection and personalized recommendations in digital mental health surveillance while evaluating its strengths and limitations.

## Technical note

The paper proposes an NLP plus generative-AI framework to identify mental health disorders, assess severity, and recommend therapies and behavior changes from Reddit posts. It combines rule/dictionary labeling (from the SMHD nine-disorder set) with LLM annotators (Llama3, MentaLLaMA, Samantha-Mistral) to label ~334k posts, then fine-tunes pre-trained models like MentalRoBERTa and DistilBERT with binary cross-entropy for multi-label disorder classification. Severity is classified into mild/moderate/severe by LLMs, which also generate personalized therapy suggestions (with confidence scores) and actionable behavior-change advice. The hybrid approach pairs pre-trained models' generalization with domain-specific LLM insight, evaluated via 5-fold cross-validation and generalization tests on unseen posts.
