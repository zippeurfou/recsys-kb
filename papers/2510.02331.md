## Technical note

ICER is a methodology (Amazon/Google) for generating behaviorally-consistent synthetic conversational-recommendation dialogues to overcome the scarcity of public CRS training data. It grounds LM-based user simulation in a behavioral model: user preferences are sampled from a distribution over embeddings learned via probabilistic matrix factorization, interactions (acceptance/rejection, critiques, elicitation responses) are generated w.r.t. that embedding, then templatized and refined via LM-prompting for natural utterances. Using ICER they release MD-DICER, 100K dialogues over MovieLens 25M, and rater evaluation shows the dialogues are more natural, factual, and preference-consistent than templatized baselines.
