## Technical note

UMRE (Kuaishou) is a Unified Monotonic Ranking Ensemble framework that replaces hand-designed nonlinear pxtr transformations and hand-tuned fusion weights used to combine multi-task predictions (pctr, pltr, etc.) in industrial ensemble sorting. It uses Unconstrained Monotonic Neural Networks (UMNN, via positive neural integrals) to learn strictly monotonic, user-specific pxtr transformations that preserve ranking order, then a lightweight fusion model assigns personalized weights per objective, with a Pareto-optimality strategy that adaptively coordinates task weights during training to balance competing goals. On public datasets (KuaiRand, Tenrec) and online A/B tests UMRE eliminates manual tuning while improving performance across all tasks.
