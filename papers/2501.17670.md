## Technical note

DiQDiff improves diffusion-based sequential recommendation, where noise is added to the next item and denoised under guidance from the user's interaction sequence. It addresses two problems: heterogeneous/noisy guidance sequences and diffusion models' tendency to generate only popular items. It introduces Semantic Vector Quantization (SVQ) to quantize sequences into semantic vectors via a codebook for richer guidance, and Contrastive Discrepancy Maximization (CDM) that uses a contrastive loss to maximize distance between denoising trajectories, preventing biased generation across users. Experiments across four datasets show DiQDiff outperforms leading baselines in sequential recommendation.
