## Technical note

FuXi-beta accelerates autoregressive generative recommenders by removing two efficiency bottlenecks in HSTU and FuXi-alpha: hardware-unfriendly indexing in relative temporal attention bias and computation of the query-key attention map. Empirically it finds the query-key attention map is redundant (removing it even improves recommendation performance while removing temporal/spatial bias hurts). It introduces Functional Relative Attention Bias (FRAB) to avoid slow indexing and an Attention-Free Token Mixer (AFTM) replacing the query-key self-attention. FuXi-beta preserves the scaling law, is much faster than FuXi-alpha, and improves NDCG@10 by 27%-47% on large-scale industrial datasets.
