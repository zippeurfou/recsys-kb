## Summary

1.  Digital advertising delivery systems face challenges automating the entire complex process, as current methods only partially automate individual tasks and rely heavily on human operators for coordination and adaptability.
2.  To address this, the paper introduces AdCoEvo, a co-evolving multi-agent system where specialized agents handle planning, bidding, diagnosis, and adjustment, learning and improving from each other's feedback over time.
3.  Experiments demonstrate AdCoEvo significantly outperforms both human-centric methods (increasing advertiser value by 14%) and static multi-agent systems (increasing value by 3.96% and reducing CPA by 1.24%), proving its effectiveness in dynamically optimizing advertising campaigns.

## Technical note

Advertising delivery (account budget planning, ad-unit tCPA adjustment, period-end diagnosis) is still coordinated by human operators; AdCoEvo (JD) decomposes it into three Qwen3-8B LoRA role agents, Planner, Optimizer and Inspector, whose diagnostic guidance feeds the next period's planning and adjustment. Roles are first trained offline with SFT then GSPO-style sequence-level clipped policy optimization with leave-one-out advantages, then co-evolve during continuous delivery: one role per period is updated in rotation from settled business feedback using a per-role PrefixValueModel baseline (GAE for the Optimizer) while the others stay fixed. In the AuctionNet simulator (Sparse and General, 5 sequences x 50 periods, Abid as fixed bidder), AdCoEvo raises advertiser value (ADV) +14.02% and cuts CPA -7.07% vs a rule-based operator baseline, beats the AdPilot single-agent Reflector-Actor by +14.51% ADV / -17.55% CPA, and co-evolution adds +3.96% ADV / -1.24% CPA over the fixed-parameter multi-agent system; it also outperforms GPT-5.6-Terra (+3.73% ADV, -3.23% CPA) at ~66% lower API cost than the cheapest foundation model.
