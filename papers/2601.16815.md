## Technical note

PI2I (Personalized Item-to-Item) is a two-stage retrieval framework deployed in Taobao's 'Guess You Like' that adds personalization to item-based collaborative filtering. In the Indexer Building Stage it relaxes truncation thresholds to preserve a high Hit Rate candidate pool; in the Personalized Retrieval Stage it replaces inner-product scoring with an interactive model for richer user-item crossing, and constructs negatives via a trigger-target (item-to-item) relationship to align offline training with online inference. PI2I outperforms traditional CF and rivals/exceeds two-tower models offline, achieved a 1.05% online transaction-rate lift, and the authors released a 130M-interaction Taobao dataset.
