Generative models have recently gained attention in recommendation systems by directly predicting item identifiers from user interaction sequences. ## Technical note

COBRA (Cascaded Organized Bi-Represented generAtive retrieval) integrates sparse semantic IDs and dense vectors in a cascade to overcome the information loss of ID-only generative recommenders like TIGER. It alternates generation, first producing an RQ-VAE-derived sparse ID as a coarse categorical sketch that conditions the end-to-end-learned dense vector, capturing both semantic and collaborative signals, and at inference uses a coarse-to-fine process plus BeamFusion (beam search combined with nearest-neighbor scores) for controllable diversity. Online A/B tests on an advertising platform with over 200 million daily users show substantial gains, and it outperforms SOTA on public benchmarks.
