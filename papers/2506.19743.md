E-commerce information retrieval (IR) systems struggle to simultaneously achieve high accuracy in interpreting complex user queries and maintain efficient processing of vast product catalogs. The dual challenge lies in precisely matching user intent with relevant products while managing the computational demands of real-time search across massive inventories. ## Technical note

NEAR2 (eBay) is a nested embedding approach for efficient e-commerce product retrieval and ranking, built on Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL) to encode information at multiple granularities within one higher-dimensional vector. Trained with IR ranking losses (multiple-negative ranking loss, online contrastive loss) on encoder-based Transformers, it achieves up to 12x smaller embedding size and ~100x less memory at inference with no extra training cost. On four test sets with challenging (short, implicit, alphanumeric) queries it improves accuracy over baselines at much smaller embedding dimensions and is robust across different IR losses.
