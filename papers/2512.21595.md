## Summary

Item-to-Item (I2I) recommendation models face enhancement challenges through either complex model architectures or data refinement. Existing data-centric methods struggle with data sparsity and noise. The paper introduces LLM-I2I, a novel framework using Large Language Models (LLMs) to synthesize new user-item interactions and filter out noisy ones, thereby improving data quality. Evaluated on multiple datasets and deployed in e-commerce, LLM-I2I significantly boosts recommendation accuracy, especially for long-tail items, and increases key business metrics like recall and Gross Merchandise Value (GMV).

## Technical note

LLM-I2I enhances lightweight Item-to-Item recommenders via a data-centric approach that avoids costly model changes, targeting data sparsity and noise. An LLM-based generator, fine-tuned on user history, synthesizes new user-item interactions especially for long-tail items, while an LLM-based discriminator filters weak or noisy interactions; refined real and synthetic data are fused to train existing I2I models. It significantly improves recommendation quality (particularly for long-tail items) across industrial (AEDS) and academic (ARD) datasets. Deployed on AliExpress, it delivered 6.02% recall-number and 1.22% GMV improvements over the existing I2I model.
