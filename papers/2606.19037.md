## Summary

This paper introduces Querit-Reranker, a family of compact multilingual cross-encoder rerankers designed to efficiently generalize across languages, domains, and tasks. The core contribution is an innovative data-centric training pipeline that minimizes the need for expensive language-specific relevance annotations during adaptation by using synthetic queries with teacher scores as soft labels. Two variants (A0.4B and 4B) demonstrate significant performance improvements on benchmark reranking datasets like BEIR and MTEB Multilingual v2, while being more efficient than existing larger models or requiring fewer retraining resources.

## Technical note

Querit-Reranker is a family of compact multilingual cross-encoder rerankers for second-stage retrieve-then-rerank, released as an A0.4B MoE-backbone variant and a 4B dense (Qwen3-Embedding) variant. Its data-centric pipeline first learns general query-document relevance from large-scale ranking data, then adapts to target distributions via document-conditioned synthetic-query mining with teacher scores used as continuous soft labels (distillation), avoiding costly per-domain human annotations. Complementary task-adapted checkpoints are consolidated with spherical linear interpolation (SLERP) into one deployable model without runtime ensembling. With a shared Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B retriever it lifts nDCG@10 from 54.11 to 59.28 on BEIR and 59.87 to 67.70 on MIRACL, with the 4B model reaching SOTA among public multilingual rerankers on MTEB v2.
