## Technical note

STCRank is a Spatio-Temporal Collaborative Ranking framework for Kuaishou E-shop's interactive recommender system (triggered by a homepage click to serve focused-browsing slots). Its Multi-Objective Collaboration (MOC) module pushes the Pareto frontier by mitigating objective overlaps/conflicts within a slot (spatial), while a Multi-Slot Collaboration (MSC) module achieves global optima across sequential slots via a dual-stage look-ahead ranking mechanism (temporal). A/B tests show co-growth of purchase and DAU (e.g. MOC adding a vtr objective gave +6.96% on one metric); fully deployed at Kuaishou E-shop since June 2025.
