## Summary

1.  The paper introduces PaperBanana, an AI framework designed to automate the generation of publication-ready illustrations for researchers, addressing a current bottleneck in the AI research workflow.
2.  PaperBanana uses advanced agents and image models to handle tasks from content planning and style retrieval to image rendering and iterative refinement, significantly reducing manual effort.
3.  Evaluated against 292 test cases from actual NeurIPS publications, PaperBanana consistently outperformed baselines in accuracy, clarity, and aesthetics, and also demonstrated capability for generating statistical plots.

## Technical note

PaperBanana is an agentic framework that automates generation of publication-ready academic illustrations (methodology diagrams and statistical plots) from a method description and caption. It orchestrates specialized agents powered by VLMs and image-generation models (e.g. Gemini-3-Pro, Nano-Banana-Pro) to retrieve reference examples, plan content and style, render images, and iteratively refine via self-critique. Evaluated on PaperBananaBench (292 NeurIPS-2025 test cases) with a VLM-as-a-judge protocol across faithfulness, conciseness, readability, and aesthetics, it beats baselines on all four dimensions (+17.0% overall).
