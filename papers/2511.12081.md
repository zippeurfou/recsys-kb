## Technical note

This work attributes diminishing scaling returns in Transformer-based CTR models to structural misalignment: standard Transformers assume sequential compositionality, whereas CTR data require combinatorial reasoning over heterogeneous fields. It introduces the Field-Aware Transformer (FAT), rebuilding the Transformer block with field-centric parameters so complexity depends on the number of fields F rather than total vocabulary n (n>>F), and a Basis-Composed Hypernetwork synthesizes field-specific parameters from shared bases to decouple capacity from field cardinality. Grounded by a Rademacher-complexity scaling law, FAT outperforms state-of-the-art by up to +4.38% AUC and delivers +2.33% CTR and +0.66% RPM in live production.
