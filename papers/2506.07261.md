## Technical note

RADAR (Recall Augmentation through Deferred Asynchronous Retrieval) from Meta improves the retrieval stage of multi-stage recommender funnels, where efficient KNN retrieval struggles to distinguish highly engaging from merely relevant candidates in billion-scale catalogs. RADAR leverages asynchronous offline computation to pre-rank a much larger candidate set per user with the full-complexity ranking model, stores the top items, and uses them as a high-quality retrieval source at online inference, bypassing online retrieval and pre-ranking for those candidates. Offline experiments show 2X Recall@200 vs a DNN retrieval baseline, and online A/B tests confirm a +0.8% lift in topline engagement under strict serving constraints.
