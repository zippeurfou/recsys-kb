## Technical note

Releases Yambda-5B, an open Yandex.Music dataset of 4.79B interactions from 1M users over 9.39M tracks, with implicit (listens) and explicit (likes/dislikes/unlikes/undislikes) feedback, CNN-based audio embeddings, and an is_organic flag separating organic from recommendation-driven events. It defines a Global Temporal Split protocol (300-day train, 30-min gap, 1-day test with frozen user states) to mimic industrial daily-refresh serving and prevent temporal leakage, plus 500M and 50M subsamples. Benchmarks ItemKNN, iALS, BPR, SANSA, and SASRec show ItemKNN and SASRec lead on Listen+ (implicit) while the simple time-decayed DecayPop wins on Like (explicit), and classic MF-style methods degrade under real-time conditions.
