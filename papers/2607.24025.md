## Summary

Mitigating Embedding and Attention Collapse via Spectral-Aware Transformer for Recommendation":

The paper identifies that standard Transformers struggle with recommendation tasks despite successes elsewhere. This performance issue arises from unique problems called embedding and attention collapse, triggered by data heterogeneity and long-tails leading to spectral dominance in model layers.

To tackle this, the authors introduce SpecFormer, a specialized Spectral-Aware Transformer architecture. It features three key components: learnable smoothing of singular values in embeddings; a spectrum-softened attention mechanism operating under uniform distribution; and Taylor-expanded position encoding that biases towards desired spectral properties.

Extensive experiments show SpecFormer significantly outperforms existing baselines on both public and industrial datasets, exhibiting strong scaling capabilities as adding layers improves performance.

## Technical note

Standard self-attention underperforms hand-designed token-mixing (OneTrans below RankMixer) as a feature-interaction backbone for recommendation, and unlike LLMs it collapses at very shallow depth. The paper attributes this to a spectral vicious cycle specific to recsys data: heterogeneous, long-tailed feature tokens form segregated sub-spaces whose embedding matrix is dominated by a few principal singular values, the resulting attention matrix is low-rank and acts as a low-pass filter in the forward pass, and in the backward pass gradients get projected onto the dominant sub-space so minor spectral directions are gradient-starved, worsening the collapse each iteration. SpecFormer (Taobao/Alibaba) breaks the loop with three per-layer components: Learnable Spectral Softening (SVD of the layer input, then a power law sigma^tau with tau = sigmoid(a) in (0,1) learned, flattening the spectrum), Spectrum-softened Attention (Q and K from the softened H*, but V kept as the raw H - ablations show projecting or softening V is much worse), and a Spectral Residual Position Encoding U P U^T whose entries come from a second-order Taylor expansion of normalized singular values anchored on sigma_1^2, plus a spatial residual from un-softened embeddings. SpecFormer beats Wide&Deep, DeepFM, GDCN, AutoInt, FiBiNet++, HiFormer, FAT, RankMixer, OneTrans, WhitenRec, and FEDIN on an industrial dataset (AUC 0.7611 / GAUC 0.6537) plus Criteo (0.7587) and Avazu (0.7574), and unlike OneTrans its AUC and attention effective rank both keep rising from 3 to 9 layers. Removing spectral softening is the most damaging ablation (Criteo AUC 0.7587 -> 0.6555). Deployed on Alibaba's e-commerce ads platform (10% traffic, 10 days) it gave +1.34% CTR, +15.97% CVR, +16.72% orders for +5 ms latency, with in-batch SVD, distributed dynamic embeddings, coalesced lookups (8% of baseline query frequency), FlashAttention (-40% per-layer attention cost) and FP16 giving +35% training efficiency.
