## Technical note

This DoorDash paper details transitioning homepage Ads ranking from tree-based models to multi-task DNNs for last-mile-delivery conversion prediction under a Cost-Per-Acquisition model where post-view CVR prediction drives the auction. It covers data foundations, model design, training efficiency, calibration, and online serving, and uses pre-trained item embeddings (OpenAI text-embedding-3-large reduced to 256 dims, 6M items, ~3GB) with weighted-sum pooling, yielding +0.6% online AUC and +0.18% merchant trial rate. Multi-task learning shares signals across pCTR, pATC, and pCVR for the nascent Ads vertical with limited data.
