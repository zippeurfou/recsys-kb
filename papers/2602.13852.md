## Technical note

This Adobe paper presents Experimentation Accelerator, a production framework that leverages historical A/B results and content embeddings to (i) prioritize which creative variants to test, (ii) explain why winners win, and (iii) surface high-potential new variants under scarce experimentation traffic. It trains a CTR ranking model on treatment embeddings and outcomes with fixed effects for contextual shifts, re-expresses the ranker in a curated semantic marketing-attribute space via a sign-consistent sparse constrained Lasso (yielding signed per-attribute contributions and natural-language insights), and computes an opportunity index combining attribute importance with under-expression to flag missing high-impact attributes; LLMs then turn ranked opportunities into concrete creative suggestions with estimated learning/conversion potential. Deployed live in Adobe Journey Optimizer, it is validated on real customer experiments showing faithful attribute-tied explanations and actionable opportunities.
