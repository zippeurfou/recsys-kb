## Summary

Current CTR prediction models often use early data aggregation, sacrificing fine-grained information needed for better scaling. To overcome this, researchers introduced EST (Efficiently Scalable Transformer), a unified model that processes all raw inputs together without loss, using specialized attention mechanisms (Lightweight Cross-Attention and Content Sparse Attention) to focus on important features. This approach yields stable and predictable performance gains as the model scales up, significantly improving ad revenue and click-through rates in real-world deployment.

## Technical note

EST (Efficiently Scalable Transformer) processes all raw CTR inputs (non-behavioral features, user and candidate behaviors, content signals) in a single unified sequence without lossy early aggregation, deployed on Taobao display ads. It is motivated by two observations: cross-attention between non-behavioral queries and behavioral keys/values carries the most information (via effective-rank/masking analysis), and content features work best as similarity priors rather than token embeddings. Lightweight Cross-Attention (LCA) prunes redundant behavior-to-behavior interactions and enables user-candidate decoupled computation reuse; Content Sparse Attention (CSA) uses frozen content-feature cosine similarity with top-K sparsification as a training-free attention matrix. EST exhibits power-law scaling in compute and capacity and delivers +3.27% RPM and +1.22% CTR online.
