## Summary

This paper introduces δ-EMG, a novel graph-based approach for Error-Bounded Approximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) search. Unlike existing methods that only guarantee high recall (ε), δ-EMG ensures that the returned neighbors are a (1/δ)-approximation of the true nearest neighbors by enforcing a monotonic geometric constraint during graph construction. The authors further optimize this framework with δ-EMQG, a quantized variant, and demonstrate its effectiveness on standard benchmarks, achieving significantly higher query-per-second rates under a recall requirement.

## Technical note

The paper proposes delta-EMG, an Error-bounded Monotonic Graph for approximate nearest neighbor search that, unlike the usual epsilon-recall-bounded methods, gives a provable (1/delta)-approximation guarantee for arbitrary top-k queries. A delta-monotonic geometric occlusion constraint during graph construction ensures greedy search converges to a bounded-error neighbor once a local optimum is reached, without backtracking. To scale, they add delta-EMQG, a degree-balanced quantized variant with near-linear construction complexity that integrates RaBitQ vector quantization plus an adaptive strategy mixing approximate and exact distance computation. On ANN-Benchmarks it reaches ~19,000 QPS on SIFT1M at 0.99 recall, outperforming NSG/HNSW/SymphonyQG by 1.2x-3.2x.
