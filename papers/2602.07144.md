## Summary

Bayesian Optimization with Natural Simplicity and Interpretability":

Standard Bayesian optimization often unnecessarily pushes parameters away from their default configurations, leading to complex recommendations hard to interpret. The BONSAI algorithm addresses this by being 'default-aware', pruning deviations that have minimal impact while controlling the loss in acquisition value. It achieves comparable regret guarantees to standard GP-UCB methods but significantly simplifies recommended solutions and is much faster than other sparse Bayesian optimization techniques.

## Technical note

BONSAI is a default-aware Bayesian optimization policy that prunes low-impact parameter deviations from a specified default configuration while controlling the loss in acquisition value, producing minimal-change, interpretable recommendations. It is compatible with expected improvement and GP-UCB, and is proven to retain the same no-regret rate as vanilla GP-UCB while provably recovering the minimal-l0 relevant-coordinate set under known ARD lengthscales. Empirically it substantially reduces non-default parameters at only ~1.5x candidate-generation cost versus 7-34x for prior sparse-BO methods (IR, ER, SEBO).
