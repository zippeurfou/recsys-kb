## Summary

1.  Existing generative recommendation methods struggle with Meituan's diverse business needs due to a "seesaw phenomenon" (difficulty capturing complex cross-business patterns) and "representation confusion" (shared semantic IDs failing to distinguish different business types).
2.  To resolve these issues, the authors introduce MBGR, a novel framework comprising three key components: Business-aware IDs (BID) for better semantic integrity, Multi-Business Prediction (MBP) for business-specific recommendations, and Label Dynamic Routing (LDR) to enhance label density.
3.  MBGR effectively addresses the multi-business challenges, as validated by extensive offline and online experiments on Meituan's platform, and has been successfully deployed in production.

## Technical note

MBGR is the first generative recommendation framework tailored for multi-business scenarios, deployed on Meituan's food-delivery platform. It targets two failures of Next-Token-Prediction generative recommenders: a seesaw phenomenon from inability to capture cross-business behavioral patterns, and representation confusion from a unified Semantic ID space causing gradient coupling. The framework has three modules: a Business-aware semantic ID (BID) with domain-aware tokenization to preserve semantic integrity, a Multi-Business Prediction (MBP) structure using a parameter-sharing mixture-of-experts tower with weighted attention aggregation, and a Label Dynamic Routing (LDR) module that converts sparse multi-business labels into dense labels. Online experiments report a 3.98% CTCVR improvement, and the system is deployed in production.
