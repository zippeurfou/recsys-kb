## Summary

Existing recommender systems often rely on interaction-driven prediction and struggle to adapt to evolving trends or diverse business needs. To address this, AliExpress introduced SIGMA, a generative recommender leveraging semantics and instructions. It uses a unified latent space for semantic grounding, hybrid tokenization, a large multi-task dataset, and a task-adaptive generation process to effectively handle various recommendation tasks and business requirements, validated through offline and online tests.

## Technical note

SIGMA is a semantic-grounded, instruction-driven generative multi-task recommender deployed at AliExpress that goes beyond interaction-driven next-item prediction to serve diverse business-specific demands via natural-language instruction-following. It grounds item entities in a unified latent space via a multi-view alignment framework capturing both general semantics and collaborative signals. It uses a hybrid item tokenization that combines Semantic-ID prefixes with item-specific ID tokens for both structural and fine-grained modeling. A large-scale multi-task SFT dataset trains the model, and a three-step item generation procedure with adaptive probabilistic fusion calibrates output distributions to trade off accuracy and diversity per task. Offline experiments and online A/B tests validate its versatility.
