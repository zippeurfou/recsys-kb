## Technical note

Uniboost is a unified traffic-allocation framework for the blending/re-ranking stage that fixes coupled allocation plans, score inflation, and poor interpretability by (1) a posterior value alignment that calibrates abstract blending scores to an 'Effective Completion Rate' anchor metric with business semantics without changing ranking order, and (2) an independent linear boosting-with-bias paradigm that subsumes both PID-based ad guaranteed delivery and cold-start boost weighting while enabling precise per-plan cost attribution. Online A/B tests show reducing overall weighted-score weight mitigates business interference and improves micro-level allocation efficiency, while aggregated dashboards give macro-level guidance for system iteration.
