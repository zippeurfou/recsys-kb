## Technical note

HCMRM (High-Consistency Multimodal Relevance Model) improves query-to-short-video relevance matching for Kuaishou search ads, closing the gap between pretraining (pairwise image-text alignment) and the downstream query-video-text triplet relevance task. Built on ALBEF with minimal changes, during pretraining it extracts important keywords from video text as pseudo-queries to perform triplet relevance modeling (alongside image-text contrastive, ITM, MLM, and hard negatives); for fine-tuning it introduces a symmetric hierarchical softmax loss over four ordinal relevance tiers to learn label order while separating positives/negatives. Deployed over a year, it cut the proportion of irrelevant ads by 6.1% and raised ad revenue 1.4%.
