## Technical note

DMI (Diffusion Multi-Interest model) refines coarse-grained user interest representations at the dimension level for multi-interest candidate matching, where attention-based aggregation of item embeddings tends to include irrelevant information. DMI injects controllable dimension-level noise into coarse interest vectors, then iteratively reconstructs personalized interest vectors using a cross-attention mechanism plus an item-pruning strategy guided by tailored collaborative information. It surpasses state-of-the-art methods on offline evaluations and an online A/B test at Xiaohongshu, and is deployed serving major traffic for hundreds of millions of daily active users.
