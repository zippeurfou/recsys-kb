Personalized Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) relies on accurately selecting user-relevant documents. In practice, existing RAG approaches often suffer from high retrieval costs and overlook that collaborative signals from similar users can enhance personalized generation for the current user. We propose ClusterRAG, a Cluster-Based Collaborative Filtering for Personalized Retrieval-Augmented Generation. ## Technical note

ClusterRAG adds cluster-based collaborative filtering to personalized RAG: it aggregates each user's profile documents into embeddings, groups users into cohorts via HDBSCAN density-based clustering, builds cluster-level ranking matrices (e.g., scored with ColBERT), and retrieves candidate profile documents from top-k similar users to form collaborative, user-only, or hybrid prompts. Evaluated on the LaMP benchmark across retrievers (ColBERT, Contriever, BGE, BM25), jointly using the target user's profile plus top similar users' profiles consistently gives the best generation quality, and the method is model-agnostic across fine-tuned seq2seq and zero-shot LLMs.
