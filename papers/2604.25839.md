## Technical note

OCARM predicts user revisit/retention at RTB bidding time for ad-driven re-engagement, where highly predictive post-conversion 'Onboarding Content' is unavailable at inference and using it directly causes feature leakage and a train-serve gap. It uses a two-stage distillation-aligned framework: stage one deliberately leaks onboarding content to train a hierarchical-attention teacher encoder (capturing intra-day and inter-day temporal causality); stage two aligns a user-side encoder to the frozen teacher via distillation so only observable features are needed at serving. A leakage experiment confirmed a substantial AUC gain from onboarding content, and offline experiments plus online A/B tests show consistent improvements in a real growth scenario.
