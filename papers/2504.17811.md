## Technical note

OmniSage is Pinterest's large-scale representation learning framework that learns universal entity embeddings by unifying graph neural networks with content-based models and user-sequence models through multiple contrastive learning tasks. It defines three contrastive tasks, entity-entity (graph), entity-feature (content), and user-entity (user activity sequence), and extends GraphSage with transformer aggregation and random-walk neighbor sampling, backed by infrastructure scaling to heterogeneous graphs with billions of nodes and edges. Deployed across five applications, OmniSage's universal representations improved sitewide repins (saves) by approximately 2.5%, and the model code is publicly released.
