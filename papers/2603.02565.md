The Generator-Evaluator (G-E) framework, i.e., evaluating K sequences from a generator and selecting the top-ranked one according to evaluator scores, is a foundational paradigm in tasks such as Recommender Systems (RecSys) and Natural Language Processing (NLP). Traditional evaluators process sequences independently, suffering from two major limitations: (1) lack of explicit cross-sequence comparison, leading to suboptimal accuracy; (2) poor parallelization with linear complexity of O(K), result...

## Technical note

FlashEvaluator redesigns the Evaluator stage of the Generator-Evaluator (G-E) reranking paradigm, which conventionally scores K candidate sequences independently with O(K) complexity and no cross-sequence comparison. It enables cross-sequence token information sharing via a list-agnostic full-item-set interaction module plus a cross-sequence feature interaction module, processing all K sequences in a single forward pass to achieve sublinear complexity and explicit inter-sequence comparison, improving both selection accuracy and throughput (QPS). Backed by theoretical proofs and experiments on recommendation and NLP tasks, FlashEvaluator is deployed in Kuaishou's online recommender system with substantial sustained revenue gains.
