## Technical note

Kuaishou's DFTopK is a differentiable Top-K operator for cascade ranking with optimal O(n) time complexity, overcoming the non-differentiability of Top-K that blocks end-to-end training. Unlike LTR losses (LambdaLoss/NDCG) that misalign with set selection or differentiable-sorting methods (ARF, LCRON) that suffer O(n^2) cost and gradient conflicts from sum-to-one permutation constraints, DFTopK relaxes normalization to admit a closed-form solution that avoids sorting and gradient conflicts. On the RecFlow benchmark (replacing NeuralSort in LCRON) and an industrial system it improves training efficiency and performance; online A/B tests yielded up to +1.77% advertising revenue and +1.5% conversions with reduced per-impression compute.
