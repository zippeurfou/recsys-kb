## Summary

This paper introduces SIDE, a novel approach addressing the scaling challenges in sequence-based recommendation models that process long user histories (O(10^3) to O(10^4) events). It proposes using compact Semantic IDs generated by a multi-task VQ-VAE framework called VQ fusion. A key innovation is a parameter-free method for converting these IDs back into their original embeddings, significantly reducing storage and inference costs compared to traditional SID approaches.

## Technical note

Industrial ad-ranking sequence models handle 10^3-10^4 events, making per-event content embeddings expensive to store and serve in real-time models. The paper injects a compact Semantic ID via vector quantization instead of many embeddings, with three innovations: VQ Fusion, a multi-task VQ-VAE that fuses multiple content embeddings and categorical predictions into one Semantic ID; SIDE, a parameter-free granular SID-to-embedding conversion that avoids large lookup tables; and Discrete-PCA (DPCA), a quantization method generalizing residual quantization. On a large-scale industrial ads system this yields a 2.4x normalized-entropy gain improvement and 3x reduction in data footprint versus traditional SID methods.
