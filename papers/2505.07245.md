## Summary

REMEDI is a novel multi-stage framework designed to predict rare future vehicle purchases effectively among existing owners (who make up the vast majority of data), addressing extreme class imbalance (<0.5% positive rate) and complex behavioral patterns. It achieves this by first training diverse base models, then creating relative performance meta-features for their effective fusion via a hybrid-expert architecture, and finally distilling the combined knowledge into a single, efficient model using supervised fine-tuning with MSE loss. This method significantly outperforms baselines on large datasets, successfully meeting business targets like identifying 50% of buyers in top recommendations, while demonstrating practical deployment efficiency for imbalanced prediction problems in industry settings.

## Technical note

REMEDI is a three-stage framework for predicting rare vehicle repurchase among ~800K existing owners under extreme class imbalance (<0.5% positive rate). It first trains five diverse base models (GBDT/LightGBM, MLP, DeepFM/xDeepFM, a focal-loss GBDT, and feature-subset specialists), then a hybrid-expert meta-learner fuses their out-of-fold predictions using relative-performance meta-features (deviation from ensemble mean, rank, spread) inspired by Group Relative Policy Optimization, and finally distills the teacher ensemble into a single LightGBM student via supervised fine-tuning with MSE loss on soft probabilities. On automotive data REMEDI hits the business target of ~10% precision at ~50% recall in the top-60K leads; the student retains ~97% of teacher performance with ~10x faster inference, and ablations show relative features, hybrid-expert architecture, base-model diversity, and soft-label distillation each help.
