## Summary

This paper introduces Deep Bayesian Multi-Target Learning (DBMTL), a framework that models multiple recommender system targets (like click-through rate, user stay time, etc.) using a Bayesian network structure learned by deep neural networks. DBMTL aims to optimize these diverse targets simultaneously, leveraging their interdependencies for improved performance, as demonstrated on Taobao live-streaming recommendations. The approach creates significant synergy by allowing targets to benefit each other, outperforming standard multi-target and single-target models, and offers a general method applicable to various target distributions.

## Technical note

DBMTL (Deep Bayesian Multi-Target Learning) jointly optimizes multiple recommendation targets (CTR, dwell time, purchase, interactions) by modeling them as a Bayesian network whose directed target-to-target links are parameterized by MLP hidden layers. Unlike vanilla MTL that assumes conditional independence between targets, DBMTL factorizes the joint likelihood as P(l,m|x,H)=P(l|x,H)P(m|l,x,H), imposing weaker assumptions and letting the model learn cross-target causal directions from data via model selection. This integrated feature-and-target network captures synergy so learning one target benefits others. Applied to Taobao live-streaming, it significantly outperforms vanilla MTL and single-target models and generalizes to sparse and continuous-valued targets.
