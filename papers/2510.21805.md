## Technical note

DiffGRM is a diffusion-based generative recommendation model that replaces the autoregressive decoder over n-digit semantic IDs with a masked discrete diffusion model (MDM), enabling bidirectional cross-digit context and any-order parallel SID generation. This addresses two structural mismatches of ARMs on SIDs: intra-item consistency (left-to-right causality blocking cross-digit evidence) and unordered digit dependencies. On Amazon Reviews (Sports, Beauty, Toys) it consistently beats strong generative and discriminative baselines, improving NDCG@10 by 6.9%-15.5%, with higher sample efficiency per effective sample pass.
