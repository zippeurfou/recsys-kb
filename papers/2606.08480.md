## Summary

1.  **Problem:** Generative Recommendation using RL (GRPO) can be enhanced by reward signals but suffers from unreliable rewards in production rankers, which are trained on biased logs and lead to detrimental effects for many samples during training.
2.  **Solution:** The paper introduces AdaGRPO, a framework that selectively incorporates reward guidance into the loss function only when two diagnostics (policy uncertainty & reward discriminability) indicate it is beneficial or safe, defaulting other samples to supervised learning.
3.  **Results:** AdaGRPO shows significant improvement over standard methods in balancing retrieval effectiveness and generative validity on an e-commerce dataset, achieving higher HR@10 while reducing hallucination, and demonstrates robust performance even with potentially noisy final rewards through production A/B testing.

## Technical note

AdaGRPO applies reinforcement learning to generative recommendation (autoregressive decoding of Semantic IDs) while addressing the untrustworthiness of production rankers used as reward models, which are trained on exposure-biased, sparse logs. A stratified analysis shows reward guidance helps only when the policy is uncertain AND the ranker can cleanly discriminate the ground-truth item from rollout negatives; elsewhere the GRPO advantage collapses to reward noise (easy samples) or pushes the policy toward popular distractors (hard, miscalibrated samples). AdaGRPO anchors every instance in supervised NLL and gates the GRPO term with a per-sample binary clip from two rank-based rollout diagnostics: policy-side difficulty (ground truth outside top rollouts) and reward-side discriminability, lifting PPO's ratio-domain trust region to the sample domain. On a large e-commerce dataset it raises HR@10 from 11.01% to 12.18% while holding hallucination <=0.22%, beating fixed NLL-GRPO mixtures on the retrieval-validity frontier, with significant A/B gains in CTR and dwell time.
