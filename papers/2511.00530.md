## Technical note

This Adobe work introduces User Behavior Trajectory Prediction (UBTP), forecasting a coherent multi-step sequence of future user actions rather than myopic next-item prediction, to capture long-term preferences. It proposes Listwise Preference Diffusion Optimization (LPDO), a diffusion-based training framework that directly optimizes structured preferences over entire item sequences via a Plackett-Luce supervision signal and a tight variational lower bound aligned with listwise ranking likelihoods, overcoming the independent-token assumption of prior diffusion methods. It introduces the Sequential Match (SeqMatch) metric plus perplexity, and on real-world benchmarks LPDO consistently outperforms state-of-the-art baselines for structured preference learning.
