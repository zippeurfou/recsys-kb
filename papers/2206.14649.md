## Summary

Modern recommender systems often use a two-stage process: a fast retriever to filter large item pools and a slower ranker to refine the best options. However, traditional methods either train these components separately or together with significant flaws, like inaccurate item distribution during training/inference, missing relevant items, or incorrect ranking orders. Therefore, the paper highlights the ongoing challenge of finding effective ways for the retriever and ranker to collaborate better.

## Technical note

CoRR is a joint training framework where a retriever and ranker in a two-stage deep recommender mutually reinforce each other, addressing item-distribution shift, false negatives, and ranking-order misalignment of prior cascade training. The ranker is trained with sampled log-softmax over hard negatives drawn adaptively from the retriever (via a multi-level inverted index enabling sublinear-time sampling), avoiding the false negatives of exact top-k sampling, while the retriever is guided by knowledge distillation that aligns its softmax-normalized ranking order with the ranker's. A key technique is a proposed asymptotic-unbiased 'sampled KL divergence' estimator that corrects the bias from item sampling, better aligning ranking order and improving retrieval quality. Experiments on four benchmarks show CoRR is a model-agnostic training method that improves both retriever and ranker over independent and prior joint-training baselines.

- Caser:  Caser is a famous model which apply several CNN units with kernels of different size to capture user intent
- BERT4Rec:  BERT4Rec applies the cloze task in sequential recommendation, which is firstly proposed in BERT in language modeling. And it captures user intent with a Transformer encoder
- SASRec : SASRec models user’s behavior with Transformer encoder, where multi-head attention mechanism is attached to great importance
- Independent: Two-Stage is a simple combination of SASRec (as retriever) and DIN (as ranker), where the two models are trained independently. Other variants of the Two-Stage based approaches, such as cascading structures, can be attached in Section [4.4](#S4.SS4) and [4.5](#S4.SS5)
- ICC : ICC is a joint training method of cascade ranking. The score of a pair of query and key is actually the weighted sum of scores in each stage. And the weight of higher(more closed to retrieval) stage is larger
- RankFlow : RankFlow is a recently proposed joint training method, which consists of two alternative training flow(self learning and tutor learning). In self learning flow, upstream models provide top-k items as negatigves for the downstream models. And the downstream models distill weak signals to upstream models in the tutor learning flow (arXiv:2206.14649)
- RankDistill : ranks of topk items are used as the weights for negative log likelihood loss
- CoDistill : items are sampled by a ranking-based sampling strategy and then a cross entropy loss is used for closing the scores of two models (arXiv:2206.14649)
