## Summary

Modern web services use deep recommender systems (DRS) that typically employ a two-stage process of retrieval and ranking. While retrieval selects relevant items efficiently, ranking provides more precise results but is slower. The paper argues that traditional methods train these components separately or simply together, leading to limitations like inaccurate item selection, distribution shifts between training and use, and poor ranking alignment, indicating a need for better collaboration between the retriever and ranker.

## Technical note

CoRR (Cooperative Retriever and Ranker) jointly trains the retriever and ranker of a two-stage deep recommender so they mutually reinforce, addressing item distribution shift, false negatives, and ranking-order misalignment in prior independent or cascaded training. The ranker learns from hard negatives drawn by the retriever via an adaptive, scalable sublinear-time sampler (avoiding exact top-k false negatives) using a sampled log-softmax objective, while the retriever is distilled from the ranker by aligning softmax-normalized predictions with a novel asymptotic-unbiased sampled KL-divergence estimate that preserves relative ranking order. As the ranker sharpens and the retriever supplies harder negatives, both improve. Across four benchmarks CoRR outperforms independent, cascade, and existing joint-training methods and is model-agnostic.

- GRU4Rec:  GRU4Rec uses a one-layer GRU to obtain latent vector of user’s behavior sequence
- Caser:  Caser is a famous model which apply several CNN units with kernels of different size to capture user intent
- BERT4Rec:  BERT4Rec applies the cloze task in sequential recommendation, which is firstly proposed in BERT in language modeling. And it captures user intent with a Transformer encoder
- SASRec : SASRec models user’s behavior with Transformer encoder, where multi-head attention mechanism is attached to great importance
- Independent: Two-Stage is a simple combination of SASRec (as retriever) and DIN (as ranker), where the two models are trained independently. We use a two-step strategy to predict: retrieve items by SASRec and then rank those items by DIN. Other variants of the Two-Stage based approaches, such as cascading structures, can be attached in Section [4.4](#S4.SS4) and [4.5](#S4.SS5)
- ICC : ICC is a joint training method of cascade ranking. The score of a pair of query and key is actually the weighted sum of scores in each stage. And the weight of higher(more closed to retrieval) stage is larger
- RankFlow : RankFlow is a recently proposed joint training method, which consists of two alternative training flow(self learning and tutor learning). In self learning flow, upstream models provide top-k items as negatigves for the downstream models. And the downstream models distill weak signals to upstream models in the tutor learning flow (arXiv:2206.14649)
- RankDistill : ranks of topk items are used as the weights for negative log likelihood loss
- CoDistill : items are sampled by a ranking-based sampling strategy and then a cross entropy loss is used for closing the scores of two models (arXiv:2206.14649)
