## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Large-scale industrial recommendation systems typically employ a two-stage paradigm of retrieval and ranking to handle huge amounts of information. Recent research focuses on improving the performance of retrieval model. A promising way is to introduce extensive information about users and items. On one hand, lifelong sequential behavior is valuable. Existing lifelong behavior modeling methods in ranking stage focus on the interaction of lifelong behavior and candidate items from retrieval stage...

## Technical note

Kuaishou's MISS brings multimodal information and lifelong user sequences into a tree-based retrieval model, addressing the difficulty of using lifelong behavior in the retrieval stage over a huge candidate corpus. It builds a multi-modal index tree from multi-modal embeddings for precise item similarity, and adds a Collaborative General Search Unit (Co-GSU) and Multi-modal General Search Unit (MM-GSU) for multi-perspective interest search over the lifelong sequence. Online experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of combining tree indexing with lifelong multimodal sequence modeling over dual-tower and prior tree-based (TDM, NANN, Deep Retrieval) baselines.
