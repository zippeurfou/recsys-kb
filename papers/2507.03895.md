## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Feature interaction modeling is crucial for deep recommendation models. A common and effective approach is to construct explicit feature combinations to enhance model performance. However, in practice, only a small fraction of these combinations are truly informative. Thus it is essential to select useful feature combinations to reduce noise and manage memory consumption. ## Technical note

TayFCS is a lightweight high-order feature-combination selection method for deep recommender models, avoiding the exponential cost of evaluating combinations one by one. Its Taylor Expansion Scorer (TayScorer) approximates each combination's importance from its sub-components' gradients via a single backward pass on a trained model, and Logistic Regression Elimination (LRE) removes redundancy among combinations by estimating information gain from prediction performance. Experiments on three benchmarks validate effectiveness and efficiency, and online A/B tests demonstrate practical commercial value.
