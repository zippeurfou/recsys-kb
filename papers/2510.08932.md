## Technical note

MATT is a model-agnostic test-time paradigm for CTR prediction that optimizes the inference phase rather than training, targeting degradation from low-frequency feature combinations. It introduces a hierarchical probabilistic hashing method to estimate the occurrence frequency (confidence) of feature combinations at various orders, then uses these confidences as sampling probabilities to generate multiple instance-specific inference paths, aggregating their predictions for robustness. Extensive offline and online experiments validate that guiding inference away from low-confidence feature combinations improves CTR accuracy while maximizing the utility of already-trained models.
