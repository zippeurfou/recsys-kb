## Summary

This study explored whether generative AI could effectively supplement one-to-one tutoring, which is typically too expensive to scale. An exploratory RCT found that students receiving support from a pedagogically fine-tuned AI model called LearnLM performed at least as well as those receiving support from human tutors on various learning outcomes. The AI model proved reliable, with high tutor satisfaction, and was particularly effective at encouraging deeper student reflection through Socratic questioning, suggesting AI could play a promising role in delivering effective, individualized learning support at scale.

## Technical note

This exploratory RCT (N=165 students, five UK secondary schools) tested whether LearnLM, a pedagogically fine-tuned generative AI model, can scale one-to-one math tutoring on the Eedi platform. Expert tutors supervised LearnLM, approving 76.4% of its drafted messages with zero or minimal edits, indicating reliable pedagogical instruction. Students supported by LearnLM performed at least as well as those with human tutors and were 5.5 percentage points more likely (66.2% vs 60.7%) to solve novel transfer problems. Tutors praised its Socratic questioning that prompted deeper student reflection, suggesting pedagogically-tuned AI can deliver effective individualized support at scale.
