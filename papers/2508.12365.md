## Technical note

TaoSR1 directly deploys a generative LLM for Taobao query-product relevance prediction, going beyond BERT-based matching that lacks reasoning. Its three-stage framework: (1) SFT with chain-of-thought to instill reasoning; (2) offline pass@N sampling with DPO to improve generation quality and reduce CoT error accumulation; (3) difficulty-based dynamic sampling with GRPO to mitigate discriminative hallucination. Post-CoT processing and cumulative-probability relevance-tier partitioning enable feasible online serving. It significantly outperforms baselines on challenging offline data and improves online side-by-side human evaluations.
