## Technical note

QDGenSumRT pioneers generative models for real-time query-driven text summarization in industrial web search, replacing the lossy multi-stage extract-rank-select pipeline. It distills a 10B teacher into a 0.1B student, then applies SFT on human-annotated data, DPO on online preference data, plus FP8 quantization, TensorRT-LLM, and lookahead decoding for deployment. The 0.1B model beats the Baidu production baseline and reaches a new SOTA while serving ~50,000 queries/sec at 55 ms average latency on 334 NVIDIA L20 GPUs.
