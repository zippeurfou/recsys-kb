## Technical note

This Walmart paper presents an end-to-end semantic ads retrieval pipeline for sponsored search. It pretrains a BERT-like classification model with product-category information to inject Walmart product semantics, uses a two-tower Siamese network for efficient embedding retrieval, and introduces a Human-in-the-loop Progressive Fusion Training method for robustness. It improves the search-relevance metric (NDCG, Irrelevant Ads Rate) by up to 16% over a DSSM baseline, and large-scale online A/B testing shows it surpasses the production model's ad revenue.
