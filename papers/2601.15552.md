## Technical note

BanditLP is a scalable multi-stakeholder contextual-bandit framework from LinkedIn that unifies neural Thompson Sampling for exploration and expressive outcome modeling with a large-scale linear program (handling billions of variables) for constrained action selection at serving time. Rewards and costs are estimated and sampled via neural TS, then an LP sub-routine selects actions satisfying interdependent multi-level constraints (platform, provider/business-line, and user-level, e.g., frequency caps, fair B2C/B2B representation). It is architecture-agnostic and web-scale. Deployed in LinkedIn's email marketing system, it delivered significant online business gains across multiple objectives and reached production, being the first email-marketing RS to combine contextual bandits with constrained optimization.
