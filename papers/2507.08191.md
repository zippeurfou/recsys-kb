## Technical note

This is the overview report for the TREC 2021 Deep Learning track, the third year, using refreshed MS MARCO passage and document collections (roughly 4x larger documents and 16x larger passages). Deep neural ranking models using large-scale pretraining continued to outperform traditional retrieval methods, and single-stage retrieval achieved good performance though still below multi-stage retrieval pipelines. The report also raises concerns about NIST judgment completeness and the quality of training labels mapped from the old collections to the new, larger ones.
