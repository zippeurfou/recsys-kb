## Summary

This paper introduces a new task called document-grounded related insight generation, designed to help users refine their answers to open-ended questions by providing additional relevant insights from supporting documents. To address this, the authors created the SCOpE-QA dataset and developed InsightGen, a two-stage approach using clustering and LLMs to select context and generate diverse insights. Their evaluation demonstrates that InsightGen produces useful insights, establishing a baseline for this new type of AI interaction.

## Technical note

This paper introduces document-grounded related insight generation, a task where a system produces additional document-grounded insights to help users improve, extend, or rethink an initial answer to an open-ended question, supporting iterative refinement rather than one-shot QA. The authors curate and release SCOpE-QA, 3,000 open-ended questions across 20 research collections. Their InsightGen method is two-stage: first build a thematic representation of the document collection by re-imagining K-Means over document chunks into a graph, then traverse neighborhoods of that thematic graph to select context that is related but non-repetitive with the current answer, feeding it to LLMs via chain-of-thought prompting. This shifts retrieval from plain similarity to structural complementarity, and evaluation over two generation models and two settings shows consistently useful, relevant insights as a strong baseline.
