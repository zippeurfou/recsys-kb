## Summary

1.  Current Virtual Try-On (VTO) systems excel at visualizing garment appearance but fail to accurately depict how ill-fitting garments (too large or small for the body) would look, as they lack datasets capturing precise size information for "ill-fit" cases.
2.  To address this gap, the authors introduce FIT, the first large-scale dataset containing over 1.13 million try-on image triplets, complete with detailed body and garment measurements specifically designed for ill-fitting scenarios.
3.  The FIT dataset was created using a scalable synthetic approach involving programmatically generated 3D garments, physics simulations for realistic fit, and a novel re-texturing model preserving geometry and identity, enabling the training of a fit-aware VTO model that achieves state-of-the-art results.

## Technical note

FIT introduces the first large-scale fit-aware virtual try-on dataset, over 1.13M try-on triplets with precise body and garment measurements including extreme ill-fit cases (e.g. 3XL on XS), addressing the absence of size-annotated data in catalog-scraped benchmarks. The pipeline programmatically generates 3D garments with GarmentCode, cross-drapes them onto parametric bodies via physics simulation (with box-mesh realignment to avoid draping failures), then fine-tunes Flux.1-dev as a re-texturing model conditioned on composite normal maps and VLM-generated prompts to produce photorealistic images while preserving geometry. An identity-preserving variant synthesizes paired person images (same subject/pose, different garment), enabling supervised rather than self-supervised training. A baseline Fit-VTO model with a custom measurement encoder sets state-of-the-art fit-aware try-on and provides a benchmark.
