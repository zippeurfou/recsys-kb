## Technical note

CreAgent is an LLM-empowered creator-simulation agent for long-term recommender-system evaluation under platform-creator information asymmetry (creators see only feedback on their own items while platforms see all). It combines game-theoretic belief modeling and a fast-and-slow-thinking framework to simulate strategic content creation, and is fine-tuned with Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO). Credibility experiments show CreAgent aligns with real platform-creator behavior, and simulations let researchers study how fairness- and diversity-aware algorithms affect long-term multi-stakeholder outcomes.
