## Technical note

CDiff4Rec is a collaborative diffusion recommender that tackles two limitations of diffusion-based CF (DiffRec): loss of personalized information under noise injection and underuse of item-side content. It converts item review words into 'pseudo-users' (via TF-IDF + min-max normalization), then for each query user identifies top-K neighbors from both real users and pseudo-users by cosine behavioral similarity, and aggregates their preference information into the diffusion reconstruction via attention weights (average pooling, behavior similarity, or parametric). Backbone is an MLP denoiser; on Yelp, Amazon-Game, and Citeulike-t it beats BPRMF, LightGCN, MultiVAE, EASE, ConVAE, and DiffRec, e.g. +12.3% Recall@10 and +12.1% NDCG@10 over DiffRec on Yelp with only ~10.5% extra wall time.
