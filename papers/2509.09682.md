## Technical note

Training sequential recommenders (SASRec) with full cross-entropy over million-item catalogs is memory-bound (logit tensor ~100GB), and it was unclear whether to maximize negatives (ns) or spend memory on batch size/sequence length. The paper adapts Cut Cross-Entropy (CCE) from LLMs to RecSys and introduces CCE^-, a Triton kernel fusing matmul+log-sum-exp on sampled negatives with no logit materialization, plus gradient filtering that exploits the near-zero (saturated) sparsity of final-layer gradients under large catalogs. Across six datasets CCE^- cuts memory by >=87% and epoch time by up to 89% (vs CE^-), and reallocating the savings to larger bs/sl/ns yields up to ~30-55% NDCG@10 gains on large-catalog data (Megamarket +54.7%). A power-law scaling analysis shows batch size dominates on 4/6 datasets, ns is second, sequence length is least influential and should be reduced first under a memory budget.
