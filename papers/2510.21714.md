Long-sequence modeling has become an indispensable frontier in recommendation systems for capturing users' long-term preferences. However, user behaviors within advertising domains are inherently sparse, posing a significant barrier to constructing long behavioral sequences using data from a single advertising domain alone. ## Technical note

Tencent presents practical long-behavior-sequence modeling for advertising where ad behaviors are sparse, so they build unified cross-domain/scenario commercial trajectories spanning ads and content domains. Within a two-stage (search + sequence-modeling) framework they add hierarchical hard search plus decoupled embedding-based soft search, and Decoupled Side Information Temporal Interest Networks (TIN), Target-Decoupled Positional Encoding and Target-Decoupled SASRec, and Stacked TIN to address feature-taxonomy gaps, inter-field interference, and target-wise interference. Deployment delivered a 4.22% GMV lift in WeChat Channels and 1.96% GMV increase in WeChat Moments.
