## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of modeling long user behavior sequences in recommendation systems, specifically for sparse advertising data. To overcome this, the authors integrated user behavior data across multiple domains and scenarios, creating unified commercial trajectories. They then proposed a two-stage framework with novel techniques like hierarchical search, decoupled embeddings, and specialized sequence models (Decoupled Side Information TIN, Target-Decoupled SASRec, Stacked TIN) to manage feature conflicts and target-specific patterns, achieving significant performance gains measured by GMV increases.

## Technical note

This Tencent paper details production practice for long-behavior-sequence CTR modeling in advertising, where ad behaviors are sparse so sequences are built by unifying cross-scenario and cross-domain (content) behaviors into commercial behavior trajectories. It addresses inter-field interference, target-wise interference, and high-order correlation within a two-stage search-then-sequence-modeling framework: a hierarchical hard search plus decoupled embedding-based soft search (DARE) in the search stage, and decoupled side-information Temporal Interest Networks, Target-Decoupled Positional Encoding, Target-Decoupled SASRec, and Stacked TIN in the modeling stage. It also adds engineering optimizations (parameter/data prefetch, GPU key-collection, multi-stream) for efficiency. Deployment yielded 4.22% GMV lift in WeChat Channels and 1.96% in WeChat Moments.
