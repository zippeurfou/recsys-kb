## Summary

This paper introduces NONTP to overcome two training signal limitations in generative recommendation: temporal locality (only optimizing single-step prediction) and spatial locality (no gradient from cross-domain context). The proposed approach uses auxiliary objectives—Temporal Contrastive Learning (TCL) for extending time coverage by aligning hidden states with future trajectories, and Trans-Domain Learning (TDL) for incorporating information across domains via shared prediction. Extensive experiments show NONTP significantly improves recommendation performance on both industrial and public datasets compared to existing methods like NTP, MBGR, and standard baselines, demonstrating its effectiveness through rigorous online A/B tests.

## Technical note

Diagnoses two structural limits of Next-Token Prediction (NTP) in generative recommendation: temporal locality (supervision confined to the single next item) and spatial locality (each target embedding's gradient comes from one preceding hidden state, starving sparse domains). NONTP adds two inference-free auxiliary objectives on an HSTU backbone with RQ-VAE semantic IDs: TCL (Temporal Contrastive Learning) uses a BYOL-style EMA teacher plus InfoNCE to align hidden states with a K-step future trajectory, and TDL (Trans-Domain Learning) mean-pools cross-domain hidden states and predicts through the shared head to open a second gradient pathway. On a four-domain Meituan dataset (full ranking) NONTP lifts HR@10 +34.3% over NTP and +18.3% over MBGR, with sparse domains benefiting most; on Amazon Movie-Book-CDs it gains HR@10 +2.8% / NDCG@10 +3.7%. Online A/B on Meituan's DSP recall stage shows CTR +1.8% and GMV +2.1% (both p<0.01).
