## Technical note

This Alimama paper introduces Net Conversion Rate (NetCVR) prediction, the probability a clicked item is purchased and not refunded, which involves a two-stage cascaded delayed feedback (Click->Conversion and Conversion->Refund) with opposing effects that standard CVR methods can't handle. The authors release CASCADE, the first large-scale open Taobao dataset for online continuous NetCVR prediction, and propose TESLA, an online continuous method featuring a CVR-RFR cascaded architecture, stage-wise debiasing, and a delay-time-aware ranking loss. TESLA outperforms the strongest baseline by an absolute +12.41% RI-AUC and +14.94% RI-PRAUC on NetCVR.
