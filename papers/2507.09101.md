## Technical note

S2SRec2 (Walmart) formulates grocery basket completion as set-to-set recommendation guided by recipe knowledge, moving beyond traditional leave-one-out methods that predict a single missing ingredient. Rather than filling one item, it predicts a set of complementary ingredients from a partial basket using recipe knowledge to complete a coherent meal, better reflecting real-world multi-item completion. The set-based approach captures ingredient co-occurrence and complementarity for improved culinary recommendation over single-ingredient baselines.
