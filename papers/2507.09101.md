## Summary

1.  The paper addresses the problem of *basket completion* in grocery recommendations by using recipe knowledge to suggest multiple missing ingredients for partial ingredient sets, recognizing that real-world scenarios often require several complements at once and ignoring interactions between them is inadequate.
2.  It introduces S2SRec2, a novel set-to-set recommendation framework based on Set Transformer technology, designed specifically to predict the complete set of needed ingredients given an incomplete basket (query).
3.  This system learns two related tasks jointly: retrieving missing ingredients from the query representation and assessing overall basket completeness after prediction – significantly outperforming traditional single-item recommendations by providing a more holistic and accurate completion approach.

## Technical note

S2SRec2 reformulates recipe/basket completion as a set-to-set problem, predicting a set of complementary ingredients rather than a single leave-one-out item. Built on a permutation-invariant Set Transformer encoder, it is trained with multi-task learning on two heads: a learnable-query missing-ingredient prediction (cross-entropy over 3,804 candidates) and a basket-completeness classifier (BCE). At inference it iteratively adds the top-predicted ingredient and stops when the completeness probability exceeds 0.5, avoiding over-generation. On a 141,782-recipe food.com dataset it outperforms LR, vanilla NN, Bi-LSTM, Kitchenette, and Reciptor baselines on precision/recall/F1 and set-cardinality MSE, with ablations confirming both the set encoder and stop head are essential.
