## Technical note

R3S (Reallocated Reward for Recommender Systems) is an offline-RL recommendation framework that jointly balances intrinsic world-model bias and recommendation diversity. It uses a diffusion-based world model to explicitly capture uncertainty in reward predictions and adds decaying penalizers that deter actions reducing state variety at local and global scales, extending diversity penalization with attenuated interactive mechanisms within an A2C-based policy. Experiments on three real-world benchmarks show improved world-model accuracy and better harmonization of heterogeneous user preferences versus state-of-the-art offline-RL baselines like BCQ, CQL, and MOPO.
