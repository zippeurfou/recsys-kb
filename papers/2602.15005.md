## Summary

This paper tackles the challenge of cross-domain news recommendation by using reinforcement learning (RL) to train large language models. The core method generates high-quality interest-driven news search queries from diverse user signals, formulated as policy optimization with GRPO and multiple reward signals, demonstrating scaling benefits. To enable practical deployment, the learned policy is distilled into a compact student model, validated through extensive offline and online A/B tests that confirm significant improvements in both interest modeling and recommendation performance.

## Technical note

The paper reformulates cross-domain news interest discovery as an interest-driven query-list generation task, training an instruction-tuned LLM with reinforcement learning to turn heterogeneous user signals into reusable search queries. Optimization uses Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with five reward signals: retrieval alignment, interest coverage, query specificity, intra-list diversity, and structural validity. The authors study two compute axes, inference-time sampling and model capacity, observing scaling-law-like improvements in interest quality and downstream retrieval. For deployment they apply on-policy distillation to compress the compute-intensive teacher policy into a compact low-latency student that retains most gains. Offline evaluation and large-scale online A/B tests in a production news recommender show consistent lifts in interest-modeling quality and recommendation performance.
