## Summary

This paper introduces an open-source evaluation framework for text-to-image generation models. It specifically focuses on assessing the effect of augmenting prompts with structured metadata. The study demonstrates that incorporating such metadata significantly improves visual realism, semantic accuracy, and model robustness across various architectures.

## Technical note

This work presents an open-source unified benchmarking and evaluation framework for text-to-image (T2I) generation models, focused on measuring the impact of metadata-augmented prompts (structured garment attributes like sleeve length, neckline, fabric) versus plain caption prompts. Using the DeepFashion-MultiModal dataset, it evaluates 10+ T2I models (Stable Diffusion, SDXL, CogView, Flux, Lumina, Context-LoRA, SANA-Sprint, KOALA) across CLIP score, CLIP cosine similarity to ground truth, LPIPS, FID, MRR, and Recall@3, aggregated into a composite Weighted Score. Metadata enrichment consistently improves Weighted Score and ground-truth CLIP cosine similarity (better realism and fine-detail fidelity for fabric/accessories) at a slight cost to prompt-image CLIP alignment. StblDffsn_lrg and Context_LoRA are the top all-round performers, and the framework doubles as a task-specific model-selection/recommendation tool via a Streamlit demo.
