## Technical note

BroadGen (eBay) recommends efficient and effective broad-match keyphrases for sponsored search, moving beyond exact-match keyphrase recommendation (usually framed as extreme multi-label classification) which suffers from shifting long-tail label distributions, high management cost, and sparse/biased supervision. It defines criteria for an ideal broad match (relevance plus reach) and uses historical search-query data with token correspondence modeling to generate keyphrases that maintain better query stability over time. BroadGen serves millions of sellers daily at eBay across over 2.5 billion items.
