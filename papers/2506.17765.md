## Technical note

CARTS (Collaborative Agents for Recommendation Textual Summarization) from Walmart is a multi-agent LLM framework for generating concise, coherent titles for product carousels/grouped item displays under strict length constraints. It decomposes the task into three stages: Generation Augmented Generation (extracting salient item features and synthesizing candidate titles), a Refinement Circle where feedback agents iteratively critique titles on relevance/style/length, and Arbitration selecting the final title. The paper frames it as constrained coverage optimization with proven approximation guarantees on refinement steps; offline experiments and live A/B tests show CARTS beats single-pass and chain-of-thought baselines, improving title relevance, CTR, add-to-cart rate, and GMV.
