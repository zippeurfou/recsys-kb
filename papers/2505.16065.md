## Technical note

Aug2Search (Meta) enhances Facebook Marketplace search EBR using GenAI/LLM-generated synthetic data in a multimodal, multitask framework to optimize query-product relevance, addressing search logs that lack diversity/detail. Using eight Llama models and 100M Marketplace data points, it follows three synthetic strategies: generate queries, enhance product listings, and generate queries from enhanced listings, training EBR on engagement data, synthetic data, or a mix. Llama models produce coherent, relevant, diverse synthetic queries/listings with low hallucination, and Aug2Search achieves up to +4% ROC-AUC with 100M synthetic samples.
