## Summary

Relate Any Identities with Their Attributes for Personalized Video Generation":

Existing text-to-video diffusion models lack precise face-attribute alignment across multiple subjects. The LumosX framework addresses this by combining a new benchmark derived from subject-specific relational priors with novel Relational Self-Attention and Cross-Attention mechanisms that enforce identity consistency and group cohesion. This dual approach achieves state-of-the-art performance in generating fine-grained, identity-consistent, and semantically aligned videos for multiple subjects.

## Technical note

LumosX is a framework for personalized multi-subject text-to-video generation, built on the Wan2.1 DiT text-to-video backbone, that enforces explicit face-attribute binding across subjects to avoid attribute entanglement and face-attribute misalignment. On the data side, a collection pipeline extracts captions and foreground/background condition images from independent videos and uses MLLMs (VILA for captions, Qwen2.5-VL for face-attribute matching, plus face detection, SAM, GroundingDINO, and FLUX inpainting) to infer subject-specific relational priors, yielding training data and a benchmark with identity-consistent and subject-consistent evaluation tasks. On the modeling side, Relational Self-Attention combines Relational Rotary Position Embedding (R2PE) with a Causal Self-Attention Mask (CSAM), and Relational Cross-Attention introduces a Multilevel Cross-Attention Mask (MCAM) that reinforces intra-group coherence while suppressing cross-group interference. LumosX reports state-of-the-art fine-grained, identity-consistent, semantically aligned multi-subject video generation, outperforming Phantom and SkyReels-A2.
