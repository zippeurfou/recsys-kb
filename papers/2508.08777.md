## Technical note

Proposes a profile-aware LLM-as-a-Judge framework for scalable offline evaluation of personalized podcast recommendations, addressing exposure bias in offline metrics and the cost of A/B testing. Stage one distills a natural-language user profile (topical interests and behavioral patterns) from 90 days of listening history as a compact interpretable content hypothesis; the LLM is then prompted with the profile and candidate episode metadata to deliver pointwise and pairwise judgments along dimensions like topic, tone, and format. In a controlled study with 47 participants, the profile-aware judge matched human judgments with high fidelity and outperformed or matched a raw-history variant (Spotify).
