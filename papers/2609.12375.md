## Summary

Pre-training Temporally Anchored Tokens for Lifelong User Modeling":

1.  The paper addresses the challenge of modeling extremely long user behavior sequences efficiently for recommendation systems, which is computationally expensive if done directly and loses information if truncated.
2.  It introduces ChronicleRec, a novel pre-training framework that compresses these long sequences once into a set of Chronicle Tokens, preserving temporal order and using a recency-aware merge process, and then uses a causal encoder to score candidates based on these compressed tokens and relevant recent query tokens.
3.  This compression is target-independent, allowing the Chronicle Tokens to be cached per user offline, thereby decoupling long-sequence processing from online candidate scoring and enabling efficient lifelong user modeling with strong experimental results.

## Technical note

ChronicleRec is Tencent's pre-train-and-transfer framework for lifelong user behavior modeling in CTR/CVR ranking, replacing target-attention retrieval (SIM, ETA, SDIM, TWIN) whose long-sequence cost is target-dependent and multiplied by the number of candidates per request, and improving on VISTA-style single-pass compression that appends query tokens at the sequence end and encodes bidirectionally, producing an unordered and redundant bag of summaries. A recency-aware multi-granularity merge splits the history into near (last 100 events, kept 1:1), mid (length 400, kernel/stride 8/4) and far (kernel/stride 10/10) segments with masked average pooling; P=11 learnable query tokens are then interleaved at anchor positions (dense near the present, sparse toward the past) among the merged tokens and encoded by a 4-layer 4-head causal Transformer, so each query has a well-defined temporal receptive field and the output is a chronologically ordered sequence-in, sequence-out prefix rather than a pooled vector. A multi-branch design runs B=5 parallel compressors masking the most recent {0, 100, 300, 600, 1000} behaviors with weights {1.0, 0.5, 0.4, 0.3, 0.2}, each deeply supervised by a Chronicle Alignment mask-and-predict BCE head that must reconstruct the held-out recent target label from compressed older history; the concatenated prefixes feed a shared 2-layer target-attention ranking head, used either compress-only or hybrid with a short recent window. On KuaiRand-27K (avg 11,811 events per user, L_max 2048) Multi-ChronicleRec reaches 0.5580 GAUC versus 0.5601 for Full-Attn, 0.5518 for VISTA and 0.5307 for 100-event Short-Attn; on the industrial Tencent AdLive set (199,914 users, 214M interactions, L_max 4000) it reaches 0.8034 versus 0.8071, 0.7995 and 0.7950. Interleaved+causal beats end+causal by 0.0144 GAUC and interleaved+bidirectional by 0.0104, and ablations cost -0.0086 (no Chronicle Alignment), -0.0076 (uniform merge), -0.0074 (no causal mask), -0.0072 (single branch) and -0.0039 (no deep supervision). Efficiency is 28.0 GB and 620 min training versus 49.1 GB and 2,997 min for Full-Attn (about 4.8x speedup) at comparable ~1.23B parameters, and because Chronicle Tokens are target-independent they are computed once per user, cached, and written back asynchronously to a KV cache, decoupling ultra-long encoding from online candidate scoring. Token-similarity heatmaps, effective rank and debiased cumulative mutual information all show ChronicleRec tokens are temporally specialized and complementary while VISTA's overlap heavily; masking the most recent 1,000 KuaiRand behaviors still yields 0.5499 GAUC, well above Short-Attn's 0.5307, showing distant history carries self-contained signal. A seven-day online A/B in the pCVR stage of Weixin Moments Ads, injecting Chronicle Tokens into both the NS-Token and S-Token pathways of the production MixFormer ranker without changing its architecture, increased GMV by +1.61% with a 95% CI of [0.678%, 2.547%].
