Feature selection has emerged as a crucial technique in refining recommender systems. Recent advancements leveraging Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) has drawn significant attention, particularly in two main categories: early feature selection and late feature selection, differentiated by whether the selection occurs before or after the embedding layer. ## Technical note

AEFS (Adaptive Early Feature Selection, Tencent) performs input-dependent feature selection before the embedding layer, combining the adaptivity of late feature selection with the parameter efficiency of early selection. It uses a dual-model architecture—an auxiliary model for per-instance feature selection and a main model for prediction—with two collaborative training loss constraints aligning them. On three benchmarks AEFS matches state-of-the-art adaptive late feature selection while reducing activated embedding-layer parameters by 37.5%.
