## Technical note

DADF is a distribution-aware second-stage debiasing framework for watch-time regression that corrects the multiplicative bias factor (y/y_hat) of a fixed deployed predictor, addressing 'pseudo-balance' where a globally calibrated model still overestimates short views and underestimates long views. It combines a dynamic distribution-aware transformation to stabilize long-tailed correction targets, a debias-factor-aware module keyed on inference-time observable video duration, and a multi-label-aware module reusing engagement-head signals. On public benchmarks it improves MAE and XAUC; in production it gives a 2.07 percentage-point ranking-quality gain and statistically significant online lifts of +0.649% average time per device and +0.656% total app time as a minimally intrusive plug-in.
