## Technical note

DUET is a set-wise pre-ranking framework for entire-space CTR that overcomes the expressiveness-vs-efficiency trade-off of lightweight two-tower pre-rankers. Instead of scoring items independently, it performs set-level prediction over the whole candidate subset in one forward pass to capture synergistic/suppressive inter-item interactions while amortizing compute; a dual-model co-training mechanism extends supervision to unexposed items via mutual pseudo-label refinement to mitigate Sample Selection Bias. Deployed at Kuaishou's pre-ranking stage (refining ~4,500 candidates to 600), it outperforms SOTA offline and lifts core business metrics in A/B tests, fully deployed in Kuaishou and Kuaishou Lite.
