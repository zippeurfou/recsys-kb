## Technical note

LAAC (LLM-guided Adversarial Actor Critic) from Amazon improves diversity and novelty in recommendations, addressing that RL exploration is often random and misaligned with user interests. It uses an LLM as a reference policy to propose novel items while training an actor against an adversarial critic, guiding exploration toward relevant-yet-novel items rather than purely click-relevant ones. The method encourages exploration aligned with user preferences, improving diversity and novelty over RL approaches that rely on random exploration.
