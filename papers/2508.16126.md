## Summary

This paper introduces **Spacetime-GR**, the first generative model incorporating spatial and temporal context for large-scale POI recommendation. To address the challenge of modeling vast numbers of POIs, it employs a geographic-aware hierarchical indexing strategy and enriches POI representation with multimodal embeddings. The key innovations are its novel spatiotemporal encoding module to capture location-time variations in user sequences and specific post-training adaptation strategies enabling deployment for hundreds of millions of users and POIs.

## Technical note

Spacetime-GR is presented as the first spacetime-aware generative model for large-scale online POI recommendation, extending generative sequence modeling with flexible spatiotemporal encoding. To handle hundreds of millions of POIs it uses a geographic-aware hierarchical POI indexing strategy for the large-vocabulary problem, a spatiotemporal encoding module that injects user/POI location and time into the action sequence, and LLM-encoded multimodal POI embeddings for semantic richness (mitigating long-tail sparsity). Training is two-stage: next-token pre-training on user action sequences with specialized data filtering, then post-training via Embedding-based Ranking SFT, Generative Ranking SFT, and DPO alignment to output embeddings, ranking scores, or end-to-end candidates. It outperforms prior methods on public and industrial datasets and is deployed to services with hundreds of millions of users and POIs.
