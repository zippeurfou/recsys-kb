## Technical note

Spacetime-GR is the first spacetime-aware generative model for large-scale online POI recommendation. It uses a geographic-aware hierarchical POI indexing strategy to handle a vocabulary of hundreds of millions of POIs, a spatiotemporal encoding module to inject space/time context into user action sequences, and LLM-encoded multimodal POI embeddings for semantics. After next-token pre-training on action sequences, three post-training adaptations (Embedding-based Ranking SFT, Generative Ranking SFT, and DPO alignment) let it output embeddings, ranking scores, or end-to-end candidates. Deployed to services scaling to hundreds of millions of POIs and users, outperforming existing methods on accuracy and ranking.
