## Summary

This paper addresses challenges in live-streaming recommendation systems where user interactions are sparse, delayed, and vary across different viewer segments compared to linear e-commerce sequences.

The key contributions include: 1) using a delayed window approach to capture feedback beyond immediate responses, 2) integrating segment-aware targeting into a multi-model architecture optimizing for diverse business objectives, and 3) employing Multi-gate Mixture-of-Experts (MMoE) integration to efficiently model correlated targets with fewer parameters.

Online A/B testing demonstrated significant performance improvements in increasing Daily Active Viewers and revenue from engaged users. The system provides low-latency ranking across diverse user groups, effectively balancing multiple business objectives.

## Technical note

Twitch ranking must jointly optimize watching, chatting, following and spending, but those signals are sparse (follow rates ~90x lower than CTR, chat ~20x), delayed (a viewer may follow weeks after exposure), and dominated by heavy users. The system aggregates positives over a 14-day delayed window for the sparse actions (chat, follow, spend) -- which increases chat positive labels ~9x at 7 days and ~12x at 14 days -- while keeping immediate labels for shallow (SMP) and deep (LMP) watch signals; a sweep over {1,7,14,21,28,35} days lands on 14 because longer windows help Spend but degrade LMP by mis-attributing actions. Architecturally a Fresh Signal Model handles SMP while LMP plus the three delayed targets are consolidated into one MMoE with K=4 experts (3 shared-bottom layers of 1000, experts 512/256, gates 4x128, towers 64), cutting the delayed-target component from 26.7M to 15.5M parameters (-41.9%); Viewer Segment Targeting then applies segment-conditioned scalarization weights w_{a,s} at inference for Early vs Dedicated viewers, after training-time up-weighting of E samples failed. Offline, FSM+MMoE+VST reaches LMP NDCG@6 0.2462 and Spend 0.0739 versus 0.2459/0.0726 for a DNN baseline, whereas a single unified MTL model over all five targets degrades LMP by 4.2-4.6% regardless of backbone (MMoE, Shared-Bottom, CGC) because the dense SMP signal dominates gradients -- the paper's main lesson is that fresh/delayed signal separation matters more than the MTL backbone. Three staged 14-day A/Bs deliver +0.09% DAV and +0.56% Dedicated capped ARPU, then +0.15% Early-viewer DAV from VST, then +0.08% DAV / +0.10% LMP / +0.27% follows from MMoE, all under 110 ms p99; the multi-model design also transferred to Twitch's mobile live feed for +1.12% positive interactions.
