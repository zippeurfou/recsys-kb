## Technical note

FEDIN (Frequency-Enhanced Deep Interest Network) improves sequential CTR prediction by exploiting a new observation that target-conditioned attention scores show distinct spectral entropy—true interests form low-entropy concentrated spectral peaks while irrelevant behaviors look like high-entropy white noise—so the target item acts as a frequency selector. Unlike target-agnostic frequency methods (FMLP-Rec, FEARec, DIFF), FEDIN adds a frequency-domain branch with a target-aware spectrum filtering mechanism (via FFT) to isolate periodic interest signals from noisy time-domain history. On three public datasets FEDIN consistently outperforms SOTA sequential recommendation baselines with superior noise robustness.
