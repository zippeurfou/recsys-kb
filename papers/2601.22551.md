## Summary

This paper presents a hybrid localization system for cross-device use in the CroCoDL 2025 Challenge. The core approach integrates a shared retrieval encoder with two localization branches: a classical geometric method using feature fusion and PnP, and a neural MapAnything branch for metric localization. To improve accuracy, a neural pruning strategy filters unreliable map frames based on translation consistency, and depth conditioning refines the localization scale and precision. This combination significantly boosted recall and accuracy on standard benchmarks, achieving a final challenge score of 92.62.

## Technical note

This CroCoDL-2025 challenge report presents a hybrid cross-device visual localization pipeline built on a shared MegaLoc retrieval encoder feeding two branches. A classical geometric branch fuses multiple descriptors (SuperPoint, GIM-SuperPoint, DISK) and matchers (SuperGlue, LightGlue, GIM-LightGlue) into COLMAP PnP pose estimation, while a neural branch (MapAnything) does feed-forward metric localization, chosen when PnP has too few inliers. Neural-guided candidate pruning by translation consistency and depth conditioning refine scale/precision, yielding a final challenge score of 92.62 (R@0.5m, 5deg).
