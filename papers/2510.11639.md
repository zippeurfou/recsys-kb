## Technical note

Kuaishou's OneRec-Think adds explicit, controllable reasoning to end-to-end generative recommendation (e.g. OneRec), which otherwise operates as an implicit predictor lacking verifiable CoT. Its three-stage framework does Itemic Alignment (mapping item semantics into the LLM textual embedding space for grounding), Reasoning Activation (Reasoning Scaffolding to induce CoT within the recommendation context), and Reasoning Enhancement (a recommendation-specific reward capturing the multi-validity nature of user preferences via RL). It achieves state-of-the-art on public benchmarks, and a deployment-friendly 'Think-Ahead' inference architecture enabled industrial deployment on Kuaishou with a +0.159% gain in APP Stay Time.
