## Technical note

HHFT (Hierarchical Heterogeneous Feature Transformer, Taobao) is a Transformer architecture for industrial CTR prediction with three designs: Semantic Feature Partitioning grouping heterogeneous features (user profile, item info, behavior sequence) into coherent blocks; a Heterogeneous Transformer Encoder with block-specific QKV projections and FFNs to avoid semantic confusion; and a Hiformer layer capturing high-order cross-feature interactions. It empirically validates that Transformers follow scaling laws for CTR, outperforming DNN baselines by +0.4% CTR AUC at scale, and after Taobao deployment delivers +0.6% GMV.
