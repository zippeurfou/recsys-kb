## Technical note

A survey of discrete tokenizers across generation, comprehension, recommendation, and information retrieval, positioning them as the interface that maps raw multimodal data into discrete tokens for autoregressive modeling and LLMs. It dissects tokenizer sub-modules and quantization strategies (VQ, RQ, e.g. VQVAE, VQGAN, RQVAE), then taxonomizes state-of-the-art methods by modality, backbone, and quantizer. For recommender systems it covers transforming item/user semantic embeddings into semantic IDs to improve generalization of personalized recommendation, and analyzes limitations and future directions of discrete tokenization.
