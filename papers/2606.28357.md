## Summary

1.  The paper introduces **ReasonRec**, a new multimodal recommendation system designed to be more transparent and efficient by adding explicit reasoning capabilities through an agent-like structure.
2.  It achieves this via specific training techniques (reasoning-aware visual instruction tuning) that convert diverse tasks into prompts requiring the model to show intermediate steps, along with methods (evidence-horizon curriculum, uncertainty-guided delegation) improving reasoning complexity for new users and managing confidence/efficiency during inference.
3.  Extensive testing shows ReasonRec significantly improves key recommendation metrics by over 30% compared to existing models while also substantially reducing inference time by delegating queries up to 35% of the time without sacrificing accuracy, thanks to its integrated reasoning mechanisms.

## Technical note

ReasonRec is a reasoning-augmented multimodal recommender built on a VLM (LLaVA1.5-7B) with an Observe-Deliberate-Act pipeline: chain-of-thought reasoning with explicit uncertainty quantification via an evidence-horizon curriculum, plus uncertainty-guided delegation of hard/uncertain queries to lightweight classical models. Reasoning-aware visual instruction tuning unifies four tasks (sequential rec, direct rec, CTR prediction, explanation). Across five datasets it achieves >30% relative gains in HR@5/NDCG@5 over SOTA multimodal recommenders while delegating up to 35% of queries to cut latency.
