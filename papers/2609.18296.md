## Summary

This paper addresses the inefficiencies of traditional multi-stage sponsored search ad retrieval systems (MCA) and LLM-based methods using discrete semantic identifiers (SIDs). It introduces the ANGLE framework, which uses LLMs to generate hierarchical text representations (ad abstract and commercial intent) directly. By integrating retrieval, relevance, and ranking within a single LLM, ANGLE improves efficiency and accuracy, demonstrated by a real-world 1.81% increase in ad consumption and 2.16% increase in GMV, as well as offline evaluations where it outperformed seven baselines across metrics like HR and ACR.

## Technical note

Tencent's ANGLE replaces the multi-stage cascade (retrieval, relevance, pre-ranking, ranking) for sponsored search ads with a single generation-discriminative-ranking LLM (GDR-LLM) that generates hierarchical text identifiers instead of RQ-VAE semantic IDs: a coarse commercial intent (about 700k deduplicated categories) plus a fine-grained ad abstract (n-grams from the ad title), mapped to ads through an hourly-updated inverted index so new ads need no retraining. The Hunyuan-1B query-side model is trained with a joint loss of token cross-entropy generation, a [CLS]-head BCE relevance discriminator on click/exposure logs, and DPO over ads ranked by actual spend, and decodes with dynamic constrained beam search (beam 64, intent up to 3 tokens, abstract up to 4 tokens, about 90 ms) that prunes intent-abstract pairs with no valid ad for the current user. Offline on 10k queries and about 220k candidates, ANGLE-1B reaches HR@10 0.196, HR@100 0.527, MAP 0.483 and 69.2% ad coverage rate versus 0.131 / 0.308 / 0.391 / 47.7% for the best BERT ANN baseline, and ablating DCBS drops HR@100 to 0.298. A five-day 20%-traffic A/B test lifted consumption +1.81%, GMV +2.16% and clicks +1.5% on the main search surface (+6.57% consumption, +5.01% GMV on a second surface), served in FP8 on L40 GPUs at about 30 QPS per GPU within 60 ms.
