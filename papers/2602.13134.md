## Technical note

RoleGen awakens dormant users (highly engaged but low-conversion) by modeling items' instrumental effect along a conversion trajectory rather than only their standalone CTR. It pairs an LLM-based Conversion Trajectory Reasoner, which models the context-dependent Functional Role of items and uses counterfactual inference to simulate diverse conversion paths (mitigating interest collapse and the Matthew effect), with a decoder-only Generative Behavioral Backbone that consumes the Reasoner's Semantic-ID candidates, trained via a Reasoning-Execution-Feedback-Reflection closed loop. On Kuaishou e-commerce, RoleGen achieves a 6.2% gain in Recall@1 and a 7.3% increase in online order volume among dormant users.
