Recently, substantial progress has been made in industrial recommendation through component-centric model scaling, where individual components such as behavior modeling, feature interaction, or task modeling are independently scaled to improve model capacity. Although recent methods such as HyFormer and OneTrans further explore cross-module co-scaling by jointly modeling behavior and interaction, their designs are still confined to the feature space and lack a unified model-centric scaling frame...

## Technical note

UniFormer is an efficient unified model-centric scaling framework for industrial recommendation that moves beyond component-centric scaling (independently scaling behavior, feature-interaction, or task modules) and feature-space-only co-scaling (HyFormer, OneTrans, MixFormer) to co-scale sequential behaviors, non-sequence features, and tasks together. It decomposes the modeling space into stacked Feature-space Interaction Modules (FIM) and Task-space Interaction Modules (TIM), uses a semantic-based tokenization for user-item decoupling and request-level inference acceleration, applies multi-sequence cross-attention plus self-attention to prevent preference collapse, and adds multi-view FFNs for flexible parameter allocation. Online A/B tests on Kuaishou and Kuaishou Lite gave +0.101%/+0.260% App Stay Time and +0.729%/+1.113% Watch Time.
