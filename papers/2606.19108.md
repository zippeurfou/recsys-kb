## Summary

1.  Airbnb developed "JourneyFormer," an advanced sequence modeling system, specifically for search ranking because standard recommendations struggled with analyzing long guest journeys involving complex explorations and sparse booking labels (since most users don't book immediately).
2.  JourneyFormer addressed these production challenges by incorporating careful design choices in event selection, embeddings, model architecture, and label handling to balance the need for effectiveness against the constraints of computational scalability.
3.  The system was successfully deployed with measurable results: it improved offline ranking metrics significantly and drove positive business impacts through online A/B tests across different Airbnb platforms.

## Technical note

JourneyFormer is a deployed Transformer-based sequence-modeling solution for Airbnb search ranking that encodes long, exploratory guest journeys with sparse booking labels. It separates guest events into a long-term sequence (past 7 years, excluding frequent events like views) and a short-term sequence (frequent events from the past 21 days) selected by event frequency, and uses a shared feature pool across guest sequences, search queries, and listing candidates. The paper centers on production design decisions -- guest event selection and capping, ID embeddings, model architecture, and booking label attribution -- rather than novel architecture, plus training strategies that boost throughput ~4x and a serving workflow combining batch and real-time inference. Deployed across two production surfaces (search ranking and promotional emails), it improved offline ranking metrics and key business metrics such as uncanceled bookers and nights booked in online A/B tests.
