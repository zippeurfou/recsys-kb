## Technical note

Impatient Bandits (Spotify) optimizes long-term recommendation reward under delay by formalizing 'bandits with progressive feedback,' where a 60-day engagement reward becomes incrementally predictable from >50 correlated, progressively revealed intermediate signals. The algorithm couples Thompson sampling (for the bandit/exploration problem) with an empirical Bayesian Gaussian filter that projects each arm's true mean reward from partially observed, censored engagement trajectories while maintaining calibrated residual uncertainty, with priors trained on historical complete trajectories. The authors prove a regret bound governed by the 'value of progressive feedback' (mutual information between censored trajectories and the eventual reward), reducing to standard delayed-feedback Thompson sampling when early signals are uninformative. On a podcast cold-start recommendation problem, it beats short-term-proxy and delayed-reward-only baselines in synthetic and semi-synthetic experiments and in a production A/B test serving hundreds of millions of users; it is now core to Spotify's recommender.
