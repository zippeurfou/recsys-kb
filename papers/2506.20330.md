## Technical note

SMAR (Semantic-enhanced Modality-Asymmetric Retrieval) from JD tackles multimodal candidate retrieval in e-commerce search where the query is unimodal (text) but items are multimodal (text plus image), an asymmetric setting under-studied versus symmetric multimodal retrieval. Built on a standard two-tower embedding retrieval base, SMAR uses a two-stage pre-training then fine-tuning approach to address modality fusion (taking supplementary but non-redundant image information) and asymmetric modality alignment. On an industrial dataset (open-sourced) it significantly outperforms baseline models in retrieval accuracy, validated by offline experiments and online A/B tests.
