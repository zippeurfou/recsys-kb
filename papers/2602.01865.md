## Summary

Traditional Deep Learning Recommendation Models (DLRMs) face challenges with long sequences and generalization, hindering their performance. To overcome this, Baidu developed GRAB, an LLM-inspired generative framework for CTR prediction that utilizes a novel Causal Action-aware Multi-channel Attention mechanism. GRAB significantly outperformed standard DLRMs, boosting revenue by 3.05% and CTR by 3.49%, and demonstrated strong scalability, improving performance with longer user interaction sequences.

## Technical note

GRAB is an end-to-end generative ranking framework for Baidu ads CTR prediction that fuses DLRM-style sparse feature engineering with HSTU-like autoregressive sequence modeling via a dense tokenizer. It introduces Causal Action-aware Multi-channel Attention (CamA), which augments self-attention with query-aware relative position/time/action biases and heterogeneous partial/full behavior tokens. To fix distribution skew from sequence packing, a Sequence-Then-Sparse (STS) training scheme decouples optimization of dense Transformer weights and sparse embeddings to resolve gradient conflict. Online A/B tests report a 3.05% revenue and 3.49% CTR increase, with AUC scaling monotonically in model capacity and behavior-sequence length.
