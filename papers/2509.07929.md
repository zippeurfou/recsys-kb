## Technical note

Smart Fast Finish (SFF, DoorDash) improves budget pacing over the industry-standard Fast Finish, which depletes remaining ad budget quickly at day's end but causes overdelivery (overspend) from delayed attribution between click and conversion under charge-per-conversion billing. SFF dynamically updates start time and throttle rate based on historical ad-campaign data plus a probabilistic throttling mechanism. Online budget-split experiments and offline simulations show SFF robustly mitigates overdelivery while maintaining ad campaign visibility, and it is deployed in DoorDash's production budget-pacing system serving hundreds of thousands of daily campaigns.
