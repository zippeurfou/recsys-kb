## Summary

Video LLM Embeddings for Retrieval":

1.  Existing Video Large Language Models (VideoLLMs) perform poorly on video retrieval tasks compared to specialized embedding models, despite excelling at tasks like video question answering and captioning.
2.  To address this, the paper introduces ViLL-E, a VideoLLM architecture with a novel embedding generation mechanism and a three-stage training process combining generative and contrastive learning, designed for better retrieval performance.
3.  ViLL-E significantly outperforms other VideoLLMs and specialized dual encoder models in standard retrieval tasks, achieves state-of-the-art performance on specific benchmarks, and demonstrates strong zero-shot capabilities in complex retrieval scenarios.

## Technical note

ViLL-E is a unified VideoLLM (built on PaliGemma-3B) that generates both text responses and video/text embeddings from one model, closing the gap between generative VideoLLMs and specialized embedding retrievers. It adds a learnable attentive-pooling embedding head ('KV-Former': LLM tokens as queries with P learnable key/value pooling tokens) triggered after the EOS token, letting the model use variable inference steps to 'think longer' for complex videos. Three-stage training combines generative next-token loss and CLIP-style contrastive loss: large-scale noisy video-caption pre-training, continued pre-training on high-quality Claude-generated captions, then multi-task fine-tuning over VideoQA, captioning, temporal localization (with sliding-window IoU<0.2 hard negatives), and retrieval/matching, using LoRA. It beats other VideoLLMs by ~7% on moment retrieval and up to 4% over dual encoders, plus zero-shot composed and long-caption retrieval gains.
