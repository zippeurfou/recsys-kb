## Summary

This paper surveys the field of information discovery within electronic commerce, highlighting its growing importance due to the rise of popular online platforms. It focuses on technologies crucial for connecting users with products, including different search methods (like exploratory vs. lookup), recommender systems, and natural language processing capabilities in e-commerce portals. The study provides an overview of algorithms, technical solutions, user behavior analysis, and other infrastructure relevant to improving how consumers find goods online.

## Technical note

This survey maps the field of information discovery in e-commerce, spanning search, recommendation, and language technology across large platforms. It reviews fundamental infrastructure, algorithms, and technical solutions, organized around user behavior and profiling, e-commerce search (exploratory vs. lookup), recommender systems, and NLP such as question answering and bots. It emphasizes enriching and exploiting knowledge graphs and handling multi-modal signals (logs, clicks, orders, reviews, images, chat). The work synthesizes academic and industrial approaches and identifies open challenges for connecting people to goods and services.
