## Summary

Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) often lose crucial visual information when generating image captions. This paper introduces CIM, a reinforcement learning framework that minimizes this information loss by evaluating how well captions retrieve relevant images. By improving the correlation between visual content and text output, CIM significantly enhances image captioning performance, even outperforming standard fine-tuning methods.

## Technical note

CIM improves LVLM image captioning without human annotations by treating caption quality as an image-to-image retrieval problem. It defines two reward signals, Gallery Representation Consistency (detail richness of retrieved images) and Query-gallery Image Relevance (caption precision), and optimizes the LVLM with reinforcement learning against them. The authors verify via Pearson correlation on Oxford-IIIT Pet that these metrics track fine-grained classification accuracy. On COCO-LN500 with Qwen2.5-VL-7B, CIM yields a 20% improvement in relation reasoning and surpasses supervised fine-tuning.
