## Technical note

ReST is a plug-and-play spatially-constrained representation enhancement framework for local-life recommendation, tackling spatial exposure constraints and long-tail sparsity where popular items dominate. It initializes item embeddings from attribute semantics and applies a spatially-constrained contrastive learning mechanism that strengthens weak ID representations by aligning them with spatial characteristics while preserving popular-item performance. On two real-world datasets it improves AUC, MRR, and NDCG, and online A/B testing shows GMV +2.804% and order volume +1.285%.
