## Technical note

LGSID is an LLM-aligned geographic item tokenization framework for local-life recommendation, addressing that simply injecting location into prompts fails to capture fine-grained spatial and distance awareness. It has two parts: RL-based Geographic LLM Alignment, which trains a list-wise reward model of real spatial relations and uses a novel G-DPO algorithm to inject spatial knowledge and collaborative signals into the LLM while preserving semantics; and Hierarchical Geographic Item Tokenization, deriving primary tokens from discrete spatial/content attributes and residual tokens from the aligned LLM's geographic representations. On real Kuaishou datasets it consistently outperforms SOTA discriminative and generative models on Hit@K/NDCG@K and improves AUC as a plug-in across five backbones.
