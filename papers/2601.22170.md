## Summary

Large language models (LLMs) process and predict sequences containing text to answer questions, and address tasks including document summarization, providing recommendations, writing software and solving quantitative problems. ## Technical note

This is a mathematical exposition paper (not an empirical recsys system) that provides a precise mathematical framework for large language models aimed at a mathematically inclined audience. It formalizes the encoding of text into token/Euclidean sequences, autoregressive factorization and next-token prediction, the probabilistic training objective over internet-scale corpora, and the parameterization of next-token models including positional encoding, the attention mechanism, and the set transformer, using ideas from information theory, probability, and optimization. It aims to enable rigorous study of LLM accuracy, efficiency, and robustness. Only tangential to recsys (LLMs mentioned as providing recommendations).
