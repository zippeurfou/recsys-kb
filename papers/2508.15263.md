## Technical note

CAU (Curriculum Approximate Unlearning) removes the influence of specific training samples from session-based recommenders without retraining, addressing two GA problems: performance degradation and ignoring the order of unlearning samples. It frames unlearning as multi-objective optimization, combining a gradient-ascent term for target samples with retaining terms and finding a Pareto-optimal solution. It processes unlearning batches easy-to-hard via a curriculum, using gradient difficulty and embedding difficulty metrics with hard-sampling and soft-sampling selection. Experiments on three benchmarks and ablations show each component improves both recommendation performance and unlearning efficacy.
