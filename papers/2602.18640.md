## Summary

Modern large-scale ranking systems face a bottleneck translating product intent into effective models. To address this, the authors introduce GEARS, an agentic framework reframing optimization as autonomous discovery using specialized agent skills encapsulating expert knowledge. GEARS enables high-level intent steering and includes validation hooks for reliable, statistically robust policy deployment, consistently finding near-Pareto-efficient ranking policies.

## Technical note

GEARS (Generative Engine for Agentic Ranking Systems) is an LLM-orchestrated framework that reframes large-scale ranking-policy optimization as autonomous discovery, addressing the 'engineering context constraint' of translating product intent into deployable hypotheses. It decomposes the workflow into three coordinated stages: programmatic candidate generation and filtering that turns natural-language intent into deterministic operations over tabular policy data with tolerance-based Pareto filtering; domain-grounded interpretation via lazily-loaded Specialized Agent Skills supplying ranking-specific knowledge (feature definitions, stability diagnostics, trade-off explanations); and deterministic lifecycle governance with validation hooks enforcing feature-stability and statistical-significance checks before promotion. On a benchmark of 100 policy-selection instructions derived from 20 billion-user-scale experiments it reaches 0.94 nDCG@1, beating prompting and code-execution baselines; deployed on nine production surfaces it cuts engineering cycle time from weeks to days at under $1,500 annual LLM cost.
