## Technical note

GEARS (Generative Engine for Agentic Ranking Systems, Meta) is an LLM-orchestrated framework that automates large-scale ranking-policy optimization, tackling the 'engineering context constraint' of translating ambiguous product intent into executable, verifiable, deployable hypotheses. It decomposes the workflow into three stages: programmatic candidate generation and filtering (natural-language intent -> deterministic Pareto-tolerance operations over tabular policy data), domain-grounded interpretation via Specialized Agent Skills (feature definitions, stability diagnostics), and deterministic lifecycle governance enforcing feature-stability and statistical-significance checks before promotion. On a benchmark of 100 policy-selection instructions from 20 experiments (0.5B users each, 10B user-experiment pairs), GEARS reaches 0.94 nDCG@1, beating prompting and code-execution baselines; ablations show programmatic filtering drives structured tasks while skills help contextual ones. Deployed on nine production surfaces with <$1,500/yr LLM cost, cutting engineering cycle time from weeks to days.
