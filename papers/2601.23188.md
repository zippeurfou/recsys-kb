## Summary

1.  Deep search agents often fail due to a lack of monitoring during complex, uncertain tasks, so the authors propose **DS-MCM**, a framework inspired by human metacognitive processes.
2.  DS-MCM integrates two hierarchical monitoring components: a **Fast Consistency Monitor** for quick alignment checks and a **Slow Experience-Driven Monitor** for selective reflection and correction based on past experiences.
3.  By embedding these monitors into the reasoning-retrieval loop, DS-MCM determines when intervention is needed and how to correct behavior, leading to consistently improved performance and robustness in deep search benchmarks.

## Technical note

DS-MCM augments LLM deep-search agents with a neuroscience-inspired hierarchical metacognitive monitoring mechanism to reduce failures where agents fail to regulate reasoning and retrieval under uncertain or conflicting evidence. A Fast Consistency Monitor tracks alignment between external Searching Entropy and internal Reasoning Entropy to detect abnormal states, and a slower Experience-Driven Monitor is selectively triggered to guide corrective intervention using patterns distilled from historical agent trajectories. Embedding both monitors into the reasoning-retrieval loop consistently improves performance and robustness across deep-search benchmarks and backbones, letting open-source agents match proprietary systems.
