## Summary

UniGD addresses the challenges of deploying generative retrieval (GR) in industrial search advertising by integrating it with relevance scoring within a single model. To manage potential conflicts during joint optimization, the framework introduces CAGE to adaptively coordinate the objectives. Additionally, UniGD utilizes CAM and HAM modules for efficient item representation learning and cross-type ad-material modeling, respectively, leading to improved effectiveness on benchmark datasets like NQ320K and MS300K, as well as significant performance gains (5.78% higher revenue) and reduced latency (33%) in real-world applications through online AB tests.

## Technical note

Industrial generative retrieval is normally cascaded with a separate relevance model, which decouples generation likelihood from query-ad relevance (relevant ads with low generation probability get truncated away) and adds an extra online hop. UniGD (Kuaishou search advertising) folds both into one decoder-only backbone that autoregressively generates hierarchical SIDs and also emits an explicit point-wise query-ad relevance score. Three parts make it work: CAM reconstructs an ad's representation as the sum of the T frozen RQ-KMeans codewords indexed by its SID over a large multimodal ad-creative encoder, so a newly indexed ad is scorable from T lookups with no online ad-side encoder -- critical where new ads are 36.9% of daily traffic; HAM keeps per-material-type codebooks, SID vocabularies and generation heads for short-video, product and live-stream ads and runs typed beam search in parallel off shared decoder states; and CAGE resolves gradient interference by projecting the discriminative gradient onto the orthogonal complement of the generative one when their cosine is negative, reweighting with alpha(c)=1+kappa(-c) and beta(c)=1-kappa(-c). UniGD reaches Recall@10 77.52 on NQ320K and 78.22 on MS300K (+8.44% and +3.19% over the strongest reproduced GR baseline) with Recall@1 72.33/60.64, and on the industrial set adds +7.04 Recall@1 and +8.06 MRR@10 over a generation-only baseline; ablations rank CAM > CAGE > HAM, and without CAGE both generative and discriminative validation accuracy decline after intermediate peaks. A one-week Kuaishou A/B lifts ad revenue +5.78% and new-ad impressions +24.60% while cutting retrieval latency 33.1% (13.00 ms -> 8.70 ms), using a 60M backbone plus only 1.1M extra parameters.
