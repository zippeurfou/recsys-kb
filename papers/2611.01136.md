## Summary

This paper introduces **SIDRec**, a novel end-to-end recommendation system that uses Semantic IDs (SIDs). Existing SID methods suffer from two main limitations: misalignment between item content and user preferences, and mismatch between SID construction and the recommendation goal. SIDRec addresses these by integrating collaborative information directly into SID learning and using a specialized architecture called MMoE for joint optimization, ensuring the SID generation process is aligned with downstream recommendation tasks.

## Technical note

Content-derived Semantic IDs used as features in discriminative rankers suffer semantic mismatch (content similarity is not interaction similarity) and objective mismatch (codes are frozen before CTR training can shape them). SIDRec (Kuaishou) fuses fixed 4,096-d multimodal item embeddings with a collaborative embedding table initialized from a trained ranking model through a learnable sigmoid gate, feeds the fusion to a pretrained RQ tokenizer (MLP encoder, 3 levels x 256 codewords, reconstruction decoder), and finetunes the tokenizer end-to-end via straight-through quantization with Gumbel-perturbed soft codeword probabilities so CTR gradients reach the encoder and codebooks. A causal Transformer over history SID tokens plus DIN target attention form the candidate-aware user representation, and an MMoE with four shared experts and per-task gates jointly optimizes a BCE CTR loss and an auxiliary next-item SID-prediction loss, plus multimodal reconstruction and VQ commitment terms. On Amazon Electronics, Amazon Beauty and MovieLens-20M (10 train / 100 eval sampled negatives per positive) SIDRec beats 15 CTR baselines including TransAct, OneTrans, HyFormer, RankMixer, DIEN and DIN, with up to +5.15% GAUC and +4.27% AUC over TransAct on Beauty; freezing the SIDs costs 1.97% AUC, removing collaborative fusion 0.39%, and removing both 7.19%, while shuffling collaborative embeddings changes 51-93% of first-level codes and assignments stabilize over training (L1 reassignment 2-4% by the last epoch). On two Kuaishou domains it improves UAUC and WUAUC on all six Show/CTR/CVR tasks (CVR up to +2.57pp UAUC).
