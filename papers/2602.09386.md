## Technical note

SMES is a scalable sparse Mixture-of-Experts framework for multi-task recommendation that translates parameter scaling into consistent gains under strict latency. It identifies two failure modes of naive sparse MoE under multi-task routing: exploded expert activation (disjoint per-task top-k unions) and expert load skew. SMES uses progressive expert routing that decomposes activation into a jointly selected task-shared expert subset plus task-adaptive private experts, bounding per-instance expert execution, and adds a global multi-gate load-balancing regularizer over aggregated utilization. Deployed at Kuaishou (400M+ DAU), online experiments show a 0.29% GAUC gain and 0.31% uplift in user watch time.
