## Technical note

PROMISE addresses Semantic Drift in generative recommendation over hierarchical Semantic IDs, where errors in early high-level tokens irreversibly derail the generation trajectory. Inspired by Process Reward Models (PRMs) for LLM reasoning, it adds a lightweight PRM that scores intermediate inference steps plus a PRM-guided beam search that uses dense feedback to prune erroneous branches, unlocking test-time scaling laws so smaller models can match or surpass larger ones by spending more inference compute. Offline it lifts accuracy substantially (e.g. NDCG improvements up to ~+47-59% at larger K+ candidate budgets) and online A/B tests on a large-scale platform confirm positive business gains.
