## Technical note

Workshop proposal (non-technical) for KEIR @ ECIR 2025, the Second Workshop on Knowledge-Enhanced Information Retrieval, organized largely by IR/NLP academics plus Google DeepMind. Motivates injecting external knowledge (external corpora, knowledge graphs, LLM-stored knowledge) into PLM-based IR and recommendation to overcome reliance on frozen pretraining knowledge for semantic nuance, context relevance, and domain specificity. Scope spans knowledge-enhanced retrieval models (passage ranking, query reformulation/expansion), knowledge-enhanced recommendation (enriching user/item representations, cold-start), knowledge-enhanced PLMs, knowledge-enhanced RAG (including multi-hop reasoning), and knowledge-aware LLM fine-tuning for IR. No new methods or results; describes format, topics, and organizers.
