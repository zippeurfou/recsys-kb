## Technical note

A fair re-evaluation of 9 recent Graph Foundation Models (GFMs) for node property prediction against strongly-tuned classic GNN baselines (GCN, GraphSAGE, GAT, Local Graph Transformer, 100 TPE trials) on the 10-dataset GraphLand industrial benchmark. Non-PFN GFMs (OpenGraph, AnyGraph, GCOPE, SAMGPT, MDGFM, TS-GNN) consistently underperform well-tuned GNNs, while only Prior-data Fitted Network (PFN) based models (GraphPFN, G2T-FM, TAG using TabPFNv2/LimiX tabular backbones with in-context learning) surpass them. Fine-tuned GraphPFN is best overall, winning all 10 datasets and topping the normalized score, but PFN-based inference costs seconds to tens of minutes versus sub-second GNN inference, a key drawback for real-time industrial use.
