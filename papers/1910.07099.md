## Summary

ESM^2 extends ESMM by decomposing the post-click funnel into deterministic purchase-related actions (DAction, e.g. add-to-cart/wishlist) and other actions, forming impression->click->D(O)Action->purchase, and trains all sub-tasks over the entire space to add abundant intermediate supervision, further mitigating conversion data sparsity.

## Technical note

ESM^2 (Alibaba) extends ESMM to fight residual conversion data sparsity by decomposing post-click behaviour into a Deterministic Action node (DAction = add-to-cart / wishlist, ~10% of clicks, far denser than the ~1% purchase rate) and Other Action, yielding the behaviour graph impression->click->D(O)Action->purchase. Four sub-probabilities (impression->click, click->DAction, DAction->purchase, OAction->purchase) are predicted in parallel from a shared embedding and sequentially composed (parameter-free) into pCVR, pCTAVR and pCTCVR; three cross-entropy losses (CTR, CTAVR, CTCVR) are all computed over the entire impression space. On >300M Alibaba impressions ESM^2 beats ESMM by +0.0088 CVR AUC, +0.0101 CTCVR AUC and +0.0145 CTCVR GAUC, with online gains, showing that adding dense intermediate post-click supervision improves the deep conversion signal.
