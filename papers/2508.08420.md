## Summary

Here are 3 sentences summarizing the paper:

1. This research addresses online regret minimization in linear bandits when prior offline data is available.
2. The proposed algorithm, Offline-Online Phased Elimination (OOPE), uses an "effective problem dimension" (`deff`) to quantify offline data quality and incorporates it via extended D-optimal designs during exploration phases, leading to a regret bound of `O(sqrt(deff * T))`.
3. OOPE achieves optimal regret performance for both well-explored (`deff=o(1)` with large offline dataset) and poorly explored (`deff~d`) datasets relative to its quality, significantly reducing regret when the offline data is rich and effectively measured.

## Technical note

This paper studies online regret minimization in stochastic linear bandits when prior offline observations from the same model are available, relevant to recommendation and advertising with logged data. It proposes OOPE (Offline-Online Phased Elimination), which within each exponentially growing phase uses an extended D-optimal experimental design mixing the offline Gram matrix with online samples, carefully weighting offline data and only reusing unseen offline samples per phase for concentration guarantees. It proves a regret bound of O~(sqrt(d_eff T log(|A|T)) + d^2), where the effective dimension d_eff (set by the offline Gram matrix eigen-spectrum) quantifies offline-data quality. It also gives the first minimax regret lower bounds explicitly depending on offline-data quality, establishing OOPE's optimality in well- and poorly-explored regimes.
