## Technical note

HOB (Alibaba) is a holistically optimized bidding strategy for a single advertising campaign across heterogeneous channels differing in ranking rules (pure eCPM vs UE-augmented), pricing (first- vs second-price), and bidding conventions. It makes marginal cost (MC) computable and alignable across channels: globally it derives channel-specific MC forms coordinated through a shared MC target (proving any interior optimum satisfies MC equalization), and locally it models free-win probability and winning-price uncertainty with a zero-inflated exponential distribution for surplus-optimal non-uniform first-price bids under organic-paid coexistence. Deployed on a commercial DSP it delivered a 3.0% GMV lift while holding ROAS constraints.
