## Technical note

The paper extends sparse autoencoders (SAE) to transformer-based sequential recommenders (GPTRec, BERT4Rec) to interpret and control model internals. Training an L1-regularized SAE on intermediate-layer activations yields features that are more monosemantic and interpretable than raw neurons, measured via point-biserial correlation, ROC AUC, and sensitivity against item attributes (e.g. genre), with SAE top-feature mean correlation 0.44 vs 0.20 for transformer neurons on MovieLens-20M. Setting a feature's activation (steering) monotonically controls the presence of the corresponding genre in recommendations while |activation|<2 keeps NDCG@10/Coverage degradation under ~10%. Downstream steering effects are on par with supervised linear probing despite SAE being fully unsupervised, enabling an 'equalizer' for flexible controllable personalization and popularity/diversity adjustment.
