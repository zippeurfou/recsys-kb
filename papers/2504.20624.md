Social chatbots have become essential intelligent companions in daily scenarios ranging from emotional support to personal interaction. However, conventional chatbots with passive response mechanisms usually rely on users to initiate or sustain dialogues by bringing up new topics, resulting in diminished engagement and shortened dialogue duration. ## Technical note

PaRT enhances proactive social chatbots at Xiaohongshu (RedNote) with personalized real-time retrieval and generation. The framework has three modules: user profiling (built via proactive questioning and memory extraction), an LLM-based intent-guided query refiner that classifies intent (natural transition, explicit retrieval, implicit retrieval) and rewrites the query into a personalized one, and retrieval-augmented generation that retrieves passages from RedNote, summarizes them, and prompts the LLM for knowledge-grounded, engagement-optimized responses. Deployed in production for over 30 days, PaRT achieved a 21.77% improvement in average dialogue duration.
