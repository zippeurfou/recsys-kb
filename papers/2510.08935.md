## Technical note

PBR (Personalize Before Retrieve) injects user-specific signals into query expansion prior to retrieval for personalized RAG. It has two components: P-PRF generates stylistically aligned pseudo feedback using user history to mimic the user's expression style, and P-Anchor performs graph-based structure alignment over the user's personal corpus to anchor expanded queries. On two personalized benchmarks it consistently beats strong baselines, with up to 10% gains on PersonaBench across retrievers, demonstrating the value of modeling personalization before retrieval to close the semantic gap.
