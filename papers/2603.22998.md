## Summary

Real-world video restoration faces challenges from diverse degradations, limiting the effectiveness of static systems and existing agent-based approaches. The paper introduces VQ-Jarvis, an intelligent video restoration agent designed to overcome these limitations through sharper degradation perception and faster decision-making. VQ-Jarvis utilizes a large new dataset (VSR-Compare) and specialized models for perception, combined with a hierarchical scheduling strategy using retrieval augmentation for simple cases and greedy search for complex ones, consistently outperforming prior methods on challenging videos.

## Technical note

VQ-Jarvis is a retrieval-augmented, all-in-one video restoration agent designed for real-world heterogeneous degradations that defeat static architectures and fixed pipelines. For 'sharp vision' the authors build VSR-Compare, a 20K-pair video enhancement comparison dataset spanning 7 degradation types and 11 enhancement operators, and train a multi-operator judge model plus a degradation perception model to guide agent decisions. For 'fast thought' a hierarchical operator scheduling strategy adapts to video difficulty: easy cases retrieve an optimal restoration trajectory in one step from a RAG library, while harder cases run step-by-step greedy search to trade off efficiency and accuracy. It consistently outperforms prior methods on complex degraded videos.
