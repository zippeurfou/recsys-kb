## Technical note

SessionRec proposes the Next Session Prediction Paradigm (NSPP) for generative sequential recommendation, arguing the standard next-item paradigm misaligns with session-level user interactions. It uses hierarchical intra/inter-session aggregation for session-level embeddings (reducing attention cost and implicitly modeling many negatives) and a session-based multi-item prediction objective, plus an intra-session rank loss that markedly improves ranking. It exhibits LLM-like power-law scaling. Public datasets and an online A/B test in the Meituan App demonstrate effectiveness of the model-agnostic paradigm.
