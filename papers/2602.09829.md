## Technical note

STAR (Single-agent Trajectory-Aligned Recommender, Tencent) internalizes a multi-agent recommender's reasoning into one efficient LLM to combine collaborative signals with semantic reasoning while eliminating multi-turn latency. A training-time teacher (MARS) uses a Collaborative Signal Translation mechanism to retrieve behavioral neighbors from the user-item graph and verbalize them into natural-language evidence, and produces reasoning-rich trajectories with tool use and self-reflection; a long-sequence abstractor compresses distant history. A trajectory-driven distillation pipeline combining SFT and GRPO then transfers planning, tool invocation, and self-reflection into the compact STAR student in a single generation pass. STAR surpasses its teacher by 8.7%-39.5% across scenarios with substantially reduced inference latency.
