## Summary

1.  The paper addresses the challenge of integrating generative recall (finding relevant items) with pre-ranking in e-commerce search, proposing VARG, a system that directly feeds generated item candidates into the existing final ranker.
2.  VARG achieves this by using a generative model (VARG-ID) to create semantically relevant candidate prefixes, combining value signals and hierarchical alignment into these candidates, and then refining the model through three-stage supervised fine-tuning and personalized training incorporating user context.
3.  Extensive offline and online evaluations demonstrate VARG's effectiveness, showing stable item identifiers, improved retrieval quality and value recall, and significant business metric improvements (GMV and PCTR) when compared to standard baselines, while also maintaining relevance with fewer candidate items.

## Technical note

VARG is a generative retrieval channel for Tmall App search that integrates recall and pre-ranking by admitting generated item candidates directly into the existing final ranker through a reserved quota, bypassing item-level pre-ranking rather than replacing the cascade. VARG-ID is a 3-token identifier: a 2-level semantic prefix from RQ-VAE (K1 = K2 = 8192, EMA-updated codebooks, deterministic K-means init) whose tokenizer adds a bidirectional query-item InfoNCE contrastive loss (lambda_QI = 0.001, tau = 0.1) on top of reconstruction and commitment, plus a third value-ordered token (K3 = 8192) encoding each item's rank within its prefix under an empirical-Bayes-smoothed conversion rate p_hat = (v + alpha*p0)/(c + alpha) with lexicographic funnel tiebreaks on purchase > add-to-cart > click then GMV. Over 51.43M Tmall products VARG-ID is 100% collision-free at construction versus 16.73% unique for 3-token RQ-VAE and 81.21% for 5-token RQ+OPQ, and its SFT base improves Q2I-GMV Recall@1/10 by 15.50/13.65 points over an RQ-VAE-3 SFT base. Three-stage full-parameter SFT on Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct across 128 GPUs progresses Item2SID (51.43M samples) to Query2SID (48.00M) to personalized User+Query+History2SID (105.69M), adding Q2I example weighting from smoothed pair conversion rates, level-aware token weights with alpha_2 > alpha_1 > alpha_3 (the L1 to L1+L2 HR gap is 16.94 points versus only 3.80 for the third token, identifying L2 as the bottleneck), context expansion with the 3 most recent queries and a cross-domain global-interest summary (+0.30/+0.31 pp HR@20/@50), and LO-SFT local ordinal supervision over a radius-3 exponentially decaying neighborhood of the true rank (third-token top-1 accuracy 36.74% to 37.02%). Prefix-GRPO then samples 8 responses per prompt over about 1.03M requests on 64 GPUs with gated multi-source rewards: -2.0 malformed, -1.0 structurally valid but unoccupied, purchase/click/exposure 3.0/1.0/0.1, a tanh-squashed ranker-advantage reward from the production ranker's z-scored score, and relevance grades +0.08/+0.03/-0.10 applied only to occupied SIDs with no matched behavior, combined with prefix-aware token weights phi(n; N) = d + (1-d)*log n / log N (floor d = 0.1), PPO-style dual clipping and KL to the frozen SFT reference (beta_KL = 0.8). Prefix-GRPO lifts GMV Recall@1 from 30.88% to 36.35% (+5.47 pp) and Q2I-GMV Recall@1 from 36.12% to 38.60%, with purchase-labeled HR@1 up 0.36 pp, and the ablation attributes most head-value gain to behavioral rewards, smaller increments to ranker-advantage plus relevance rewards, and consistent but small gains to prefix weighting. Serving uses Trie-constrained beam search with layer-wise widths (20, 50, 500) and a dual-loop daily refresh that freezes the RQ-VAE within a primary SID version so existing item addresses never move, because full within-prefix reordering drops complete-SID HR@100 by 14.45 points and third-token teacher-forcing accuracy from 36.75% to 21.24%, with monthly SID rebuilds and replay-mixed daily continued training. A 14-day A/B on 20% of Tmall search traffic gave +1.45% GMV, +0.22% IPV per user, +0.31% PCTR, and +0.13% UCTR and UCTCVR, while 6K+ AI shopping-guide queries showed a 76.77% relevance Good rate at a smaller candidate quota versus 76.71% for the original pipeline at its largest quota; an exploratory lightweight parallel decoder with prefix-conditioned heads over a single prompt-end hidden state cuts mean latency from 10.41 to 0.94 ms for about 11.1x QPS but loses 1.13/8.38/10.77 pp HR@1/20/100 and is not deployed.
