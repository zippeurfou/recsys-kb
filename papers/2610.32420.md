## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of estimating Gross Transaction Value (GTV) in e-commerce platforms using container-based displays, where ranking occurs at the container level but transactions happen at the item level, creating a critical granularity mismatch. To tackle this, the authors propose Hierarchical GTV Estimation (HGE) framework, incorporating a Hybrid Set-Aware Encoder, a Proxy-Label Learning strategy for label variance, and a Residual Correction and Ensemble Fusion module for label sparsity. HGE significantly outperforms existing baselines on large-scale datasets, achieving substantial improvements in offline metrics and confirmed by online A/B tests, and has been deployed into production.

## Technical note

Meituan addresses post-click GTV estimation for sponsored search in container-based displays (stores/POIs or livestream rooms), where ranking happens at the container level but purchases happen on individual items inside it, causing high label variance, extreme item-level label sparsity and dynamic item heterogeneity. HGE encodes the top-K=10 displayed items with a Hybrid Set-Aware Encoder (Transformer self-attention over items, with user/POI/context/query features injected after MHSA), decomposes expected GTV via Proxy-Label Learning into POI-level conversion probability times the sum over items of item purchase probability x expected quantity x unit price (justified by the Law of Total Variance), and a Residual Correction and Ensemble Fusion module adds a residual tower and gates the result with a universal DIN+DCN regression tower through a learned per-sample weight; training is multi-task with BCE for the conversion heads and Huber loss for the regression heads. On 712M Meituan ad records with an out-of-time split, HGE reaches XAUC 0.8158 vs 0.8105 for RankMixer and 0.8095 for CREAD, with PCOC 0.9858 (vs 0.75-0.79 for baselines) and the lowest MAE; removing PLL drops XAUC to 0.7873. A 13-day online A/B yielded +1.42% Revenue Per Search (95% CI [+0.80%, +2.05%]) and HGE is fully deployed on main traffic.
