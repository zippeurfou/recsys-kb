## Summary

1.  The paper addresses the challenge of retrieving visual documents (like PDFs) within RAG systems, proposing models that better handle visual information than standard text-based methods.
2.  Nemotron ColEmbed V2 introduces three large multimodal models (3B, 4B, 8B) based on Vision-Language Models (VLMs), achieving state-of-the-art results on the ViDoRe benchmark, particularly the 8B model scoring highest.
3.  These top-performing models utilize techniques like late interaction and specific training strategies, overcoming storage challenges, and offer improved retrieval accuracy for visual document content.

## Technical note

Nemotron ColEmbed V2 is a family of late-interaction embedding models (3B, 4B, 8B) built on vision-language models for retrieving visual documents such as PDFs in RAG systems. Rather than text-only pipelines, it encodes page images and applies ColBERT-style late interaction (multi-vector matching) to better capture visual content. Training strategies and techniques to mitigate the storage cost of multi-vector representations accompany the models. The models achieve state-of-the-art results on the ViDoRe benchmark, with the 8B variant scoring highest.
