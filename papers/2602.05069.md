## Summary

This paper introduces a unified framework connecting sum-of-squares decompositions with variational two-particle reduced density matrix theory, showing a natural duality. It provides explicit sum-of-squares constructions for electronic structure Hamiltonians, including spin-free and full rank-2 versions, which strictly enforce symmetry constraints. Numerical tests on molecules and clusters demonstrate that these representations, closer to frustration-free, improve quantum simulation efficiency by boosting spectral gaps and reducing algorithmic costs.

## Technical note

This work presents a unified framework linking sum-of-squares (SOS) Hamiltonian decompositions with variational two-particle reduced density matrix (v2RDM) theory, showing the weighted SOS ansatz recovers the dual of the v2RDM program and enables strict enforcement of particle-number and spin symmetries. It gives explicit SOS constructions for the Hubbard model and electronic-structure Hamiltonians, from spin-free approximations to full rank-2 expansions, and connects them to block-invariant symmetry shifts (BLISS). SOS decompositions certify variational lower bounds on ground-state energies. Numerical benchmarks on molecules and iron-sulfur clusters show these near-frustration-free representations amplify spectral gaps and reduce block-encoding costs in quantum algorithms.
