## Summary

This paper introduces IVF-RaBitQ, a GPU-native approximate nearest neighbor search solution. It aims to overcome the limitations of existing methods by combining cluster-based indexing (IVF) with RaBitQ quantization, addressing the trade-offs between index build time, search speed, recall, and storage. Experiments show that IVF-RaBitQ significantly improves upon state-of-the-art methods, achieving higher recall, faster search throughput, faster index build times, and reduced storage requirements on GPUs.

## Technical note

IVF-RaBitQ is a GPU-native ANN solution that integrates IVF coarse clustering with RaBitQ quantization to jointly optimize index-build time, search throughput, recall, and storage. Unlike PQ it needs no codebook training and gives theoretically bounded distance estimates that avoid reranking against raw vectors. Index build uses a cluster-at-a-time, block-per-vector pipeline with a two-phase parallel grid search over RaBitQ rescaling factors; search uses a two-stage estimator—a 1-bit filtering stage (lookup-table and bitwise-decomposition inner-product kernels) followed by ex-code refinement—fused with top-K selection into a single kernel over a coalescing-friendly index layout. Integrated into NVIDIA cuVS and cuVS Bench, at Recall~0.95 it delivers up to 5.6x higher QPS than CAGRA with up to 13.1x faster build, and up to 31.4x higher throughput than IVF-PQ.
