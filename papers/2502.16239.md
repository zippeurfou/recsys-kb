## Technical note

SCCDR addresses training instability in cross-domain recommendation caused by mixing intra-domain and inter-domain contrastive learning tasks. It separates intra-CL (distinct contrastive views per source/target domain) from inter-CL and adds a stop-gradient operation, then applies an inter-domain curriculum-scheduling stage that orders negative-sample difficulty anchored by overlapping users. On open-source datasets, an offline industrial dataset, and an online A/B test, SCCDR achieves state-of-the-art matching-stage performance, stabilizing embedding learning versus mixed-CL baselines.
