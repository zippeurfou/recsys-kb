## Technical note

UniRec is a unified multimodal encoder that lets LLMs reason over heterogeneous recommendation signals across four modalities: text, images, categorical features, and numerical attributes. It uses modality-specific encoders for aligned embeddings, represents each attribute as a (name, type, value) triplet to disentangle schema from raw inputs (distinguishing e.g. price vs. timestamp even though both are numeric), and a hierarchical Q-Former aggregates attributes into item-level then user-level embeddings to preserve the nested structure of interaction histories. Integrated with a pretrained LLM, UniRec outperforms SOTA multimodal and LLM-based recommenders by up to 15%.
