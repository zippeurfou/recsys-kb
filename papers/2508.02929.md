While scaling laws promise significant performance gains for recommender systems, efficiently deploying hyperscale models remains a major unsolved challenge. In contrast to fields where FMs are already widely adopted such as natural language processing and computer vision, progress in recommender systems is hindered by unique challenges including the need to learn from online streaming data under shifting data distributions, the need to adapt to different recommendation surfaces with a wide dive...

## Technical note

Meta's Foundation-Expert Paradigm deploys hyperscale recommendation foundation models by training a central FM on lifelong cross-surface multimodal user data and transferring its knowledge to lightweight surface-specific 'expert' models via target-aware embeddings. It addresses streaming one-epoch data and low teacher-student transfer via HyperCast, a production infra re-engineering training, serving, and logging. Deployed at Meta serving tens of billions of daily requests, it shows online metric gains over the prior one-stage system while improving developer velocity—claimed first successful Foundation-Expert deployment at this scale.
