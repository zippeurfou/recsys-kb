## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of applying Transformer models, successful in language tasks, to noisy behavior sequences in industrial recommendation ranking systems. The proposed solution, ReST, is a recommendation-native scaling framework designed to handle the unique data characteristics and computational asymmetry requirements. Experiments show ReST achieves higher accuracy than existing methods, scales better with model size and sequence length, and successfully deployed in production improves key metrics while adhering to strict latency constraints.

## Technical note

ReST is ByteDance's recommendation-native Transformer for scaling behavior-sequence modeling in TikTok Shop Ads CVR ranking. It factorizes ranking into a compute-heavy causal sequence encoder T and a lightweight cross decoder C driven by four semantic query tokens (CLS, context, user, ad). The encoder replaces LLaMA-style blocks with Dual-Gated Attention (a sigmoid value gate filtering V before aggregation plus a sigmoid output gate, chosen over HSTU's unbounded SiLU gate which diverged at scale), a head split between RoPE heads for ordinal position and RoTE heads that rotate queries and keys by timestamps bucketized at head-specific granularities from seconds to weeks, and Stabilized Residual Normalization (Mix-LN placement switching at about 25% depth plus a per-branch learnable scalar initialized to 0.01). The decoder reuses encoder states directly as keys and values (projection-free, dropping W_K, W_V and the value gate) and gives each query token its own W_Q, output-gate and FFN parameters. Two training-only objectives fight what the authors call sequence starvation, where the dense DLRM branch shortcuts the main loss: an auxiliary sequence-only CVR BCE head (lambda=0.1, raising encoder gradient norm over 3x) and a SigLIP-style sigmoid contrastive alignment between the CLS sequence state and the non-sequential representation. At matched FLOPs ReST gains +0.92% AUC over the production DLRM in the Large setting versus +0.72% for a ModernBERT-style encoder-decoder, +0.70% LLaMA and +0.61% HSTU, and it wins AUC and log loss on ML-1M (0.8099), ML-20M (0.8135) and Amazon-Books (0.7571). Fitting dAUC = a*F^b gives exponent 0.101 for ReST versus 0.090 LLaMA and 0.088 HSTU, and ReST keeps improving to 16k-token context, deeper than 16 layers, and d=512 where baselines saturate; adding MoE to the encoder gave +500% params and +0.00% AUC. User-level shared-prefix training raises training throughput 5.8x and request-level shared-prefix serving cuts per-request sequence inference cost up to 20x. A one-week A/B on 20% of traffic gave +1.31% streaming AUC and +11.93% Advertiser Value (p<0.01) inside a 50ms P99 budget, and ReST is fully deployed. Caveat: industrial AUC is only reported as a relative delta over an undisclosed production baseline.
