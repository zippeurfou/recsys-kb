## Technical note

MMQ (Multimodal Mixture-of-Quantization) trains a multimodal tokenizer for semantic IDs that balances cross-modal synergy with modality-specific uniqueness and bridges the semantic-behavioral gap. Stage 1 is a shared-specific tokenizer with modality-shared and modality-specific experts plus orthogonal regularization (to prevent expert collapse) grounded in Partial Information Decomposition; Stage 2 does behavior-aware fine-tuning that adapts semantic IDs to downstream objectives while a multimodal reconstruction loss preserves modality info. It serves both generative retrieval and discriminative ranking, validated by offline experiments and online A/B tests.
