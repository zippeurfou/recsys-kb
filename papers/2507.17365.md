## Technical note

DynaSearcher (Alibaba) is a multi-step agentic search system that augments LLM-based retrieval with a dynamic knowledge graph and multi-reward reinforcement learning, addressing factually inconsistent intermediate queries and inefficient search trajectories. Knowledge-graph guidance constrains reasoning and query generation, while multi-reward RL jointly optimizes retrieval quality, factual consistency, and trajectory efficiency. On multi-hop QA benchmarks it improves reasoning accuracy and reduces redundant search steps versus prior agentic retrieval systems.
