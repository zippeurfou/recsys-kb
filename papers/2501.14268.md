Existing multi-domain learning can alleviate this problem. ## Technical note

Treats a billion-parameter industrial recommender (Online Large Recommender, OLR) as a large pre-trained model and fine-tunes it per domain (multi-scene/region/period) instead of costly multi-domain retraining. Frames fine-tuning via an information-bottleneck theory (knowledge compression then knowledge matching) and implements it with a plug-and-play Information-Aware Adaptive Kernel (IAK), an MLP encoder-decoder whose Gaussian-initialized parameters are regularized by a KL term while the base model is frozen; deployment uses parallel inference with domain activation and dynamic batch-aware learning rates. On 11 real food-platform datasets OLR+IAK beats Shared-Bottom, ESMM, and MMoE and improves over zero-shot OLR by up to +0.66% CTR-AUC / +0.49% CTCVR-AUC; a 23-day online A/B test lifted Net GMV +1.62%, orders +1.86%, and it is deployed on the platform homepage.
