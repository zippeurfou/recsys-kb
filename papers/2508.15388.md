## Technical note

Kuaishou's TrackRec enhances LLM reasoning for recommendation by generating validated recommendation chain-of-thought (RecCoT) that serves both as explanations and auxiliary features. It pairs a RecCoT generator G and validator V (both small models like Qwen2.5-7B): G is distilled from a larger LLM's CoT, then improved via DPO using feedback from V, while V is Rec-tuned on G's inferences, iterated through alternating feedback learning. Extensive experiments on public and industrial datasets show TrackRec surpasses state-of-the-art methods, and it was deployed on a large advertising platform serving hundreds of millions of users with substantial gains.
