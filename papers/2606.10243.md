## Summary

Offsite conversion prediction requires handling both click and conversion signals effectively despite their statistical differences (dense vs sparse). Existing models use a single encoder for all data, which is suboptimal due to these disparities. DUET introduces dual transformer encoders specifically designed for clicks (using multi-layer self-attention) and conversions (using interleaved cross-/self-attention), demonstrating improved prediction accuracy without exceeding serving latency constraints.

## Technical note

DUET (Dual User Embedding Transformers) targets offsite conversion-rate (OCVR) prediction, where conversion signals are sparse, long-delayed, and often unattributed while click signals are dense and short-horizon. Rather than a single undifferentiated encoder, it uses domain-aware data routing to split behavior into a click/onsite-CVR stream and an offsite-conversion stream, then pre-trains a dedicated transformer per stream: multi-layer self-attention (LLaTTE) for the dense click stream and interleaved cross-/self-attention for the sparse conversion stream. Both encoders consume event-based feature sequences with timestamps, ID attributes, and content-derived semantic IDs (RQ-VAE), producing complementary ClickAUE and ConvAUE embeddings composed by the downstream ranker. Asynchronous event-triggered inference with quantization keeps serving latency negligible. It reports up to 0.38% NE reduction and A/B gains.
