## Summary

Internalizing Discrimination into Generative Recommendation" in 3 sentences:

Generative recommendation models face challenges incorporating user behaviour signals effectively. OxygenREC-v2 addresses this by integrating these signals directly during training without adding separate objectives, using logged data to condition the generation process and supervise learning. This unified approach shows significant improvements in online performance metrics compared to its predecessor.

## Technical note

OxygenREC-v2 (IDGR: Internalizing Discrimination into Generative Recommendation) is built on a unified generative recommender that replaces the retrieval-then-ranking cascade with a single model decoding SID sequences, and its contribution is folding the behavior signal (click/cart/order) into that generator instead of a separate ranker or reward model. In pre-training a behavior-aware instruction I_b enters the decoder prefix to condition SID generation on the target behavior (with a behavior-value-weighted loss); in post-training EA-TOSD combines verifiable hit-rate rewards for best-trajectory optimization with entropy-aware dual self-distillation from a privileged teacher that sees the user's near-future interactions, giving reward-model-free policy optimization on one 3B-parameter, 1B-activated MoE backbone. It raises HR@1 4.62% to 5.64% and HR@512 43.24% to 44.14% (leading a proxy-reward baseline on 6 of 7 metrics, biggest gains on the sparse order behavior), and online on JD.com lifts UCTCVR +1.6-4.4% and GMV +2.8-6.8%. Uses a unified single-model generative pipeline that reports both retrieval and ranking from one backbone, skipping the cascade.
