## Summary

Current multimodal AI models are limited to single-step reasoning, insufficient for complex tasks needing multi-hop capabilities. To address this, the authors introduce the MMhops benchmark, featuring complex tasks requiring dynamic, multi-step reasoning chains and external knowledge integration. They also propose MMhops-R1, a novel retrieval-augmented generation framework using reinforcement learning, which significantly outperforms existing models on their benchmark and demonstrates strong generalization to simpler reasoning tasks.

## Technical note

The paper introduces MMhops, a 31K-sample benchmark for multimodal multi-hop reasoning with Bridging and Comparison tasks requiring 3-4 reasoning steps over Wikipedia knowledge across visual and textual modalities. It proposes MMhops-R1, an RL-trained multimodal RAG agent (Qwen2.5-VL-7B) that dynamically plans reasoning paths, invoking image and text retrievers via a defined action space with a composite reward (outcome, format, gated action). Training uses a DAPO-style objective with dynamic sampling and masks retrieved-observation tokens from the loss. MMhops-R1 outperforms open-source MLLMs, text-only multi-hop RAG, and multimodal RAG baselines, and generalizes to INFOSEEK and E-VQA.
