## Technical note

Studies heterogeneous influence maximization in user (inviter-invitee) recommendation, where recommendation methods model interaction willingness but ignore spread capability, and IM methods maximize propagation but ignore willingness. HeteroIR uses a two-stage framework to estimate spread profits and unleash candidates' dissemination potential; HeteroIM incrementally selects the most influential invitee to recommend and reranks based on the number of reverse reachable (RR) sets containing inviters and invitees. Both significantly beat SOTA baselines (p<0.05) and, deployed on Tencent's online gaming platforms, gain 8.5% and 10% respectively in online A/B tests.
