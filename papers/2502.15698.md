## Summary

This paper introduces two AI tools, Agentic-RAG and Graph-RAG, designed to automate breast cancer treatment planning according to complex NCCN guidelines, addressing difficulties oncologists face with frequent updates. Both methods leverage Large Language Models (LLMs) on JSON-formatted guideline data: Agentic-RAG iteratively refines recommendations, while Graph-RAG converts the data into structured graph relationships for querying. Evaluated against human experts and other LLMs, both proposed tools demonstrated high adherence to guidelines without hallucinations, providing detailed recommendations with specific NCCN references (Table 1).

## Technical note

Oncologists struggle to keep up with frequently updated NCCN cancer-treatment guidelines encoded as flowcharts, and raw LLMs hallucinate or give outdated advice. This study automates breast-cancer treatment recommendation with two AI methods over guideline JSON: an Agentic-RAG pipeline (three LLM calls for title selection, JSON retrieval, and recommendation generation, plus an insufficiency check that iteratively refines) and a Microsoft-style Graph-RAG that converts JSON to text, summarizes it, and maps treatment relationships into graph communities queried for answers. Evaluated on patient descriptions with four questions each, Agentic-RAG reached 100% guideline adherence with no hallucinations, Graph-RAG 95.8%, and GPT-4 91.6%, both citing NCCN page numbers.
