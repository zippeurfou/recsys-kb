Sequential recommendation (SR), which encodes user activity to predict the next action, has emerged as a widely adopted strategy in developing commercial personalized recommendation systems. A critical component of modern SR models is the attention mechanism, which synthesizes users' historical activities. ## Technical note

CAPE (Contextual-Aware Position Encoding) is presented as the first position-encoding method designed specifically for sequential recommendation, where conventional learnable per-position vectors give only modest gains and models like DIN/DIEN use none. CAPE accounts for heterogeneous concatenated feature embeddings, diverse SR attention mechanisms, and multi-level (session/time-span) position information rather than item-independent additive vectors. On AmazonElectronics and KuaiVideo it consistently improves multiple backbones (DIN etc.) in gAUC/AUC/logloss over RoPE and CoPE, and is validated in an industrial deployment.
