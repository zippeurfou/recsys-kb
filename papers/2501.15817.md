## Technical note

Long-term Interest Clock (LIC) provides fine-grained time perception for streaming recommenders, replacing discrete hour embeddings and the coarse, short-term Interest Clock that suffer periodic online instability. LIC uses a two-stage lifelong-sequence design: Clock-GSU retrieves a top-K=100 sub-sequence from ~one-year (ten-thousand-length) long-term behaviors scored by candidate-item similarity plus a learned relative-time-gap similarity centered on the current time, and Clock-ESU applies a 4-head time-gap-aware attention (fed [delta, sqrt-delta, delta^2, log(delta+1)] features) to produce the current-interest embedding. Built on a DCN-V2 multi-task ranking model and deployed in Douyin Music App (10M+ DAU, DouyinMusic-20B dataset), it lifts offline AUC by +1.16% RelaImpr over base and beats Gaussian Interest Clock, with an online A/B gain of +0.122% user active days (vs ~0.05% typical).
