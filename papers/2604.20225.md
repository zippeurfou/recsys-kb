## Summary

This paper introduces the GaoYao benchmark, designed to comprehensively evaluate Large Language Models' multilingual and multicultural abilities, addressing limitations found in existing benchmarks. GaoYao features a unified framework categorizing tasks into cultural layers and cognitive sub-layers, and boasts extensive coverage across 26 languages and 51 cultures with high-quality, expertly localized data. By conducting detailed analyses on numerous models, the benchmark reveals significant performance disparities geographically and by task type, providing a valuable resource for future LLM development.

## Technical note

GaoYao is a multilingual/multicultural LLM benchmark with 182.3k samples across 26 languages and 51 nations, addressing fragmented dimensions, MT-driven translationese in subjective tasks, and shallow leaderboard analysis. It organizes tasks into three cultural layers (General Multilingual, Cross-cultural, Monocultural) grounded in the Cultural Iceberg / Three-Layer models, and nine cognitive sub-layers derived from Bloom's Taxonomy. Construction uses integration of existing sets (seven sub-layers), expert native-quality localization of instruction-following and multi-turn dialogue into 19 languages, and an expert-verified synthesis pipeline generalizing cross-cultural tests to 34 cultures (up to 111% coverage increase). Evaluation of 20+ flagship and compact models reveals geographic digital-divide disparities and gaps between mature and frontier tasks.
