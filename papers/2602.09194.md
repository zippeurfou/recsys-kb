## Summary

1.  The paper introduces ML-DCN, a novel deep learning model designed to improve ad click-through rate prediction at scale, addressing the challenge of balancing model performance with the strict computational efficiency constraints of production serving.
2.  Existing models like DCNv2 and MaskNet, while popular, showed limited gains in performance when scaled further within the fixed computational budget used by Pinterest.
3.  ML-DCN combines the strengths of DCNv2 and MaskNet by integrating a mask into a low-rank crossing layer, enabling more efficient computation and offering superior performance (state-of-the-art AUC) and a better performance-compute trade-off compared to previous methods at matched computational cost, validated both offline and through online A/B tests.

## Technical note

ML-DCN (Masked Low-rank DCN) is a feature-interaction module for Pinterest ads CTR prediction that integrates an instance-guided mask (from MaskNet) inside a low-rank DCNv2 cross layer to select and amplify salient interaction directions under a fixed FLOPs budget. The masking operates in the low-rank interaction space, raising the maximum polynomial interaction order to 2^(l+1)-1 for l layers versus l+1 for DCNv2. It achieves a better AUC-FLOPs trade-off than DCNv2, MaskNet, WuKong, and RankMixer at matched compute, and works as an expert within MMoE. Online A/B tests showed +1.89% platform-wide CTR at neutral serving cost.
