## Technical note

This work proposes explicit uncertainty modeling for video watch-time prediction, addressing an 'uncertainty paradox' where distribution-modeling methods (quantile prediction, ordinal regression) must keep high uncertainty to capture out-of-distribution samples yet lose accuracy/confidence otherwise. It wraps the base watch-time predictor with a confidence-prediction module that estimates how likely the predicted distribution covers the observed label, acting during training as a selector between predicted value and ground truth, optimized in an adversarial framework. Deployed on an industrial video-sharing platform with hundreds of millions of DAU, it lifted user video watch time by 0.31% in online A/B testing, and offline experiments on two public datasets confirm effectiveness across watch-time backbones.
