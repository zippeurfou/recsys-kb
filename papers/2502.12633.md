Large language models (LLMs) have been increasingly employed in various intelligent educational systems, simulating human tutors to facilitate effective human-machine interaction. ## Technical note

PACE (PersonAlized Conversational tutoring agEnt) is an LLM-based mathematics tutor that models each student's learning style using the Felder-Silverman model to align with their persona and develop individualized teaching strategies. It applies the Socratic teaching method for instant feedback and deeper thinking, and constructs personalized teaching data to train the model to identify and adapt to each student's needs. Using multi-aspect evaluation criteria, PACE outperforms existing tutoring systems in personalizing the educational experience and motivating students versus one-size-fits-all scaffolding.
