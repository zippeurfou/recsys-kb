## Summary

1.  QwenLong-L1.5 introduces three key innovations for superior long-context reasoning: a pipeline synthesizing complex multi-hop reasoning tasks from documents, stabilized RL training techniques addressing instability, and a memory-augmented architecture managing ultra-long sequences.
2.  These advancements are built upon the Qwen3-30B-A3B-Thinking base model, enabling it to achieve performance comparable to leading models (GPT-5, Gemini) on standard benchmarks and significantly improve performance on tasks exceeding 4 million tokens.
3.  The model's long-context capabilities also enhance its performance in general domains like scientific reasoning, memory tasks, and extended dialogue.

## Technical note

QwenLong-L1.5 is a post-training recipe for long-context reasoning built on Qwen3-30B-A3B-Thinking. It contributes a data-synthesis pipeline that deconstructs documents into atomic facts and programmatically composes verifiable multi-hop questions over globally distributed evidence; stabilized long-context RL using GRPO with a DAPO-style token-level loss, task-balanced sampling, and task-specific advantage estimation plus Adaptive Entropy-Controlled Policy Optimization (AEPO); and a memory-agent architecture with multi-stage fusion RL that processes documents chunk-by-chunk with recurrent memory and planning for tasks beyond the 256K window. It surpasses its baseline by 9.9 points on average, rivals GPT-5/Gemini-2.5-Pro, and gains 9.48 points over the agent baseline on 1M-4M-token tasks.
