## Technical note

This Meta paper establishes the first robust power-law scaling laws for continually pre-trained LLMs in recommendation by fixing the data paradigm rather than the model, arguing prior scaling failures (e.g. PLUM's 3B not beating 900M) stem from noisy, position/popularity/exposure-biased interaction logs. It proposes a layered synthetic-data 'curriculum': Layer 1 grounds semantic and collaborative knowledge via item-text alignment and CF/association-rule data; Layer 2 generates position-debiased user interaction histories via graph-based random walks with no intrinsic order. Sequential models (GRU4Rec, NARM, STAMP, SASRec) trained on this synthetic data beat models trained on real data (+130% Recall@100 for SASRec), and across 0.6B-8B parameters on 163B tokens they observe consistent scaling with UIH exponent alpha~0.45-0.59, CF ~0.35, item-text ~0.15, plus asymmetric transfer where CF data cuts UIH asymptotic loss 31%.
