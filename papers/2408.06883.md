## Summary

This paper introduces **DMSG**, a novel generative framework called Prompt-to-Slate based on diffusion models for creating coherent item lists directly from natural language prompts, unlike traditional methods focusing solely on individual items or requiring step-by-step generation. DMSG learns high-dimensional patterns of entire slates together and is evaluated in music playlists and e-commerce bundles, showing superior relevance and diversity compared to existing baselines like retrieval or autoregressive models. The system offers a fast, scalable, prompt-driven recommendation solution with increased user engagement shown via an A/B test on a real platform.

## Technical note

DMSG frames slate (multi-item list) recommendation as generative modeling with diffusion models, avoiding explicit combinatorial optimization and the single-item-interaction assumption of ranking-based methods. Conditioned on a natural-language prompt (no explicit personalization), it models the joint distribution over slates by training the diffusion model on concatenated continuous item embeddings and decoding generated latents back to catalog items, using DDIM for low-latency sampling. Its stochasticity yields diverse, coherent slates for the same prompt, improving freshness and long-tail discovery. On music-playlist and e-commerce-bundle tasks it beats retrieval baselines (up to +12.9% AP, +17% NDCG), and a two-week production A/B test cut duplicate content -13.4% and raised active interactions +6.8%.
