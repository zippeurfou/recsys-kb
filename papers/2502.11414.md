## Technical note

This paper proposes a query-level click propensity model for unbiased learning to rank, challenging the pointwise examination hypothesis: on Baidu-ULTR, ~2/3 of clicked sessions have one click and ~1/3 have two, motivating 'relevance saturation bias' where users stop clicking after satisfying their need. It introduces DualIPW, combining query-level and position-level Inverse Propensity Weighting, and proves it learns an unbiased ranker. Experiments on the real-world Baidu-ULTR dataset show significant gains over ULTR baselines (GradRev, Drop, UPE, IOBM).
