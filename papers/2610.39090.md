## Summary

1.  Existing ad rankers struggle with personalizing offers for consumers due to reliance on hand-crafted signals from sparse, high-cardinality interaction histories.
2.  PALATE addresses this by training a GPT-style generative model on each user's engagement history to predict future actions, using its last hidden state as a learned, dense embedding representing the user.
3.  This embedding significantly boosted offline ranking performance (Eval-AUC +0.57-0.59%) and shipped successfully online, increasing conversion rates (up to +1.50%) and revenue (+0.79-0.94%) for both Discovery and Search surfaces, thanks to specific design choices targeting the marketplace's repurchase focus.

## Technical note

DoorDash's DCNv2 ads ranker personalized only through hand-engineered aggregates and order-agnostic pooled history, missing a learned per-consumer sequence representation for a sparse, high-cardinality and repurchase-heavy store space. PALATE trains a 3-layer, 768-wide decoder-only GPT over each consumer's chronological store-engagement sequence (store and business IDs plus day-part, weekend, hour, weekday and sponsored context, 200-position window) to predict the next few businesses with a horizon-weighted sampled softmax whose negatives mix a fixed ~60K popular-business pool, businesses shown but not ordered, random and in-batch samples; a click pretrain is followed by a conversion fine-tune, and an exploration-weighted loss (0.9 for businesses new to the user, 0.1 for repeats) counters repurchase dominance. The frozen 256-d last-token hidden state and the softmax-row business embeddings are refreshed by a daily job and consumed by the ranker as [u, c, u*c, cos(u,c)]. Hard negatives raise intrinsic R@10 +14.3% and 9:1 weighting raises new-business R@10 +29.1% at the cost of overall recall; in the ranking ladder the 9:1 embedding is the largest single-feature step (Eval-AUC +0.59% Discovery, +0.57% Search) whereas the uniform-weighted embedding gives ~0 and an added DIN module only +0.03%. Shipped inside bundled ranker retrains, the launches lifted revenue +0.79% and CVR +1.17% on Discovery and +0.94% / +1.50% on Search.
