## Summary

This paper introduces EAGER, a framework for generating query suggestions in e-commerce search directly from clicked items. Existing methods either rely solely on historical logs or use generic LLMs disconnected from platform-specific user behavior. EAGER combines a supervised enrichment stage using a curriculum with a reinforcement alignment stage, achieving effective results through offline experiments and online deployment.

## Technical note

Alibaba / Taobao, item-to-query (I2Q) recommendation: when a user clicks an item in the recommendation feed and returns, the system shows clickable query suggestions to capture immediate search intent. Existing production methods mine item-query co-occurrence from logs and depend on a pre-existing query pool, so they collapse to generic head queries for long-tail and new items; off-the-shelf LLMs are fluent but generic and platform-unaware. EAGER is a two-stage framework on a Qwen3-1.7B backbone. Enrichment stage: SFT targets are constructed as ordered multi-source labels [clicked query; post-click issued queries; LLM-expanded queries], where the LLM-expanded tail is ranked by PARM, a generative click predictor fine-tuned from Qwen-4B on production logs that emits a yes/no token and is scored as 1 - p(no | item, user, query), so supervision ordering and the RL reward share one production preference signal. A four-task rationale-augmented curriculum scales information and reasoning depth: I2Q (item only), Enhanced I2Q (with rationales distilled from a 235B teacher), UI2Q (adds user context), Enhanced UI2Q (both). Two augmentations follow: a diversity regularizer penalizing mean pairwise similarity among the K generated queries (optimal lambda=0.1) and self-distillation from high-temperature sampled generations (temperature and top-p peaking at 0.9 to 1.0) filtered for format, length and business-rule violations. Alignment stage: GRPO with a hybrid reward R = alpha R_rule + beta R_model, where R_rule is a weighted sum of eight verifiable rules covering display format, compliance and fluency, and R_model is the mean PARM click reward over the generated query set. Offline, EAGER reaches Soft HR@1 77.96 / @all 93.82, APD 72.96, Distinct-2 83.04 and Rule score 9.16, beating GPT-5.2 (75.73 / 94.52 / 67.74 / 77.17 / 8.57) and Gemini 3.1 despite the 1.7B backbone, and it scales with size (EAGER-8B Soft HR@1 78.65, EAGER-4B best APD 79.13). Ablations are unusually clean: GRPO mainly buys business-rule compliance (Rule 9.16 versus 7.21) with relevance essentially flat (Soft HR@all 93.82 versus 93.76); the diversity term trades 0.38 Soft HR@1 for +4.72 APD and +4.18 Distinct-2; removing self-distillation drops Hard HR@all from 0.87 to 0.65 and APD to 69.62; the curriculum contributes +6.57 Soft HR@1 and +6.97 APD overall, with user context the biggest single step (+4.06 Soft HR@1) and rationale supervision the biggest APD step (+3.17). A 9-day online A/B on 5 percent of traffic adding EAGER as an extra query-recommendation source lifts UCTR +0.83 percent, PCTR +1.28 percent, DAC +2.98 percent, Pay Count +3.49 percent and L2P +2.38 percent, all p<0.05. Caveats stated by the authors: PARM inherits position and exposure bias from logs, the eight rules are hand-designed and business-specific, Hard HR stays under 1 percent because I2Q is open-vocabulary and one-to-many, and downstream ranking and conversion are not jointly optimized.
