## Technical note

ACT (Automated Constraint Targeting) automatically finds the minimal hyperparameter changes needed to keep secondary recommender metrics above guardrail thresholds while maximizing a primary objective. It casts the problem as minimizing the L2 norm of weights subject to guardrail constraints, solved via grouped grid search over an offline pairwise estimator computed from randomized item-pair data (unbiased off-policy evaluation). Deployed on YouTube, ACT counteracted a -13.4% drop in a secondary metric to -2.25% (vs fixed weights), and its offline estimator correlated 0.82 (Pearson) with online movements across 100 A/B tests.
