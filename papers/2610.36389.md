## Summary

1.  **Problem:** Evaluating natural language recommendations is crucial for trust, but current methods, both costly human evaluation and inconsistent automated single-agent LLM-based evaluations, are inadequate for large-scale systems.
2.  **Solution:** The authors propose MAREval, a multi-agent framework using LLMs. This system employs a planner (using Chain of Debate), a moderator (handling prompt drift), an arbitrator (aggregating results), and Monte Carlo sampling to generate robust, human-aligned evaluations.
3.  **Results:** MAREval significantly outperforms existing single-agent and multi-agent baselines on public and proprietary datasets, showing better alignment with human judgment and much improved stability. It also passes rigorous human-annotation quality checks and demonstrably boosts user engagement and revenue in online A/B tests, proving its scalability and reliability for production use.

## Technical note

Natural-language recommendation explanations need quality gating before exposure, but human review does not scale and single-agent LLM judges (vanilla, CoT, Self-Refine, G-Eval) and loosely coordinated multi-agent debate (ChatEval) are prompt-sensitive and inconsistent. MAREval orchestrates gpt-3.5-turbo agents with a planner that emits synchronized per-round, role-specific plans (Chain of Debate), several evaluator agents, a moderator that compresses each round into a summary to curb prompt drift, an arbitrator that issues the final judgment, and Monte Carlo aggregation over independent pipeline runs or token probabilities. On TopicalChat it reaches the best overall-quality correlation with humans (Pearson 0.548, Kendall 0.478 vs 0.505/0.438 for the best baseline) and on a proprietary Walmart explanation set it lifts relevance agreement to Pearson 0.338 / kappa 0.334 vs 0.238/0.225 for ChatEval, with the lowest run-to-run std (0.0197 vs 0.0227 for G-Eval); ablations show moderation and MC sampling each matter and gains saturate around 4 evaluators and 5 MC runs at ~8 s per call. In a human-annotation launch gate MAREval-filtered explanations passed 100% of hard and 98.6% of soft criteria (G-Eval: 91.0%/76.1%), and showing only MAREval-approved explanations for ~300K items across 14 categories lifted add-to-cart +1.18% (p=0.05) and GMV +1.36% (p<0.01) in an online A/B.
