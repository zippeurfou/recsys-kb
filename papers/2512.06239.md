## Summary

1.  The LOCUS system automates the construction and training of specialized NLP models by using a small amount of labeled data (few-shot learning). It achieves this through targeted data retrieval from a broad repository and the generation of synthetic training samples.
2.  LOCUS significantly outperforms strong baselines, including GPT-4o, on benchmarks like Named Entity Recognition (NER) and Text Classification (TC), while drastically reducing costs and model sizes.
3.  By employing parameter-efficient tuning methods (like LoRA) and optimizing model memory, LOCUS delivers models retaining nearly full accuracy (99%) but using only 5% of the memory and 1% of the parameters of full fine-tuning, making specialized models more accessible.

## Technical note

LOCUS is a pipeline for low-cost customization of specialized NLP models from few-shot data, targeting named entity recognition (NER) and text classification (TC) under dynamic label definitions. It performs sentence-level targeted retrieval from a broad repository, generates additional training samples via in-context synthetic data generation, and fine-tunes with either full or low-rank (LoRA) parameter-efficient adaptation. LOCUS consistently outperforms strong baselines including GPT-4o while its memory-optimized LOCUS-mini retains ~99% of full fine-tuning accuracy using only ~5% of the memory and under 1% of GPT-4o's parameters.
