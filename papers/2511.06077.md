## Technical note

This ByteDance/Douyin system scales end-to-end long-sequence recommendation to 10K-length user histories in production. Stacked Target-to-History Cross Attention (STCA) replaces history self-attention with stacked target-to-history cross-attention, cutting complexity from quadratic to linear in sequence length; Request Level Batching (RLB) amortizes user-side encoding across multiple targets in a request; and a length-extrapolative regimen trains on ~2K-average windows but infers on 10K without extra training cost. Offline and online experiments show predictable monotonic gains with history length and model capacity (scaling-law behavior), and full-traffic deployment on Douyin delivers significant engagement improvements within latency budgets.
