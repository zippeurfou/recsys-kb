## Summary

Wolt's Universal Venue Ranker (UVR) stacks a BERT4Rec-style bidirectional transformer next-purchase classifier under a CatBoost listwise ranker, trained per country across restaurants and retail, replacing four legacy rankers. Purchases are typed post hoc as trial (new store), reorder (rec), or cold-start, and purchase-type-aware sample/session weights plus label smoothing deliberately bias the ranker toward trial: offline trial MRR rises +12% to +30% over production while reorder MRR regresses in five of six countries. Online, V1 delivered +5.5% Merchant Trial Rate and +0.16% Global CVR (p=0.017); V2 and V3 added trial-rate gains with Global CVR statistically flat, so the offline reorder regression did not surface in the blended online metric.

## Technical note

Problem: a delivery-platform store ranker must surface new stores for trial without degrading sessions with reorder intent; Wolt's prior stack used four separate rankers (NCF first-pass, DNN second-pass, GBDT cold-start for restaurants, GBDT for retail). Method: UVR stacks a 4-layer bidirectional transformer next-purchase classifier (masked target step with unmasked time, H3 cell and domain; label-smoothed softmax CE with eps=0.2 and per-sample weights w_new > w_cs > w_rec; checkpoint chosen on trial-slice MRR) under a CatBoost YetiRankPairwise:NDCG ranker whose per-session weights w_new/w_rec in {1.5,2.0,3.0} and max_pairs trace a trial-vs-reorder Pareto frontier; purchase type (cs/new/rec) is derived post hoc from whether the purchased store is in the user's prior purchase set, not predicted at inference. Evaluation: MRR per purchase type on the held-out last day of single-purchase converted sessions only (non-purchase sessions discarded), over the full candidate slate; roll-out gate requires trial MRR to beat a reorder-indicator+popularity benchmark at 95% confidence, reorder MRR is a non-blocking guardrail, operating point = largest trial uplift with reorder regression within -15%. Results: V2 ranker trial MRR +12.5% to +30.3% vs production across six countries with reorder MRR negative in five of six (DEU -27.3%, ISR -12.5%); online 50:50 user-split A/B tests gave V1 +5.5% Merchant Trial Rate and +0.16% Global CVR (p=0.017), V2 +0.45% MTR and -0.05% Global CVR (p=0.398), V3 +1.31% retail MTR and +0.04% Global CVR (p=0.457), i.e. the offline reorder regression left blended Global CVR statistically unchanged (|delta|<0.1%, p>0.39). Ads revenue regressed under V1 and V3 (-0.43% for V3); serving p99 about 60 ms.
