Generative recommendation (GR) typically encodes behavioral or semantic aspects of item information into discrete tokens, leveraging the standard autoregressive (AR) generation paradigm to make predictions. ## Technical note

CAR (Chunk AutoRegressive Modeling) is a generative recommendation paradigm from Huawei that unifies semantic IDs (SIDs, the 'why'/semantics) and unique item IDs (UID, the 'what'/collaborative signal) into a single autoregressive transformer via an 'act-with-think' perspective. Each item is packed as a SIDs-UID conceptual chunk and modeled with chunk-level autoregression, letting each decoding step make a holistic prediction that captures the interdependence of semantic and collaborative information. CAR improves Recall@5 by 7.93%-22.30% over traditional AR baselines on three public datasets and exhibits a scaling effect with SID bit number, emulating a slow-thinking reasoning mechanism.
