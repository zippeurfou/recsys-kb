## Technical note

GREAM (Huawei) is an end-to-end framework that adapts pre-trained LLMs into generative reasoning recommendation models. It integrates: (i) Collaborative-Semantic Alignment, fusing item titles/descriptions/reviews into stable discrete item indices with auxiliary alignment tasks; (ii) Reasoning Curriculum Activation, a synthetic Chain-of-Thought dataset with a five-stage curriculum (evidence extraction, latent preference modeling, intent inference, recommendation formulation, denoised sequence rewriting); and (iii) Sparse-Regularized Group Policy Optimization (SRPO) with Residual-Sensitive Verifiable Reward and Bonus-Calibrated Group Advantage Estimation to stabilize RL under sparse feedback. It supports Direct Sequence Recommendation (fast) and Sequential Reasoning Recommendation (interpretable) and beats generative and LLM baselines on Beauty, Sports, Instruments.
