## Technical note

This Pinterest paper is an applied study of making the Deep Hierarchical Ensemble Network (DHEN) work for web-scale conversion-rate (CVR) prediction of oCPM/conversion ads, where labels are sparse, delayed, and noisy compared to CTR. It builds a multitask framework with a single DHEN backbone predicting all off-site conversion tasks (ablating which feature-crossing modules like MLP/DCN/Transformer/DeepFM/DIN help), models both on-site real-time and off-site conversion event sequences end-to-end (beyond PinnerFormer embeddings), and adds a self-supervised future-action prediction auxiliary loss to combat label sparseness. The method achieves state-of-the-art performance versus single feature-crossing baselines.
