## Summary

Large-scale recommender systems benefit from increased model depth, but previous research (like RankMixer) showed that the quality (rank) of user/item representations can actually decrease in deeper layers. This suggests representation capacity doesn't scale proportionally with model size. To address this, the paper introduces RankUp, a new architecture designed to enhance representation quality and prevent degradation by using techniques like randomized permutation splitting and multi-embedding. RankUp has been successfully deployed in production systems, demonstrating significant improvements in business metrics (GMV).

## Technical note

RankUp targets representation collapse in deep MetaFormer-based industrial rankers, where effective rank (exp of Shannon entropy over normalized singular values) follows a damped oscillatory trajectory and degrades in deep layers because token mixers give only bounded rank expansion and per-token FFNs are rank-contractive. Five mechanisms enrich latent diversity: Randomized Permutation Splitting (decorrelates tokens vs semantic grouping, verified via lower pairwise mutual information), a Multi-embedding Paradigm (K independent tables per feature), Global Token Integration, Cross Integration of pretrained two-tower user/item embeddings via element-wise product, and Task-Specific Token Decoupling to reduce multi-task gradient interference. Deployed on a 2-layer, ~100M-param backbone across Weixin Video Accounts/Official Accounts/Moments for CVR (32 objectives), yielding GMV lifts of 3.41%, 4.81%, 2.12% and strong new-ad cold-start gains.
