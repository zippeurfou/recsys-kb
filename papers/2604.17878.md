## Technical note

RankUp targets representation collapse in deep MetaFormer-style recommendation rankers, where prior RankMixer analysis showed the effective rank of token representations follows a damped oscillatory trajectory that fails to grow with depth. It enhances latent-space diversity via randomized permutation splitting over sparse features, a multi-embedding paradigm, global token integration, cross-integration of pre-trained embeddings, and task-specific token decoupling. Deployed in Tencent production for CVR optimization, RankUp yields GMV gains of 3.41% (Weixin Video Accounts), 4.81% (Moments), and 2.12% (Official Accounts) with real-time AUC lifts over the baseline.
