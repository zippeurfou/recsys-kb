## Summary

1.  Existing Vision-Language Models (VLMs) struggle with efficiently integrating external knowledge for complex video question answering (Video QA), despite being good at visual reasoning.
2.  To address this, the authors propose VideoSpeculateRAG, combining speculative decoding (using a fast draft model and a powerful confirmation model) with a simple similarity-based filtering technique to correct retrieved knowledge, significantly speeding up inference while maintaining or improving accuracy.
3.  Experiments show that VideoSpeculateRAG achieves comparable or better accuracy than standard RAG methods while accelerating inference by about 2x, demonstrating an effective way to enhance both the efficiency and reliability of knowledge-intensive multimodal tasks.

## Technical note

VideoSpeculateRAG (FastV-RAG) is an efficient VLM-based retrieval-augmented framework for knowledge-intensive video question answering. It adapts speculative decoding: a lightweight draft VLM rapidly proposes multiple answer candidates from retrieved documents and a stronger heavyweight VLM verifies and refines them, cutting inference latency without sacrificing accuracy. To address a key error source, incorrect entity recognition in retrieved knowledge, it adds a fine-grained entity-alignment step where the drafter extracts entities and reasoning traces and the verifier scores candidate-to-frame alignment via CLIP similarity, ensuring answers are factually accurate and visually grounded. On VideoSimpleQA and Encyclopedic VQA it matches or exceeds standard RAG accuracy while delivering roughly 2x speedup.
