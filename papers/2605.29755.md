## Technical note

Rec-Distill is an industrial distillation pipeline that transfers large-recommendation-model scaling gains to latency-constrained serving models, combining large-teacher scaling with student-side transfer via decoupled training, black-box distillation, a debiasing mechanism, and a hybrid batch-streaming pipeline suited to continuously-updated recommendation. It scales teachers up to 24B dense parameters and 20K behavior-sequence length while letting lightweight students recover a substantial share of teacher gains, with distillation transferability exceeding 60% in the best setting. Offline and online experiments across recommendation and advertising scenarios show the transferred gains translate into measurable business improvements under industrial constraints.
