## Technical note

This Microsoft paper proposes a knowledge-graph-augmented RAG framework for e-commerce customer support QA. Offline it constructs a domain KG from product catalogs, reviews, and past support tickets (entities and relations); online a customer query triggers parallel retrieval of a relevant KG subgraph and text documents from the support archive, which a novel answer-synthesis algorithm fuses for the LLM. The system reports a 23% improvement in factual accuracy and 89% user satisfaction in e-commerce QA scenarios (numbers from the paper's own evaluation).
