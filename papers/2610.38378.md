## Summary

This paper addresses three key limitations found in the existing GR4AD generative recommendation framework for advertising, which, despite boosting revenue and enabling real-time deployment, had issues with SID reliability, model scaling efficiency, and value misalignment. The authors introduce GR4AD-X as a novel solution incorporating Multi-Path assignment for more reliable SIDs, serving-efficient capacity scaling for better performance under constraints, and UniVGD for aligning the generation process with commercial value through optimization and reranking. Large-scale online tests confirm that GR4AD-X improves cumulative revenue by 3.07% while maintaining strict serving requirements.

## Technical note

GR4AD, Kuaishou's deployed SID-based generative ad retriever, had three weaknesses: hard first-level RQ-KMeans assignment makes boundary ads unreachable once their prefix is pruned (48% of 7.56M ads have nearest/second-nearest centroid distance ratio >=0.90), naive backbone scaling collapses QPS for small revenue gains, and sequence-level value rewards give no credit to intermediate SID decisions (HitRate@10 between top-eCPM and top-probability ads is ~0.02). GR4AD-X adds Multi-Path SID (keep top-m first-level centroids, each with its own residual trajectory, trained with blockwise multi-target masks and deduplicated at inference), five serving-efficient scaling dimensions (richer inputs, SwiGLU encoder, layer-specific plus prefix n-gram hashed SID memory tables gating the FFN (PCGM), diagonal Hyper-Connection multi-stream residuals, and a semantically anchored reasoning token before the first SID), and UniVGD: prefix-level eCPM values propagated bottom-up and learned with per-level heads via listwise RSPO, PV-BeamSearch fusing likelihood and value ranks with RRF over a 2k pool from level 2 on, and an in-model discriminative rerank step with a LambdaLoss eCPM head and an ordinal behavior-depth head fused by RRF. Scaling 0.16B to 0.49B this way gives +1.41% revenue at 85% QPS vs +0.53% at 33% QPS for a 0.50B dense backbone; Multi-Path SID lifts first-level Hit@1 from 14.38% to 17.58%. Cumulative sequential online A/B tests on Kuaishou advertising reach +3.07% revenue over GR4AD (Multi-Path SID +0.58%, scaling to +2.00%, PV-RSPO +0.28%, PV-BeamSearch +0.21%, discriminative reranking +0.58%).
