## Summary

This paper introduces GR4AD-X, an enhanced generative recommendation framework for large-scale advertising. It addresses three key limitations of its predecessor: unreliable SID assignment, scaling inefficiency, and poor value alignment during generation. The improvements include multi-path SID generation for reliability, model capacity scaling for efficiency, and value-guided generation techniques, leading to a significant 3.07% revenue increase while maintaining performance constraints.

## Technical note

Kuaishou's deployed SID-based generative ad retriever GR4AD had three production problems: hard first-level RQ-KMeans assignment is fragile (48% of 7.56M ads have a second-nearest first-level centroid within 10% of the nearest, so a pruned prefix makes the ad unreachable), naive backbone scaling cuts QPS faster than it adds revenue, and next-token likelihood is misaligned with eCPM (HitRate@10 between top-eCPM and top-probability ads only 0.02). GR4AD-X adds Multi-Path SID (keep the top-m first-level centroids, each completing its own residual trajectory plus the shared hash token, trained with a blockwise multi-target loss and deduplicated per ad at inference), and scales capacity along five axes instead of depth/width: 20+ extra profile/context features, 16-to-64-d key embeddings with SwiGLU, Prefix-Conditioned Gated Memory (per-layer hashed 1/2/3-gram SID-prefix tables that replace the dense FFN gate), diagonal Hyper-Connections with 4 residual streams, and a Semantically Anchored Reason Prefill token before s1 supervised by a coarse prototype loss. UniVGD aligns generation with value end to end: PV-RSPO propagates eCPM rewards bottom-up through the SID prefix tree and fits per-level value heads with a listwise RSPO loss, PV-BeamSearch expands a 2k pool at levels >= 2 and fuses likelihood and value ranks with RRF, and a final decoder step with LambdaLoss (eCPM) and ordinal-regression (conversion depth) heads reranks the beam, fused again by RRF. Five-axis scaling from 0.16B to 0.49B gives +1.41% revenue at 85% QPS versus +0.53% at 33% QPS for a 0.50B dense backbone, UniVGD lifts Full HR@10 against eCPM from 0.021 to 0.298 at 75% QPS, and sequential online A/B tests accumulate to +3.07% revenue over deployed GR4AD (Multi-Path SID alone +0.58%, discriminative reranking the largest single step at +0.58%).
