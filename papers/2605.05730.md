## Technical note

EKTM (Effective Knowledge Transfer for Multi-task recommendation Models) tackles CVR sparsity by letting each conversion task learn from other user-behavior tasks via explicit representation-level transfer rather than shared bottoms or scalar-output transfer. It adds a centralized router module that integrates and distributes knowledge across tasks, a per-task transmitter module that inspects and imports only relevant knowledge to avoid negative transfer, and an enhanced CVR-prediction module to ensure positive transfer aligned with each task's objective. Experiments on public benchmarks beat SOTA MTL baselines, and Huawei online A/B testing yields a 3.93% eCPM uplift with full deployment across two main-traffic scenarios.
