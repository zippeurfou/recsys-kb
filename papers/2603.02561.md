## Technical note

SOLAR introduces SVD-Attention, an attention mechanism that exploits the low-rank structure of user behavior sequence representations to reduce softmax-attention complexity from O(N^2 d) to O(Ndr) while remaining theoretically lossless on low-rank matrices and preserving softmax (unlike linear attention, which drops softmax and suffers magnitude neglect). Built on this, SOLAR supports ten-thousand-scale behavior sequences and thousand-scale candidate sets in the cascade without filtering, and the paper proves that point-wise scoring incurs ranking bias and a generalization penalty in set-wise recommendation. Deployed in Kuaishou, SOLAR delivers a 0.68% Video Views gain plus other business improvements.
