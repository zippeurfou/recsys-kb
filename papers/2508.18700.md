## Technical note

Addresses the one-epoch overfitting phenomenon of ID-based embeddings (driven by power-law tail entries) that limits web-scale CTR models to a single epoch. Proposes a two-stage strategy: Stage 1 pre-trains foundational ID embeddings in a minimal dot-product model with contrastive loss using in-batch plus uniform random negatives (reducing the effective degrees of freedom of tail entries and enabling 10x more data and multi-epoch training without overfitting); Stage 2 shares and fine-tunes those embeddings in downstream recommenders. Deployed at Pinterest, it lifts site-wide engagement by 2.2%, with two-stage fine-tuned beating frozen and single-stage variants.
