## Technical note

HMUM is a Heterogeneous Multi-treatment Uplift Modeling framework for trade-off optimization in short-video recommendation, where strategy responses (e.g. watch time vs. video view counts) conflict. An offline Hybrid Uplift Modeling (HUM) module captures both synergistic and individual causal effects of multiple treatments, while an online Dynamic Decision-Making (DDM) module estimates per-user value weights of responses in real time for personalized decisions, replacing biased fixed-weight balancing. Evaluated on two public datasets, an industrial dataset, and Kuaishou A/B tests (deployed in Ranking and Edge Rerank), it shows superior offline performance and significant consumption-metric gains; fully deployed serving hundreds of millions of users.
