## Technical note

LoKA makes FP8 practical for large recommendation models (LRMs), which are numerically sensitive and dominated by small GEMMs followed by normalization—naive FP8 (TorchAO) gives 1.3x slowdown and up to 2.5% relative log-loss degradation. It uses system-model co-design via three components: LoKA Probe, statistically grounded online profiling of activation/weight statistics to find per-layer safe/unsafe FP8 sites; LoKA Mods, reusable model adaptations improving numerical stability and efficiency; and LoKA Dispatch, a runtime selecting the fastest FP8 kernel meeting accuracy constraints. Deployed on Meta production LRMs (Wukong, Interformer, ELFM) across H100/B200/GB200/MI300X/MI350X, it delivers up to 20% higher training throughput and 40% faster inference with no quality loss.
