## Technical note

FORGE (Taobao/Alibaba) is a benchmark for forming semantic identifiers (SIDs) for generative retrieval, systematically studying SID construction strategies across data modalities, encoding schemes, and ID-collision mitigation. It proposes two training-free SID quality metrics (Embedding HitRate and Gini coefficient) strongly correlated with GR performance, avoiding costly full GR training for evaluation. Its discovered strategies delivered a 0.35% transaction-count increase in Taobao's 'Guess You Like' A/B test and are deployed at full scale; it releases AL-GR, a 14-billion-interaction, 250-million-item industrial dataset with multimodal features.
