## Technical note

PrefEval is a benchmark evaluating LLMs' ability to infer, memorize, and adhere to user preferences in long-context, multi-session conversations, with 3,000 curated preference-query pairs over 20 topics in explicit and implicit forms, scored via generation and classification tasks. Benchmarking 10 open/proprietary LLMs (Claude, GPT-4, Mistral, LLaMA) up to 100k tokens, zero-shot preference-following accuracy falls below 10% by 10 turns (~3k tokens) and degrades with longer context even with prompting and RAG; fine-tuning on PrefEval significantly improves preference following and generalizes to longer contexts.

The output was structured in JSON format to facilitate subsequent processing
