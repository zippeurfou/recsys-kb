## Summary

(Abstract fallback)

Conversational recommender systems (CRS) aim to provide personalized recommendations via interactive dialogues with users. While large language models (LLMs) enhance CRS with their superior understanding of context-aware user preferences, they typically struggle to leverage behavioral data, which have proven to be important for classical collaborative filtering (CF)-based approaches. ## Technical note

CRAG (Collaborative Retrieval Augmented Generation) is the first approach combining state-of-the-art LLMs with collaborative filtering for conversational recommendation. It links dialogue-mentioned items to a database (with LLM-based reflection/correction of matches) and adds a context-aware collaborative retrieval module that leverages historical interactions to augment LLM recommendations. On two movie CRS datasets (a refined Reddit-v2 and Redial), CRAG improves item coverage and recommendation accuracy over CRS baselines, with gains driven mainly by better accuracy on recently released movies.
