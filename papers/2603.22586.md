## Summary

The paper introduces a foundation model for instruction-conditioned in-context learning of time series tasks, using a quantile-regression T5 encoder-decoder architecture. This model employs a structured tokenization scheme and a hierarchical Transformer decoder that focuses on demonstration pairs, enabling adaptation to various downstream tasks without fine-tuning. Trained on large-scale real and synthetic data with multiple self-supervised objectives, the model demonstrates strong performance across diverse time series forecasting benchmarks and related tasks like classification and anomaly detection.

## Technical note

iAmTime is a time-series foundation model trained with instruction-conditioned amortized meta-learning, where tasks are specified by explicit input-output demonstrations rather than task-specific fine-tuning. Each episode is a structured prompt using specialized semantic tokens that partition [TARGET]/[EXOG] series into history and future-known blocks, exchange information across demonstrations, and inject inferred task info into the query. A Hierarchical Multi-Scope Transformer Encoder captures temporal and covariate dynamics and infers latent task structure, while a Task-Conditioned Patch Decoder adapts decoding via expert-based (MoE) routing. It is trained on large-scale real and synthetic (multivariate-augmented) corpora with supervised and self-supervised objectives spanning forecasting, imputation, reconstruction, classification, anomaly detection, and source de-mixing, improving zero-shot point and probabilistic forecasting over strong TSFM baselines (fev-bench, GIFT-Eval) while remaining competitive on non-forecasting tasks.
