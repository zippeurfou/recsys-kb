## Technical note

This paper presents two techniques for industrial multimodal content understanding in recommendation, search, and advertising. kNN-based Latent Space Broadening (LSB) improves active learning by finding semantically similar hard samples via k-nearest neighbors in the model's hidden-embedding space using a Lookalike Threshold, achieving up to 9% recall improvement at 80% precision. Vision-Language Modeling with Audio Enhancement (VLMAE) is a mid-fusion approach integrating audio into pretrained vision-language models plus supervised fine-tuning, yielding another up to 9% recall gain at 80% precision; both are deployed in multiple production systems with online A/B business gains.
