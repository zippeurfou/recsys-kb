## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of efficiently modeling extremely long user behavior sequences (up to 100K interactions) for recommendation systems, where processing the entire sequence is computationally prohibitive. The proposed framework, ECHO, balances efficiency and relevance by using a Distilled Selector (DS) to filter a subset of a user's event records and a Target-aware Fine-grained Retriever (TFR) to retrieve relevant details for each candidate item. Experiments show ECHO significantly reduces online computation (FLOPs by ~160x) while improving recommendation quality (G A U C by 0.113%-1.520%) compared to state-of-the-art models, and online A/B tests demonstrate positive effects on user engagement.

## Technical note

Target-aware ranking over 100K-event lifetime histories is prohibitive because SIM/TWIN-style search units rescan the raw sequence per candidate while compression methods (C-Former, SequenceO1, TWIN V2) lose item-level addressability. ECHO is a distillation-guided cascade: a user-only Distilled Selector scores every event with multi-head inner products against the user embedding and materializes the top K=10,000 original records (IDs, timestamps, side features intact) to a store, supervised by a candidate-aware full-history teacher through temperature-softened attention-distribution KD plus a boundary-targeted pairwise loss that pulls teacher-preferred misses into the top-K; online, a Target-aware Fine-grained Retriever picks M=100 records per candidate and TWIN-style target attention aggregates them, with retriever and scorer reusing the teacher's relevance parameters. Common-feature sharing, 43-byte packed record encoding and key-side projection absorption cut memory, storage and data movement, and at L=100K the sequence-branch FLOPs are ~160x below end-to-end STCA. ECHO tops DIN, SIM, TWIN, STCA, TWIN V2 and SequenceO1 on all eight tasks (relative GAUC +0.113% to +1.520%, e.g. KuaiRand Comment 0.6347 vs 0.6252), the selector recalls 99.98% of the teacher's top-100 events within 10K retained records vs 57.9% for recency, and a 7-day 5% A/B on top of production long-sequence models lifts total app usage time +0.240% on Kuaishou and +0.373% on Kuaishou Lite.
