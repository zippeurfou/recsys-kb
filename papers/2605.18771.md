## Technical note

LWGR transfers users' personalized LLM world knowledge into generative recommendation via two axes: knowledge extraction using learned soft instructions from optimized product quantization codebooks (with IBQ straight-through gradients), and knowledge fusion via cross-attention formulated as a constrained optimization with explicitly bounded performance degradation, solved by a Lagrangian primal-dual method. It adds two training strategies for different LLM scales plus nearline precomputation with lightweight online serving. LWGR beats eight SOTA baselines (KAR, SeRALM, TIGER) by up to 11.23% and delivers a 1.35% ad-revenue lift and 1.17% CTR increase in online A/B tests.
