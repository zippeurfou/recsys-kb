## Summary

This paper introduces **Lfm4Ads**, a novel framework addressing the limitations of using only user representations extracted from large foundation models (LFMs) for online ads recommendation. Lfm4Ads comprehensively transfers all available representations – including user, item, and cross-user-item interactions – by identifying optimal extraction layers and aggregating them into transferable forms.

To enhance this multi-representation transfer effectively, the framework employs three distinct mechanisms: non-linear adapters at the feature level, an Isomorphic Interaction Module for module-level interaction handling, and Standalone Retrieval for model-level retrieval. This allows bridging the gap between upstream LFM training and downstream ad recommendation tasks more thoroughly than previous methods.

The proposed framework has been successfully deployed in Tencent's large-scale industrial advertising platform, processing billions of daily samples with terabyte-scale parameters. Its implementation resulted in a 2.45% GMV lift across production scenarios, leading to significant estimated annual revenue increases for the company.

## Technical note

LFM4Ads is an all-representation, multi-granularity transfer framework that extracts and reuses user, item, and user-item cross representations from a large-scale pre-trained foundation model for ads recommendation, unlike prior methods that transfer only user representations. It identifies the optimal interaction layer for cross representations and aggregates sample-level CRs into coarser user-/item-level forms via time-interval decaying aggregation to make them transferable. Transfer occurs at three granularities: non-linear adapters (feature-level), an Isomorphic Interaction Module (module-level), and Standalone Retrieval (model-level), with the principle that stronger foundations enable thinner downstream models. Deployed on Tencent's platform (tens of billions of daily samples, terabyte-scale parameters), it achieved 10+ launches and an overall +2.45% GMV lift, with IRs specifically alleviating item cold-start.
