## Technical note

This paper reframes the item cold-start problem from the usual user-centric 'best item for a user' to an item-centric 'best users for a new item' paradigm, arguing user-centricity obscures the ideal audience for nascent content in large, fast-growing inventories. Its realization is an item-centric control in an exploration system using a Bayesian model with Beta distributions to score candidate items on a predicted balance of user satisfaction and item quality. Online evaluations in a large-scale short-video recommender show improved cold-start targeting, higher user satisfaction with explored content, and increased exploration efficiency.
