Large embedding tables are indispensable in modern recommendation systems, thanks to their ability to effectively capture and memorize intricate details of interactions among diverse entities. As we explore integrating large embedding tables into Pinterest's ads ranking models, we encountered not only common challenges such as sparsity and scalability, but also several obstacles unique to our context. ## Technical note

Pinterest scales ads-ranking embedding tables to tens of billions of parameters but found training from scratch gave neutral metrics due to overlap with existing pretrained embeddings (GraphSage, PinnerFormer, ItemSage). They introduce a multi-faceted pretraining scheme combining user-Pin contrastive learning and large-scale heterogeneous knowledge-graph embedding, plus a CPU-GPU hybrid serving infra with INT4 quantization. Deployment achieved 1.34% CPC reduction and 2.60% CTR increase with neutral latency, with pretraining methods giving orthogonal gains on CTR and CVR.
