Ensuring the products displayed in e-commerce search results are relevant to users queries is crucial for improving the user experience. With their advanced semantic understanding, deep learning models have been widely used for relevance matching in search tasks. While large language models (LLMs) offer superior ranking capabilities, it is challenging to deploy LLMs in real-time systems due to the high-latency requirements. ## Technical note

Walmart distills a 7B-parameter LLM teacher into an efficient BERT-Base cross-encoder student for e-commerce search relevance ranking under low-latency constraints. The teacher is trained as a classifier with soft-label targets from human editorial labels; the student learns the relevance margin between product pairs for a query via MSE loss, trained on greatly expanded unlabeled data auto-labeled by the teacher. Student performance keeps improving with more augmented data and can even surpass the teacher; the model was deployed in Oct 2024 as the dominant feature powering tail queries on Walmart.com with positive offline and online metrics.
