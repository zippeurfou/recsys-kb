## Technical note

This paper builds a multimodal session-based music recommendation framework enriching LastFM-1K with three signals: audio and lyric embeddings from pretrained music/text models, LLM-generated semantic metadata (MGPHot schema), and listening completion ratios. It extends the E4SRec framework with these features and swappable ID encoders (SASRec, BERT4Rec, GRU4Rec) and LLM backbones (LLaMA-2-13B, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, LLaMA-3-70B) in zero-shot and fine-tuned settings. Integrating content features improves over ID-only baselines by up to 95% in Recall and 79% in NDCG, though naive multimodal fusion is not always additive; a multimodal benchmark is released.
