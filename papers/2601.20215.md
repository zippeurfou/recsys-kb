## Technical note

EASQ enables end-to-end real-time alignment of ranking models with true user satisfaction from sparse questionnaire feedback, which is otherwise overwhelmed by abundant behavioral signals (clicks, watch time) that are only indirect proxies. It uses a decoupled multi-task architecture with independent expert networks for the main task and the satisfaction-alignment task, plus a lightweight LoRA module at the backbone's lower layers to pre-inject semantic preferences during embedding and protect sparse questionnaire signals. Deployed in the ranking stage of an industrial short-video platform, large-scale A/B tests show consistent satisfaction gains (e.g. LT7 +0.042%, AppStayTime +0.401%) and stable business benefits.
