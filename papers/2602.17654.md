## Summary

This paper introduces the "Mine and Refine" framework to optimize graded relevance in e-commerce search, addressing the challenge of handling user queries that range from exact matches to acceptable substitutes or complements. The two-stage approach first trains a retriever using human annotations to understand relevance levels, then mines and re-annotates hard samples to sharpen similarity boundaries between these levels using specialized loss functions. The framework shows significant improvements in search relevance and business impact through extensive offline evaluation and production A/B testing.

## Technical note

Mine and Refine is a two-stage contrastive training framework for multilingual semantic e-commerce retrieval under three-level graded relevance (exact match, substitute/complement, irrelevant), where clear separation of similarity scores across strata aids hybrid blending and thresholding. Scalable policy-consistent supervision comes from fine-tuning a lightweight LLM on human annotations under a three-level guideline, with an engagement-driven audit reducing label noise. Stage 1 trains a Siamese two-tower retriever with a label-aware supervised contrastive objective; Stage 2 mines hard samples via ANN, re-annotates them with the policy-aligned LLM to avoid false negatives and surface hard positives, and refines with a multi-class extension of circle loss that sharpens boundaries between relevance levels. Robustness is boosted with spelling augmentation and synthetic query generation. Offline (NDCG/recall/precision, better score separability) and production A/B tests show significant add-to-cart, conversion, and GOV gains.
