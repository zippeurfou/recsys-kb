Click-through rate (CTR) prediction, which estimates the probability of a user clicking on a given item, is a critical task for online information services. Existing approaches often make strong assumptions that training and test data come from the same distribution. However, the data distribution varies since user interests are constantly evolving, resulting in the out-of-distribution (OOD) issue. ## Technical note

DiseCTR is a disentangled CTR prediction model that introduces a causal perspective to alleviate the out-of-distribution (OOD) problem caused by constantly evolving, multi-faceted user interests. It performs a causal factorization of CTR into user interest, exposure model, and click model, implemented as a deep interest encoder with sparse attention, a weakly supervised interest disentangler that learns independent interest embeddings, and an attentive interest aggregator for prediction. On three real-world datasets (Amazon, Wechat, Kuaishou) it achieves best OOD accuracy and robustness, improving AUC and GAUC by over 0.02 and reducing logloss by over 13.7% (e.g. GAUC +0.040 on Kuaishou OOD-hard).
