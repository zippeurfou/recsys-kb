## Summary

Bridging the Comprehensiveness Gap for Deep Research Agents":

1.  Researchers introduce DeepSearchQA, a new 900-prompt benchmark designed to evaluate AI agents' ability to perform complex, multi-step information search across 17 fields, focusing on generating comprehensive and precise answers.
2.  Unlike standard benchmarks testing single answers or broad facts, DeepSearchQA tasks require agents to manage fragmented information, avoid duplicates, and determine appropriate stopping points within causal chains, highlighting three crucial but often missing capabilities.
3.  Evaluating current top AI models reveals significant limitations in balancing exhaustive recall with accuracy; common failures include stopping too early or providing overly broad, low-confidence answers, demonstrating substantial room for improvement in deep-research agent design.

## Technical note

DeepSearchQA is a 900-prompt benchmark across 17 fields for evaluating agents on difficult multi-step information-seeking tasks that require exhaustive answer lists rather than single answers. It explicitly stresses three under-evaluated capabilities: collating fragmented information from disparate sources, de-duplication and entity resolution for precision, and reasoning about stopping criteria in an open-ended search space, with each task structured as a causal chain that demands long-horizon planning. Answers are open-web grounded but objectively verifiable. Evaluations of state-of-the-art agents show they cannot balance recall and precision, exhibiting premature stopping (under-retrieval) and hedging behaviors that inflate recall with low-confidence answers.
