## Summary

This paper introduces NAMeGEn, a novel multi-agent framework designed to solve the challenges of creative Chinese baby name generation. It addresses LLM difficulties with multi-objective user requirements and interpretive complexity by iteratively extracting goals, generating names, and evaluating them. Extensive experiments show NAMeGEn effectively produces creative names meeting personalized constraints and providing explanations, outperforming existing methods.

## Technical note

The paper tackles Chinese short-form creative text generation (e.g. baby naming, advertising), where extremely brief outputs give too few cues to verify satisfaction of diverse personalized constraints. It formalizes the task as heterogeneous multi-objective optimization (HMO) over explicit user objectives and implicit explanation-reliability objectives, and proposes MAGIC-HMO, a training-free multi-agent framework (manager, generator, evaluator) that shifts from outcome-oriented to explanation-oriented optimization, using structured explanations as intermediate cues verified for factuality/completeness/clarity. It contributes CBNames and a classical-poetry (CPoetry) dataset, and outperforms six baselines across multiple LLM backbones on the Chinese Baby Naming benchmark.
