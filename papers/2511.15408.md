## Technical note

MAGIC-HMO addresses Chinese short-form creative NLG (e.g., baby naming, advertising), where extremely brief outputs offer limited cues to verify whether diverse personalized constraints are satisfied. Motivated by Brunswik's Lens Model, it formalizes the task as heterogeneous multi-objective optimization (HMO), jointly optimizing multiple personalized constraints plus explanation reliability, and uses explanations as extra verification cues while mitigating LLM explanation hallucination/incompleteness/ambiguity. MAGIC-HMO is a training-free multi-agent framework iterating generation and verification under an explanation-oriented multi-objective strategy, and on the Chinese Baby Naming benchmark it significantly outperforms six strong baselines across various LLM backbones.
