## Summary

This paper presents a new approach, Utility-Aware Generative Recommendation (UAGR), for recommendation systems. It tackles two main issues: traditional SID generation often misses item utility signals, and generation likelihood can misalign with downstream utility during decoding. UAGR addresses these by developing utility-enriched SIDs that preserve semantics while adding utility information, and utility-guided decoding that scores beam search paths based on estimated post-exposure utility, preventing premature pruning of high-value items. Both SID construction and decoding are aligned with multi-objective utility, and the method has been shown to be effective through experiments and has been fully deployed on Kuaishou.

## Technical note

Kuaishou short-video feed. Problem: in SID-based generative recommenders the residual-KMeans SIDs ignore post-exposure utility (long views, completion, watch time, likes, shares) and collide, and beam search prunes on generation likelihood alone, so prefixes of high-utility items are discarded before any post-generation reranker or sequence-level RL reward can act. Method: UAGR (a) Utility-Enriched Semantic Codes replace the final RQ-KMeans level with lookup-free quantization (straight-through binary codes with entropy regularization and a commitment loss, B_s=10 semantic bits) and spend the B_u=3 least-discriminative bits on a bucketized item-utility code that is refreshed hourly with weekly bucket-boundary recalibration and no quantizer retraining; (b) Utility-Guided Decoding adds stop-gradient MLP heads on the decoder hidden state that predict M post-exposure behaviors per candidate token and mixes their normalized weighted sum (lambda, w_m) into the beam score at each SID level, applied only at the current step so utility is not double counted. Results on KuaiRand-27K with a LoRA-tuned OneRec-1.7B backbone (3x8192 SIDs, beam 200, leave-one-out): Recall@3 2.774 / NDCG@3 2.274 vs 2.575 / 1.952 for the best baseline RecForest (+7.7% / +16.5%), also beating HSTU, TIGER, EAGER, LIGER and plain OneRec; UGD alone (R@3 2.578) outperforms both GRPO reward alignment (2.418) and post-generation utility reranking (2.307), UE-SID lifts the collision-free rate (ICR) from 0.957 to 0.990 and beats LCRec, LETTER, FORGE and RQ-OPQ tokenizers, and UGD retains more long_view=1 prefixes through beam pruning. One-week online A/B replacing the production base model: +0.206% video watch time, +0.164% video views, +0.074% app usage time at -0.03% QPS; fully deployed since September 2026.
