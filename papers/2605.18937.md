## Technical note

This study evaluates whether providing Personal Health Record (PHR) clinical data as context improves LLM (Gemini 3.0 Flash) answers to patient health queries. Across 2,257 queries from three distributions (web-search, chatbot-template, patient-call questions) matched with de-identified PHRs, responses were generated with no context, a basic demographics/conditions/medications summary, or full clinical notes, and rated by autoraters plus clinicians (n=95) using the SHARP framework and a new PHR-specific error taxonomy. PHR context significantly improved helpfulness for all question types (p<0.001) with potential gains in safety, accuracy, relevance, and personalization, while revealing error modes like temporal disorientation and rare confabulations.
