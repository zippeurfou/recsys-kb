## Technical note

UniPinRec achieves full-stack unification of retrieval and ranking at Pinterest—one input format, one shared transformer, one training stage, deployed in existing serving infra—encoding the user action sequence into candidate-independent representations that branch into retrieval (ANN dot-product) and ranking (cross-attention) heads. Three ideas enable it: Masked Action Modeling to avoid interleaving/context doubling, blended training examples pairing action sequences with feedview impression slates, and cross-stage KV-cache sharing that reuses retrieval's user-history computation for ranking. In production it delivers roughly +1% online engagement lift while cutting end-to-end serving latency 11.1% and lifting QPS 63.6%.
