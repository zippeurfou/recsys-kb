## Technical note

Single-vector two-tower retrievers cannot serve users with diverse interests, and multi-interest models trained by hard argmax routing suffer routing collapse (under-utilized heads) and provide no per-user estimate of head importance. BACH casts multi-interest two-tower retrieval as a per-user mixture of softmaxes over k contrastive heads, fit by variational inference with a sample-free spherical recognition posterior (power-spherical by default, or von Mises-Fisher); soft responsibilities replace the argmax so every head receives gradient, and an amortized gating tower yields per-user mixture weights pi_u reused at serving, with a global-codebook (LDA-like admixture) variant enabling precomputable retrieval. On MovieLens-20M, Taobao, and Netflix, p-BACH/v-BACH outperform hard-routing multi-interest models (MIND, ComiRec), single-vector, SASRec, and the serving-consistent all-multihead baseline at every head count (e.g., Netflix v-BACH k=32 is +12.7% AUPRC over all-multihead), with learned concentrations self-regularized (kappa ~17-30) without any prior.
