## Technical note

This Alibaba work gives a theoretical explanation, grounded in Rademacher complexity, for the one-epoch overfitting phenomenon in CTR/CVR models built on large-scale sparse categorical features, connecting feature sparsity to sharp post-first-epoch performance drops. Rather than heuristic fixes like MEDA's per-epoch embedding reinitialization (which loses information), it proposes an adaptive regularization method that constrains the norm budget of embedding layers. This both prevents multi-epoch overfitting degradation and improves single-epoch performance, and it has been deployed in online production systems.
