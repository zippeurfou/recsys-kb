## Summary

This study explores how high school students use generative AI (specifically Digital Humans) in debates to foster self-reflection. By designing and observing these AI personalities, students gained a deeper understanding of AI capabilities while critically examining their own thoughts, values, and beliefs. The research proposes "Reflecting with AI" as a new literacy skill, enabling individuals to use AI as a tool for objective self-examination.

## Technical note

This HCI/education study explores 'Digital Human Debates' (DHD), where high-school students design LLM-based personas via prompt engineering and RAG that embody their own thinking, then observe those personas debate autonomously. Using a Research Through Design method with nine students, the authors analyze generative-AI-literacy tests, interviews, and logs. They find that experiencing one's own creation as an 'other' fosters reflective self-examination of cognition and values, and propose 'Reflecting with AI' as a new generative-AI-literacy competency beyond understanding, application, criticism, and ethics. It is not a recommender-systems technique paper; ML content is limited to prompt engineering and RAG.
