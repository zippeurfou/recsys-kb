## Summary

This paper addresses the challenge of allocating inventory in retail stores under uncertain supply chain conditions and dynamic constraints (like receiving limits and order variability). The authors propose a real-time, scalable heuristic Decision Support System (DSS) framework based on set-oriented cumulative filtering to solve this complex allocation problem efficiently. An extensive case study demonstrates that this framework significantly improves order fulfillment rates and same-day coverage while reducing store capacity issues.

## Technical note

This paper frames omnichannel store replenishment under uncertainty as an extended constrained Multidimensional Knapsack Problem, subject to receiving capacities, vehicle limits, product priorities, route-category quotas, and planner-controlled warehouse precedence. Because exact MILP is intractable at real-time industrial scale, it proposes a set-oriented cumulative-filtering heuristic embedded in a Decision Support System with O(N log N) complexity and feasibility preservation. A before-after case study on 212,278 order records (June 2025-April 2026) uses January 2026 as go-live cutoff. Weighted ship-to-order ratio rose from 54.1% to 67.8%, same-day coverage from 24.3% to 37.8%, and over-limit store-days dropped 48.6%.
