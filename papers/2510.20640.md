## Technical note

DiRecGNN is a Microsoft attention-enhanced entity-recommendation framework that recasts selecting which dimensions a cloud-service monitor should track as ranking dimensions over a production-scale heterogeneous monitor entity graph (monitors, metrics, dimensions), which suffers from sparse, long-tailed, homophilic structure. It uses transformer-style graph convolutions with multi-head attention over heterogeneous neighbors and their attributes, attends over paths sampled by random-walk-with-restart to capture long-range dependencies, and adds an attention-alignment plus multi-faceted loss for sparse data. It achieves a 43.1% increase in MRR over existing methods, and product teams rated the deployed feature 4.5 out of 5.
