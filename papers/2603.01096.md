## Summary

This paper introduces **V-SONAR**, a unified vision-language embedding model built upon the existing SONAR text embedding space. It achieves this by aligning vision representations into the SONAR space, enabling competitive text-to-video retrieval and surpassing state-of-the-art models on video captioning. Using V-SONAR, the authors extend the Large Concept Model (LCM) to operate across both text and vision, creating **V-LCM** which combines vision and language inputs. V-LCM demonstrates state-of-the-art performance across diverse tasks and significantly outperforms existing models on 61 out of 62 tested languages, particularly low-resource ones.

## Technical note

v-SONAR extends the text/speech SONAR embedding space to image and video by post-hoc aligning a Perception Encoder into SONAR via teacher-student MSE training over a coarse-to-fine curriculum (12M image-caption, 2M synthetic video, 200K human-checked video pairs). The frozen SONAR text encoder acts as teacher while only a lightweight temporal projector and the vision encoder are updated. v-SONAR achieves SOTA zero-shot text-to-video retrieval and captioning, and lets the text-only Large Concept Model process visual embeddings zero-shot. v-LCM adds vision-language instruction tuning with a latent-diffusion next-embedding objective, beating VLMs on 61 of 62 languages.
