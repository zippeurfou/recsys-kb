## Technical note

POPE (Pluralistic Off-Policy Evaluation) is the first framework for offline evaluation and alignment of LLMs under pluralistic (diverse) human preferences. It defines a unified reward combining a collaborative-utility component (from human preference signals like upvotes) and an entropy-based diversity component for coverage, and derives decomposable inverse-propensity-scoring estimators that separately evaluate relevance and diversity, proven to establish a variance lower bound. The off-policy value function then enables direct differentiable policy optimization; experiments show POPE improves pluralistic response generation without degrading general LLM capabilities.
