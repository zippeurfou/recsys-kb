## Technical note

HiGR is an industrial-scale hierarchical generative framework for slate (ranked-list) recommendation deployed at Tencent. It learns structured semantic IDs via a Prefix-Contrastive Residual-Quantized VAE (PCRQ-VAE) that adds global quantization plus prefix-level InfoNCE alignment so similar items share high-level SID prefixes; a Hierarchical Slate Decoder (HSD) shifts autoregressive decoding from token-level to coarse-grained preference embeddings; and a reference-free ORPO listwise alignment optimizes ranking fidelity, genuine interest, and diversity. HiGR beats SOTA baselines by >10% in offline quality with a 5x inference speedup, and online A/B tests improved watch time 1.22% and video plays 1.73%, serving hundreds of millions of users.
