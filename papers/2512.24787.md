## Summary

1.  Existing generative slate recommendation methods struggle with slow decoding and semantically messy item tokens. The paper introduces HiGR, a new framework designed to overcome these limitations.
2.  HiGR uses an auto-encoder for better item tokenization, decouples generation into hierarchical stages (global planning then item selection), and optimizes slate quality directly using user feedback.
3.  Evaluated extensively, HiGR significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods offline (over 10% better quality, 5x faster) and shows positive online results (1.22-1.73% increase in key engagement metrics).

## Technical note

HiGR is an industrial hierarchical generative framework for slate (whole-page) recommendation, deployed on Tencent platforms. It learns structured semantic IDs with a Prefix-Contrastive Residual Quantized VAE (PCRQ-VAE) so high-level prefixes share semantics, then a Hierarchical Slate Decoder plans the slate at coarse preference-embedding level before a lightweight generator decodes per-item SIDs, cutting the M*D token decoding to yield ~5x inference speedup. Token-level likelihood is bridged to slate quality via reference-free ORPO listwise alignment on implicit feedback, optimizing a triple objective of ranking fidelity, genuine user interest, and diversity. Offline it beats baselines by over 10%; online A/B tests improved watch time by 1.22% and video plays by 1.73%.
