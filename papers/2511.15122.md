## Technical note

MACRec (Multi-Aspect Cross-modal quantization, Meituan) improves semantic-ID construction for generative recommendation by injecting multimodal (text + image) information into both ID learning and generative training. Cross-modal quantization during ID learning reduces token conflict rates and improves codebook usability by complementarily integrating modalities, addressing RQ-VAE's semantic loss in deep hierarchies and poor discriminability of single-modality embeddings (e.g., same-brand items). It further adds multi-aspect cross-modal alignments (implicit and explicit) to strengthen the GR model's generative ability. Experiments on three recommendation datasets demonstrate the method's effectiveness.
