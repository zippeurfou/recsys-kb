## Technical note

This work proposes an LLM-driven multimodal reranking framework for long-tail short-video search at Kuaishou, where sparse behavior on ~20% long-tail queries triggers a Matthew effect amplifying clickbait and shallow content. Using LLM world knowledge for query-aware multimodal parsing (titles, ASR, OCR, covers, key frames), it produces user-behavior-agnostic 'experience scores' via two-stage training: supervised fine-tuning on multimodal-evidence annotations then pairwise preference optimization. These scores drive label reconstruction to attenuate position/exposure bias and shape an nDCG-style page-level reinforcement-learning reward. Offline gains in AUC, NDCG@K and human preference, plus a 15%-traffic online A/B test improving user-experience and consumption metrics.
