## Summary

Existing AI "scientist" systems are often static and lack the ability to learn from past experiments, leading them to repeat mistakes and miss opportunities. To solve this, the authors introduce **EvoScientist**, a novel multi-agent framework comprising specialized agents for idea generation, execution, and self-improvement, along with persistent memory modules for research directions and execution strategies. Experiments demonstrate that EvoScientist significantly outperforms existing AI scientists in generating novel, feasible, and relevant scientific ideas, while also substantially improving the success rate of executing those ideas.

## Technical note

EvoScientist is a self-evolving multi-agent framework for end-to-end scientific discovery with three specialized agents: a Researcher Agent for idea generation, an Engineer Agent for experiment implementation, and an Evolution Manager Agent that distills interaction histories into reusable knowledge. It maintains two persistent memory modules: an ideation memory summarizing feasible research directions and recording failed ones, and an experimentation memory capturing effective data-processing and training strategies from code-search trajectories. Both the RA and EA retrieve relevant prior strategies and append them to prompts, improving idea quality and code-execution success over time. Three evolution mechanisms are introduced: idea direction evolution, idea validation evolution, and experiment strategy evolution. The system reportedly outperforms seven baselines on novelty/feasibility/relevance/clarity, and all six generated full papers were accepted to ICAIS 2025.
